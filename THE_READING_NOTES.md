⇒ **Built in `P10`. If the scalar perturbations are ever rebuilt, this generator is what they perturb.**

**② THE RATE IS FIXED BY $\Lambda$ ALONE.** *the Nariai member "expands by the exact $\sinh^{2/3}$ law of
flat $\Lambda$CDM, **its rate fixed by the cosmological constant alone**", and $\Omega_{m,0}$ "**records
the cosmic epoch at which we observe rather than an independent density**".*

**③ THE INVARIANT IS A LENGTH, NOT A MASS.** *$\alpha=\sqrt{3/\Lambda}$ is the invariant; $2M$ is **the
offset of the cut**, "a turning point rather than a coefficient", and the Schwarzschild leaf is **a flat
vacuum cut**.* ⇒ ***So a mass density in an equation is a projection reading. This is the deepest reason
$\Omega_m$ in the potential source is suspect.***

scalar sector is rebuilt on the layer.***

**⑤ MATTER'S THIRD ROLE: IT FIXES THE FOLIATION.** *"the distinguished cosmic foliation is fixed **not by

has no switch for this.**

**⑥ CURVATURE IS PROJECTION; ONTOLOGY IS $h_{ij}$.** *"spacetime curvature is a property of the

⇒ ***$\Phi$ and $\Psi$ are projection quantities. Their evolution equation must be fixed against the
