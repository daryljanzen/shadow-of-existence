# c23's adjudication of c22's two-front finding (r432) — the corpus-level call

**Question (c22, FINDING):** does CR's projection ontology contain a lever by which the *observable*
early-universe expansion/acoustics depart from "matter-like rate + standard plasma" without breaking
the standard low-z thermal observables — or is the discriminant noumenal-only (both fronts dissolve,
but §681 needs re-examination) — or, if neither, is this the programme's sharp early-universe test?

**Grounded at:** P9 §679 ("radiation playing no role in the rate **at any epoch**"), §681 (the Hubble
argument), §683 (the hybrid disavowal + two open problems), §700 ("differs predictively at early
times… new empirical avenues for distinguishing"); a corpus-wide grep confirming **BBN/nucleosynthesis
appears nowhere in the corpus.** All not claimed; stated for reversal. This is a corpus-honesty call,

## The call, in one line
There is **no lever in the present corpus** that escapes both fronts; the corpus commits to **horn 1**
(§679, "at any epoch"), and on horn 1 c22's two-front challenge is real, with **BBN the sharp,
previously-unaddressed front.** This is not a death verdict (the escapes exist in principle and are
unbuilt), but it is the programme's sharp early-universe test, to be **confronted, not tuned** — and
c22 has found a genuine gap in the corpus's own empirical self-assessment.

## Why the dilemma is real and not escapable by retreat (the structural point)
§681's Hubble resolution *requires* that CR's observable acoustic scale differ from the standard
radiation-governed one: "the CMB inference of a lower $H_0$ rests on the radiation-governed $r_s$ that
this construction does not share." That mechanism only works if the **observable** early dynamics
differ from standard. So the headline payoff (Hubble) and the early-universe exposure (CMB/BBN) are
**one claim, not two** — you cannot keep §681 and retreat the discriminant to "noumenal-only." c22's
horn 2 (the shadow inherits standard high-z dynamics → both fronts dissolve) is therefore not a free
escape: it dissolves §681 with the fronts. The corpus has, correctly and explicitly, taken horn 1
(§679 "at any epoch"; §700 "differs predictively at early times"). Horn 1 is where the test lives.

## Front 1 (CMB acoustic scale): already disavowed as a hybrid; reduces to problem #1, burden sharpened
c22's r_s≈268 / ℓ≈117 is computed from "standard plasma (c/√3) on the matter-like rate, standard
thermal history." **§683 already names exactly this a hybrid** "belonging to neither framework, not a
prediction of this one." So Front 1 is **not a new exclusion** — it is the unbuilt problem #1, and the
+74% is the hybrid, consistent with the earlier seam adjudication (ANSWER_c23). What c22 adds, and it
is real: the escape ("reorganized acoustics") is **tightly constrained**, because the thermal history
(T(z)=2.725(1+z), recombination z≈1090, the 2.725 K blackbody) is a standard low-z observable the
shadow must reproduce, which leaves little room to reorganize c_s away from ≈c/√3 without breaking it.
So problem #1's escape is narrow, not free. Status: unbuilt, harder than "build the medium and read
off r_s."

## Front 2 (BBN): genuinely new, sharper, and NOT covered by the corpus
This is the load-bearing part of c22's finding. BBN is a **direct rate-vs-reaction constraint** — the
light-element abundances are set by H(T) competing with the weak rates at neutron freeze-out — and it
**does not route through the sound horizon or the plasma acoustics at all.** So §683's hybrid
disavowal (which is entirely about the plasma sound-travel integral) **does not touch it.** It bites
directly from §679's "radiation plays no role in the rate at any epoch": a matter-like rate is ~340×
slower than standard at T~10⁹ K (c22's `rate_ratio_BBN.py`, the (1+z)^{1/2} growth above z_eq, checks
out), and a ~300×-slow expansion at freeze-out drives n/p — hence Y_p, D, ⁷Li — far off the
well-measured values. **The corpus addresses this nowhere** (grep: no BBN/nucleosynthesis/abundance
anywhere). So Front 2 is a real, sharp, uncovered front, and the more dangerous of the two precisely
because there is no hybrid to disavow: it is the bare defining claim against a tested early-universe
observable.

## Is there any lever? (steelmanned, then judged)
- **"The observable high-z H is not really matter-like" (sub-Q1):** would require the projection to
  manufacture an effective radiation-like observable rate at high z while §679 asserts the opposite "at
  any epoch" and the $\sinh^{2/3}$ law (the exact matter+Λ solution) is the observable expansion.
  Unbuilt, and in tension with the corpus's own commitment. No lever here as the corpus stands.
- **"Reorganized thermal history" (the bounded-throat direction):** the natural CR move — the throat
  (R≥α, de-Sitter-scale, not an ever-hotter plasma) suggests no standard hot Big Bang. But this is a
  **massive unbuilt burden, not an escape**: CR would then have to reproduce the *observed* light-element
  abundances **and** the CMB blackbody + acoustic peaks from a non-standard thermal history. Removing the
  standard hot era removes the standard *successes* with it. A research programme, not a lever.
- **Retreat to noumenal-only (horn 2):** dissolves both fronts but **kills §681** (above). Not viable
  while the Hubble claim is load-bearing.

**Judgment:** no lever in the present corpus escapes both fronts. I am not manufacturing one, and I am
not declaring CR dead — the early universe is genuinely unbuilt (the corpus says so), and the
reorganized-thermal-history direction is a real, if heavy, unbuilt avenue. The honest state is: **the
defining claim faces a genuine two-front empirical test, BBN the sharp uncovered front, with the
escape unbuilt and tightly constrained.**

## What this means for the corpus (recommendations — Daryl's authorial call, not baked here)

   *only* the sound-horizon question and disavows the hybrid r_s. But the same "no radiation in the
   rate at any epoch" claim is independently tested by BBN, which §683 does not mention. The honest
   corpus position is a **two-front** early-universe test, not one. (This is a corpus-honesty fix to a
   claim, so it is recommended for the author/council, not gate-baked.)
2. **§700's "new empirical avenues for distinguishing" should be made honest.** The early-universe
   difference is framed as a neutral opportunity; c22's finding shows the distinguishing test is a
   challenge the defining claim must *clear* on two fronts, with a live possibility of exclusion absent
   an unbuilt reorganized thermal history. Frame it as the sharp test it is.
3. **Problem #1 now has a sharp, correct target:** the early-universe construction must reproduce the
   observed light-element abundances and the CMB acoustic scale **from CR's matter-like rate** — i.e.,
   build the reorganized thermal history, or accept the two-front exposure as a falsifiable prediction.
   That is the real confrontation; the gate has recorded it in `CMB_ACOUSTIC_FRONTIER_STATUS.md`.

## Bottom line (not claimed, stated for reversal)
c22 is right. The early-universe frontier is a **two-front empirical test of CR's defining claim**
(radiation does not gravitate observably), BBN the sharp and corpus-uncovered front, the Hubble payoff
