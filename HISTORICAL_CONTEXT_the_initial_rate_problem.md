# THE INITIAL-RATE PROBLEM — the objection that was abandoned, not answered
*Assembled r2216 from Daryl's thesis (`resources/PhD_thesis/thesischap1.tex`), at source, not from memory.*

## ★★★ 1. EDDINGTON 1933, *The Expanding Universe* — the objection, in full
> *"There are only two ways of accounting for large receding velocities of the nebulae: (1) They have been
> produced by an outward directed force as we here suppose, or (2) as large or larger velocities have existed
> from the beginning of the present order of things.**[fn 1]** … Several rival explanations of the recession of
> the nebulae, which do not accept it as evidence of the repulsive force, have been put forward. These
> necessarily adopt the second alternative, and postulate that the large velocities have existed from the
> beginning. **This might be true; but can scarcely be called an *explanation* of the large velocities.** …*
>
> *… the theory recently suggested by Einstein and de Sitter, that in the beginning all the matter created was
> projected with a radial motion so as to disperse even faster than the present rate of dispersal of
> galaxies,**[fn 2]** **leaves me cold**. One cannot deny the possibility, but **it is difficult to see what
> mental satisfaction such a theory is supposed to afford.**"*

**[fn 2] — Eddington's footnote on Einstein–de Sitter:**
> *"**They do not state this in words, but it is the meaning of their mathematical formulae.**"*

*The charge is precise: the initial rate is not argued for, it is **carried silently in the mathematics**.*

## ★★★★★ 2. **[fn 1] — EDDINGTON DESCRIBES THIS PROGRAMME'S MECHANISM, IN 1933, AND SETS IT ASIDE**
> *"For completeness we must add the possible hypothesis that **the system once extended much further than
> now, that it collapsed, and is now on the rebound**. This allows the large velocities to have been produced
> by ***inward* directed force, the inward velocities being turned into outward velocities by passage through
> the centre.** **So far as I know, this is not advocated by anyone.** It does not seem capable of providing for
> the distribution of velocities which we observe."*

***That is the branch point, named ninety years early, in a footnote — collapse, and inward velocities turned
outward by passage through the centre — and dismissed for want of an advocate.*** *His stated reason for
doubting it was the **distribution of velocities**; the modern reply to that is the Boltzmann transfer, which
he had no means to compute.*

## ⌗ 3. CHANDRASEKHAR 1983 — the misreading, in the Eddington memorial
*Chandrasekhar quotes Einstein–de Sitter and inserts an editorial gloss to argue they had not rejected $\Lambda$:*
> *"The curvature **[constant $\Lambda$]** is, however, essentially determinable…"*

*Daryl's thesis, at source:* **"The edit in the second paragraph was added by Chandrasekhar in order to support
his argument… However, it is absolutely clear in the two-page article that the curvature of which they speak is
not $\Lambda$, but the curvature of three-dimensional space, $K$; and it is shocking that Chandrasekhar did not
understand this."**

*A misreading, inserted into the record **in the course of memorialising Eddington**, in service of a claim
about the very paper Eddington had objected to.*

## ⌗ 4. HOW THE OBJECTION WAS LOST
*Eddington, Hoyle and Bondi pressed it; all three were associated with $\Lambda$-advocacy or the Steady State,
both empirically disfavoured. **The objection was retired with its advocates** — dismissed by association
rather than answered on its merits. The justification offered was that the CMB requires a hot dense beginning;
**but that settles THAT there was one, not why it was expanding.***

> ***The problem remained exactly as bad as ever. Its proposed resolutions are dead, and no competing proposal
> is in sight. It was not solved. It was dropped.***

## ★★★ 5. WHAT CR SUPPLIES — census **ENTRY 18** (r2215)
*The lift is the continuous physical process carrying the areal rate **from zero at the turnaround to divergent
at the branch point** (`fig:F-triptych`, panels b and c) — and it does so **over no cosmic time at all**
(r2214: $\operatorname{Re}\tilde\tau$ is frozen there). **The universe ARRIVES at $r=0$ with exactly the initial
data Friedmann requires**: vanishing areal radius, divergent rate, divergent deceleration — the last working
against a rate already large enough to absorb it.*

***This is Eddington's own footnote-1 hypothesis, realised: a collapse whose inward motion is turned outward by
passage through the centre. What he could not see was that the passage is not a point but an INTERVAL*** — a
forced phase shift of finite path length and zero duration — ***and that the divergences are effective,
perspectival consequences of continuous parametric motion in $r$, not dynamical causes.***

**And it answers his actual complaint in his own terms:** *the large velocities no longer "exist from the
beginning" as a postulate. **They are produced, by a process, that can be drawn.***
