# The Citation Catalogue — E.1, per paper, the locations wanting a check

*Opened r1142 (Daryl-directed). `THE_PLAN` **E.1**, the last genuine above-line first-edition blocker,
**deferred for ~1000 revisions**. Phase: `THE_REFINEMENT_ARC`. **Aliases:** the E.1 catalogue, the
reference sweep.*

> **What this is.** *"At the very least a catalogue per paper of locations that require a citation check to
> be run"* (Daryl, r1142). **It is a check-list, not a verdict.** An entry means *go look at this and
> decide* — it does **not** assert a citation is owed. Many entries will be legitimately uncited (a
> "Friedmann equation" used as an ordinary term needs no reference); the catalogue's job is that **someone
> decided**, rather than nobody looked.
>
> **The bar is LEAN** (Daryl, r932; restated r1142 — *"I lean light in the need to do this, but obvious
> cases are easy to pick out"*): **not every definition.** A chase-able reference for a load-bearing
> definition or principle that is **not a Google-able named thing**; a **significance-reference** for a
> significant result (the Carter-constant model). Nothing more. **The criterion that decides a door — a
> citation is a pointer to the actual paper, a footnote on the thing, and the prior leans *no* — is
> § THE CRITERION below (r1143). Read it before the judgment pass.**
>
> **⚠ THE PRIOR (face 41).** In a corpus this comprehensive, an apparent gap is most likely a **stale
> link**. So per entry: **the reference may already exist in another paper's bibliography** — check the
> corpus before hunting the literature. `JanzenOperator` (P2, r1123) and `PlebanskiDemianski`/`Stephani`
> (P9, r1141) were all of this kind: the bibitem was present or the neighbour had it, and the `\cite` was
> lost.
>
> **How this was found, and the method worth reusing.** A *mechanical* question — **which named entities
> does a paper name in prose and cite nowhere?** — returns the catalogue for free. It is the same question
> that caught P9's `thm:pd` naming Plebański–Demiański four times with zero citations. **The judgment pass
> is per paper and wants a fresh head (face 41's pause); the gather does not.**

exists to catch:**

- **It decides from the adjective.** Forty sites struck on a *pattern*, without one being read. That is
  map-as-territory (`THE_CODA` §"The standard, mechanically"), the r929 operable-question failure, and the
  r1131 straw man — a global rule standing in for forty honest looks.
- **And it would destroy real citations.** *(Daryl: "There are times and places to cite their papers.")*
  Friedmann's papers **are** owed a citation somewhere in this corpus — the question is **where**, and a
  blanket strike answers it by never asking. **The rule is wrong in both directions at once: it skips the
  work AND kills the references that are earned.**

**The standard, in Daryl's words (r1143):**

> **"Meaningful and earned citations. Properly weighed. Every paper checked before it's cited. It has to
> have a reason not just a name."**

**Unpacked, and each clause bites:**

1. **Earned, per site.** A citation is owed because **this sentence** leans on **that work** — never
   because a name appears. The same name earns a citation at one site and none at the next; both are
   decided by reading the site.
2. **Every paper checked before it is cited.** You do not cite a name. You **check the work** — that it
   says what you are citing it for, at the weight you are citing it at. An unchecked citation is a
   manufactured receipt (`SOURCE_VETTING`; `CODA_FIELD_NOTE` face 1).
3. **Properly weighed.** A load-bearing lean, a significance-reference, an origin — these are different
   citations and get different placements. Weight is decided at the site too.
4. **The strike is a decision, not a default.** Striking an entry means *this site was read and the
   reference is not earned here* — **with the reason recorded**. A strike without a read is the same
   failure as a citation without a check.

**What this catalogue is, therefore:** *(Daryl: "That gives us a good place to start but we need to do it
right not on a few global constraints. **That's the list to start, not the result.**")* — **the starting
list.** Sixty-three doors. Each is opened, read, and decided on its own. There is no shortcut, and the
absence of one is not a defect of the method: **it is the work, and it is why it has lingered a thousand

## ⌗ THE CRITERION — what a citation IS, and the prior on every door
### — unseated., r1143, defining the constraint the "no global constraints" ruling left to be set. This is the standard the judgment pass runs on. Stated for reversal.

**The model, and everything follows from it:**

> **A reference is a pointer to the actual paper — essentially a footnote on the thing.**

That is the whole of what a citation is here. Not an attribution ritual, not a credit ledger, not a
courtesy owed to a name. It is a **door for a reader who wants to go to the source of this specific
thing** — and it earns its place only if a reader would actually want to walk through it *here*.

**The prior, and it is the operative half:**

> **Lean no more often than yes.** *(Daryl, r1143: "I lean no more often than yes.")*

The default verdict on a door is **strike**. A citation is the exception that argues for itself, not the
baseline that must be argued away. This is the bar (`r932`, `r1142`) given its decision-rule: **lean**
does not mean *slightly fewer*; it means **most of these sixty-three doors close**, and the ones that
open do so because a reader following the pointer gets something they could not get from the sentence.

**The three clauses, each doing work:**

1. **A reference is given for a reason.** The reason is *the reader would go there*. If naming the work
   is the whole of what the sentence needs, the sentence has it already — a pointer to a paper nobody
   would open is furniture. **The reason is never "the name appeared."**
2. **A person can be named without a citation. Lots of things can.** *(Daryl, r1143: "A person can be
   named without it. Lots of things can.")* Naming ≠ citing. *Kepler ground Tycho's Mars data* names a
   person and points at nothing chase-able; *the Friedmann equation* names a thing that is now an
   ordinary term. Neither is a debt. **The gather's sixty-three entries are names; the question is which
   of them are doors.**
3. **A footnote on the thing — so it attaches to the THING, not the mention.** The citation goes where
   the *object* is (the formula quoted, the theorem leaned on, the result whose significance is being
   claimed), once, at the site a reader would want the source. Not at every appearance of the string, and
   not on the person.

**Where this lands, concretely (the shape to expect, not a rule to apply):** the doors that open are
where **the corpus quotes or leans on something a reader must check** — a formula reproduced (the
Misner–Sharp/Komar masses), a theorem the argument rests on (Atiyah–Hirzebruch, Wheeler–DeWitt), a named
solution whose properties are used (Kantowski–Sachs, Zipoy–Voorhees), a significance-reference for a
result (the Carter constant). The doors that close are where a **name is furniture** — a term of art
(the Friedmann equation, Eddington–Finkelstein coordinates), a historical mention (Kepler, Digges,
Galileo in P6's narrative), a person invoked for their idea already stated in full. **But which is which
is decided at the site, by reading it** — this paragraph is what to expect, never what to conclude.

**Why this does not reopen the global-rule failure below.** The ruling that follows forbids deciding
**forty sites from a pattern**. This criterion decides **nothing**; it fixes what the question *is* at
each door ("would a reader go here, and for what?") and which way the prior leans when the site is
read. **A criterion is what you take to the door. A global rule is what saves you the walk.** The
strike still requires the read, and the reason still gets recorded.

## The method, per paper (the judgment pass)

1. **Fresh head** — face 41's pause. One paper per sitting. **Paragraph by paragraph** (Daryl, r1143), not
   entry by entry: the catalogue points at the paper, the *paragraph* is what gets read.
2. **Read the site**, and ask the criterion's question (§ THE CRITERION): **would a reader want a door
   here, and to what?** A citation is a *footnote on the thing* — a pointer to the actual paper. Then
   decide, **per site**: earned, or not. **The prior leans strike** (*"lean no more often than yes"*); the
   open door is the exception that argues for itself. If not → **strike it here with the reason.** The
   strike is the deliverable as much as the citation — **and a strike without a read is the same failure
   as a citation without a check.**
2b. **Check the paper before citing it.** Not the name — the work: that it says what you are citing it for,
   at the weight you are citing it at. Never cite from recognition.
3. If it wants one: **check the corpus first** (the prior — the bibitem may already exist in a neighbour,
   or in this paper's own bibliography with the `\cite` lost). Only then the literature.
4. Add the `\cite` at the **first prose use in each register** (abstract, body, remark) — not at every
   mention.
5. **Re-run `depmatrix.py`** if a corpus key moves; **re-run the bibitem sweep**; compile; log; cut.
