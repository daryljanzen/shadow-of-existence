## ⌗ THE BOUNDARY — what this bake did not reach

- **The ordering family itself.** *`P10` states that $\gamma$ attains a maximum of $1/4$ "across the
  natural ordering family". **The family is not written out**, so the maximum is not independently
  checkable from the paper's text. That is the one place a reader must take the number on trust, and
  it is named here rather than treated as verified.*
- **The interacting tower.** *`P10` says plainly what is open — the spectrum of $\hat\Gamma$ and the
  ultraviolet definition of the tower sums — and this bake does not touch it.*
- **`P07`'s global-hyperbolicity argument.** *The information-paradox resolution turns on there being
  no hidden interior sector to trace over, which is a causal-structure claim carried by `P01`. Read,
  not re-derived.*
- **Whether the branch-point verdict belongs in a paper.** *`L-264` established it and this bake
  observes that no paper carries it. **Whether it should is a routing decision and is not made here.***

⛔ **AND THE ONE THING THIS BAKE MUST NOT BE READ AS SAYING:** *the bounce is not a criticism turned
away. `P10` answers every probe built against it, several of them better than the probe. What the field
returns is a routing fact and a coincidence disarmed — and a field that returns that much while
bouncing has still been worth throwing.*
