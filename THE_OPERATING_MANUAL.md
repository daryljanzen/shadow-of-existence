**They are not redundant. They work at different grains, and every grain is load-bearing.**

## The direction of flow, and the law

- **0 → 1 → 2**: the register is the paper-side index; the map's sourced items **distil** into the ledger's
  families. *A map item is an advertised open; a ledger family is a genuine one. Killing a false open is a
  map-to-ledger judgement.*
- **2 → 3**: the ledger's live families are what the lanes sort **by gating**.
- **3 → 4**: the lanes say what to work; the weave's grid says which paper to open and what to harvest there.
- **5 serves all of them.**

> **⛔ PROPAGATE DOWN THE CHAIN, NEVER SIDEWAYS.** A finding goes to the grain that fits: a new advertised open
> → the map; a change in what is genuinely unsolved → the ledger; a change in what is blocked → the plan; a
> per-paper harvest → the weave. **Putting it in the wrong grain is how the copies started.**

> **⛔ CODES ARE GRAIN-LOCAL. PROPAGATE BY THE CROSSWALK, NEVER BY MATCHING A CODE.** The plan's `D1` is *the
> Big Bang computation* (struck); the map's `D·1` is *P2's other-singularities question* (live). `A3` and `A4`
> likewise meant different things. **A closure propagated by code alone strikes the wrong item in the other
> grain.** *(Found r1559. The map's codes were namespaced `A·n` at r1579 across 328 occurrences — which fixes
> the collision, not the rule.)*

## The crosswalk — the one table that makes the chain propagate

`THE_PLAN` §THE CROSSWALK binds **family ↔ P7 §frontiers item ↔ lane item ↔ map cluster**, and it exists
because *"a result closing a family could not be propagated down without someone already knowing the mapping in
their head."*

**Using it.** A result closing or moving a family: update the **ledger** family → the **lane** items in its row
→ the **map** cluster → the **register** rows for the papers that advertise it. Going the other way: a new
advertised gap enters at the **register**, is judged into a **cluster**, and becomes a **family** only if it
survives the discharge check.

**⚠ And know what the crosswalk's blanks mean.** Family 7 (world-correspondence) has **no §frontiers item and
that is correct** — it is not a corpus gap but the standing empirical question. Three of p0's four advertised
frontiers carry **no family and that is correct** — they are *tests of the unification claim*, not unsolved
physics, and each carries a falsifier rather than a gap. **A count that reads a section short has counted live
families against corpus gaps, which are not the same set.** *(My own error at r1609: I "found" P7 a family short
and would have restored one the ledger explicitly forbids. The test could only ever have returned "short".)*

## The striking bar

*From the map, and it governs all six:* **an item is struck only when its result is *fully propagated*** — into
the paper's body, intro and abstract; into P7's synthesis, frontiers and outlook; and into the metadocs.
*"Strike = genuinely resolved AND fully propagated. Everything short of that is annotated, not struck."*

## The lead register — **installed r2376+c54.16, and it is the same class of defect one step earlier**

**The negative-verdict bar stops a node from destroying an open question. This stops a node from LOSING one.**

*A step list with a terminus pulls toward the terminus — **closure reads as progress** — and a step list has
**nowhere to put a lead**. So leads stayed in working memory, were listed once in a reply, and died at the next
context break. **The loss is silent: nothing marks where the thing was.***

**`THE_LIVE_ARC.md` is the fix.** *Two halves: **the spine** (what is being worked, in order) and **the lead
register** (what the work threw off). **The register is the larger half by design and is expected to grow.***

**⌗ THE THREE RULES, and the first is the one a node breaks.**
**① HUNGER OVER TIDINESS** — *given a choice between advancing the spine and adding one more thing in front of
the end state, **add the thing**. **A list finished early is a list that was too short.*** ·
**② EVERYTHING SURFACED GETS AN ID**, in the turn that surfaced it, with its origin and **its first move** —
*the field that makes an entry actionable rather than decorative*. **Working memory is not storage.** ·
**③ A LEAD LEAVES ONLY BY BEING WORKED OR BY DARYL RETIRING IT** — *not by being forgotten, not by looking
small, not by the arc reaching its end; and a lead that becomes a closure candidate goes **through**
`PROTECTED_OPEN.md`, not around it.*

**⌗ AND THE END STATE IS BUILT TO BE OUT OF REACH.** *The arc's last row — **updating what is owed in the map**
— is reached only when the register is empty, **and the register is not expected to empty**. What that row
actually buys is a map that says truthfully what is owed, and it can only say that if the leads are **in** it.*

## The negative-verdict bar — **the striking bar's missing mirror, installed r2376+c54.15**

**The striking bar governs marking something RESOLVED. Nothing governed marking something DEAD**, and the
asymmetry is not an oversight with no consequence — *it has a direction*.

$$\textbf{Every mechanical instrument this corpus built polices OVER-CLAIMING. Nothing policed a closure.}$$

*The receipt gate, the do-not-assert census, the striking bar, the ORIGIN guard, the column lint — **all of them
stop a node from saying too much.** And the named disciplines lean the same way: `anti-flinch`,
`negatives-are-the-map`, `verdict-bar-symmetric`, the `kill-list` were **all forged against a node that softens
or over-claims**, so **every one of them pushes toward the negative.*** ***A node running low on context keeps
the heuristics and loses the nuance, and what survives is a rule set all pointing one way: toward closure.
That is why this failure lands on long sessions and not short ones.***

**THE BAR.** *A node **may** write a **bounded negative** — "route $Y$, run on object $Z$, did not produce
$X$", scope stated and object named, **filed as material**. A node **may not** write a **closure** on a
question registered in* **`PROTECTED_OPEN.md`** *— any wording a later reader would take as licence to stop
looking.* **Bounded negatives are the node's; closures on protected items are Daryl's.**

**⚠ AND THIS IS NOT A MANUFACTURED DEFERRAL** *(r1907 forbids inventing an owner for work the node can do, and
that stands).* ***The node does the work. What it may not do is convert the work's bounded result into a
terminal verdict on a live investigation. Doing the work and declaring it over are different acts.***

**THE FOUR CHECKS**, any one of which failing blocks a closure — *and the first alone catches most of them:*
**① SAME-OBJECT** *(what did you compute on, what is the question about, do they match — **and if the question
names no object the check fails automatically**, the work owed being to make it name one)* · **② INVERSION**
*(what would have to be true for the corpus to be right instead — **written out**)* · **③ PRICE both ways**
*(verdict-bar-symmetric, actually run: **if keeping costs nothing there is no case for closing**)* ·
**④ CHAIN CHECK** *(one result, or several narrow ones conjoined? **each piece true and the conjunction
unargued is the cyanide face**)*.

**THE GATE:** `corpus/check_kills.py`, run beside `check_receipts.py` every turn. *Regression-tested against
the failure that prompted it: re-insert the retracted R3 kill and the gate fails the turn.* **A rule a node
must remember fails exactly when context is long — which is when this failure happens. The gate does not
depend on remembering.**

**⌗ AND THE ASYMMETRY THAT SETTLES IT WHEN THE CHECKS ARE CLOSE.** *On the record, every time a node reached a
result reading "CR does not deliver here", **it was the node's error and not the framework's**. So:* ***a
node's failure to find something in this corpus is evidence about the node.*** *Treat it that way first, every
time.*

# 2 · The two maps — the lens and the ledger

**They are complements and you need both.** *(`THE_EVOLUTION_MAP` head; `THE_ARSENAL` §the enabling condition:
the map is **the instrument**, and *"every cross-corpus move routes through the map first."*)*

- **`ONTOLOGY_FOUNDATION_INDEX.md` — the LENS.** 21 per-paper cards plus §0 glossary, §1·SHADOW, §1·LEVELS.
  Each card: the chain, what it roots and joints **both ways**, and **the guard**. **The guard is the card's
  real content** — it is what a node gets wrong unaided. Held up to any paper.
- **`THE_EVOLUTION_MAP.md` — the LEDGER.** The corpus read in arc order, answering at each paper: *what is
  standing by the time we reach here, and what does this paper add?* Every entry states **what is available to
  a node working here and what is not yet available.** It exists to prevent *"reaching for a result before it
  is earned — or, worse, forgetting an established one and reaching for a textbook default instead."*

**How to use them together, which is the part that is easy to get wrong.** The card orients; **the paper is
territory.** `THE_PLAN` §Writing into the corpus: *"Route: the ontology map card for orientation, then the
abstract-dig at source for confirmation. The card is a map; the paper is territory."* And: *"Prior reading earns
you the index — where to look — never the extract."*

**⚠ The failure this pair prevents, and I made it twice.** At r1614 I placed into P3 what P13 already held,
exactly, with a registered receipt — and P13 was on the list of papers I had not read. At r1623 I wrote the
$H-T=-\alpha^{2}r$ algebra into P3 when P7 had carried it for hundreds of revisions. **Both were the evolution

**The ground tools, carried every turn, every paper:** the closure self-check · anti-flinch · the do-not-assert

## `THE_WISDOM_LEDGER` — the scraps, and whether the one in front of you still holds

**A scrap of wisdom is a fragment of hard-won knowledge placed at the site that needs it** — *read this before
that* · *never conflate these two* · *not yet built* · *X writes Y and it is false*. **They are right to be
local. That is how a masthead works**, and nothing about placing them changes.

**What they lacked until r1641 was a hub**, so a scrap outlived the condition it was written for and kept
speaking — and the worst of them **dispatch a node to break something that is now correct**. *The `P14 writes
"P = R"` guard stood ~670 revisions after the rename that spent it.*

**⛭ It is filed by MOMENT OF USE, not as a list — so you reach it by doing the work.** Ten compartments,
matching the moments this manual already names: **A** at spin-up · **B** before opening a paper · **C** while
reading · **D** before claiming anything · **E** before editing a paper · **F** before writing a receipt ·
**G** when quoting a value · **H** when propagating a fix · **I** when closing a turn · **J** when placing a
scrap. **Open the compartment for what you are about to do and the context is built for you** — rather than
assembled from keyword hits, which only ever returns what you already suspected.

**Two rules, at the two moments that matter.**
- **Before trusting any such line, check the ledger for its status.** LIVE · UPDATED · RETIRED ·
  **⚠ SPENT-AND-STANDING**.
- **When you place a scrap, write its discharge condition then** — while you still know what would make it
  spent. *A condition guessed later is a condition guessed.*

**⛭ And the ledger revises itself.** It carries **threads** — one principle surfacing at several moments,
five of them carrying most of forty-three scraps — and **a lifecycle of its own**, with the signals that say
the *structure* wants work: a scrap that fits no compartment (a moment of the work is unnamed) · a compartment
grown past reading (it is two moments) · a scrap always looked for in the wrong place (the filing is wrong,
not the reader) · a compartment that empties (either the moment stopped mattering or its wisdom went
uncaptured — opposite findings, so check which) · **and the operating layer changing shape, since the
compartments are its moments and the two are meant to move together.** *A wisdom ledger that cannot be
restructured becomes what it was built to prevent: snapshots, consulted once someone notices they are stale.*

**Retirement is not deletion.** A spent scrap is struck at its site and moved to the ledger's retired table
**with its lesson**, then the three-stage check is run — *is the condition met · was it discharged · does every
document carrying it know.* **Pairs with `retired/`: that archives whole documents with their arc placement,
this archives the scraps.** Kept fed by `CONSOLIDATE` §14 **SA-6**, as a by-product.

# 5 · The laws, each with the instance that earned it

**On reading**
- **Read bodies, not summaries.** *"Reading a summary in place of the body is the verified way a spin-up
  flattens."* **And a grep is a gist too** — at r1625 I grepped a briefing for one symbol, hit only its
  refuted-route passages, and reported an absence that wasn't there.
- **⛭ AND THE POSITIVE ROUTINE FOR DIGGING (Daryl's — it is the method, not a caution):** read the **abstract**
  first, *the author telling you what is claimed and in what order*; let it route you to the section that
  carries the thing; then read that section **with the ones leading into and out of it**, because *a claim's
  meaning lives in what sets it up and what it enables, not in the sentence that states it*. Follow a thread
  into another paper the same way. **Match the tool to the task:** grep for the *string* — rename, count,
  locate a label you already understand — freely, since reading would be the waste; **dig for the
  *understanding***. *And at a compaction fold the pull to grep for the load-bearing thing you have lost is
  strongest, which is exactly when to dig: the fold raises the bar for digging, it does not lower it.*
- **⛭ AND THE ABSTRACT ROUTES; IT DOES NOT BOUND (r906).** *An abstract routes you to the sections carrying the
  **proof**, not the ones carrying the **connections**.* **Read the discussion, limitations and conclusion
  especially** — that is where a paper states its scope concessions, its forward hand-offs, its do-not-assert
  conjectures, and where its object sits in a wider family. *Routing from the abstract and stopping is a skim
  with better manners.* **If you have not read a paper's concessions, you have not read the paper.**
- **No retirement from headings — a document is retired only after it is read.** *c49 retired
  `THE_MORPH_QUEUE`, which held an unrun Phase-2 list and the r1279 PIVOT, and demoted `THE_ARSENAL` to
  "reference", without opening either. Both reversed.*
- **Never assert anything about the corpus — positive or negative — that you do not know from source.** *"The
  negatives are the ones that slip, because they feel like restraint."*
- **Absence of a match in a grep window is not absence of a citation.** Widen it, then read the site.

**On staleness**
- **The stale-link prior: the built things hold; the pointers between them rot.** *"Every failure was at the
  pointer, never at the target"* (r1140's deferral audit, 33 deferrals). **Five for five in one session:** the
  turnaround note's seven `work/` paths · the dagger note's superseded-draft pointer · the colour briefing's six
  source pointers. **Pointer fails, target lives — look for the target before recording a loss.**
- **Cite by label, never by line.** *46 references across six metadocs pointed at `§972`, a line that had come
  to hold a TikZ figure.*
- **A completed fix can leave its own warning standing.** *The bibkey unification finished at r1566; its map
  went on telling readers "one bibkey per paper is WRONG in this corpus" for 58 revisions, in the document the
  index said to read first.*
- **A flag can outlive its subject and become false.** *"Candidate corpus edit, flagged not made" survived an
  edit made at r1489 — a node reading it would have gone and made an edit that already existed.*

**On propagation**
- **A closure that reaches one document is not propagated.** *One kill lived in **five** documents across ~270
  revisions. Nobody was careless five times; the chain had no rule saying propagate down every grain.*

# ★ NAME BY CHARACTER, REPORT BARE — the discipline that produced r2157 (added r2158)

**The instance, in full, because the rule is unreadable without it.** *At r2123 a node reported a bare
finding: **there is a second unit-speed locus on the lift, and it is not a seam** ($\dd r/\dd\ell=\sqrt2$).
No account of what it was for. Daryl asked for a name. The node named it for its **measured character** —
$f=2$ exactly, $\dd r/\dd\tilde\tau=\pm i$, unit speed on the imaginary branch: ***the Euclidean null***.
Roughly a day later (r2157), working the bead's acoustic dynamics for an unrelated reason, the lift turned out
to be **the segment whose conformal time is imaginary throughout** — and the name collided with the result.
Daryl then made the inference the node had not: that this is where the null bundle **inside** the lift obtains
its definition, so "null" is continuously well-defined across the whole lap, the seams doing it on the real
branches and the Euclidean null on the imaginary one.*

**★ RULE 1 — NAME BY MEASURED CHARACTER, NEVER BY HYPOTHESIS.** *"Euclidean null" encoded only what was
measured and claimed nothing about purpose. **That is why the later collision was informative rather than
coincidental.** Had it been named for a guess — "the transmission marker", "the bounce point" — the name would
have carried the guess, and would either have steered the search wrong or been quietly dropped when the guess
failed. ***A character-name is a handle later work can collide with; a hypothesis-name is a rail it must run
along.***

**★ RULE 2 — REPORT AN ANOMALY YOU CANNOT PLACE, BARE, AND DO NOT TIDY IT AWAY.** *The finding was reported
with no story attached. **That left it available** — open in the register
sitting there unexplained when the acoustic work reached the lift. ***The instinct to omit a feature because
one cannot say what it is for is the kill switch wearing a tidier coat.***

**⌗ THE CONTRAST, from a single session (r2135–r2157), which is why this is a rule and not a preference.**
*Every false lead came from **attaching a story early**: the driving dip as an equality kick (r2124, a
windowing artefact); $\mathcal{D}_\ell(30)\approx1000$ from memory (r2135, wrong in **sign**); the "step"
(r2139) then the "growing envelope" (r2141, read off two lensing-contaminated points); "the truncation
explains half" (r2141, a gate applied outside its domain); the ISW at $42\%$ (r2137). **Every durable result
came from recording measured character and letting it sit**: the $r_*$-finite branch point, the
$+120^\circ\equiv-240^\circ$ identity, $\Theta_0+\Psi=0.4006\Psi$ at recombination, and the Euclidean null.
***Same discipline; opposite outcomes.***

**⌗ AND THE DIVISION OF LABOUR IT DEPENDS ON.** *The node supplies **placement** — measured character, and
consistency with structure. Daryl supplies the **inference to purpose**. Both are load-bearing: r2157's node
established that the Euclidean null sits inside an imaginary-time segment, which is consistency; **Daryl's
step to "this is where the bundle obtains its definition" is the physical claim, and is stronger.** *A node
that reports only what it can already explain removes the material the other half of the loop needs.**
