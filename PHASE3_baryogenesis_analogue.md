## Worked at source

So the substrate supplies the *structure* (the conjugate pair) and not the *choice* between them.
- **The baryon asymmetry is charge-based, not chirality — walled.** Matter-vs-antimatter as a *baryon* (charge-conjugate, C) asymmetry is a gauge-charge structure, and the gauge sector is walled (su(3)⊄so(5,1), P14). The substrate's $\pm M$ pair is the *chirality/mass* conjugate (R=γ⁵), not the charge-conjugate; identifying the R-pair with the matter/antimatter (C) asymmetry would need the walled charge structure. So the baryon asymmetry is not the substrate's to supply — external, the ordinary route.
- **The choice is R-symmetric — external.** Even at the chirality level, which member (+M "matter") is realized over its R-image (−M) is not forced: the two cosmogeneses are R-conjugate, on equal footing, and the crest is *not* R-fixed (Phase 1; P14 §141), so no parity-breaking event selects one. The selection is external.
- **The ρ_r/ρ_m IC is measured.** CR_cosmology §85/§279: a *measured datum*, the initial conditions inherited (§163) — the η-analogue, not derived. External/measured, as F1 settled.
