## ⛭⛭ 10 · A RESULT TO CARRY ACROSS, not a correction — added r2410

*Everything above is a defect. **This one is not**: it is a result from the main line's R-M station Ⓒ, and it is
offered because **landing it in a paper is the fork's territory and not this line's**.*

**⌗ THE STATEMENT.** *The corpus's two three-fold structures are **not related as covers**.*
*· the **root cover** — base the $2M$-plane, fibre the roots of $r^3-\alpha^2r+2M\alpha^2$, deck $S_3$, branched
where the roots collide at $\lvert r\rvert=\alpha/\sqrt3$ (Nariai);*
*· the **turnaround cover** — base the $r^{D-1}$-plane, fibre the cube roots, deck $\mathbb Z_3$ from
$\tilde\tau\mapsto\tilde\tau+2\pi i\alpha/(D-1)$, **which fixes $2M$**, branched at $r=0$.*

**⌗ THE COMPUTATION, in one line.** *With $r$ a root of $r^3-r+2M$ (gauge $\alpha=1$):*
$$(\omega r)^3-\omega r+2M \;=\; r^3+2M-\omega r \;=\; r-\omega r \;=\; r(1-\omega),$$
***which vanishes only at $r=0$.*** *So $\omega r$ is a root of the same cubic only at the branch point:* **the
turnaround deck fixes the root cover's base and does not permute its fibre.**

**⌗ WHY IT IS WORTH CARRYING.** *`lem:twoturnings` says **no affine change of variable identifies the horizon
roots with the turnaround's** — a statement about **roots**.* ***This is the statement about covers and is
strictly stronger: neither three's deck acts on the other's fibre at all, so they cannot be related by any
covering-space construction over the $2M$-plane.*** ⌗ *And it **retroactively licenses `sec:whichthree`**: seating
the generations on the turnaround rather than the hinges required the two threes to be genuinely different
objects, and **had the turnaround deck been a storey of `X1r`'s tower, the question would have had no room to
move**.*
⌗ *A companion result, offered at lower weight: the two covers' **branch sets are disjoint** — $\pm\alpha/\sqrt3$
and $0$ — which are **exactly the two loci P7 insists on prying apart** ("quantities never to be conflated";
"conflating the two reads a discontinuity into what is one smooth curve").* ***So that naming guard has an
analytic cause: conflating the loci identifies a point where one cover is branched with a point where the other
is.***
⚠ *Not shown: that the covers share no structure. They share $r$ and $2M$, and **both count three because $f=0$
has degree $D-1$** — which `sec:whichthree` states and this does not touch.*
*Receipts: `C2_the_two_threes_are_not_one_tower` (10 checks) · `C1_the_two_covers_branch_where_the_corpus_pries`
(12).* **Suggested homes: beside `lem:twoturnings` in P7, and beside `sec:whichthree` in P14 — the fork's call.**

## ⌗ 9 · A convention worth having, if the fork wants it

*Offered rather than reported, because it is this lineage's to settle and it was never written down tightly:*

> ***`\rcpt{X}` means: the claim in this sentence is CHECKED by X, and X would FAIL if the claim were false.***

*Three things follow: **a script that computes and prints is a NOTE, not a receipt** — what makes an artefact a
receipt is not that it ran but that it could have come out false; **the disposition of a cited transcript is
decided by whether the cited claim is computational** (if it is, the receipt owes an assertion of that claim; if
it is not, `\rcpt{}` was the wrong citation and the sentence should point at the section); and **the definition is
what makes a receipt gate mean something** rather than an opinion about style.
