## ⌗ 9 · A convention worth having, if the fork wants it

*Offered rather than reported, because it is this lineage's to settle and it was never written down tightly:*

> ***`\rcpt{X}` means: the claim in this sentence is CHECKED by X, and X would FAIL if the claim were false.***

*Three things follow: **a script that computes and prints is a NOTE, not a receipt** — what makes an artefact a
receipt is not that it ran but that it could have come out false; **the disposition of a cited transcript is
decided by whether the cited claim is computational** (if it is, the receipt owes an assertion of that claim; if
it is not, `\rcpt{}` was the wrong citation and the sentence should point at the section); and **the definition is
what makes a receipt gate mean something** rather than an opinion about style.
