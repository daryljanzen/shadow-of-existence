**What this is, and what it is for.** Pass 1 pinned each forcing *on its own*. This map reads the corpus

> **What is established by the time the corpus reaches this paper — and what does this paper add to that

The two products are complementary and both are needed:
- The **index** (`ONTOLOGY_FOUNDATION_INDEX.md` §1b–§1j…) is the *lens*: each forcing whole, with its
  chain, its joints, and its guard. Held up to any paper.

  working in paper N knows exactly what it may stand on and what it may not yet assume.

**Why the ledger matters as much as the lens.** The failure it exists to prevent is not ignorance of a
result but **reaching for a result before it is earned** — or, worse, *forgetting an established one and
reaching for a textbook default instead* (the FLRW veer; the collapse stopped at horizon crossing; the
"radiation must source the rate" category error). A node that knows the running total cannot make either
mistake: what is above the line is standing and citable; what is below it is not yet available.

**What it adds — and it is the arc's central object, appearing before anything can name it.**

3. **One curve, continued.** The maximal analytic extension is a single analytic curve — **hyperbola,

4. **The Kretschmann divergence is a chain-rule artefact of the chart.** K=48M²/r⁶ diverges at r=0

5. **THE ONE CIRCLE.** The horizon and r=0 are **the two r-poles of one homogeneous circle** — metric

   in which value the chart's origin assigns each, not in the curve."*
