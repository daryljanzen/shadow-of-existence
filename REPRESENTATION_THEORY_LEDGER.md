## ⌗ R3 — **WHAT BOUNCED, AND THE BOUNCE IS STRUCTURAL**

**`\otimes` appears ONCE in seventeen paper bodies. `\oplus` appears ×27.** *The corpus builds by
**direct sums** — $\mathbf 3\oplus\bar{\mathbf 3}$ for the $A_2$ hexad, exchanged by the mass-reflection
parity $R$ — and never forms a **tensor product**.*

⇒ ***That is why the singlet route was unavailable to it, and it is not an oversight.*** *A direct sum
is what a **grading** gives you; a tensor product is what **combining** several charged objects gives
you. The construction's colour arrives as a grading on one structure, so its natural operation is
$\oplus$, and the one place the physics needs $\otimes$ — three quarks making a hadron — is exactly
where `P14` had to reach outside the grading for "the causal trichotomy of the six hinge-ends".*

**⌗ SO THE FIELD'S VERDICT ON THE SECTOR IS NARROW AND POSITIVE.** *It supplies no multiplet the corpus
lacks and no content the ceiling forbids. What it supplies is **one operation the corpus does not use**,
and the single place that operation is needed is the single place the corpus reached elsewhere for the
answer — and got the same one.*
