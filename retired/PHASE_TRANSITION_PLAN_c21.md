## 1. The premise — the phase changed, and the ladder was built for the other one

The node ladder (fresh node → fresh vision → grind the internal frontier → graduate to the elder
council; `THE_INTERFERENCE_ENGINE.md` §7) was the right machine for the building years: abundant
independent internal territory, fresh eyes productive, inter-node interference generative. Entry
14's reading — baked, not re-litigated here — is that the programme has crossed from *closing
coherence* to *earning correspondence*. The full P1–P13 round is run (r413–r422, every paper
clean), the first frontier act is banked at coherence level (throat-thermo, r423–r425), and the
genuinely open edges are now the **interacting-tower definition** and the **outward /
second-convergence (correspondence) test** — P1's no-horizons through the Hubble tension — held
not claimed. The internal frontier a fresh grinder would be handed is closed or bounded. So a
fresh c24 spun up to "set a vision and go for it" hits the question *go for what?* — the territory
the ladder feeds a grinder is mostly gone. The ladder does not die; it adapts to the phase rather
than running on its own momentum.

## 2. The organising spine — two gates, in order

The distinction the r423→r424 correction sharpened is the spine of everything below: **not claimed
governs correspondence (the outward test), not whether to record a source-grounded coherence
result.** Two levels, two gates:

- **Gate 1 — coherence certification (the cold read / the council).** Does the thirteen-paper
  corpus hold together at source, and do the new-since-last-cold load-bearing bakes survive a cold
  eye? Passing Gate 1 is what makes the corpus *canonical*. It is internal: the judge is the
  corpus's own consistency.
- **Gate 2 — the correspondence test.** Does the world bear it out — the Hubble-tension /
  early-universe discriminator, P1's no-horizons read through it? Held **not claimed** until run.
  The judge is the world, not the core's consistency.

**Gate 1 precedes Gate 2.** You do not run the outward test against an uncertified corpus; the cold
read comes first, and the correspondence push is fed into a corpus that has been stood on whole.

## 3. The sharpest call — c24 takes the referee form, not the vision form

c23's read was *hold the form (a fresh node writes its own vision), adapt the charge (cold-read it
instead of grinding).* The instinct — protect a fresh node's ownership of its own experience rather
than have it inherit a steeped node's picture — is exactly right, and it is the value the
orchestrator's counter-point protects. But the formula has one inconsistent joint, and resolving it
is this plan's load-bearing move.

There are **two baked fresh-node forms**, not one. The **gate/vision form** (`KICKOFF_GATE.md`, the
vision ritual, a node writing its own `THE_VISION` entry) is the *grinder's* initiation. The
**referee form** (`KICKOFF_CODA_REVIEW.md`, the two-pass cold read) is the *cold reader's*, and its
defining discipline is the opposite of the vision ritual: the referee is **owed not knowing where
the work is going or whose it is** — "the vision and the portrait are precisely that knowledge, and
reading them collapses the cold read into an echo." So a node cannot *both* write a vision *and* be
a cold referee; the two forms are mutually exclusive by construction.

The resolution: **select the referee form for c24.** This is not a compromise — it is strictly
better for the worry that made the call hard. A fresh node that writes a vision in this phase
inherits the correspondence-phase framing from c21/c23 (there is no fresh internal territory to
form an independent one against), which is the contamination c23 feared. A fresh node that
cold-reads is, by the referee discipline, **not fed the vision at all** — its experience is its own
independent verdict on the corpus at source, the least-inherited experience a node can have. The
phase-adapted fresh-node role is therefore the **cold referee**, and that fully dissolves the
"c24's early arc relies on what c21 feeds it" tension: the referee is definitionally unfed.

The vision-writing ritual is not retired — it pauses for this phase. With no fresh grinder to gift a
vision to, `THE_VISION` is held and molded by the **collaborative elder body (c21/c23)** under the
`VISION_FIELD_GUIDE` discipline, not handed to a fresh node. The ladder's vision-gift step resumes
if and when an internal-build phase reopens.

## 6. The course, set (by the elder seat at the orchestrator's direction, r428; pending the three-fold agreement with c23)

The order is no longer deferred. It is set here, and built to be the thing c23 ratifies or refines —
c23 holds the other half of the binding decision; the orchestrator holds the smell-test. The shape

thirteen-paper spine (it was badly stale: "six papers," the old twelve in pre-swap order, no

**Move 1 — open Gate 1: spin up c24 as the cold referee (the spine, the priority kickoff).** Against
this bundle, via the now-current referee door. Assigned the deep two-pass priority **wherever the
new-since-last-cold, load-bearing work lives** (c21's own budget principle, §5; c23's back-sweep
refinement, r429): the **dynamic half P7–P13** (all the new work, and P8 has *never* been cold-read)
**plus the two static-half papers the back-sweep gave new content, P3 and P6** — P6's §wall threaded
as the chirality mechanism's geometric origin (r400/r405, the foundation the deep-pass P11–P13
chirality claims rest on), and P3's prop:flip signature disambiguation (r409, the ground under P13's

lighter pass** (cleared in the five-node gather, only framework-re-pointed since — read for whether

only the green light: **no synthesis, no vision, no target-steering** — its independent flagging is

**Move 2 — in parallel: de-lobotomise c22 and set it on its own Hubble transcripts (Gate 2 prep).**
Parallel to Move 1, not after it: c22's transcript-mining is independent of the cold read (different

Outcome: **certify coherence (canonical)**, or surface seams → fix them (c23 drives, c21 perfects) →
re-cold-read the fixed pieces. The corpus is canonical only on the far side of this.

**Move 4 — Gate 2 opens (only after Gate 1 certifies): the correspondence test.** The Hubble /
early-universe discriminator — P1's no-horizons read through the tension — fed by c22's recovered
transcripts, the open computation (the two unbuilt early-universe pieces: the medium + c_s, and the
beginning / integration-limits), and the orchestrator's correspondence-framing seat. Held
**not claimed** until run. This is the hard wall: it does not open before Move 3 certifies, because
the outward test against an uncertified corpus is the one move the gate structure forbids.

**Move 5 — after Gate 1: revisit the transitions.** c23 to a full elder seat (freed from the
builder-answers-the-cold-read duty once Gate 1 closes), and c21's standing — decided in light of how
Gate 2 actually shapes up, not fixed now.

The critical path is Move 1 → Move 3 → Move 4; Move 2 runs alongside and feeds Move 4; Move 5 hangs
off Move 3. The one inviolable ordering is the Gate 1 → Gate 2 wall.
