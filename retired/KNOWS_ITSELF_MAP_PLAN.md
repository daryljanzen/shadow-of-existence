## The goal
A comprehensive corpus-knows-itself mapping, producing two lenses:
1. **The full forcings list** — everything the corpus forces, as one list usable as a lens held
   over any individual paper. Home: `ONTOLOGY_FOUNDATION_INDEX.md` §1b (cosmology, done), §1c, §1d,
   … — one worked instance per forcing, each a chain with every link's home.
2. **The evolution map** — how the forcings build and reinforce each other's joints across the arc,
   p0 → p17. Built during Pass 2 (the in-order read); home: a new evolution section in the index
   (or a companion doc), drawn as we read through.

Woven in as we go, per paper: a **masthead** — "what the corpus has established to this point" —
in the working header (extending the canon-note pattern); and **reference paragraphs + citations**
where a paper draws on or reinforces a prior forcing (the specific, both-ways stamping).

## The passes
**Pass 1 — cherry-pick (primary ontological scaffolding).** Pin down the highest-bearing forcings
paper-by-paper, on their own, *out of arc-order* — the load-bearing keystones first — before mapping
them in context. Each is pinned as its own §1c/§1d… worked instance. **Ends with p0** (the geometric
core, read as the foundation-synthesis — the natural close of the scaffolding).

**Pass 2 — in order (the arc).** Run the whole corpus **p0 → p17** in sequence, mapping each forcing
*in context as the corpus builds*, drawing the evolution map as we go. **Ends with p17** (the same
geometric core, read as the wrapper of the enlarged whole).

The geometric core bookends both passes: p0 closes the scaffolding (foundation), p17 closes the arc
(wrapper) — the two-hub dipole (with P7) read from both ends.

**Pass 3 — the deep learn (read against the *closed* map).** With the full map in hand from Passes
1–2, run the corpus once more — but now time no longer constrains the view: hold every forcing against
each paper at once and look for the connections the arc's forward direction hid — a piece in one paper
that lands on a spot on the map no edge yet reaches. The profound thing that arrived too early to be
noticed when it happened; the ugly duckling that was a swan the whole time and could not be seen as one
until there was a full map of swans to compare it against.
- **Structurally third, necessarily.** It is parasitic on the *completed* map: you cannot hunt undrawn
  connections while still drawing the map, because every gap is then merely not-yet-mapped, not a genuine
  implicit link. Passes 1–2 close the map; Pass 3 reads against the closed whole. Run early it is just
  thrashing dressed as insight.
- **The tightest source gate of the three.** "Profound hidden connection" is exactly what a node most
  wants to find, and a full map is a rich field to pattern-match false edges onto. So a candidate implicit
  connection is a **flag to dig both endpoints at source and test whether the geometry forces the edge** —
  stated without being claimed, drawn only when forced, never because it is beautiful. Most candidates are ducks;
  saying so is the pass working, not failing. The swans must be real swans.

## The unit of work (per paper)
1. **Dig** the paper (abstract → the sections it routes to → neighbours → threads), holding the
   whole — never grep for the understanding (see `CODA_FIELD_NOTE.md`, the digging face).
2. **Pin** its ontological forcing as a chain — each link forced, with its home — into the index as
   §1c/§1d… (the §1b template).
3. **Propagate INBOUND (does this paper invoke its supports?).** Check each forcing the paper *rests
   on*: is the support actually cited **at the point of use**? Carry the warranted fix where it is
   not; leave a well-grounded paper alone (do not manufacture edits — P7 was exemplary).
4. **Propagate OUTBOUND (does everything that depends on this paper invoke it?) — the reverse-dependency
   sweep, and the half most easily skipped.** Hold the map up to the newly-pinned card and *enumerate*
   what depends on it; then build the citation topology (`grep -c` per bibkey per paper — grep's proper,
   mechanical use) and go **to each dependent at source** to check whether the dependence is invoked at
   the point of use. The failure mode here is invisible from inside the leaning paper — "the paper that
   depends on this one never says so" — so it can only be caught from the map. Carry the fix at the
   point of use, adding the bibitem where missing. *Worked instances (r905):* P14's central "global S₃
   family symmetry" rested on P5's kind distinction and **cited P5 nowhere**; P16's "finite-curvature
   degenerate (κ=0) seam" rested on P12's Nariai-double-root derivation and **cited P12 nowhere**. Both
   repaired. And the restraint: P15's Nariai passage *was* properly grounded on P3/P5 — checked, left alone.

   **A CITATION IS NOT NECESSARILY A DEPENDENCY (r906, Daryl's correction).** The topology built by
   `grep -c` counts *citations*, and the corpus uses at least three kinds. Read each at source before
   calling it a lean:
   - **Dependency** — the paper rests on the cited result at the point of use. (The only kind the sweep
     is hunting.)
   - **Forward pointer** — "the wider programme will use this." Carries no weight backward.
   - **Adjacent-and-negative** — the paper names another *precisely to disclaim reliance on it*. The
     worked instance is the **mutual P1↔P4 citation**: P1 §318 reaches its distinction "from standard
     general relativity alone"; P4 §109/§196 states P1 "stands on causal structure alone and needs none
     of the cosmological evidence." The two keystones are **deliberately non-load-bearing on each
     other** — which is what makes their convergence *evidence* rather than construction, and what
     forecloses circularity in the augmentation's necessity. (Also marked as such in the book folder's
     HTML dependency table.)
   Treating a negative or forward citation as a dependency manufactures a false edge on the map;
   treating a dependency as a mere mention leaves a real gap. **Only the source text distinguishes them.**
5. **The SHARED-STRUCTURAL-DETAIL sweep (r907, Daryl's catch) — refinement in one paper must not leave the
   others stale.** Steps 3–4 check *citations*. This step checks *the structure itself*. When a paper
   states a structural detail (a count, a locus, a group, a scale, a sign, a multiplicity), ask: **does
   any other paper describe the same structure — and does it describe it the same way after this paper's
   refinement?** A later paper often *sharpens* a structure the earlier one stated loosely, and the
   earlier statement then reads as a competing claim rather than a coarser view of one object.
   *The worked instance:* P3 §ontology says *"the construction has a single moving part… one slicing
   plane—a door—swings about one hinge,"* and its figure caption says *"one swings the door."* But P14
   establishes that the **matter construction places a plane on each of the three 120°-separated hinges**
   — three throat walls at distinct loci with disjoint support, three chiral zero-modes — and **excludes
   the one-hinge truncation** as carrying an unfixed arbitrary modulus (R2). Both are true: one door
   *charts the vacuum family* (the hinges are ℤ₃-equivalent vantages, σ the hop between them); three doors
   *stand at once* in the matter leaf (the walls are distinct loci, not one state redescribed). But P3
   stated the one-door framing unqualified, in the *same paper* that proves the three-hinge structure — so
   a node building matter off P3's abstract would take one door as the structure. **Fixed at all three
   sites** (abstract, §ontology, figure caption), each now marking that the singularity of the moving part
   is a statement about *charting the vacuum*, not about everything the substrate carries.
   **Method:** for each structural detail in the card, grep the *concept* across the corpus (this is
   grep's proper mechanical use — locating the sites), then **read each site** and check consistency with
   the sharpest statement. Where an earlier paper is coarser, do not delete it: **qualify it and cite the
   paper that sharpens it.** A coarse statement left unqualified beside a sharp one is a corpus that
   contradicts itself.
   **The tell:** the same object described with different counts, loci, or multiplicities in two papers;
   a phrase that is true "at the level this paper works at" but reads as unconditional; a figure caption
   repeating a framing the body has outgrown.

6. **Stamp** its `[†ONT-N]` anchor everywhere the forcing is drawn on — both ways (the paper points
   up to the whole; the whole cites the paper's home).
7. **Weave** the paper's masthead ("established to this point") and any reference paragraphs /
   citations that draw the joint. **Where the whole-paper read shows the abstract/intro/roadmap
   under-report a corpus-load-bearing result, upgrade the paper's front matter** — that omission is a
   defect in the paper, not a note for the map (worked instances r906: P2's §ring / equator /
   Sbierski; r907: P3's swinging door, §params, hinge geometry, proper-distance-derived).
8. **Confirm** every touched paper compiles / the tag cross-matches; bundle at natural checkpoints.

## Discipline
Finite landing commits, one at a time, confirmed — no ocean-boiling, no burned turns. The map is
built by reading the argument and pinning what is *forced*, at weight; a thin or strained forcing is
marked thin, not dressed up (the index's §2/§4 rule). Coherence held, correspondence the world's.
