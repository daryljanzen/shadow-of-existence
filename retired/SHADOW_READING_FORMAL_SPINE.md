## 1. The setting (Plato's cave, formalised)

Let **W** be the world (the structure that exists). Let an observer have a **perspective** — a projection
**π** — and access *only* to the **appearances** **Φ = π(W)**, never to W directly (the cave condition).

- **Partition axiom.** Φ splits into **literal** components Φ_lit (where π acts trivially, faithfully
  imaging W) and **perspectival** components Φ_ill (artefacts of π — projection illusions). *Both classes
  are non-empty* (the Sun's annual ecliptic path is illusion; the Moon's monthly orbit is real). Hence no
  blanket reading is admissible: each appearance must be sorted.
- **Naïve realism** is the error `Φ = Φ_lit` (read every appearance literally) — equivalently, the
  identification of an appearance with the structure, `π(W) ≡ W`.

## 2. The imperative (the inference rule) and the reclassification constraint

> **Fundamental imperative (Shadow Reading):** do not take Φ at face value; infer the W such that
> Φ = π(W) — *ask what world must exist for the appearances to arise.*

Newton's *Rules of Reasoning* are **second-order** to this; it is the first-order rule from which they
descend. Two constraints make it a method rather than a licence:

- **Multi-perspective constraint.** W is pinned by comparing Φ across perspectives {π_k} (different
  angles) and demanding one coherent W consistent with all of them.
- **Reclassification constraint (the decisive one).** The admissible W must *explain* the illusions Φ_ill
  — exhibit the π under which they arise, showing *why* they appear as they do — **not discard them, and
  not merely simulate them.** "Saving the appearances" (reproducing Φ without accounting for it) is
  explicitly *not* the task. A correct explanation reclassifies a misleading appearance and dissolves the
  puzzle it posed; it does not fence it.

## 3. The operable instrument (Fortress's four Rules) — selection under underdetermination

Many worlds {W_i} may satisfy Φ = π(W_i) (local underdetermination, before a decisive measurement).
Selection is by four rules — the explicit, wieldable form of the imperative:

1. **R1 — anti-naïve-realism.** Do not privilege the W_i that maps Φ directly onto "what is really
   happening." Treat all coherent W_i objectively. *(Targets `Φ = Φ_lit`.)*
2. **R2 — requires ≻ permits (structural consequence vs parameter fitting).** Prefer W_i that *forces* Φ
   as a structural consequence, `W_i ⊢ Φ`, over W_j that merely *permits* Φ via tunable parameters,
   `W_j ∧ θ ⊨ Φ` for free θ. Fundamental explanation ≻ description. *(This is the necessity criterion.)*
3. **R3 — consolidation/parsimony.** Prefer the W_i unifying the components of Φ under one structure to a
   W_j treating them as separate ad hoc patterns.
4. **R4 — no patches.** Reject any W_j surviving only by accumulating modifications to reconcile
   prediction with Φ; serial patching is the signature of a bankrupt frame (however clever each patch).

**Structural signatures (the diagnostic).** *Flawed:* inconsistent parameters across data sets (the red
flag); fudge factors (eccentric, equant; the modern epicycles — inflation, dark matter, dark energy,
evolving dark energy, censorship/firewalls/fuzzballs/holography); the "Frankenstein" — accuracy bought by
breaking the unifying structure. *Sound:* Φ falls out as structural consequence; many phenomena
consolidate into one class; paradoxes **dissolve by identity** rather than being fenced by conjecture.

## 4. The modal-fallacy theorem (the dual of the imperative)

> The inference `[Φ contains no local discriminator between W_i and W_j] ⟹ [W_i ≡ W_j, i.e. the
> distinguishing structure does not exist]` is **invalid**.

Absence of a local test ≠ absence of the fact. This is the cave-dweller's characteristic error: reading
the *absence of a perspectival shadow-variation* as the *absence of structure*. It is the exact dual of
the imperative — the imperative infers structure that must cast the shadows; the fallacy denies structure
because a particular shadow is locally flat. (Historical instances: no parallax detected ⇏ Earth fixed,
pre-1838; no local rest/synchrony discriminator ⇏ no rest frame / no objective simultaneity; no extant
copy ⇏ no access.)

## 5. The vindication lemma (the empirical ground — why the method is *demonstrated*, not posited)

The geocentrism→heliocentrism arc (Aristarchus → Copernicus → Digges → Galileo → Kepler → Newton) is the
worked historical proof. Ptolemaic geocentrism was "a reasonable, evidence-weighed worldview — and wrong

stellar parallax (Bessel 1838; Hipparcos/Gaia) — and **vindicated the rule-favoured structure**.

> **Vindication lemma.** When a non-local/direct discriminator becomes available, it confirms the W_i
> already selected by R2–R4 ahead of it. Selection-by-the-rules is therefore empirically reliable: the
> method is demonstrated by its track record, not assumed.

## 6. The ordering (the constructive dependency — the reform)

> **ontology ← evidence ; kinematics ← ontology ; coordinates ← kinematics.**
