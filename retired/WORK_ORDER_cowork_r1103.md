## THE CONSTRAINTS — hard-won, and they bind all three

1. **★ Do not farm these to parallel readers against a written spec.** That is exactly what the r1063
   sweep did — eleven papers, seven farmed out, one delta-spec — and it returned **"not one edit
   needed"**, which was **the method reporting on itself**. You hold the whole; the readers don't.
   A reader handed the answer as a spec can only return compliance. (`THE_METHOD` §4b, the **perimeter
   defect** — new in this bundle, and it is why your own Δ6 could exist.)
2. **★ Before any sweep, name the finding that would falsify its spec.** If you cannot, it is a
   compliance check and can only return clean. **Write the falsifier down before you start.**
3. **The gradient.** The corpus is the thing under examination, **never the referee**. Forward documents
   are ahead by construction; where they disagree with a paper, that disagreement is **the work product**.
   *"Stale" is a word for the corpus.* **And its complement:** the gradient rule is **not licence** — a
   forward document can be wrong about what it is holding, and when the corpus catches that, fold and
   log it as *genuine input*. Discriminator: **could re-reading have supplied it?**
4. **Restraint is a result — with its discriminator.** A sweep that *could* have returned work and
   didn't is a verdict. One that *could not* is the tell. **All-clean right after a major upgrade is a
   symptom.**
5. **The register's holes, stated so a clean run is not over-read:** it finds reasons that were
   **killed, registered, and still in the registered words**. It cannot see an unregistered death, a
   paraphrase, or a reason living in your head — **which is where all three of the r1088 firings lived.**
   A clean run means *"no registered corpse in registered language."* **Say that, never "clean."**
6. **★ Staleness is a tracer, and it is worth exploiting deliberately.** A document nothing references
   is a document nothing corrects, so corpses pile up exactly where the web has broken. **The register
   is a cheap orphan detector** — it is how `THE_P13_POSITIVE_CLOSURE_ARC` was found (an orphan in the
   chat node's bundle, referenced by nothing, silently rebuilt from scratch). **If a hit lands in a
   document nothing points at, wire it in the same turn.**
