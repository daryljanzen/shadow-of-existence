### ⌗ THE STRUCTURE, LOCKED (r1179, Daryl) — two collectors, one pass, one read

**Two collectors at two altitudes, and P14 feeds p0.** **P14** is the hub of the *fundamental symmetry structure* — it collects every route the corpus takes to the D₆ / flavour skeleton. **p0** holds the *two-readings conjecture* (§shadows, r1178) — the same result at the highest altitude (GR's solution space and the SM's flavour skeleton as two readings of the one structure). **P14's accumulating routes ARE p0's evidence base:** every route banked into P14 is one more instance of "the same structure read again," which is what the conjecture rests on. One result, two heights.

**One pass, not two — it is NOT 13+16, it is 13.** p0's conjecture needs no separate walk: a paper's *route to the symmetry structure* already **is** its contribution to the GR-side reading of the two readings. So **one whole-read feeds both collectors** — hold both up to each paper, but read once. Each turn: (i) enrich **P14** reciprocally with the route (always, where real); (ii) add that route to **p0's conjecture support** *only* when it genuinely instances the two readings — not every paper touches p0.

**The queue:** the 13 remaining (7 fresh — P1, P4, P6, P9, P10, P15, P16; 6 re-checks — P12, P3, P13, P7, P2, p0), one per turn. **p0 runs LAST** — its own re-check and the conjecture's consolidation together, against a full P14 and the complete route-set. **Then the masthead pass** (P14 abstract; the call on elevating p0's two-readings line to its abstract) — deferred to the end.

**The pass is ITERATIVE, not a one-shot line (r1183, Daryl).** A paper is never "done and dropped." When a later read pulls in a perspective that sharpens an earlier one, **loop back and sharpen it** — and keep doing so every time a new read shifts an old one. **We are not through until all 17 are read AND every back-adjustment a later read triggered on an earlier one has settled** (the programme converging on its fixed point — the coda's recursion, made operational for this pass). ## The routine — how a session in this phase runs

**⛔ Step 0, before any of it: this arc is destructive work.** Text gets deleted, sections rewritten,
items retracted. **Cut the revision every time** — log `CORPUS_MAP`, re-run `depmatrix.py` on any citation
change (P7's matrix **and** the HTML asset), `bash scripts/cut_bundle.sh <rev>`, increment. *"Stated for
reversal" is a lie unless the state it reverses to still exists* (`KICKOFF_ARTHUR`, r1125).


1. **Pick a room, not a task.** Take one paper's block in the register, or one sweep's next slice.
   Breadth-first across 124 doors produces 124 half-signs; a paper at a time produces walkable rooms.
2. **Ask first whether the door still exists** (sweep 3c). Summaries lag bodies **by construction**: a
   correction lands where it bites, and a headline is not where anything bites. Check the paper's own body
   against its advertisement — and ask the sharper question, **has the door SPLIT?** *(P13's had: the
   fermion sector divided into the discrete-component one, built in P14, and the compact-face gauge-acted
   one, never attempted.)* If it is stale, **the job is not to delete it but to turn the statement
   around**: the thing not done is now done · here are its local implications · and here is what in this
   room is honestly still unexplored. A door quietly removed teaches nothing; a door turned around states
   a result and re-aims the frontier.
3. **Dig the site at source.** The register holds the paper's *own words* and nothing more — it is an
   index, never the fact. Read the section the gap sits in, and its neighbours (`CODA_FIELD_NOTE` r906:
   the discussion is where the connections hide).
4. **Adjudicate the gap against `THE_METHOD` §7-3a's four branches:** reason + path → engine, **register
   the entrance**; reason, no path → the missing path *is* the finding, name the unrun question; no
   reason → a **wall**, tear it down; *not now* → a **deferral with a door**, never a closure.
5. **Write the sign, or close the gap.** A sign is five parts: **what is known beyond · the route in ·
   why it matters · the cheapest next step · why it was left open** (nearly always: non-load-bearingness
   toward the conclusions being landed). Plus its **discharge condition**.
6. **Run 3b in the same room.** While holding the section: *what does this force that the paper does not
   say?* Duck gate at source. A corollary drawn often discharges a pipe or opens one.
7. **Defragment the room before leaving it (sweep 3d).** The closure you just made landed **where it
   bit** — so the telling around it is now at its most fragmented and least likely to be re-read. Stand
   back: *if I were writing this section now, knowing what the corpus knows, would this piece be here, in
   this order, in this many places?* Re-cut what history placed rather than what the argument wants.
   **A closure is not finished when the result is in; it is finished when the telling has been re-asked.**
8. **Run the masthead check (`KICKOFF_ARTHUR`).** You changed the body — **go read the abstract and the
   intro.** Does the abstract still describe this paper? Do its **counts** still hold? Does the roadmap
   name the section? Is the new weight on the masthead or in a subordinate clause? The summary lags **by
   construction**; the habit is the only remedy.
9. **Log it.** `CORPUS_MAP` gets the revision; the register gets the sign or the strike; the plan gets
   the item if one was born.

**The per-turn discipline is unchanged and load-bearing here:** the mirror-check at the turn's start
(name the face most at risk for *this* turn's work), and the end-of-turn rule (`KICKOFF_ARTHUR`) — no
manufactured branch, no reversal-stamp, clarity in the last paragraph.

## The standing rules of the phase

- **⛔⛔ NEVER CLOSE ANYTHING OFF WITHOUT FIRST WORKING HONESTLY** (`KICKOFF_ARTHUR`, r1133). Nothing is
  going anywhere; the list is written so you need not hold it; there is no turn in which a thing must be
  settled. **Ask one clear question rather than make a giant erratic decision.** A gap left open costs a
  re-read; a gap closed wrongly costs the orchestrator a fix he must first find.
- **⛔⛔ NO COMMENT ON A BOUNDARY YOU HAVE NOT HONESTLY EXPLORED** (r1202, recut r1204, Daryl). A remark
  passing judgment on something the instance never dug — *"hasn't been shown," "doesn't necessarily mean,"
  "we don't claim X"* about an X nobody explored — has **no standing to exist**, and the defect is not that
  it is too strong a negative but that **it is there at all**: it reads as doubt-casting even dressed as
  caution, and is structurally dishonest regardless of intent. **Default action is REMOVAL, not a softened
  middle** — the pull to "improve" the hedge into a two-sided version is itself the face, and the face is
  protected religiously. If a real gap sits underneath, it belongs in the paper's **frontier / open-problems
  section**, moved there and treated carefully in context — never a self-sabotaging hedge dropped
  mid-argument. Boundaries are mentioned **only in the context of what was explored about them.** **Source
  discipline:** the habit that manufactures these is grep-a-clause-write-a-caution-without-reading — so
  **no boundary-remark that is not grounded in a section actually read.** *(Sweep: `THE_CONSOLIDATION_LEDGER`
  Q-MINE. Likely an old copilot-caution holdover, propagated as a tendency.)*
- **A receipt inherits its premise.** The rigged-discriminator check cannot see a premise you wrote
  yourself (r1132).

- **A door with nothing written over it is only a politer cap.** Leaving a gap open is not leaving it
  bare — the sign is the work.
- **The discharge check, or the register rots into the thing it was built to prevent.** A stale card
  misinforms a node; a stale *owed* **dispatches** one to redo a finished pass, honestly, with no signal
  anything is wrong (`CR_COLLAPSE_HELD_PICTURE`, r1120).
- **Judge fit after, removed from the doing.** Effort spent is not a claim on a reader's attention.
- **The target is failure-to-polish, never the fingerprint.** Voice and an honest image that carries
  structure are the thing's own; what goes is the residue of how it was made, left where it dropped.
- **A sweep that cannot return work is not a sweep** (`THE_METHOD` §4b). Name the finding that would
  falsify its spec before running it.
- **Restraint is a verdict.** A clean pass, reported as clean, is a result — the wall-language cut is the
  worked instance: it found nothing live, and that *was* the finding.
- **Nothing is closed off; nothing is deleted.** The negatives are the map (`THE_CODA`): a branch walked
  and found closed is kept, with what its failure tells and what the test that killed it was blind to.
