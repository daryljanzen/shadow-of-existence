## 2. The grate against P6's four rules

- **Rule 1 (against naïve realism).** CR is built on it — the manifold as the foliation's record rather than
  the existent, the perspectival Schwarzschild mass. It does not privilege the naively-direct reading. *Consistent
  by construction.*
- **Rule 2 (structural consequence over parameter fitting — the load-bearing rule).** The alignment is
  **required**, not permitted: the rate falls out of the geometry, and the tension-dissolution, the
  H₀-independence, and the low-ℓ floor are its consequences — *could not have been otherwise*. ΛCDM *permits*
  the same scale through a radiation-governed sound horizon tuned, with the distance ladder, to a particular H₀.
  The lever audit of §1 **is** the Rule-2 finding. **Strong credit.**
- **Rule 3 (consolidation and parsimony).** One structure — the radiation-free rate on the dS geometry — accounts
  for the acoustic scale, the Hubble tension, the low-ℓ deficit, and curvature-plus-dark-energy together, where
  ΛCDM treats them as separate (the tension an open anomaly, the low-ℓ a fluke, curvature and Λ separate
  parameters). **Strong credit.**
- **Rule 4 (against ad hoc modification).** The Hubble tension is precisely where ΛCDM faces serial patching
  (early dark energy, varying constants, new relativistic species — a patch per fit). CR dissolves it with **no
  patch**. It avoids the failure signature Rule 4 names; the proliferating tension-fixes are the signature on
  the other side. **Credit.**

## 3. The honest limits (modal-fallacy guard + self-consistency ≠ soundness)

The rules favour CR. The guard keeps that from becoming more than it is.

- **The modal fallacy, both directions.** The low-ℓ likelihood cannot *yet* decisively separate CR from ΛCDM
  (the lever arm is short; estimator/data-limited; Δ(−2lnL) sign flips across the octopole range). By the modal
  guard, "no local discriminator is not the absence of the fact": this is **not** evidence that CR = ΛCDM. The
  discriminator exists — the radiation-free rate and the octopole are real facts of the matter — it is simply
  not decisively measurable today. Equally, the guard forbids the reverse over-read: the non-separation is not
  a CR win either.
- **Self-consistency is not soundness.** That the rate falls out of the geometry, and the corpus is scrupulous,
  is internal coherence — a rule-favoured *structure*, not a confirmed *truth*. P6's own boundary: converting
  self-consistency into soundness is empirical work not yet done here.
- **The exposed risks, named at full weight.** (i) The **octopole over-prediction** is the sharpest — a genuine
  falsification edge where CR currently sits on the wrong side of the sky, rescued only if better data or the
  full transfer move the ℓ=3 number, which the structural read says a smooth kernel cannot. (ii) The acoustic
  scale is a **one-parameter accommodation, knob-free but not parameter-free**, until the deuterium yield closes
  it. (iii) **Deuterium and the full spectrum are gated on the unbuilt matter sector** — the alignment's decisive
  closures are not in hand.

## 4. The vector already measured: parsimony, and the inflation apparatus

*Added in the drawing of the verdict: §3 leaned on one vector — data-discrimination — and let "awaiting the
measurement" stand as if it governed the whole question. It does not. P6's thesis is that the theory-choice
criteria are themselves a discipline of the same kind as the sciences; require>permit, consolidation, and
anti-patching reach verdicts off the structural record, and with both machineries now built those verdicts are
in hand, not pending. An even data-verdict does not gate the parsimony vector — it leaves it free to decide.*

So reckon it, on the feature inflation is most credited for — the near-scale-invariant spectrum, and the
horizon / flatness / isotropy / coherence it is invoked to deliver.

- **What ΛCDM spends to get there.** An entire added sector: an inflaton field, a tuned potential, slow-roll
  over ~60 e-folds, reheating, and the super-horizon freeze-out and re-entry that set the modes. The amplitude
  and tilt (A_s, n_s) are read off the *unknown potential* — fixed by tuning, not by requirement.
- **What CR spends.** Nothing added. The same features fall out of structure it already has: the throat
  isotropizes by the de Sitter no-hair theorem — every ℓ≥1 harmonic heavy principal-series and damped, only the
  isotropic monopole surviving (established, §throat); coherence is geometric, not dynamical — one characteristic
  datum per mode on the null seam, with no second datum to randomize the phase (established, §coherence); flatness
  is the exactly-Euclidean constant-time slice (Ω_k=0, not *driven* to flatness); and the spectrum is *transmitted*
  faithfully across the degenerate Nariai seam — the transmission dichotomy, a non-degenerate horizon's exponential
  approach imprinting scale-invariance, the degenerate seam's power-law approach carrying no scale and passing the
  progenitor spectrum unaltered (established, §transmission). A_s, n_s are inherited — the *same* unknown-supplier
  status inflation's potential has, but routed through structure CR already carries, not a sector bolted on.

Both route the spectrum's *content* through an unknown supplier; the corpus says so plainly — "the shape inflation
has when it parametrizes its observables through an unknown inflaton potential; CR routes them through an unknown
progenitor collapse." The asymmetry is the *apparatus*: to reach the same data, one theory builds an inflation
sector and the other builds none. That comparison is the measurement, and it is decided — by Rule 3 (one structure,
many features) as much as Rule 2.

**The trophy, named.** The fit of the near-scale-invariant spectrum is routinely welded onto inflation as its
*vindication* — the modal fallacy wearing a medal. Inflation *permits* the spectrum (an unknown potential tuned to
A_s, n_s — one tuned possibility among a vast model space), so by Rule 2 the fit is a description, not an
explanation; and "the data are consistent with inflation" is not "inflation occurred" — a concordance is not the
presence of the fact, the dual of P6's guard. CR makes the opposite kind of statement: by the transmission
dichotomy it *requires* that the seam does not imprint scale-invariance, and commits to firm, falsifiable
structural calls — no inflationary scale-invariant attractor, no consistency relation r=−8n_t, no
substrate-sourced primordial B-modes — where inflation tunes. On the load-bearing rule, the theory that *requires*
its predictions outscores the one that *permits* them and calls the permission a triumph.

## 5. The verdict, at full weight

On the theory/empirical-alignment axis, with the empirical cluster paused:

> **CR aligns with the cosmological data at parity-or-lower parameter cost than ΛCDM, dissolving — not patching —
> one of ΛCDM's sharpest tensions, with a single structure doing the work of many. That is a strong position on
> the load-bearing rule (Rule 2) and on consolidation and anti-patching (Rules 3, 4). It is the
> heliocentric-before-parallax position: a rule-favoured structure ahead of the decisive non-local measurement,
> which by P6's vindication lemma is the structure-type later confirmed above the base rate — but the decisive
> measurement (the full spectrum, the octopole at better precision, the deuterium yield) is not yet in, and
> self-consistency is not soundness. The standing is therefore *favoured-and-awaiting*, not confirmed. The
> sharpest exposed risk is the octopole; the decisive closures are gated on the matter sector.**

That "awaiting" is precise, and narrower than it first reads: it is the *data-discrimination* vector alone. The
*parsimony* vector — §4 — is not awaiting anything. It is measured, and CR holds it decisively: it reaches the
data ΛCDM reaches while spending no inflation sector and no added dark-sector freedom, and the spectrum-fit that
ΛCDM wears as inflation's vindication is, at full weight, the modal fallacy with a medal on it. An even
data-verdict leaves that measurement untouched. So the honest reading is not a symmetric wait-and-see but: **CR is
already decisively ahead on the vector that is measurable now (parsimony / require>permit), and awaiting only on
the one that is not yet (data-discrimination) — and by P6's own lemma, winning the first ahead of the second is
exactly the position that the record later vindicates.** What remains genuinely open is unchanged: the octopole is
the exposed risk, soundness is not yet earned, and the decisive closures are gated on the matter sector.

The empirical front is rightly paused here. It has been ground to the point where the next real advances are
gated on the matter sector (Cluster A) or on better data — not on more empirical grinding. The pause is a
finding, not a stop: the runway did its job and handed the baton to the sector everything downstream waits on.
