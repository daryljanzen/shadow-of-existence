## ⌗ WHAT THAT MEANS FOR RULE 2, AND IT IS NOT WHAT EITHER SIDE WOULD WANT

**⌗ IT IS NOT A LICENCE.** *A coin-flip base rate means a least-arbitrariness argument is **not evidence on its
own**. Any programme resting its central claim on one such argument and nothing else is at even odds, and the
corpus should not represent Rule 2 as more than that.*

**⌗ IT IS ALSO NOT A DEBUNKING**, *and the failure mode of this exercise would be to treat it as one. **50% is
extraordinarily high for a heuristic that costs nothing to apply.*** *Most methods of generating physical
hypotheses do far worse; the argument form has produced the neutrino, the antiparticle, colour, charm, the
third generation and general covariance.*

**⌗ AND THE DISCRIMINANT IS VISIBLE IN THE TABLE, which is the actual finding.** *Sorting by outcome, the
successes and failures differ systematically:*

| | the successes | the failures |
|---|---|---|
| **what was called arbitrary** | *an **exception** to a rule already in force* (Maxwell: continuity; Pauli: conservation; Dirac: discarding solutions; Noether) | *an **absence of explanation** for a free parameter* (Kepler's spacings; $SU(5)$'s hypercharges; SUSY's hierarchy) |
| **what was proposed** | *the removal of the exception* | *a new structure to explain the number* |
| **the tell** | ***the argument DELETES something*** | ***the argument ADDS something*** |

***Least-arbitrariness arguments that remove an exception succeed; ones that add machinery to explain a number
fail.*** **That is the base rate's real content, and it is sharper than the ratio.**

## ⌗ AN ENTRY OF A DIFFERENT KIND (added c54.114) — *what makes a speculation worth writing down, measured on ten of them*

**⚑ THE OCCASION.** *Cluster J is one speculative document's preserved yield, grated into the map at

| settled | what it claimed | how it settled |
|---|---|---|
| `J·9` | the exit at the branch point has **three** options | **count confirmed and forced**: the monodromy is $\mathbb{Z}_3$ |
| `J·8` | the seat/operator interlock is **semidirect** | **negative**: $T$ and $R$ commute, so direct |

***Both made a CHECKABLE STRUCTURAL CLAIM — a cardinality and a group extension.*** *Each has a
definite answer that a computation can return, and each got one. `J·9`'s count was right and its

**⌗ THE EIGHT THAT DID NOT MOVE ALL PROPOSED AN INTERPRETATION RATHER THAN A STRUCTURE.** *"Mass is

## ⌗⌗ THE TWENTY-FIRST ENTRY (added c54.158) — *the sweep, and the instrument that had to exist before it*

**⚑ THE CASE.** *The assertion debt is being cleared by dispatched workers — 48 receipts this revision, $172\to124$.
The work is mechanical enough to dispatch, and ***that is precisely what makes it dangerous***. A worker asked to
make a file "able to fail" can satisfy the count with a check that cannot fail: `assert True`, `assert x == x`,
or the subtler and far commoner form*

```
y = <expression>
assert abs(y - <the same expression>) < tol
```

***which re-derives the value it is testing and therefore tests the interpreter.***

**⌗ SO THE LINT CAME FIRST, AND BEFORE ANY OF THE SWEEP WAS WRITTEN.** *`scripts/lint_assertions.py` parses
every assertion in the tree and classifies it **HOLLOW** (provably cannot fail), **UNPINNED** (can fail, but
