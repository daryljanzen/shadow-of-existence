\subsection{The signature at the directly measured rate}\label{sec:refit-bound}

Two features of this cosmology's acoustic sector are measured against the sky here, and they
come out differently. The spacing of the peaks is right; the phase of the series is not. This
section states both at the strength the instrument supports, and separates what is a statement
about this cosmology from what is a statement about the instrument used to test it.

\paragraph{The instrument, and why it has two arms.} The comparison is between the two arms of
one instrument---this cosmology's spectrum and a $\Lambda$CDM control carried through the same
transfer---rather than between this cosmology's spectrum and a Boltzmann code's, which would
charge the construction for the instrument's own error\rcpt{P15_two_arm_control_and_guard}. The
transfer is a line-of-sight integration on a photon hierarchy\rcpt{P15_the_line_of_sight_transfer,
P15_the_photon_hierarchy_in_the_transfer}, with the diffusion damping restored from the standard
tight-coupling integral, $k_D^{-2}=\int[R^{2}/(1+R)+8/9]\,\dd\eta/[6(1+R)\tau']$, evaluated on
each arm's own opacity and rate\rcpt{P15_the_derived_diffusion_damping}. That returns
$r_D=6.58$~Mpc where a Boltzmann code gives $6$--$7$~Mpc, with nothing
fitted\rcpt{P15_the_control_entered_the_regime_and_the_arm_did_not_move}.

\paragraph{The spacing is right, and it does not move.} The ratio
$\ell_{1}/\ell_{A}=0.5703$ holds through six distinct states of the instrument---a delta-function
transfer, a line-of-sight transfer, a derived damping envelope, a scan of that envelope's scale, a
derived shear coefficient, and the full hierarchy---to four
figures\rcpt{P15_the_spacing_is_right_and_the_acoustic_phase_is_wrong}. It survives the one number
this paper fits: pinning the starting-redshift target anywhere between $260$ and $340$ leaves the
spacing where it is\rcpt{P15_the_spacing_deficit_survives_the_one_fitted_parameter}, and it
survives both freedoms in the inherited datum\rcpt{P15_both_datum_freedoms_opened_and_the_spacing_still_holds}.

\paragraph{The phase is wrong, and that is where the disagreement lives.} The construction's
acoustic phase misses the control's by $0.62$ in $\phi/\pi$. The seam datum's own phase freedom is
a real lever on it and spans a third of it: at the opposite phase the gap closes from $0.615$ to
$0.408$, thirty-four per cent, and no further\rcpt{P15_the_phase_moves_with_the_datum_and_only_the_spacing_does_not}.
The one fitted number moves it by three per cent of the discrepancy. The attribution is complete:
the phase is set by the driving, and the two undriven arms
agree\rcpt{P15_the_phase_is_the_driving_and_the_undriven_arms_agree}. What is \emph{not} settled
is why the coupling behaves differently, and an attribution is not an explanation.

\paragraph{The first peak, and its cause.} The first peak sits $23\%$ low against a $1.5\%$
control floor on the delta-function transfer, and $21.9\%$ against a $0.16\%$ floor once the
transfer is rebuilt---the most suspect component of the instrument was replaced wholesale and the
deficit did not follow it down. Its cause is derived rather than described: a rate carrying no
radiation source has no era in which the potential decays on the sound-crossing time, so the two
couplings that oppose one another in $\Lambda$CDM do not
here\rcpt{P15_the_driving_shift_by_subtraction, P15_which_coupling_carries_the_k_dependence}. The
observed $k^{-0.62}$ scaling is not a power law of anything: it is the crossover between a flat
channel and a $1/k$ one\rcpt{P15_the_gradient_coupling_in_closed_form}. The first peak is inside
that transient; the series is not\rcpt{P15_the_first_peak_is_the_seam_datum_and_the_spacing_is_not}.

\paragraph{The likelihood, stated as what it is.} Scored on $185$ covered bins with a single
fitted amplitude, the $\Lambda$CDM arm returns $\chi^{2}/\mathrm{dof}=103$ and this cosmology's
arm $558$. The instrument's own floor, measured as a $\chi^{2}$ distance between the two models in
the comparison's own metric and with no data in it, puts them $21$ apart over those bins---about a
ninth of a $\chi^{2}$ unit per bin, or a third of a standard
deviation\rcpt{P15_the_floor_is_a_distance_between_models_not_a_number_from_the_data}. Carried at
the same wavenumber range on both arms, the control's $\chi^{2}/\mathrm{dof}$ falls to $1.18$
against $1.01$ for a reference $\Lambda$CDM lensed spectrum on the same
bins\rcpt{P15_where_the_likelihood_sits}. Across the eighteen admissible readings the arm costs
between two hundred and four hundred and sixty times the control on identical bins, so the
pre-registered rule fires throughout; the best-fitting reading is among the eight excluded ones,
and its spacing improves precisely because its higher peaks are gone---a reading fits better by
having less structure to disagree with.

\paragraph{What is a measurement discrepancy and not a verdict.} What the comparison establishes
is that this cosmology's acoustic \emph{phase} disagrees with the sky at a strength the instrument
supports, and that its \emph{spacing} does not. It is a measurement discrepancy and not a verdict
on the construction, and the difference matters: a phase acquired at the crossing is a property of
what drives the fluid, not of the geometry that sets the scale.

\paragraph{What is not downstream of this spectrum's shape.} The metric-singularity structure, the
continuation, the light-element abundances, the acoustic scale met at the directly measured
$H_{0}$, and the expansion-rate resolution do not depend on any of the above and are not in
question either way.

\paragraph{A second determination of the onset, and one that is not $H_0$-independent.} Pinning
the measured acoustic angle $100\theta_*=1.04109$---equivalently $\ell_A=\pi/\theta_*=301.76$---on
the derived integrals returns $z_{\mathrm{onset}}=6797$, and returns it identically at $H_0=67.4$,
$70.0$ and $73.0$: the same $H_0$-independence that carries the acoustic scale out of the Hubble
tension, recovered from the onset side rather than
assumed\rcpt{P15_zonset_determinations, H0_acoustic_angle_and_seam}. Held apart from it, the
inherited datum taken alone---$\rho_r/\rho_m=2$ exactly at onset---fixes
$1+z_{\mathrm{onset}}=2\omega_m/\omega_r$, and that determination is \emph{not} $H_0$-independent:
at fixed $\Omega_m$, with $\omega_r$ set by the measured $T_{\mathrm{CMB}}$, it returns
$z_{\mathrm{onset}}=6840$, $7378$ and $8024$ at those three rates. It agrees with the angle-fixed
value near $H_0=67.4$ and not across the range.

\paragraph{What remains open here.} Why the gradient coupling behaves differently on this rate
than on $\Lambda$CDM's is not answered; the attribution locates the phase in the driving without
explaining the driving. And the phase's own derivation---as against its measurement---waits on a
derivation of the seam datum's phase, which is not supplied here.
