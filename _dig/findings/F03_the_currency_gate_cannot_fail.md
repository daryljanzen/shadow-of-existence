# F03 — `check_currency` reads its clock from one of the documents it is checking, so it cannot fail

*status: BOUNDED NEGATIVE (verified at source, this cut). Patch drafted and demonstrated: `DRAFT_check_currency_patched.py`.*
*scope: `corpus/check_currency.py`, `FORK_c54.md`, and the five registers it silently passes.*

---

## The defect

`corpus/check_currency.py` line 19:

```python
cur = rev(os.path.join(ROOT, 'FORK_c54.md'))
```

`FORK_c54.md` is the anchor for every comparison the gate makes, and it is **not in `WATCH`**. So
the one document whose freshness the whole gate depends on is the one document the gate never
checks. When it stopped being updated, every lag started being measured from a stale clock.

Measured at this cut:

```
  fork state: c54.35   staleness window: 6 revisions
         CONSOLIDATE_THE_PLAN_AND_INDEX_THE_PROGRAMME.md  c54.32  (lag 3)
                                         THE_LIVE_ARC.md  c54.108  (lag -73)
                             THE_OPEN_PROBLEMS_LEDGER.md  c54.103  (lag -68)
                                           CORPUS_MAP.md  c54.32  (lag 3)
                                                INDEX.md  c54.35  (lag 0)
                                        WHATS_TEED_UP.md  c54.65  (lag -30)

  Every standing register is current.
```

`FORK_c54.md` states `**Fork state:** r2376+c54.35`. The tree's highest `c54.N` mention anywhere
is **108** (`THE_LIVE_ARC.md`). The fork record is **73 revisions behind**.

## The gate prints the proof of its own breakage

`lag -73`. A negative lag says *this register mentions a revision that is 73 ahead of the clock*.
That is not "very current" — it is impossible unless the clock is wrong. The test is
`lag <= WINDOW`, so every negative lag sails through, and the three registers that are actually
being maintained are the three that make the anchor's staleness visible. The gate had the
evidence in its own output and no assertion looking at it.

## What it is hiding

Recomputing against the true head (c54.108):

| register | states | lag vs head | gate said |
|---|---|---|---|
| `FORK_c54.md` | c54.35 | **73** | *(not watched)* |
| `CONSOLIDATE_THE_PLAN_AND_INDEX_THE_PROGRAMME.md` | c54.32 | **76** | lag 3, current |
| `CORPUS_MAP.md` | c54.32 | **76** | lag 3, current |
| `INDEX.md` | c54.35 | **73** | lag 0, current |
| `WHATS_TEED_UP.md` | c54.65 | **43** | lag −30, current |
| `THE_OPEN_PROBLEMS_LEDGER.md` | c54.103 | 5 | current ✓ |
| `THE_LIVE_ARC.md` | c54.108 | 0 | current ✓ |

Five stale registers against a window of six, reported as none. And `WHATS_TEED_UP.md` is
independently checkable: its frontmatter says it is *"regenerated by parsing THE_LIVE_ARC each
turn"*, it says **"44 open leads"**, and the register says **14**. It was last regenerated at
c54.65, 43 revisions ago. The claim about its own regeneration cadence is the thing that is
false, and the currency gate is the instrument that should have caught it.

## Why this one matters more than its size

The gate's own docstring states the reason it exists:

> `check_currency.py -- L-42: CONSOLIDATE's register has lapsed twice (brought current at r1730
> after 130 revisions, again at c54.13 after ~500). **A recurring defect wants a gate.**`

`CONSOLIDATE` has now lapsed a **third** time, 76 revisions, *with the gate installed and green*.
The gate was built for exactly this failure and did not see the third instance of it. That is a
worse state than not having the gate, because a green gate is read as a positive result: the
ten-gate suite reporting all-green is what a reader takes as evidence the standing registers can
be trusted, and here it is the reason they went unexamined.

## The patch, and it is two lines of substance

`DRAFT_check_currency_patched.py` (drop-in, same interface, run and demonstrated):

1. **The clock is the maximum over the whole watched set**, not one file's self-report:
   `head = max(r for r in seen.values() if r is not None)`. No single file falling behind can
   drag the reference backwards, and there is no privileged document.
2. **`FORK_c54.md` joins `WATCH`**, with its own lag reported. The fork record is a standing
   register like the others; treating it as an exempt clock is what created the hole.

A negative lag is now impossible by construction (head ≥ every entry), which is the right way to
retire that class of bug — not by adding a `lag < 0` check but by making the quantity unable to
go negative.

Run against the live tree, the patched gate returns **rc=1**:

```
  head: c54.108   (highest mention across the watched set: THE_LIVE_ARC.md)
  fork record `FORK_c54.md` states: c54.35   staleness window: 6 revisions
  ⚠ THE FORK RECORD IS ITSELF 73 REVISIONS BEHIND THE HEAD

  STALE REGISTERS: 5
    [FAIL] FORK_c54.md · CONSOLIDATE · CORPUS_MAP.md · INDEX.md · WHATS_TEED_UP.md
```
