✔ **CONTROL: the D=4 column reproduces §sec:tour exactly** — twelve marks at 30°, zeros at
0/60/…/300, crests at 30/90/…/330, three of each sign.

⇒ **The twelve become eight, and the four that vanish are the antifundamental.** At D=4 the crests

crest for R to exchange with**, and the zeros are double rather than simple, so they coincide with
the minima instead of standing between the crests.

**The general form:** the surviving harmonic is (D−1)w. At **even** D the mass is *odd* in the offset,
so the harmonic is a **sine** — both signs, simple zeros interleaving ± crests — giving **4(D−1)**
marks. At **odd** D it is *even*, so the harmonic is a **cosine** — one sign, double zeros — giving
**2(D−1)** marks. *12 at D=4, 8 at D=5.*
