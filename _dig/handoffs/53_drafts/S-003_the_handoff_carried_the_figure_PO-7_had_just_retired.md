## ⇒ THREE THINGS WRONG, EACH DEMONSTRABLE FROM THE FOLD'S OWN RUNS

**① "Invariant" was established on the wrong axis and the fold knew the other one.** ℓ₁ = 201.3 is
invariant across the **seam** — four values across the corpus's whole band for z_onset, 201.3 to
201.8. *It is not invariant across the initial data, and the fold measured that itself:*

| the fold's own initial-data variations | ℓ₁ |
|---|---|
| flat Φ₀ *(the surviving datum)* | **201.3** |
| `ENVELOPE=1` | **173.9** |
| `RADZERO=1` | **174.1** |
| `PHIPOIS=1` | *first peak buried below ℓ = 453* |

⇒ ***The fold reproduced PO-7's instability — a spread comparable to the {150, 165, 315} the row
cites — and then reported one datum's value as an invariant of the construction.*** *The seam
invariance is real and worth having; the word carried it onto the axis where it is false.*

**② The raw-ℓ₁ headline is the comparison PO-7 says cannot be made.** The fold's own `L-107` found
the same asymmetry independently — *"the CR arm runs at H₀ = 73.0, Ω_m = 0.3066; the ΛCDM arm at
67.40, 0.3150 … every cross-arm statement must be made in l_A-normalised form"* — **and then the
handoff led with a raw-ℓ₁ table anyway.** *The finding was in the arc and did not reach the summary,
which is `THE_METHOD` §3c exactly: the correction propagates into the passage it corrects and not
into the layer that summarises it.*

**③ And the comb — the thing PO-7 says IS stable — is where the fold's result actually lives.**
`S-004` shows the fold's comb and the phase are one object, and the c54 line's registered residual
(0.72–0.79 of π/r_s) is the same measurement. **The handoff has the comb nowhere in its headline.**
