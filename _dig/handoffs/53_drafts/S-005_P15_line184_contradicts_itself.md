## ⇒ ① AND ② ARE IN THE SAME PARAGRAPH AND CONTRADICT EACH OTHER ON $\rs$

① puts $\rs$ on the leaf's rate. ② says putting $\rs$ on the leaf's rate *re-manufactures the very
tension the section dissolves.* ③ and ④ state the opposite of ① explicitly, in the same paper, in
matched vocabulary — **and ④ says "not a radiation-included one" in italics, which is exactly what
"the leaf's" means by the paragraph's own decomposition
$H_{\text{leaf}}^{2}=H_{\text{stack}}^{2}+H_0^{2}\Omega_r(1+z)^{4}$.**

⌗ **And `ONTOLOGY_FOUNDATION_INDEX` §1·LEVELS holds the resolved form**, which is why the defect is a
sentence and not a confusion:

> *"the sound horizon and the diffusion length are **both** L1 (radiation-free) … The *microphysics
> inside* them — the baryon-loaded sound speed $c_s$, Thomson scattering, Saha/Peebles recombination
> — is ordinary GR on the real content (radiation present, behaving normally); **it is the
> *expansion these processes compete against* that is L1.**"*

## THE DIAGNOSIS: THE LIST IS RIGHT AND THE PREDICATE IS WRONG

*$\rs$, $r_D$, recombination and the perturbations genuinely are "processes running in the content"
— that is what distinguishes them from a comoving separation read across leaves.* **What does not
follow is that they take the leaf's *rate*.** The paragraph's own distinction is between **where the
microphysics lives** (the content) and **which expansion it competes against** (L1) — and ③ and ④
draw it correctly while ① collapses it into a single predicate.

## THE PROPOSED CORRECTION, in the paper's own words rather than mine

> *"A comoving separation read across leaves takes the stacking rate. A process running in the
> content—$\rs$, $r_D$, recombination, the perturbations—runs its **microphysics** on the content,
> radiation present and behaving normally, **while the expansion it competes against remains L1**;
> only a self-gravitating local excursion takes the leaf's rate."*

*Every clause of that is lifted from ③, ④ or §1·LEVELS. Nothing new is asserted.*
