# B-6 · The Λ>0 singularity theorems, confronted with CR's finite-curvature seam

**The item (P2 §399 / A1.1-adjacent):** CR reads the cosmic beginning as the *finite-curvature*
Nariai degenerate-horizon seam, not the r=0 curvature singularity. Does this contradict the
Hawking–Penrose singularity theorems, which force a "singularity" in gravitational collapse and in
cosmology? **Verdict: no contradiction — CR honours the theorems' actual conclusion and denies only
a stronger claim the theorems never make.** Worked below; the specialised Λ>0 citations are flagged
for the references pass (AB-1).

## What the theorems actually prove — and don't

The Penrose (1965) and Hawking–Penrose (1970) theorems conclude **causal geodesic incompleteness**:
some timelike/null geodesics are inextendible at finite affine parameter. They do **not** prove a
curvature singularity (a blow-up of a scalar invariant), and they say **nothing** about what the
incompleteness *is* — a genuine curvature pathology, a coordinate artefact, or a boundary at which
the description hands off. This gap between "geodesic incompleteness" (proved) and "curvature
singularity" (assumed in the folklore) is the whole space CR occupies. It is exactly the P1/P2/P3
result read forward: the r=0 "singularity" of the Schwarzschild reading is a **perspectival areal-
coordinate divergence** (the chain-rule artefact of the forced off-axis sweep, P3 §sweep), not a
divergence of the de~Sitter substrate, which is C∞ across the locus the chart labels r=0.

## The hypotheses, with Λ>0

- **Trapped surface (Penrose, collapse).** Gravitational collapse still forms a closed trapped
  surface with Λ>0 (Λ weakens but does not prevent horizon formation for a sufficiently compact
  source). So Penrose's hypothesis is met on the collapse side, and **incompleteness is forced** —
  which CR does not resist: the infalling geodesics reach the seam in finite proper time. CR *agrees*
  the collapse is geodesically incomplete in the black-hole reading.
- **Energy conditions.** The classical theorems use the strong (Hawking, cosmology) or null (Penrose)
  energy condition. Λ>0 **violates the strong energy condition** — pure de~Sitter is geodesically
  *complete* and singularity-free, so Hawking's cosmological theorem does not apply to a Λ-dominated
  expansion, and the modern Λ>0 statements (Andersson–Galloway-type; flag for AB-1) require a trapped
  surface, not mere convergence. This is *favourable* to CR: the expanding, Λ-set cosmology it lands
  on the far side of the seam is not itself forced to a past singularity by the theorems; what is
  forced is the *collapse-side* incompleteness, which is the seam.
- **The null energy condition** (Penrose) is respected by ordinary matter and by Λ, so the collapse
  trapped-surface argument stands — again yielding incompleteness, again resolved at the seam.

## How CR discharges it

The theorems force incompleteness; they do not name its character. CR names it: the incomplete
geodesics terminate at the **Nariai degenerate seam** (the double-root horizon, κ=0, finite
curvature K_G = 1/α² − M/r³ *bounded* there — P3 prop:curvature; **not** the r→0 divergence), the
**finite-curvature species** of metric singularity P1 isolates (a null hypersurface whose spatial
extent also contracts, distinct from the r=0 infinite-curvature species P2 catalogues). Across that
seam the manifold is smooth (the signature flip is confined to the 2-D slicing surface, spacetime
Lorentzian throughout, P3 prop:flip), and the collapse continues — reassigned — as the expanding
cosmology (P7 thm:cosmogenesis, "collapsed matter must become a universe"). So:

> **Incompleteness in the black-hole reading = the finite-curvature seam = the cosmogenesis branch
> point.** The theorem's conclusion is honoured exactly; its folklore gloss ("⇒ a curvature
> singularity at r=0 where physics ends") is the extra step CR removes, on independent grounds
> (P1–P3), and replaces with a finite-curvature boundary across which the description continues.

This is the same move the programme makes everywhere: read the shadow (the r=0 divergence, the
incompleteness) back to the casting structure (the finite Nariai seam of the one dS substrate), and
decline the naïve-realist identification of the coordinate pathology with a physical end (P6).
