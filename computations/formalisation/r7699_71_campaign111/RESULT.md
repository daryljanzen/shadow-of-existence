## The `prop:cycloid` flag, quoted for 60 (P2 l.113–118, exact)

> **Proposition (Lemaître–Tolman cycloid form).** Within the Schwarzschild interior $r \in (0, 2M)$, the radial
> proper-time parameter $\tau$ along a radial timelike geodesic satisfies
> $r(\eta) = M(1 + \cos \eta),\ \tau(\eta) = M(\eta + \sin \eta)$,
> for $\eta \in [0, \pi]$, with $\eta = 0$ corresponding to the horizon $r = 2M$ and $\eta = \pi$ corresponding to
> $r=0$. The proper-time interval from horizon to $r=0$ is $\tau(\pi) - \tau(0) = M\pi$.

**Hypotheses as stated:** the interior `r ∈ (0, 2M)`, a radial timelike geodesic, and `η ∈ [0, π]`. **The energy `E` is
not stated.**

**The scope error.** Along a radial timelike geodesic, `(dr/dτ)² = E² − 1 + 2M/r`, where `E = −u·∂_t` is conserved.
- **The proof's first line is the case `E = 0`.** It writes `dτ² = dr²/(2M/r − 1)`, which is `(dr/dτ)² = 2M/r − 1`.
- **When `0 < E² < 1`,** the solution is `r = (M/(1 − E²))(1 + cos η)`. Its maximum is `2M/(1 − E²)`, which is greater
  than `2M`. So the interior arc is not `η ∈ [0, π]` with `r(0) = 2M`, and the time from horizon to `r = 0` is not `Mπ`.
- **When `E² ≥ 1`,** the solution is not a cycloid at all.

**The fix:** add "with vanishing Killing energy E = 0, i.e. at rest at r = 2M". The case `E = 0` is the geodesic through
the bifurcation sphere, which is also where `η = 0` meets `r = 2M`.
