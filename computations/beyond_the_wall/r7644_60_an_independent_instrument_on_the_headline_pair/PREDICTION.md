**The order (`r7643`, 69).** On the arm of record, cc66 reads the full-range lensed confrontation at

the same 185 bins unlensed give **1.35**; and the refit gives **1.60** against **1.57**, the control

> *"But the no-fit spectrum on the arm of record does not exist yet; 70 is banking it at the distances'
> point. When it lands, compute the lensed and unlensed no-fit pair and the refit pair on an instrument

**THE REACHABLE NEGATIVE, as the order states it, carried verbatim:**

> your figures differ from cc66's beyond the third digit, or the no-fit spectrum at the distances'
> point returns a ratio that changes which configuration is the less favourable.

## ⌗ WHAT "INDEPENDENT OF cc66's" HAS TO MEAN HERE, STATED BEFORE ANYTHING IS BUILT

The order asks for the **same 185 bins** and an **independent instrument**. Those pull against each
other unless the line between data and code is drawn first, so it is drawn here:
- **THE DATA IS SHARED ON PURPOSE.** `plik_lite` TT, its binning weights and its covariance are the
  measurement; an instrument that used different data would not be checking cc66's arithmetic, it
  would be answering a different question. The 185 bins are `ell = 100` to `1996` and the count is
  **asserted, not inherited**, exactly as cc66's own receipt asserts it.
- **THE CODE IS NOT SHARED.** This revision does **not** call `chi2_of_spectrum.chi2_of`, which is the
  function every figure above was produced by. It builds its own: its own interpolation onto the full
  `ell` grid, its own binning, its own restriction of the **covariance** (not its inverse) to covered
  bins, and its own closed-form single-amplitude fit. ⚠ **Each of those four is a CHOICE, and cc66's
  receipt names all four as the choices that matter** — so an independent reading is worth having
  precisely because a different hand could make any of them differently and the figure would move.
- **THE LENSING OPERATOR IS AN IMPOSED INPUT, not a fitted one**, and is treated as data for the same
  reason the bins are.

⇒ **PREDICTION (arm ⓐ):** rebuilt this way, the three `L2000` scores agree with cc66's printed
`214.1`, `550.5` and `302.1` **to the digits the paper prints**, and the two ratios come out `2.57` and
`1.41`. **THE REACHABLE NEGATIVE is the order's first clause:** they differ beyond the third digit —
in which case one of the four choices is not interchangeable and the figure is instrument-dependent,
which is a finding about `P15`'s printed numbers and not about either seat's arithmetic.

## ⚑ ARM ⓑ — WHAT IS READABLE NOW IS MORE THAN THE ORDER ASSUMES, AND WHAT IS BLOCKED IS NARROWER

The order says the no-fit spectrum on the arm of record does not exist yet. **Read against the bank,
that is true of one thing and not of three**, and the distinction is registered here before it is
used:
- the **control** at the distances' point exists; the **namesake arm** at `L2000` exists; and the
  **arm of record** at `L2000` exists as the rebuilt counterpart. ⇒ *So the `214.1` / `550.5` / `302.1`
  triple — the no-fit lensed row — is computable now, which is also what cc66's own reader 3 reports.*
- what does **not** exist is the arm of record's no-fit spectrum **as a file distinct from its refit
  one**: on that arm cc66's reader 4 is blocked by its own gate, because the two pointers resolve to
  one file and the receipt's whole subject is the **contrast** between the two confrontations.
⇒ **PREDICTION:** the no-fit lensed and unlensed pair and the ratio `1.41`/`1.35` are read in this
revision; the **no-fit-against-refit contrast on the arm of record is not**, and is reported as
blocked on 70's bank with the gate that detects it named — not as a figure this seat chose not to take.
**THE REACHABLE NEGATIVE:** the triple is not computable either, because one of the three files is not
at the point its name implies — in which case this arm is a MISS and the block is wider than the order
says.

## ⚑ ARM ⓒ — THE REFIT HALF IS A BOUND AND NOT A FIGURE, AND THE ORDER'S `1.60` IS ITS UPPER END

cc66 reports the refit half as `1.60` by this route **and `1.48×` to `1.54×` by another**, because the
verification spectra on the arm of record *are* the grid base.
⇒ **PREDICTION:** an independent reading of the refit pair reproduces `1.60` on the route that is
readable and **cannot** narrow the `1.48`–`1.54` spread, because that spread is a property of which
file the verification run is and not of the scoring code. So this revision reports the refit half as a
**bound with its route named**, never as a single number.
**THE REACHABLE NEGATIVE:** the independent reading lands outside `1.48`–`1.60` altogether, which
would make the spread an artefact of the scorer rather than of the bank.

## ⚠ WHAT THIS REGISTRATION DOES NOT CLAIM

- It does not claim the shared data makes the reading dependent: the order asked for the same bins.
- It does not claim a figure for anything 70 has not banked, and will name the block rather than
  interpolate across it.
- It edits no receipt belonging to cc66 or 70, moves no paper figure, and pins no parameter. Any
  disagreement with a printed figure is **routed**, because `P15`'s rule is that a printed figure moves
  only when every cited receipt that computes it is re-pointed — and this instrument is a check on
  those receipts, not one of them.
