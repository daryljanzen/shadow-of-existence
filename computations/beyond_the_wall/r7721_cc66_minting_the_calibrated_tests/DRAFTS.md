## ⓵ `l.1397`–`1401` — the sharpness sentence, with the licence the receipt now carries

Printed now:

> …and it is sharp, a factor of about $1.8$ on all three widths reached within about a tenth of a comb
> period---\emph{narrower than the narrowest window that resolves it}.

Drafted:

> …and it is sharp, a factor of about $1.8$ on all three widths reached within about a tenth of a comb
> period---\emph{narrower than the narrowest window that resolves it}, and at every width beyond all of
> $10^{6}$ step-free surrogates of the curve's own scatter and autocorrelation ($p<10^{-6}$)%
> \rcpt{P15_the_located_transition_is_beyond_a_million_step_free_surrogates_and_the_source_exclusions_printed_clause_is_licensed_only_at_the_two_wider_smoothings}.

⌗ **Two things to weigh, and both are yours.**

1. ⚠ **`10^{6}` and `p<10^{-6}` are figures, so the clause brings them under `check_figures_pinned`.**
   The receipt pins them, so the gate is satisfied — but note that `p<10^{-6}` is a **one-sided bound**,
   and that gate's own rule is that a one-sided bound never pins. ⇒ *It is pinned here because the
   receipt also asserts the two-sided fact underneath it: that `0` of `10^6` surrogates reach the step.
   If you would rather the prose carried no figure at all, the alternative is below.*
2. **The figure-free alternative**, which keeps the substance and adds no pin:

   > …\emph{narrower than the narrowest window that resolves it}, and beyond every one of a million
   > step-free surrogates of the curve's own scatter and autocorrelation at all three widths%
   > \rcpt{...}.

⇒ **I recommend the second.** *It says the same thing, it is what the receipt actually establishes, and
it keeps a printed decimal out of a sentence that has done without one until now.*
