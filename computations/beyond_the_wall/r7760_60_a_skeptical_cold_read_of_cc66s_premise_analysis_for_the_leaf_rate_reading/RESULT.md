
whose **radial** metric function is `∂_χ r` and whose **angular** one is `r`. They are different
functions with different logarithmic rates — computed here at five redshifts, and the radial

* the leaf is **not isotropic in this chart**, and "the" scale-factor history is two functions;

  a chart that does not supply it**;
* **and the anisotropy does not rescue the other side either** — the corpus's two rates are not the
  chart's two metric functions. The step is unsupported in both directions, which is the honest
  verdict.
