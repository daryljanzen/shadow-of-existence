# The rows nobody opened

`P10` — `canonical_time.tex`, 2,019 lines — was read whole, and it needed no edit. What
needed correcting was a ruling of mine.

## What r7377 got right

`PO-23`'s strike stands. That row's object was *the ultraviolet definition of the mode sums*,
and its own column-2 condition read: "a definition of the mode sums, or a demonstration that
the obstruction is the general one." **A definition was delivered** — the spectral function,
calibrated against $\zeta(0)=10$ before being used, continues the interacting sum to
$-\tfrac43$ exactly, at a cost of one subtraction more than the free case, three against two,
and `P10` says so in its own words: *"So the interacting sum is defined"* (l.1091).

## What r7377 got wrong

That ruling went on to say "what remains is `P10`'s wording, which is now the odd statement
out." **`P10`'s wording is right, and I had ruled the item from `P7`'s side without reading
`P10`.**

Read whole, `P10` states its open item precisely and three times:

- l.1827: *"So what remains open is one thing, and it is not a category — and it is the
  **completion of a definition** rather than a verdict this construction could fail."*
- l.1836: *"the one open thing is the ultraviolet definition of the interacting tower's mode
  sums — with the renormalised value at the paying dimension unclaimed and the invariant it
  is the coefficient of unnamed, the degeneracy on this background leaving the representative
  indistinguishable rather than forced."*
- l.1206: *"What is not claimed at this dimension is the renormalised value, the coefficient
  being what is computed here and not the constant a scheme assigns it."*

And then the sentence that decides the ruling, at l.1485:

> *A definition that holds at one order of an expansion and is silent at the next is a
> definition with an order attached, however small the next order is. **This row exists
> because a predecessor was struck on an inference of just that shape — a result true where
> it was computed, read as true where it was wanted** — and a quantity being negligible is
> not the same as a scheme being specified.*

`P10` anticipated the inference, named the strike that had already been made on it, and
refused it in print. I made it anyway.

## So the two objects share a title and are not the same

`PO-23` asked for **a definition** and got one. `P10` states open **the definition's
completion**, in the same words. That is `r7013` ① — a different kind of object — so the
consequence is a row, and nothing is unstruck.

**`PO-89` is opened**, in three parts that are deliberately unlike one another:

| part | status | why |
|---|---|---|
| the renormalised value at operator dimension six | **data-gated** | the coefficient is computed exactly at $-15405/8192$; a renormalised value is a measurement, and the construction takes the horizon's temperature and never its entropy |
| which invariant that coefficient belongs to | **blocked on the background** | five dimension-six scalars are pointwise independent on a general scale factor; on the one-parameter family two vanish and **each of the three survivors alone reproduces the residue after a single rescaling** — a rank of one makes representatives indistinguishable rather than forced, and the count is a lower bound, so completing the basis can only deepen it |
| the nine-level closed forms | **workable** | six fitted, three held out against a discriminating control, and not proved from the recoupling algebra; two routes tried and neither closed — while the *parity* they carry **is** proved, by the channel list's own mirror symmetry |

`C1` is therefore not a disagreement about a fact. `P7` and `P10` agree on every computed
thing. They differ on whether the row's narrow discharge settles `P10`'s wider item, and
`P7` l.1937 — *"of the three items named above, all three are settled, the ultraviolet
definition of the mode sums with them"* — is the over-claiming sentence. It is owed a
correction in `P7`'s own turn. **`P10` needs no edit.**

Every one a **correct** strike with the paper's item lying beyond it. Three is where it stops

papers' list should be the same list." It is turning out to be: the register asked good
questions and answered them, and the papers kept asking the larger ones, and nobody wrote the
larger ones down.
