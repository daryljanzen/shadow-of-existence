## ① AND ② HOLD, AS REGISTERED AGAINST THE ORDER'S NEGATIVE

On the arm of record: $F_2 = 288.04$ and $\chi^2_{\rm sep} = 378.36$, the separation **$1.77$ times**

## ⛔ MISS ①, AND IT IS THE REVISION — ARM ⑦

## ⛔ MISS ③, AND ITS MECHANISM SURVIVES ITS ORDERING — ARM ③

**Registered:** $\lvert F_2 \rvert/\chi^2_{\rm sep}$ sits further from $1$ on the arm of record than
on the `LEAFREC=0` arm.
**Measured: closer — $0.761$ against $0.706$.** *The discarded reading is kept.*

The registered reason was the cross term; the registered **ordering** was a guess, and a bad one,
because both $185$-bin arms are near the data ($2.346\times$ and $2.572\times$) and that pair cannot
discriminate. ⇒ **The correct form is a bound, not an ordering.** From
$F_2 = \chi^2_{\rm sep} - 2\,\delta^{\mathsf T} F r_c$ — *checked on every row, not asserted* — and
Cauchy–Schwarz,
$$\left\lvert \frac{F_2}{\chi^2_{\rm sep}} - 1 \right\rvert \;\le\; \frac{2}{\sqrt{\chi^2_{\rm sep}/\chi^2_c}} .$$
It holds on all nine rows. **So the agreement of the two measures is CONTINGENT at a small ratio and
forced only at a large one** — the bound alone does not reach $5\%$ until the ratio exceeds $1600$.
At $58.20$ the bound is $0.262$, which is what made the source's $4.84\%$ available at all; at
$1.77$ it is $1.50$, and nothing forces agreement to anything. ⇒ **The $5\%$ agreement was never a
property of the two measures. It was a property of the ratio** — and the ratio is the
configuration's.
