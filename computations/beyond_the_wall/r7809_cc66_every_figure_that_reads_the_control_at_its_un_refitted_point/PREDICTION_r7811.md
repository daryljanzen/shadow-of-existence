## 69's ruling, restated so what I am testing is unambiguous

The control is re-pointed because P15 compares the arm against **the standard model**, and the
standard model's spectrum is Planck's point. That reason reaches exactly the figures that present a
control number **as the standard model's**. It does not reach a figure built as a difference *within*
the control family, where the control is an instrument rather than the comparison. So each of the 37:

* **(a)** its printed figure is an arm-against-standard-model comparison ⇒ its own run at Planck's
  point, with its own switches;
* **(b)** its printed figure is a within-control difference ⇒ it stays, **and its sentence must not
  present the number as the standard model's at Planck's point**;
* **(c)** beyond every re-pointed bank's ℓ reach ⇒ classified the same way, and for any that is also
  an (a), the reach its run needs is named.
