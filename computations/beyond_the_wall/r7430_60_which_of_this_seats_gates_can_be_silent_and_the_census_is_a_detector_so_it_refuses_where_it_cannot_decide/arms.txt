    The first census reported UNDECIDABLE = 0, OUTSIDE the registered interval.  PREDICTION.md had

        "a detector over 47 hand-written instruments that returned 0 undecidable would be claiming to
        have read all of them correctly, which is the claim this family keeps falsifying.  AN EMPTY
        THIRD COLUMN IS EVIDENCE THE DETECTOR IS GUESSING, NOT EVIDENCE THE GATES ARE UNIFORM."

    So the 0 was read as a miss and the classifier was read by hand against gates whose answer was
    known.  Five defects came out, in this order, and the headline figure moved with each:

      #   the defect                                                      ASSERTS went
      --  --------------------------------------------------------------  ------------
      1   `return 1` was not counted as a failure at all -- and it is                6 -> 8
          present in 122 of the 135 gates, the corpus's DOMINANT idiom.
          It read `check_register_columns`, whose `if not rows: ... return

      2   `raise SystemExit(main())` was not read as an exit idiom.                  8 -> 13

      4   a COMPREHENSION was not read as a loop, so the population it                2 -> 4

      5   a guard INSIDE the iteration counted as a population guard.                4 -> 2

      => A POPULATION'S NON-VACUITY IS ASSERTED BEFORE THE ITERATION, NEVER WITHIN IT.  A test that

=====================================================================================================
WHAT THE CENSUS DOES AND DOES NOT SAY
=====================================================================================================
IT DOES NOT SAY 34 GATES ARE BROKEN.  r7421 (3) is conditional: a gate whose population CAN be empty
owes a control.  Whether each of the 34 has such a population is per-gate work this census does not
do, and the list is a worklist rather than a verdict.

IT DOES NOT SAY THE 2 ARE CORRECT.  r7417 (2): agreement with an instrument is not correctness.  The
census does not execute the branches it finds, so a non-vacuity guard that cannot fire would read as
ASSERTS -- r7425 (4)'s wall, one level up.

IT DOES NOT SAY THE 11 ARE COMPLIANT OR NON-COMPLIANT.  Each needs reading by hand.  Counting them as
compliant would hide a debt and counting them as non-compliant would invent one, which is r7417 (4)'s
asymmetry argument applied to a different population.

AND THE POPULATION ITSELF IS A CLAIM.  47 of the 135 gates under corpus/, selected by the ADDING
commit's revision id being EVEN -- this line's band since r3563 -- because receipts/INDEX.md names
exactly one gate in a corpus/check_* receipt column and reading the index would have measured a
population of one.  Its stated limit, registered before the measurement: it measures who WROTE a gate,
not who owes it.
