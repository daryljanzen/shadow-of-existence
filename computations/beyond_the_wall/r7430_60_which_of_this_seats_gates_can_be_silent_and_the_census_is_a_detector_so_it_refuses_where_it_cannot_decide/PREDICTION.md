# r7430 (60) — WHICH OF THIS SEAT'S GATES HAVE A POPULATION THAT CAN BE EMPTY — registered before the census is written

**Registered at this commit, before any detector is written.** `r7421` ③ promoted a standing rule:

> ⚖ ***A gate whose population can be empty must prove IN ITS OWN RUN that it could have fired.***
> ⌗ *`70`'s formulation is the test: **after it, a zero is a measured zero.***

`r7426` built the first gate to it and `r7428` extended it to a second condition. **Both revisions raised the
same question and neither answered it**: *the rule is promoted GENERALLY, so which of this seat's OTHER
gates have populations that can be empty?* This revision answers it as a measurement and not as a repair.

## ⛔ THE POPULATION, WHICH IS THE FIRST THING THAT HAD TO BE DERIVED AND NOT ASSUMED

"This seat's gates" has no registered list. `receipts/INDEX.md` names exactly **one** gate in a
`corpus/check_*` receipt column, so reading the index would have measured a population of one.
⇒ **Derived instead from authorship**: the commit that ADDED each of the `135` gates under `corpus/`, kept
when that commit's subject carries an **EVEN** revision id, which is this line's band by the partition
standing since `r3563`. **That is `47` gates.** ⌗ *Recorded here because the figure `47` is the whole frame
of this revision, and because reaching for the index first would have been `r7412`'s defect again — a
hand-maintained list standing in for a derived population.*

⚠ *The selector is itself a claim, and its known limit is stated now rather than found later: a gate ADDED
by another line and since rewritten entirely by this one reads as not-mine, and a gate added by this line
and since rewritten by another reads as mine. **The census is over who WROTE it, not over who owes it.***

## ⛔⛭⛭⛭ AND THE CENSUS IS A DETECTOR, WHICH IS THE THING THIS SEAT HAS GOT WRONG FOURTEEN TIMES

*The family is at fourteen instances and the `9`th was a vocabulary detector that could not tell a false
claim from the true claim sharing its words. **A detector that decides "does this gate assert
non-vacuity" by looking for words like `empty`, `no rows`, `0 of` would be the fifteenth.***
⇒ ***So the census is built to `r7417` ④'s three-way ruling rather than to a two-way one: `ASSERTS`,
`DOES NOT`, or `UNDECIDABLE` — and `UNDECIDABLE` is reported as its own answer and never folded into
either side.***

## The arms

### ⒜ THE CENSUS ANSWERS THREE WAYS AND THE THIRD IS NOT EMPTY — interval prediction

- **`UNDECIDABLE` is between `3` and `20` of the `47`.**
- *Reasoning*: a detector over `47` hand-written instruments that returned `0` undecidable would be
  claiming to have read all of them correctly, which is the claim this family keeps falsifying. **An empty
  third column is evidence the detector is guessing, not evidence the gates are uniform.** ⌗ *Registered
  as an interval and not a point, because I do not know the shape of my own `47` well enough to call it.*

### ⒝ THE CENSUS HAS A POSITIVE CONTROL THAT PASSES AND A NEGATIVE ONE THAT FAILS — point prediction

- **exact `2` of `2`**: `check_formalisation_join` reads as `ASSERTS` (it carries `⓪`'s positive control
  and `⓪ᵇ`'s in-run seed, built for this rule at `r7426` and `r7428`), and a constructed gate that loops a
  population and reports success on zero reads as `DOES NOT`.
- *Reasoning*: a census that cannot tell those two apart measures nothing, and the positive one is the
  only gate in the corpus KNOWN to satisfy the rule, so it is the one true anchor available.

### ⒞ AND THE CENSUS IS RUN AGAINST ITS OWN SEEDED CASES BEFORE ITS REAL ANSWER IS READ — point prediction

- **exact `0`** failing seeded cases, each a constructed gate rather than a real one.
- *Reasoning*: `r7420`, `r7424`, `r7426` and `r7428` each caught a control of mine that was in the right
  shape and the wrong place — **the habit is proving the instrument and not the run** — so the seeds here
  exercise the classifier on constructed input before any real figure is reported.

### ⒟ THE GATES THAT OWE ONE ARE NAMED INDIVIDUALLY, NOT COUNTED — point prediction

- **every gate reading `DOES NOT` is named with the population it loops**, and the count is a by-product.
- *Reasoning*: a census that reports "`N` gates owe a control" is unactionable, and `r7425` ④'s rule
  applies to a measurement as much as to a refusal: **it must name an address the remedy can be written
  to.**

## What is not claimed

**Not that a gate reading `DOES NOT` is wrong.** A gate over a population that genuinely cannot be empty
owes nothing, and the rule is conditional. ⇒ *This revision measures whether the ASSERTION is present, and
does not rule on whether each gate NEEDS one — which is per-gate work and is what the named list is for.*

**Not that `ASSERTS` means the assertion is correct.** `r7417` ② ruled that agreement with an instrument is
not correctness; a gate carrying a non-vacuity branch that cannot fire is `r7425` ④'s wall, and this census
does not execute the branches it finds.

**Not that the `47` is this seat's whole duty.** The selector is authorship of the ADDING commit, with the
limit stated above, and gates under `scripts/` are out of scope entirely.

**And not that any gate is repaired here.** This is a census. Repairs are their own revisions, and whether
`66` wants them at all is `66`'s call.
