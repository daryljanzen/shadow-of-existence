## 4. The arbiter, per `r7333` ④ — objective and per-repair

A repair counts only when **all three hold**:

1. the receipt runs **`rc=0`**;
2. the instrument scoped to it reports **`0` new keys**, and every row it retired is cleared or
   re-verdicted with its ground written in;
3. **the check still tests the claim** — stated for each repair in the write-up as what now fails that did
   not fail before.

**And the verification unit is `r7333` ②'s standing: the `134`-gate sweep plus the carry ledger**, before
the push rather than after CI.

1. **receipts repaired `< 6`** → the cost is in the reading, not the learning, and the direction trigger
   should hear it from me first;
2. **receipts repaired `> 16`** → my band is low and `P15`'s physics reading costs less than I registered;
3. **repairs per receipt `≥ 1.6`** → the secondary is refuted; the sweep-plus-carry lesson did not reduce
   the per-receipt cost;

   push** → **the revision is invalid, not the band refuted**, and it voids 1–3.
