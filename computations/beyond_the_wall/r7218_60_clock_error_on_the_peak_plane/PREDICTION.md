## 1. What is computed, and on what

The clock pair is `r7216`'s, DERIVED here and not quoted: the lap's leg parameter `$u$` and the
conformal parameter `$\eta=\int\sinh^{-2/3}u\,du$`, whose ratio on one pair of seam-bounded
stretches is `$2$` exactly on the first and `$2.3042$` on the second.

A tight-coupling photon–baryon oscillator is integrated in conformal time with the standard
loading `$R\propto a$` and a decaying gravitational drive. TWO integrations per configuration:
the drive read on the oscillator's own clock, and the drive read on the OTHER clock through the
lap's own map. Peaks are taken from the resulting projected spectrum and reduced with
`cc66`'s OWN estimator — `$\varphi=\langle \ell_n/\ell_A-n\rangle$` and
`$\mathrm{alt}=\langle r_n(-1)^n\rangle$`, same five peaks, same de-tilt.

## 2. The positive control that has to pass FIRST

⛔ **If the oscillator does not reproduce the control arm's KNOWN driving signs, nothing below is
worth reading and the revision reports that instead.** The control's driving pair gives
`$\Delta\varphi=+0.126$` and `$\Delta\mathrm{alt}=-0.0247$` — drive-off minus drive-on, opposite
in sign on both components to the baryon direction's `$-0.0278$` and `$+0.0108$`.

**Pre-registered pass condition:** switching this oscillator's drive off must move `$\varphi$`
POSITIVE and `$\mathrm{alt}$` NEGATIVE, and the baryon direction must move both the other way.
Magnitudes are not required to match — this is a toy, not the instrument — only the four signs.

## 3. The branches, with the likelier named out loud

| # | branch | what it would mean |

**⇒ The likelier branch, named before the run: ⓐ.** The reason, so the prediction is falsifiable
and not a hedge: the alternation is produced by the loading making compressions differ from
rarefactions, and a clock error applied to the DRIVE does not touch the restoring force that
carries that asymmetry. So `$\mathrm{alt}$` should move only at second order while `$\varphi$`
moves at first.

**⇒ And a sign ambiguity is predicted, not discovered:** a clock error has no preferred direction
until one says WHICH clock is substituted for which, so the expectation is a LINE through the
origin of the `$(\varphi,\mathrm{alt})$` plane rather than a ray. If a ray comes out, that is a
finding against this pre-registration.

## 4. `r7216`'s limit travels, and is tested rather than restated

The `$15.2$` per cent is measured on ONE pair of stretches. This revision reads the clock
disagreement on a SECOND pair from the same object — the conformal horn pieces, whose ratios are
`$1:\sqrt3:2$` — and reports whether the phase consequence depends on which pair it is read on.
**If it does, that dependence is the finding and the `$15.2$` per cent is not.**

## 5. What would make this revision report a failure

- the positive control's four signs do not come out ⇒ report that, claim nothing else;
- the three-parameter fit finds the clock effect indistinguishable from a spacing change ⇒ branch ⓓ,
  say it and stop;
- the two stretch pairs disagree about the sign ⇒ the pair-dependence is reported as the result and
