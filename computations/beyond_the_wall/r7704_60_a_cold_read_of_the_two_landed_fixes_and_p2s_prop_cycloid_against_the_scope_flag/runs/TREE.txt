THE COMPUTATION THIS REVISION MAKES ITSELF
------------------------------------------
71's flag is CHECKED rather than accepted.  The interior proper time from the horizon to r = 0 is
integrated here for the general radial geodesic, dtau = dr / sqrt(E^2 - 1 + 2M/r), under the
substitution r = r_max sin^2(theta) that removes the turning-point singularity analytically:

    E      r_max        interval
    0.00   2.000 M      3.141593 M   (= pi M to 5e-15, the paper's figure)
    0.25   2.133 M      2.370868 M
    0.50   2.667 M      1.891199 M
    0.75   4.571 M      1.566498 M
    0.90  10.526 M      1.418173 M
    1.00   unbounded    1.333333 M   (= 4/3 M, the from-rest-at-infinity member)
    1.20   unbounded    1.189787 M

So the printed pi M is the E = 0 member's, it is the largest of the seven values computed here,
and the family's value falls monotonically across them.  (Seven points are seven points: this is
a measurement of the family at those values, not a proof that pi M is the supremum.)
