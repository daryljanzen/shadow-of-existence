- A replacement is **instrument-read** when its minted ledger row survives the quote-pin gate's own

- A retired row counts as **invisible** when its repair mints no key at all (a structural assertion
  parsed out of a syntax tree), when its replacement is a form the instrument cannot key, or when it
  is withdrawn.
- ⚠ **An invisible replacement is not necessarily a loss** — a parsed structural assertion is
  stronger than the text pin it replaced — **but it is not a ledger gain either, and the two must not
  be added together.** That is the whole content of the order's second report.
