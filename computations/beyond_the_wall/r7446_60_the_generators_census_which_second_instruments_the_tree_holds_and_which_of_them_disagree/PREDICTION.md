## THE METHOD, AND WHY IT CARRIES NO SIMILARITY JUDGEMENT

*The obvious way to find a reimplementation is to ask which foreign pattern LOOKS like a gate's. **That is a
classifier, and `r7430` already paid for one: a classifier's confidence is not evidence.*** `r7442`'s own
`\b[0-9a-f]{12,40}\b` against the gate's `held at ([0-9a-f]{8,40})\b` shares no literal anchor with it at
all, so no similarity rule that a person would write would have caught the instance that produced the rule.

⇒ **So candidacy here is EXTENSIONAL and measured, with no judgement in it.** *A foreign pattern `f` and a
gate pattern `g` are a pair when `f ≠ g` as source text **and they both match at least one line of the same
population** — overlap is the evidence that they are about the same thing. The pair DISAGREES when the
symmetric difference of their match sets on that population is non-empty.*

## ⛔ WHAT THIS DOES NOT DO, AND THE READING REGISTERED IN ADVANCE SO IT CANNOT BE INFLATED LATER

⛔ ***A DISAGREEING PAIR IS NOT A DEFECT AND THIS REVISION WILL NOT REPORT ONE AS A DEFECT.*** *Overlap and
disagreement on a uniform population prove that **a second instrument exists**. They do not prove that the
receipt holding it ever asserted anything false — a pattern may be kept to measure a disagreement, as
`_MY_FIRST_TRY` is, or scoped to a population where the two agree. **The failure mode this whole family is
named for is not a wrong number but a wrong accusation, and three of this cycle's four instances would have
been filed against another seat's work.***

**It routes nothing to any seat as a defect.** *The output is a class, a size and a rule.*

**It edits no gate and no other seat's receipt**, and it rewrites no pattern anywhere.

**It does not rule on `PO-78`.** *That is the row seat's call; this supplies the one figure the generator's
rule needs in order to be priced.*

**Its population is stated and its limit with it:** *one uniform line population for every pair. Agreement on
it is agreement ON IT, never equivalence; a pattern matching no line of it is silent rather than absent.*
