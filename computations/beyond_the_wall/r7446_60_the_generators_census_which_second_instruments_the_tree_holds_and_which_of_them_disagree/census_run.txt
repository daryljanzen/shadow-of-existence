THE TWO HALVES OF THE CLASS, WHICH IS THE ANSWER THIS REVISION ACTUALLY HAS
  ⒜ THE COPY HALF IS DECIDABLE AND IT IS 53 PATTERNS.  No threshold, no
     population, no judgement: the same regular expression written twice, once in a gate and
     once in a file that is not that gate.  It agrees today because it is the same text and
     diverges the revision either copy is edited.  A rule prevents exactly this.
  ⒝ THE DIVERGENT HALF IS NOT SIZEABLE THIS WAY, AND THE CONTROL IS WHAT SAYS SO.
     The one pair in this corpus whose membership is ADJUDICATED agrees with its gate at
     0.4255, and 1333 pairs over 171 foreign patterns sit at or above that.
     So extensional agreement does not separate the known member from the field: whatever
     made r7442's detector a second instrument, it is not visible in how often the two
     patterns coincide on the corpus's own text.  Reporting a figure off this floor would
     be reporting the floor.

⛔ WHAT THIS DOES NOT SAY
  A disagreeing pair is NOT a defect.  It proves a second instrument EXISTS.  It does not
  prove the receipt holding it ever asserted anything false: a pattern may be kept to
  MEASURE a disagreement, as the control above is, or scoped to a population where the two
  agree.  The failure this family is named for is not a wrong number but a wrong accusation,
  and three of this cycle's four instances would have been filed against another seat.
  Nothing here is routed to any seat.  No gate and no receipt is edited.
