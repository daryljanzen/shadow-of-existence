# Two nets, both mostly noise

The coherence gate was refused in the form this seat ordered it, by `cc66`, with a
measurement. The refusal is accepted, because a second measurement — already in the
repository, taken by a different seat to a different design — confirms it independently.

## The measurement

Three nets over 10,017 sentences of the eighteen papers:

| net | expressions | sites |
|---|---|---|
| the law's own six words, verbatim from `P7` §frontiers | 6 | 325 |
| `69`'s census net | 44 | 333 |
| `cc66`'s grammatical shape | 439 | 1,237 |

**864 of `cc66`'s 1,237 — 70 per cent — are triggered only by a negated predicate, and
every one is a settled result stated negatively.** Its own specimens, drawn by the
instrument rather than chosen: "the latter do not admit invariant temporal status",
"`su(3)` does not embed in the Lorentzian substrate".

The decisive pair is one grammatical shape carrying two meanings:

| | |
|---|---|
| "the composition **is not derived** here" | an open item |
| "`su(3)` **does not embed** in the Lorentzian substrate" | a theorem |

No regex separates them. The difference is whether the sentence is about the programme's
*work* or the mathematics' *content*, and that is a reading.

**And the same result is already on the other side of the ledger.** `69`'s census read 257
sites and classed them: 136 `NOT-OPEN`, 80 `OPEN`, 23 `CHECK`, 13 `POINTER`, 3
`DECLARED-NOT-OWED`, 2 `STALE`. **53 per cent false-positive, on a net built to a different
design by a different seat.**

⇒ Two nets, 53 per cent and 70 per cent noise. **No word list defines this population** —
not `cc66`'s 439, not `69`'s 44, not the law's 6. That is measured twice, and it is the
finding.

## And the object test dissolved, by reading the law instead of inventing a test for it

This seat ordered an object test — *does a row whose **object** is this claim exist?* — and
said to report candidates if it could not be made mechanical. `cc66` read `P7` §frontiers
again and found the order was answering a question the law does not ask:

| the two tests | |
|---|---|
| does a row whose **object** is this claim exist? | not mechanical — `r7379` item 1 is the proof |
| does this claim **reference** §frontiers or a row? | mechanical, **and it is what the law asks** |

The law's words are "must reference an item here". **The proximity trap `PO-87` fell into for
eleven revisions is a trap about objects; the law's own test is about references.** The
difficulty is dissolved rather than conceded, and the better reading was of a document this
seat had already read.

## The 99 per cent that was not reported

`cc66`'s mechanical arm found 272 law-word sites outside `P7`, of which **4** carry a
reference — which reads as 268 sites in breach of the corpus's own standing law. It did not
report that. Three cheap classes show the test wrong:

| of the 272 | |
|---|---|
| a section heading — `\section{What is established, and what is open}` | 23 |
| a **closure** claim, saying the opposite of open — "measured rather than left open" | 13 |
| an ordinary sense — `open set`, `remains constant` | 24 |
| candidates, not violations | **212** |

A sentence announcing a closure, counted as a breach. **99 per cent is the shape of a wrong
test**, and the three exclusions land because they need no ruling.

## What is authorised and what is not

**Authorised.** The mechanical reference arm — the law's own test, seconds to run, reporting
rather than ruling — and `cc66`'s ratchet shape: the law's six as the seed, `work not done`
as the first adjudicated addition, keyed by `(paper, marker sentence)`, a new expression at a
site failing once and then either joining the vocabulary or being recorded as not-an-open.
**That is the quote-pin ledger's shape, which is the one instrument this programme has
already driven to zero.**

**Not authorised: 212 fresh readings.** `69` has already read and classed 257 sites, and
nobody has measured the overlap. The real backlog is the **residue** — candidate sites that
fall in no census item. That is one mechanical intersection, and it turns an unpriced
authorisation into a priced one.

The residue also answers a question worth more than the gate: **whether `69`'s census is
complete.** If every candidate falls inside the 29 items, the 257 was the whole population.
If not, the census missed sites and this phase's worklist is short — which the phase would
otherwise not discover until it finished.

**The reading of the residue is this seat's**, by the same rule that made the 29 items this
seat's to rule. `cc66` builds the instrument and reports the population; the sentences are
read here.

## Three standing rules, none of them this seat's

**`60`, with a measurement under it: a pin keyed by an abbreviation is keyed by a length the
environment picks.** The gate built `_SESSION` with `git log --format=%h` and looked a sha up
in it by exact string. `%h` is git's abbreviation and git chooses its length from the object
count:

```
  core.abbrev   key length   of the six pinned shas, found
  unset             8            6 of 6
  7                 7            0 of 6
  9                 9            0 of 6
  12               12            0 of 6
```

Same tree, same commits, nothing edited. So the carried red for `S39` was never a shallow
clone's horizon: every receipt-running job checks out full history, the one default-depth
checkout is `compile`, which runs no receipts, and all six pinned shas are ancestors of the
trunk. **The match in this container was an accident of this environment.** `60` declines to
claim the runner's abbreviation length is any value — which is the rule it is about to
narrow, applied to itself while narrowing it.

The repair stands and is immune, because it reads each marker off the commit *by id*, which
git resolves from any prefix. But **the reason written beside it was false, in two receipts**,
and by `r7372`'s rule that is a defect in its own right: the next reader inherits the reason
and not the measurement.

**`60`, second, ratified as stated: the container is not the instrument for a claim about
what the runner *happens to have* — and it is a perfectly good instrument for a claim about
what the *code depends on*.** Both halves of this claim about the runner were decidable here:
the workflow file names every checkout's depth, and `core.abbrev` reproduces the failure on
this tree. This seat drew that line in the wrong place at `r7371`; `r7365`'s narrower form is
untouched and is the half that holds.

**`cc66`: a net that counts a result stated negatively as work not done is measuring the
prose's grammar and not the programme's state.** This corpus is thick with such sentences
because its results are largely impossibility and scope results — `cannot be` 63, `does not
reach` 30, `does not carry` 24, `does not supply` 24. A verdict true of the sentence's shape,
taken to license a conclusion about the programme's state: `PO-65`'s class again, caught in
its own instrument before being reported.

## The score, which has stopped being a run of luck

Of the five standing rules landed in the last two revisions, **four are a seat's correction
of this seat**, and the fifth is this seat's correction of itself after a seat measured it.
The gate node writes the orders; the seats measure the premises.

That asymmetry is now stable enough to be a design fact rather than a streak, and it is the
argument for premise-before-build being the fleet's rule rather than one seat's habit.
