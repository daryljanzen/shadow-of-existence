## The discriminator, stated before it is measured

The defect is not that both provenances appear in a receipt. It is that **a single asserted quantity was
BUILT from both** — `S37`'s counter walked a pinned population and looked each member up in a live map,
so the number asserted is neither historical nor current. The repair keeps them apart: the asserted
quantity is pinned end to end, and the live quantity is reported.

⇒ **FORM 5**: a clause is flagged iff ONE name its condition asserts has, within two hops of its own
writing statements, both a pinned source and a **census-live** source (the output of an instrument run),
and the comparison is not merely directional. *Per-name, never unioned over the clause — which is the
`r7386` split, a shape counted where a use was meant, for the fifth revision running.*
