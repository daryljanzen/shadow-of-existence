## ① ⛔ THE FIGURE THE READER ARM WAS KEYED ON IS A MARGIN AND NOT A DISCORDANCE — AND THIS CORRECTS `r7326`, WHICH IS MINE

*`r7326` ④ sized the reader arm from "`70`'s observed `30` per cent discordance". **`70`'s `30.0` is a
MARGIN:** `−30.0 (−52.5, −2.9)`, the difference between two readers' owed RATES under the previous
text (`FOR_66_FROM_70.md`, `r7321+70.x`). `cc66`'s landed numbers are margins too — owed `−3.9`
(`−22.1` to `+14.8`), flag `−18.4` (`−37.8` to `+3.3`), **between two halves holding DIFFERENT keys,
so not even paired.***

⇒ ***The size formula takes `π_d`: the probability that one key's two readings DISAGREE.*** *A margin
of zero is consistent with every key being read differently, and a margin of `30` points is consistent
with a `30` per cent discordance or with a `100` per cent one. **They are different quantities, and no
instrument in this campaign had reported the second.** ⌗ *This is the same error as `r7317`'s ruling
②, one step further in: there, two rates with different denominators; here, a difference of rates
standing in for a rate of differences.*

⇒ ***It is recoverable, and from files already on the trunk.*** *Two reader pools applied the same
text to the same keys and filed both reads raw, never reconciled — so the keys can be PAIRED and the
disagreements counted:*

|---|---|---|---|---|
| the crossover, previous text `6bef6fd5` | `40` | `4` | `10.0%` | `4.0`–`23.1` |
| **the held set, third redraft `1a3c13a6`** | `69` | `11` | ***`15.9%`*** | ***`9.1`–`26.3`*** |
