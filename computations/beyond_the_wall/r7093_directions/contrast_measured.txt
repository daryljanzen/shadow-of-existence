CORRECTED AT cc66.78 (r7095 Q3) -- THE CEILING, NOT THE ARITHMETIC
================================================================================
The note above says the swing did NOT flatten under the forbidden repair at
LMAXL=1300: crossings 36 -> 30 and the longest run 16 -> 18. That reproduces
exactly on its own pair and is banked here unchanged, because it was measured
correctly.

What it got wrong is the inference. Read to ell <= 1040, LMAXL=1300 is 80 per
cent of that run's own reported ceiling (k_max = 2 l_max / D_M) against 52 per
cent of an LMAXL=2000 run's. At LMAXL=2000 the SAME comparison reverses:
crossings 30 -> 44 and the longest run 18 -> 12.

A four-point ceiling scan localises why. Longest run of one sign:

                ell<=700   ell<=800   ell<=900   ell<=1040
  banked           8          8          15         18
  licensed         9          9          13         22
  forbidden        9          9           9         12

Below ell ~ 800 all three configurations are indistinguishable, so a reading
taken there decides nothing. The discriminating feature lives above ell ~ 850 --
exactly where the shorter run could not see it.

AND THE FORK THIS NOTE SET UP RESOLVES THE SECOND WAY. The licensed
configuration (LEAFREC=1, geometry left on the stacking rate) moves the contrast
coefficient by -0.08 sigma with |c| RISING 1 per cent, lengthens the longest run
18 -> 22, worsens chi2/bin 3.05 -> 3.20, and is the worst of the three on the
peak heights. It moves neither statistic. So this is a conflict between the rate
rule and the sky to adjudicate, not an implementation defect to repair -- and
r7095's ruling against LEAFGEOM=1 stands either way.
