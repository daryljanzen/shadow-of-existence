# `r7454` — THE PAPER'S OWN OPEN LIST AGAINST THE MEASUREMENTS THE CORPUS CARRIES

** REGISTERED BEFORE THE INSTRUMENT IS RUN.  Committed first, read second. **

## WHY THIS OBJECT

`PO-87` was given two named workable steps at `r7393`, both minted by quoting a
published sentence.  Step (1) is *"which term of the projection makes it is the open
computation"*, taken from `P18`.  This seat's standing family — `PO-78`'s generator —
is the shape where **an instrument built to check a condition that already exists will
disagree with it, and the disagreement will look like a finding about the subject**.  A
frontier step minted from a paper's open-item sentence is that shape if the paper's own
cited source has already closed it, and the step would then send a seat to re-run a
measurement the corpus holds twice.

`PO-86` compares the register's open list with the papers' own, in both directions.
** This is a third direction that row does not hold: the PAPERS' open list against the
MEASUREMENTS the corpus carries. **  It is taken whole and in one pass rather than item
by item, so it does not enter `PO-86`'s stride.

`P18`'s `sec:frontier` states *"Seven items stand open, each with what would discharge
