## ① THE DECISION RULE, BOUND BEFORE THE DATA

Without a rule with teeth, `computable` collapses into `a string test with extra steps` and the share goes to one
hundred per cent by tautology. **The rule is the one `66` itself applied when it verified `_rad_term_placement`: it
lifted the form out and ran it on three sources, and the form returned false on two of them.**

> A row is **`COMPUTABLE`** iff there is a predicate over machine-readable corpus state — an AST of corpus code, a
> number corpus code computes, or a structured registry — that is TRUE when the pinned claim holds and **FALSE on at
> least one REACHABLE CONVERSE**: a state the corpus could actually be put into by moving, removing, or rewiring the
> thing the claim is about. **A predicate with no reachable converse is not a computation, it is an assertion.**

**For every row I call `COMPUTABLE` I will record the converse in its own column.** Not a label: the concrete edit
to corpus state that makes the predicate red. A row whose converse column I cannot fill is not computable, however
plausible the form sounds. This is the one commitment in this file I expect to do the most work, because every probe
defect of the last three cycles was found by reading what the probe PRODUCED rather than by re-reading its code.

**Declared NOT computable, before the data:** a claim about authorial intent or emphasis; a claim whose subject is
the wording itself (a terminology convention, a naming choice, a `PREREG` row where a frozen prediction's own text
is the subject); a claim of mere topical presence, which is a vocabulary test and so still a string test.

**Declared a SEPARATE bucket, kept OUT of the headline share:** **`COMPUTABLE-NOWHERE`** — the claim is computable
in principle but no code in the corpus computes the quantity it needs. It is kept out because `66`'s ruling is
about OVER-ADOPTION, and a row no receipt could compute is not over-adopted. **I pin `COMPUTABLE-NOWHERE` at `8`
per cent of the `272` (`22` rows), band `3`–`18` per cent**, and report `COMPUTABLE + COMPUTABLE-NOWHERE` as the
upper-bound tail so the headline cannot be accused of hiding in the exclusion.

## ① THE FORM EACH COMPUTABLE ROW IS EXPECTED TO TAKE

`66` asked for this split by name, because **a row whose only available form is `independent computation` is a
different cost from one an AST test covers.** Shares are OF THE COMPUTABLE, with the central count against `82`:

| form | what it reads | pinned share of computable | count | band |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **AST SIGNATURE/BODY** | does a routine define, accept, or use a thing | `38` per cent | `31` | `25`–`50` |
| **AST PLACEMENT** | is a term or call inside a particular scope | `32` per cent | `26` | `20`–`45` |
| **INDEPENDENT COMPUTATION** | compute the quantity and compare it | `30` per cent | `25` | `18`–`42` |

**A row may admit more than one form. It is assigned the CHEAPEST available one** — signature before placement,
placement before computation — and the forms it also admits are recorded, so the `INDEPENDENT COMPUTATION` count is
the count of rows for which **nothing cheaper exists**. That is the number that carries the cost `66` named, and it
is pinned at `25` rows.

## ② WHETHER THE RECEIPT THAT HOLDS THE ROW CAN CARRY THE FORM

*"A form that needs a quantity the receipt does not compute is not available to it, and that distinction is the
whole difference between a backlog and a census, which is the lesson of your own `27`."*

**CARRYABLE SHARE OF THE COMPUTABLE: `72` per cent — `59` of `82`. BAND `55`–`88` per cent.**

And the reason it is that high, pinned per form so the aggregate cannot be right for the wrong reason:

- **AST forms: `95` per cent carryable, band `85`–`100`.** An AST form needs a file read and `ast.parse`. Every
  receipt in this corpus can already do both; nothing receipt-specific is required. **If an AST form is NOT
  available to its holder the reason will be jurisdictional rather than technical** — the holder reads a paper and
  has no admissible read of the source the form needs — and I will record which.
- **INDEPENDENT COMPUTATION: `20` per cent carryable, band `5`–`40`.** This is the form the lesson of the `27` bites
  on. The quantity has to be one the holding receipt already computes or can obtain from a digest-pinned file it
  already reads. A receipt that would have to grow a solver to carry its own pin **cannot carry it**, and that row
  is a backlog item rather than a census row.

**So the pinned shape of the answer is: of `272`, `82` computable, `59` carryable by their own holder, and of the
`23` not carryable the large majority are `INDEPENDENT COMPUTATION` rows whose holder does not compute the
quantity.**
