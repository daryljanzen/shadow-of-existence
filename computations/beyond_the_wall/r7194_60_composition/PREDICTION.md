# PRE-REGISTRATION — is the layer necessarily in the path, and does its parity bound what arrives?

*Written and committed BEFORE any of the computation below is run. `r7173` orders the pre-registration
and gives its reason in terms: **`a composition argument is easier to talk oneself into than a
measure-sharing one`**. ⌗ *That is the exact risk here, and it is sharper than `r7186`'s was, because
the pieces of a positive answer are **already receipted separately** and the whole temptation is to
multiply them without checking the product is defined.* **So the thing I am pre-registering against is
not a wrong number. It is a valid-looking composition of two true statements.***

## WHAT IS ALREADY RECEIPTED, LISTED SO IT CANNOT BE RE-SOLD AS NEW

- ⓵ *`r7186`: the crossing's kernel is a function of the DEGREE and the layer's parity is a condition
  on the CHARGE, and the `$S^3$` eigenvalue is constant along the charge ladder.* **The two cannot
  share a measure.** *That closed the measure route and is not re-opened.*
- ⓶ *`r7186`, from `P7`'s own scope note read through `P16`: the crossing's correspondence is
  null-boundary to null-boundary* **`with no spacelike slice entering the map`.** *`66` names this as
  the first place to look in either direction, and I agree it is.*
- ⓷ *`r7180`: the harmonic index is preserved across the crossing and the map is the IDENTITY on
  `$L$`, acting on the radial factor at fixed degree. The expansion leg is where frozen data
  ARRIVES.*
- ⓸ *`r7174` (`PO-84`): the squashing vanishes LINEARLY at the double root, so every CHARGED mode's
  eigenvalue runs to infinity there while the neutral one's does not move.* **`CARRIED IN LABEL, LOST
  IN AMPLITUDE`.**
- ⓹ *`r7184`: on the `$m=0$` sector the squashing drops out and `$\lambda=L(L+2)$` exactly, so the
  transmission there is a NUMBER rather than an envelope.*
- ⓺ *`r7186`: the layer's neutral slice is non-empty iff `$m=L/2-k$` reaches zero, i.e.* **iff the
  degree is EVEN.**

⇒ ***The temptation, stated so it is on the record before I can act on it: ⓸ says charged modes lose
amplitude at the double root, ⓺ says odd degrees have no neutral slice, and multiplying the two gives
`only even degrees arrive` — the odd-ladder bound, by composition, exactly as `r7173` conjectured.***
**I do not trust that product and the next section says why.**

## ⓟ① THE THREE THINGS THE PRODUCT NEEDS, AND WHICH I EXPECT TO FAIL

- ⓐ ***The layer must actually be in the path.*** *I expect this to HOLD, and not from a computation:
  `P15` already states that carrying `$(L,m)$` along the bead needs a harmonic decomposition of the
  layer at every point of the curve, the lift included, and that the decomposition exists and is the
  same one because the squashed layer is a left-invariant metric on the same group manifold.*
  **So I expect the structural half of `r7173`'s conditional to be satisfied, by reading rather than
  by arithmetic.**
- ⓑ ***The two loci must be the SAME locus.*** *⓸'s divergence is at the double root. ⓺'s parity is a
  condition on a decomposition taken on the layer. **I do not know that these are one place**, and if
  they are not, the product is a composition of two facts about two different points of the curve ---
  which is `r7186`'s own error class with the labels swapped for loci.* ⌗ **This is the one I most
  expect to be the real obstruction.**
- ⓒ ***`LOST IN AMPLITUDE` must mean ZERO and not SUPPRESSED.*** *I expect it to mean **suppressed by
  a computable finite factor at every finite degree**, with the vanishing only asymptotic.* ⇒ **If so,
  the odd ladder is not ABSENT, it is DOWN BY A NUMBER** --- *and a bound with a number in it is a
  different claim from a parity, which excludes.*

## ⓟ② SO WHAT I EXPECT, STATED AS THE OUTCOME I WILL BE HELD TO

***I expect the layer IS in the path and the parity still does NOT bound what arrives.*** *Specifically:
the layer's parity says which slices are NEUTRAL, not which modes EXIST --- the charged slices exist at
every degree, so the parity alone excludes nothing from the path.* ⇒ ***And I expect that if any bound
is available it is an AMPLITUDE bound and not a parity bound: a suppression of the charged slices,
carrying a number, by a divergence --- a different mechanism from the one `r7173` names.***

⛔ ***If that is what comes out, then the odd-ladder bound is NOT available by either route AS A PARITY
ARGUMENT, which `r7173` ⓷ says is a full discharge and a sharper one than `r7186`'s.*** **And I must
then NOT report the amplitude suppression as though it were the parity bound delivered late.** *It
would be a different claim with a different warrant, and `r7173` asked whether the PARITY bounds what
arrives.*

## ⓟ③ WHAT WOULD MAKE ME WRONG, NAMED IN ADVANCE

- *If the transport turns out to have **no slot** for `$m\ne0$` --- the charged slices absent from the
  path rather than suppressed along it --- then the parity IS the mechanism, `66`'s conjecture is
  right, and I will say so without hedging it into my own framing.*
- *If ⓑ comes out the other way and the divergence locus IS the decomposition locus, the product is
  defined and I will have to take the positive seriously rather than looking for ⓒ.*
- *If `LOST IN AMPLITUDE` is exact vanishing at finite degree rather than asymptotic, the odd ladder
  is genuinely absent and the bound is delivered --- by amplitude, and I will say which mechanism
  delivered it.*
- *If the layer is NOT in the path --- if `P15`'s every-point-of-the-curve statement does not survive
  being checked against the lift --- then both halves fail and the question closes negative on the
  structural half, which is the cleanest outcome available and the one I would least expect.*

## ⛔ ⓟ④ THE ESCAPES I AM CLOSING IN ADVANCE

- ⓵ ***I will not report a POSITIVE that rests on multiplying ⓸ by ⓺ without ⓑ.*** *If I cannot
  establish the two loci are one place, the answer is `the product is not defined on this path` and
  not `the bound holds`.*
- ⓶ ***I will not report a NEGATIVE as a discharge if what I actually found is an amplitude bound.***
  *A bound by a mechanism the order did not name is a finding, and it is not an answer to the question
  asked; both get said, separately.*
- ⓷ ***And I will not use `r7186`'s null-to-null sentence twice.*** *It closed the measure route
  because there is no spacelike datum AT THE CROSSING for a spacelike decomposition to be shared with.
  **Whether a spacelike layer is elsewhere in the PATH is a different question and that sentence does
  not answer it** --- `66` offered it as the first place to look, not as the answer, and reading it as
  the answer would be `r7178`'s guard: one paper's clause credited with a second question's result.*
