## ② ⛔ THE POSSIBILITY THE REGISTRATION HAS TO ADMIT UP FRONT

`P1` gives the `15.5` per cent extent to `χ` (70's `E4` failure, and my `−12.77` is its `−12.83`).
`P2`, by construction, takes that span difference OUT of the map leg — so it must put it somewhere, and
the only other place is `g`.

⇒ *** So the extent has to land in one leg or the other, and WHICH leg is a choice of pairing. That
means the `g`/`χ` attribution may not be well-posed at all, and "the distance map carries it" and "the
visibility carries it" may be two conventions over one object. *** ⚠ **This is registered as a live
outcome, not raised as a doubt afterwards** — `r7737`'s lesson is that the outcome you have not written
down in advance is the one you report badly.

⌗ *Which is also why `P2` is not a tautology to be dismissed: it is the complementary assignment, and
the pair of them brackets the convention.*

## ③ WHAT IS PAIRING-FREE, AND THEREFORE WHAT SURVIVES WHATEVER HAPPENS

Registered as a **code-level assertion to be checked, not a measurement**: an object is pairing-free
when no `η` grid of one arm ever meets the other's.

* ✔ `r7729`'s scalar cross term — it scales the source arm's **own** `x0` by `D_M(kernel)/D_M(source)`.
* ✔ the full arm swap, diagonal to diagonal — both arms native.
* ⇒ ✔ **therefore `r7733`'s `93.6` per cent**, which is computed on `r7729`'s four arrays alone.
* ✗ every Tier 1 hybrid (`X_cr` on an `lcdm` source and vice versa) and every Tier 2 `G`/`A` with
  mismatched `g` and `χ`.

⇒ **The check**: assert, from the arrays' own provenance, that the `93.6` per cent path touches no
hybrid. *If that assertion fails, the printed figure is in question too and I say so immediately.*
