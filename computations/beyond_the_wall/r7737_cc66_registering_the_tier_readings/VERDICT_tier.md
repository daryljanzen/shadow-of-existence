## ③ TIER 2 — `B1b` FAILED, AND THE REASON MATTERS MORE THAN THE FAILURE

| | |
|---|---|
| `B1a` the interaction residual | **`0.0002`** — the three terms are additive to two parts in ten thousand |

⛔ **Before calling that a finding I measured the fit's conditioning against this project's own
standing threshold** (`r7723` amendment 2: `|r| ≤ 0.70`, `VIF ≤ 2.0`):

| | corr | VIF | |
|---|---|---|---|
| visibility against map | **`−0.9838`** | **`31.0`** | ⛔ over |
| map against comb period | `−0.8710` | `4.1` | ⛔ over |
| visibility against comb period | `+0.7695` | `2.5` | ⛔ over |

⇒ *** The three-term fit FAILS the standing threshold on every pair. *** The amplitudes are
numerically *determined* (`±0.0056`, `±0.0003`, `±0.0042`; each over a hundred sd from unity, so the
failure is not noise) — **but determined and interpretable are different things.** At `|r| = 0.98` the
fit can move *shape* between the terms almost freely. What it cannot move is their *size*.

⇒ **So `B2`'s shares are NOT the evidence.** The evidence is the fit-free statistic: the
`l`-structured rms of each single-term swap, with no amplitude anywhere — `1.32e-02` (map),
`5.86e-04` (visibility), `9.83e-05` (comb period). *For the visibility to carry the map's
contribution it would need an amplitude near `22`, not near `1`.* And `B2` recomputed at unit
amplitudes gives `110.85 %` against the fitted `104.19 %` — **different digits, same conclusion**,
which is how a result that survives its own fit's degeneracy behaves.
