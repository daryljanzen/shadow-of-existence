## THE METHOD, AND WHY IT NEEDS NO GENEALOGY

*The tempting approach is to name each receipt's author: read the adding commit, map its revision to the seat
that held that counter then, compare. **That requires reconstructing a seat genealogy across generations that
no longer exist — `54`, `56`, `cc54`, `57`, `59`, `61` — and a reconstruction is a second instrument built to
check a condition that already exists, which is `PO-78`'s own newest face.***

⇒ **So the test is ONE-DIRECTIONAL and sound without naming anybody:**

> ***If the receipt was added to the tree BEFORE the adjudicating seat's first appearance anywhere in the
> record, then the adjudicator CANNOT be the author.***

*Each seat's first appearance is read from the log — the earliest commit whose subject carries that seat's own
stamp — and the receipt's birth from `git log --diff-filter=A` on its path.* ⌗ **This can only UNDER-count:
every `CANNOT-BE-AUTHOR` it reports is proved, and a key it does not report may still be one.** *That is the
conservative direction, and it is the direction a measurement about other seats' work has to err in.*

⌗ **And the third state is reported APART and never folded in**: *a receipt whose birth or whose adjudicating
seat cannot be read is `UNATTRIBUTABLE`, not a match and not a mismatch —* `r7436`'s rule, *that an instrument
which cannot say must say which nothing it means.*

## ⛔ WHAT THIS DOES NOT DO

**It does not re-open `r7447` `⑤`.** *The ruling stands and this revision is the act of taking it seriously.*

**It accuses no seat of anything.** *`CANNOT-BE-AUTHOR` is a statement about dates, not about care — and the
whole point of the figure is that it is nobody's fault.*

**It names no receipt's author.** *The test is one-directional precisely so that it never has to, and a
reconstruction of who wrote what across three seat generations is exactly the second instrument this row's
own newest face warns about.*

**It rewrites no pin, edits no receipt, and touches no baseline row.**

**It does not rule `PO-78` discharged.** *That is the row seat's call. What this supplies is whether the last
two words of its discharge clause are satisfiable by anyone now, which is a fact that call needs.*
