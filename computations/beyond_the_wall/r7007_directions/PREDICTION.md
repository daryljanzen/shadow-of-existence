## ⛭ AND THE OUTCOMES

| outcome | what it means |
|---|---|
| **① The fraction is near one and of the right sign** | The candidate is the carrier; the row closes on a prediction. |
| **② A minority share of definite sign** | ***The candidate is a real contributor of measured size and is not the carrier.*** What remains is a second source. |
| **③ A minority share whose sign is not definite across the bands** | The candidate contributes at the level of the measurement's own scatter and **cannot be told from zero band by band**, which is weaker than ② and is not "excluded". |
| **④ The wrong sign throughout** | The candidate works against the excess and the filter was testing the wrong object. |
| **⑤ Settles nothing** (the third failure mode above) | *A real outcome, tabled first.* |

**⛔ AND WHAT WOULD NOT BE CLAIMED UNDER ANY OF THEM.** That the excess is explained, or that the
candidate is excluded. *A minority share is neither.*
