==============================================================================
  STEP 2 -- ⒜ DO THEY AGREE ON PARITY THROUGH THE BRANCH POINT?
==============================================================================
  (a) P3's branch point is theta = 0, where r = 0.  Reflect theta -> -theta:
        r_P3(-theta) = -alpha*sin(theta)      = -r_P3(theta)?  True
      ⇒ r is ODD through the branch point.  The curve crosses onto r < 0,
        which is exactly the 'negative branch' P3 names.

  (b) P2's law in tilde_tau.  First the naive reflection:
        r^3(-tilde_tau) - r^3(tilde_tau) = 0
        so r^3 is EVEN in tilde_tau: True
      ⛔ AND THAT IS NOT A CONTINUATION THROUGH THE BRANCH POINT.  sinh^2 >= 0
         for real tilde_tau, so r^3 >= 0 on the whole real axis: the
         reflection never leaves the forward branch.  It is the SAME leg
         traversed backwards, not the other side.

  The continuation P2 actually uses is the imaginary offset.  Taking it at
  the conjugate critical point, tilde_tau -> -i pi alpha/3 + t:
        r^3 -> -2*M*alpha**2*cosh(3*tilde_tau/(2*alpha))**2
        at the critical point itself (t = 0):  r^3 = -2*M*alpha**2
        which is -2 M alpha^2:  True
  ⇒ r^3 is NEGATIVE on the continued branch.  So P2's continuation also
    carries r through zero and out the other side.

  ** ⒜ ANSWERED: the two continuations AGREE -- both carry r through zero
     onto a branch where it is negative -- and they agree on ODD, not even.
     ⛔ My pre-registered ⒜ said 'both give r^3 even'.  The EVEN reading is
     the reflection, and the reflection is not the crossing.  Half right:
     the agreement holds, the parity does not. **

==============================================================================
  STEP 3 -- ⒝ THE DUST STRESS-ENERGY, COMPONENT BY COMPONENT
==============================================================================
  Dust: T^{mu nu} = rho u^mu u^nu, pressure zero.  Two quantities carry the
  parity -- the density and the radial velocity -- and they are not the same.

  ⛔ THE NAIVE DENSITY IS NOT A DENSITY.  Writing rho = 3 M_b /(4 pi r^3)
        rho_naive(r)  = 3*M_b/(4*pi*r**3)
        rho_naive(-r) = -3*M_b/(4*pi*r**3)
        odd in r: True
     ⇒ it changes SIGN, and a negative mass density is not a state the
       matter can be in.  Taken at face value this is C3, undefined.

  ⌗ But the volume in the denominator is a VOLUME, and P3's own sentence
    settles which function of r it is: the areal radius is SIGNED and
    g_thth = r^2 is insensitive to its sign, so the proper volume of the
    swept 2-sphere's interior goes as |r|^3 and never as r^3.
        rho(r)  = 3*M_b/(4*pi*r**2*Abs(r))
        rho(-r) = 3*M_b/(4*pi*r**2*Abs(r))
        EVEN in r: True
     ⇒ the density is EVEN, and the sign of r is pure gauge in it.

  And the radial velocity.  On the arc-length picture u^r = dr/dl:
        dr/dtheta = alpha*cos(theta)
        at -theta: alpha*cos(theta)      EVEN in theta: True
     ⌗ dr/dtheta is EVEN in theta -- but r itself is ODD, so the velocity
       RELATIVE TO THE MATTER's own outward direction (the sign of r) flips:
       d|r|/dtheta = sign(r) dr/dtheta, and sign(r) is odd.
        d|r|/dtheta = Piecewise((0, Eq(sin(theta), 0)), (alpha*sin(2*theta)/(2*Abs(sin(theta))), True))
        at -theta:    Piecewise((0, Eq(sin(theta), 0)), (-alpha*sin(2*theta)/(2*Abs(sin(theta))), True))
        ODD: True

  ** ⒝ ANSWERED, and it splits exactly as priced: the energy density is
     EVEN, the radial momentum flux (rho times the areal rate) is ODD, and
     dust's pressure is identically zero and so trivially even. **

==============================================================================
  STEP 4 -- THE CONTROL: a species whose parity is the OTHER one
==============================================================================
  If 'even' came out of the algebra rather than out of the physics, then a
  species with a different equation of state would come out even too.
  species                           rho(r) ~    rho(-r)/rho(r)  parity
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------
  dust        (w = 0)                   r^-3                -1  ODD on the signed radius
  radiation   (w = 1/3)                 r^-4                 1  EVEN on the signed radius
  stiff       (w = 1)                   r^-6                 1  EVEN on the signed radius

  ⇒ ** THE CONTROL BITES: on the SIGNED radius radiation and stiff matter are
     EVEN while dust is ODD, purely because 4 and 6 are even and 3 is not.**
     So the signed-radius reading makes the crossing's behaviour depend on
     the equation of state's exponent PARITY -- which is not a physical
     distinction, and is the tell that the signed radius is the wrong
     variable for a density.  On |r| every species is even, as a density
     must be.  ⌗ That is the control's whole job: it shows the EVEN answer
     comes from the volume being a volume, not from the arithmetic.

==============================================================================
  STEP 5 -- ⒞ AND ⒟: IS THE MAP A BIJECTION, AND DOES COLLAPSE BECOME EXPANSION?
==============================================================================
  The crossing's data are (rho, d|r|/dl) at the branch point.  From STEP 3:
    rho          EVEN  -- the post-crossing density history is the mirror
                          of the pre-crossing one, value for value
    d|r|/dl      ODD   -- infall becomes outflow

  So the map is (rho(l), v(l)) |-> (rho(-l), -v(-l)).  It is its own inverse:
    applying it twice gives (rho(l), v(l)) back, so it is an INVOLUTION and
    therefore a bijection on the state space.  Nothing is lost and nothing
    is added: every moment of the approach has exactly one image.

  ** ⒞ = C1, a BIJECTION.  ⒟ = YES: the sign flip of the areal rate IS
     collapse becoming expansion, and it falls out of the parity rather
     than being put in. **

  ⌗ AND WHAT THAT SETTLES ABOUT THE ROW'S WORDING.  P15 says the crossing
    'carries whatever survives it unchanged ... because there is no interval
    in which anything could act'.  Measured here: the crossing is not a
    filter, so 'whatever survives' is EVERYTHING -- the survival clause is
    vacuous, not restrictive.  And the row's 'transmitted datum' is the
    whole approach, not a few numbers off it, so C2 is excluded rather than
    merely unchosen.
