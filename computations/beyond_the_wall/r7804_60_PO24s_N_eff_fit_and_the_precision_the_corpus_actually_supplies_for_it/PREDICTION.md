## ⛔ ONE PREMISE OF THE ORDER IS WRONG, AND IT CHANGES WHAT THE DECIDING ARM MEASURES

The order says `P16` **fixes** `N_eff` through the network. **`P16` says the opposite, in its own
words:** the network *"carries three thermalized neutrino species with the standard post-annihilation
temperature ratio … that is, the effective number `N_eff = 3.046`, the Standard Model value including
the small non-instantaneous-decoupling correction, **and it is adopted here rather than derived**"*,
and *"**this framework therefore makes no `N_eff` prediction**; the standard value is adopted."*

⇒ **So there is no corpus-derived `N_eff` and no corpus-derived error on it.** For `ω_b` the corpus
printed `η₁₀ = 6.13 ± 0.04` — an inherited datum with its own measured precision, which is what `r7800`
judged the excursion against. **For `N_eff` the corpus supplies an ADOPTED value and quotes an
EXTERNAL constraint:** *"Planck's `N_eff = 2.99 ± 0.17` is the number a departure would have to move."*

**This seat decides the fork rather than routing it**, because the order's own instruction — judge
against what the corpus supplies — has exactly one answer once the text is read:

- **the reference is `2.99 ± 0.17`, as the corpus itself quotes it**, and the deciding arm reports the
  excursion in those sigmas;
- **and the excursion is ALSO reported in absolute `N_eff`**, so any other prior can be applied to it
  by a reader who disagrees with that choice.

⌗ **And the asymmetry is itself a registered finding:** `0.17` on `2.99` is `5.7` per cent, against
`ω_b`'s `0.65` per cent. **`N_eff` has about nine times more room in sigma terms**, so an absorption
that `ω_b` could not pay for might be affordable here — which is precisely why the row could close on
this parameter and not on that one, and why the two cannot be compared in sigmas without saying so.

## ⌗ And one mechanical offset, named before it bites

`70`'s bank is centred on `N_eff = 3.0`; the corpus adopts `3.046`. **The response is measured about
`3.0`, so every excursion must be stated relative to `3.046` with that `0.046` offset carried
explicitly**, or a `0.046` shift would be reported as physics. `Ⓑ③` asserts the bank's centre so the
offset cannot be forgotten.
