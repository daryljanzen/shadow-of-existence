## ⛭⛭ THE STEP, AND WHY IT IS THIS ONE AND NOT THE OBVIOUS ONE

The obvious step is to pick a side.  **This revision does not start there**, because this corpus's own
guard says it cannot: *before two stretches are identified by a label, check how many-to-one the label
is on the object*, and *a sentence located in the paper is a fact about the object the paper is talking
about, and which object that is has to be established first*.

So the first question is **whether the two routes' ratios are the same ratio at all**.  Each is a
quotient, and a quotient has two halves that are each defined by a convention:

- the NUMERATOR radius — a kinematic caustic radius, versus a zero-velocity surface;
- the DENOMINATOR mass — what each route calls "the virial mass", which is an overdensity convention
  in the cluster case and need not be one at all in the Local Volume case.

A direction is contested only if both halves correspond.  If either does not, the two numbers are not
two readings of one quantity and the row's arithmetic must be restated on whichever one it actually
needs.

## ⇒ THE PREDICTION, STATED SO IT CAN FAIL

**I expect the disagreement to be REAL rather than definitional** — that is, I expect the two routes'
quotients to be comparable objects, and the contest to survive the audit.

**PASS CONDITION (my prediction holds):** the numerator radii are the same kind of object to within a
stated factor, AND the denominator masses reduce to one convention with a conversion factor whose
distance from unity is smaller than the gap between the two reported ratios (`~1.2–2.2` against
`~0.6`, a factor of about `2` to `3.7`).  Then the direction is contested in substance and the next
revision picks a side on evidence.

**FAILURE OF THE PREDICTION (a negative, to be reported AS a negative):** if either half fails to
correspond — above all if the denominators differ by a factor comparable to or larger than the gap —
then ***there is no contested direction, there are two different quantities***, and what this
revision delivers is the restatement rather than the verdict.  **I will report that as my prediction
failing, not as the question being answered.**
