## The registered negative — what would show I have a convention WRONG

For each count, the receipt will compute the figure under **my** convention AND under at
least one competing convention, and print both. **If a competing convention lands on or near
the paper's printed figure, that is evidence the paper's convention was the competing one and
mine is the imposition** — and in that case the right outcome is to adopt the paper's and say
so, not to keep mine and call the paper stale. I register that I will report any such case
even where it costs me the finding.
