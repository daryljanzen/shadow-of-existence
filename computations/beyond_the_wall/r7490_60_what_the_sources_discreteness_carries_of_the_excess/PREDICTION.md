## ⌈ THE ORDER, AND THE RULING IT CARRIES

`r7489` ① — *"with the components converged and the discreteness effect sized, say what part of the
`0.045` excess the discreteness carries once amplitude and phase are read separately, since the two
moves cancel in the projected statistic and need not cancel in the likelihood's own metric."*

**And the ruling that makes the question well posed, which is `69`'s and is taken as given:** the
closed `S³` source spectrum is **discrete by degree**, one mode per `L` at `k_L = √(L(L+2))/r₀`, and
`P15`'s own transfer is the `L ≥ 2`-floored sum over that ladder; the ladder reaches
`k_max D_M ≃ 4000` at one mode per degree, so **it IS that spectrum and not an approximation to an
integral.** ⇒ **The continuum is the check, and `r7480`'s `9.6` and `9.3` per cent are the measured
effect of the source's discreteness on the contrast — a property of the construction.**

## ⌈ THE ARMS

- **Ⓐ** ⚑ **THE IDENTITY THE WHOLE DECOMPOSITION RESTS ON:** the projected statistic is **exactly
  the product** of the amplitude ratio and the phase cosine, `proj = rms × cos`, by the algebra of
  the three sums. *Predicted to hold to machine precision on all four banked spectra, not just to
  the five printed decimals.* **If it fails, there is no clean decomposition and the run says so.**
- **Ⓑ** on the ladder — the arm's own spectrum by the ruling — the excess is **almost entirely
  amplitude**: the amplitude part above `1` exceeds the phase part's deficit by more than two orders
  of magnitude. *Predicted: amplitude `≈ +0.0455`, phase `≈ −0.0003`.*
- **Ⓒ** on the continuum the phase part is **an order of magnitude larger** than on the ladder,
  while the amplitude part grows by under a tenth of itself. *Predicted: phase `≈ −0.0045`.*
- **Ⓔ** ⚑ **THE ANSWER:** in a metric that does not let the two moves cancel, the discreteness
  carries **between `10` and `20` per cent** of the `0.045` excess, against `0.09` per cent in the
  projected statistic. *Registered as a band before the arithmetic, because a number produced after
  choosing the metric is not a prediction.*
- **Ⓕ** the two moves are **opposite in sign and nearly equal in magnitude** in the product's own
  terms — `Δ(rms)·cos` against `rms·Δ(cos)` — to better than `10` per cent of each other. *That near
  equality is why they cancel, and it is a fact about this pair of spectra rather than a general
  property, so it is registered as a measurement and not assumed.*
