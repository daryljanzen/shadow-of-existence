========================================================================================================
  Ⓐ THE IDENTITY THE DECOMPOSITION RESTS ON:  proj  ==  rms × cos
========================================================================================================
      It is the algebra of the three sums and nothing else:
        proj = S_AB/S_BB,   rms = sqrt(S_AA/S_BB),   cos = S_AB/sqrt(S_AA·S_BB)
        rms × cos = sqrt(S_AA/S_BB) · S_AB/sqrt(S_AA·S_BB) = S_AB/S_BB = proj
      ⇒ so it must hold to machine precision, and that is what is checked -- NOT the
        five printed decimals, which could agree by rounding.
