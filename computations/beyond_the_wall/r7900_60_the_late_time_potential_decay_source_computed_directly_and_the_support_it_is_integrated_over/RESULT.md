### ⛔ `Ⓔ⓸` IS REFUTED, AND IT WAS THE STRONG EXPECTATION

The registration asked whether the difference is **under one per cent** of TT across the band. It is
**thirty-eight per cent**, at every sum. ⌗ *`r7896`'s and `r7898`'s expectations were registered as
weak and both were refuted; this one was registered as the **strong** kind, on a structural reading of
the instrument rather than an extrapolation, and it was refuted too. The grounds were better and the
answer was still no.*

**The reading that failed was not the support fact — it was the inference from it.** The support fact
stands exactly as registered: the solve stops at `ETA_END = 1893.12`, which is `z = 53.545`, against
`eta_0 = 14145.33` at `z = 0`. What was wrong was concluding that a term integrated over `13` per cent
of the conformal time to today must therefore be small in this band. **Early ISW is inside that range,
lands at exactly these multipoles, and carries the band.** The registration named this as the reachable
negative and said it would be the more useful result. It is.

### AND THE TERM DOES RESPOND, MONOTONICALLY

The fraction rises `0.381250` → `0.392215`, a spread of `1.097e-02` and a relative rise of `2.88` per
cent across the registered sums. Monotone at every step.

⚠ **`Ⓔ⓷` as registered cannot be decided, and the fault is the registration's.** It asks for flatness
*within the instrument's own reproducibility*, and this instrument is **deterministic** — a re-run of a
point is bit-identical, so that spread is zero and no real measurement can satisfy the criterion. ⌗
*It is reported as degenerate rather than quietly replaced with a bar chosen after the numbers were
seen, which is the one thing registering in advance exists to prevent. The response's size is printed
above so a reader can judge it on evidence rather than on a threshold I would be choosing now.*

⛔ **`Ⓔ⓵` and `Ⓔ⓶` cannot be decided at all**, because they compare against CAMB and `cc66`'s `⑱ ③`
has not landed on `main`. The registration fixed in advance what happens then: the instrument's side is
reported alone and the comparison is **named as waiting**. It is waiting.

The potential-decay term's **own power responds about three and a half times as strongly to the sum as
the monopole's does**. That is why the band's ISW fraction rises at all: a term that moved with the
monopole would leave the ratio flat. ⌗ *The registration asked whether the term responds; the pair
basis answers the sharper question of which part of it does, and that question was not put in advance,
so it is carried as unregistered and could not and did not change the outcome.*

It also fixes how the thirty-eight per cent must be read. At Σ = `0`, `sw*isw` carries `24.2` per cent
of the band's TT and `isw*isw` only `8.5`. **Most of the with/without difference is interference with
the monopole, not the term's own power** — so "the ISW accounts for `38` per cent of this band" would
be a misstatement, and is not made here.

## ⇒ WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE ORDER'S QUESTION

The order asked whether **the instrument's late-ISW term** responds to Σ as CAMB's does, and said that
if it does not, that is a mechanism located exactly where the move is.

**The switch the order names cannot answer that question**, and this revision establishes why rather
than guessing: the term it switches is integrated only to `z = 53.545`, so what responds in this band is
the **early** ISW. The late ISW — the Λ-driven decay at `z ≲ 1` — is outside the instrument's support
entirely, and so is neither measured nor measurable by turning this switch.

⇒ *So the answer is in two parts, and both are needed. The instrument HAS a potential-decay term in
this band, it is large, and it responds to the sum more sharply than the acoustic monopole does. And
the instrument has NO late-time potential decay at all, because its solve ends at `z = 53.545`.* Which of
those is the mechanism the order is looking for cannot be settled by this measurement, and saying
which would be the reading the registration declined to make.

## WHAT IS NOT CLAIMED

- Not that the instrument's early-ISW response **agrees** with CAMB's. That is `Ⓔ⓵`/`Ⓔ⓶` and it waits
  on `cc66`'s split; nothing here is a comparison.
- Not that the missing late ISW **causes** the low-end lean. A missing term in the band where the lean
  sits is a coincidence of location and a candidate, exactly as `r7892` recorded of a different
  candidate. Establishing a cause would need the lean to move when the term is supplied.
- Not that supplying it is safe. Extending `ETA_END` to today changes what the instrument is, every
  banked spectrum was computed without it, and whether the perturbation equations hold to `z = 0` is a
  separate question. The registration declined that extension and this revision keeps the refusal.
- Not a limit. `r7888`'s `193.545` meV is untouched and no number here is a limit.
- Not a statement about where in `eta` the response accumulates. That would need the eta-resolved
  source, which the instrument can save through an existing switch; it was not run here, and it is the
  measurement that would turn the support reading from a structural fact into a profile.
