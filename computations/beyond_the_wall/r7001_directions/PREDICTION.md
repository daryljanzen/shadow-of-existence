*The order's ⓶ requires it: **"pre-register the null again: a candidate that passes above the floor and
fails below it is a real outcome, and so is one that cannot be measured there at all."***

## ⌗ ⓵ — WHAT THE ESTIMATOR HAD TO CLEAR BEFORE ANYTHING IT SAYS BELOW THE FLOOR WAS READ

*This ordering is the order's, and it is the standing guard **"a proxy that fails where its object
lives is worse than no proxy"** made procedural.*

1. **The held period is MEASURED, not assumed.** The acoustic period in $q$ is $2$ by construction and
   is not $2$ in the fields. It is read off the drift of the recovered phase and iterated to
   self-consistency, per field and per arm, and its own uncertainty is taken from disjoint sub-ranges.
2. **It agrees with the window estimator where the window estimator is sound.** Same quantity, same
   fields, same range — means compared, with each estimator's ripple reported against its own window
   width.
3. **The leak is measured against the dependence being measured.** The held period is perturbed at the
   scale it is known to, commonly and per field, and the induced shift in the candidate's span across
   the bands is compared with that span. ⇒ ***If the leak is not small against the span, the estimator
   cannot answer the question and that is the result.***

## ⛭ ⓶ — WHAT EACH OUTCOME MEANS, ALL FIVE, INCLUDING BOTH NULLS

| outcome | what it means |
|---|---|
| **① Passes sign, growth and curvature on the newly-reached bands** | The candidate survives every test the filter has. `PO-56`'s list is down to one candidate that has failed nothing, and what remains is a coupling calculation rather than a search. |
| **② Passes sign and growth, fails curvature** | The candidate is excluded on a pre-committed tooth, and the list closes empty. *That is the strongest available negative result and it is claimable here.* |
| **③ Fails sign below the floor after passing above it** | ***A real outcome and the order names it.*** The candidate's departure would change sign exactly where the target's growth is concentrated — which excludes it more sharply than a curvature failure, because sign is the first tooth. |
| **④ THE FIRST NULL — the new bands are reached but the tooth that would decide cannot be applied** | The target's own curvature is determined only on a range the candidate cannot be measured on. ⇒ *The filter would be out of teeth rather than the candidate out of chances, and the result is a statement about the FILTER.* |
| **⑤ THE SECOND NULL — the estimator does not reach below the floor either** | The floor is a property of the fields and not of the method: below the first acoustic excursion there is no oscillation amplitude to estimate, whatever the estimator. ⇒ *`PO-56` closes as unresolvable with this statistic, and the row says so.* |

**⛔ AND WHAT WOULD NOT BE CLAIMED UNDER ANY OF THEM.** That the candidate's *size* matters: the filter's
teeth are sign, growth and curvature, and the channel's coupling is not fixed, so an amplitude ratio
five times smaller than the target's departure is not thereby excluded. *Size is reported and is not a
tooth.*
