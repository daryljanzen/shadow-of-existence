## THE FOUR CONSTRUCTS, READ FROM THE CORPUS AND NOT INVENTED

| | construct | where it lives |

| `C1` | a **heading** that asserts a status of its contents | e.g. `§What stays open`, `§Scope and open problems`, `§What is held open, and on what footing`, `§What is genuinely unpaid, at its actual size`, `§What is not on this list` |

**A site CARRIES a classification** iff ≥1 of `C1`–`C4` applies to it.
**It DISAGREES** iff the construct asserts a status its own prose contradicts: a construct saying
*closed / established / boundary / nothing-left* over prose stating something open, or a construct
saying *open / unpaid / held-open* over prose stating the thing answered or not owed.
