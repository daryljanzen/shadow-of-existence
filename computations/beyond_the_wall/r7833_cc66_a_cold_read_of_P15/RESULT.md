## ⌈ ① THE DEFECT LIST — complete and unfiltered

### ⛔ ① line `466`–`467`, `§coherence` — the bound is `3.30` and the sentence says "within three"

**It says:** *"the wavenumber-independent `$0.4835\,\Psi_i$`, **within three per cent** of the frozen
value `$\Psi_i/2$` the free oscillator carries at every wavenumber."*

`0.4835/0.5` is **`3.30` per cent** low. `3.30 > 3`, so the bound is **false as written**. And the
cited receipt says so itself, and adds the part that matters more:

> *`$0.4835/0.5$` is `3.30` per cent low. On the SPECTRUM that is `$(0.4835/0.5)^2=0.93509$`,
> **`6.49` per cent** … The comparison the paper makes on the potential is **half** the one that
> reaches the spectrum.*

⇒ **What it should say:** `3.3` per cent below `$\Psi_i/2$` **on the potential, and `6.5` per cent on
the spectrum** — the latter being the figure the acoustic heights carry, and the one a reader who
takes "three per cent" as the size of the approximation is being under-told by a factor of two.

### ⛔ ② line `2141`–`2142`, `§refit-bound` — the heights claim, where the receipt already fixed the PEAKS half and not this one

**It says:** *"height ratios `$2.291$` and `$2.348$` against `$2.291$` and `$2.349$`: within a tenth
of a multipole on every peak and **within a fiftieth of a per cent on the heights**."*

| source | the height agreement |
|---|---|
| the receipt's measurement (arm-of-record row, one matched locator) | **`-0.011\%`** and **`-0.017\%`** |
| the paper's stated bound, *"a fiftieth of a per cent"* | `0.02\%` |
| **what the paper's own printed pair implies** (`2.348` vs `2.349`) | **`-0.043\%`** |

⇒ **The bound is TRUE of the measurement** (`0.011` and `0.017` are both inside `0.02`) **and FALSE
of the evidence the sentence prints** (`0.043 > 0.02`). Four-significant-figure rounding overstates
the real agreement about threefold.

**And the receipt already found exactly this, for the peaks, in these words** (`r7653 ⓵`):

> *Put BOTH paths through the one matched locator and they agree to under a tenth of a multipole at
> every peak and to a hundredth of a per cent on the heights … **So the claim HOLDS, and comfortably;
> what does not hold is the pair of figures offered as its evidence.***

*The peaks half was repaired* — the paper's offsets `+0.00/+0.02/-0.01/-0.09` now track the receipt's
`-0.00/+0.01/-0.02/-0.08` and sit inside a tenth of a multipole. ***The heights half was not.***

⇒ **What it should say:** give the agreement as the receipt measures it, `0.011\%` and `0.017\%` on
`$P_1/P_2$` and `$P_1/P_3$`, instead of inviting the reader to difference two independently rounded
four-figure ratios.

⇒ **So each fix is a coordinated paper-and-receipt change, and in ① the question is whether the
receipt's finding should be DISCHARGED by rewording the paper or KEPT STANDING as a live criticism of
