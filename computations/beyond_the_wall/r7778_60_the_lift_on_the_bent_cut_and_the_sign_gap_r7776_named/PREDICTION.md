## Ⓐ — what the fork is about

corpus.*
**REACHABLE NEGATIVE:** one of the six is stated on `μ` itself ⇒ `r7776`'s fork **survives** there

### ② The parity of the radiation term in the contour's radicand
**PREDICT:** with `μ = 2M + K/r`, `1 − f = μ/r + r²/α² = 2M/r + K/r² + r²/α²`; the radiation term
`K/r²` is **EVEN** in `r` and the dust term `2M/r` is **ODD**, so radiation enters the comoving
velocity with the **same** sign on both branches. Checked symbolically and at `±|r|` numerically.
**REACHABLE NEGATIVES:** the bent radicand is not `μ/r + r²/α²`; or the radiation term enters oddly.

### ③ The parity of the radiation density
**PREDICT:** radiation's contribution to the rate is `K/r⁴`, **even and positive** at `+|r|` and
`−|r|` alike; so the radiation density on the conjugate branch is positive, and `r7776`'s *"a
negative radiation term is not radiation"* was a fact about `μ`'s bookkeeping in a **signed**
coordinate, not about a density.
**REACHABLE NEGATIVE:** the density read off the rate is negative on the conjugate branch.

### ④ Two maps, and whether they are the same map

`r = −r₀cosh^{2/3}(3 Re τ̃/2α)` into `(dr/dτ̃)² = 2M/r + r²/α²` with `2M > 0` is an identity (to

## Ⓑ — what the bend does to the lift

### ⑤ The lift's inner boundary
On the vacuum cut the radicand `2M/r + r²/α²` is negative on exactly `−r₀ < r < 0`, `r₀ = (2Mα²)^{1/3}`,
so the lift runs from the comoving turnaround **to the branch point**. **PREDICT:** on the bent cut the
radicand has **TWO** roots at `r < 0` — an outer one near `−r₀` and a **NEW inner one at
`|r| ≈ K/2M ≈ 1.5 Mpc`** — so the imaginary excursion is an interval **strictly inside** `(−r₀, 0)`
and **does not reach the branch point**.
**REACHABLE NEGATIVES:** no root at `r < 0` (radiation removes the lift outright); exactly one root
(the lift still reaches `r = 0`); an inner root off `K/2M` by more than a factor of two.

### ⑥ The outer root's displacement
**PREDICT:** the comoving turnaround moves **inward** (smaller `|r|`) by a relative amount of order
`K/(2M r₀) ~ 3e-4`.
**REACHABLE NEGATIVES:** it moves outward; or by more than `1e-2` relative.

### ⑦ The imaginary bound
**PREDICT:** quadrature on the vacuum cut reproduces `πα/3` to `≤ 1e-8` relative; on the bent cut the
accumulated `|Im τ̃|` across the lift is **STRICTLY LESS** than `πα/3`, by a relative amount at or
below `1e-3`. **⇒ the printed bound is independent of `M` and *not* independent of `K`.**
**REACHABLE NEGATIVES:** equal to `πα/3` on the bent cut (`K`-independent after all); greater than it;
or divergent.

### ⑧ The lap's segment count
**PREDICT:** on the bent cut the radicand is **positive** on `−1.5 Mpc ≲ r < 0`, so `Re τ̃` advances
there and the lap's segments rise from **three** (collapse, lift, expansion) to **four**.
**REACHABLE NEGATIVE:** three, with ⑤'s negative.

### ⑨ The approach from the conjugate side
**PREDICT:** `d ln|r| / d ln|τ̃|` measured by quadrature between `1e-4` and `1e-3` Mpc on the bent
cut's conjugate branch is `0.500 ± 0.001` — **radiation's**, with `τ̃` **real** there — matching the
matter-branch exponent `r7776` measured at `0.500022`.
**REACHABLE NEGATIVES:** `2/3` (dust's); a complex `τ̃` on that interval; no power law.

### ⑩ The two branches' rates
**PREDICT:** at `|r| = 1e-3` Mpc the rates `h²(+|r|)` and `h²(−|r|)` agree to better than `1e-8`
relative (radiation dominates and is even); at `|r| = r₀` they differ by order unity (dust dominates
and is odd).
**REACHABLE NEGATIVES:** agreement at `r₀`; or disagreement at `1e-3` Mpc.
