WHAT WAS COMPUTED TWICE, AND WHY

  arm 7's two imaginary accumulations.  The measured direction REVERSED the registered one, and a
  reversed sign must not rest on one quadrature through an inverse square root.  addendum.py
  recomputes both at 50 digits with mpmath and with each simple zero of the radicand substituted
  away (u^2 = |s - s_end|).  The vacuum accumulation returns pi*alpha/3 to 2.3e-29 on that
  machinery, which is what licenses the bent-cut number beside it.
  ⌗ A u == 0 guard inside that substitution is NOT enough: at 50 digits a quadrature node can be
    close enough to the endpoint that lo + u**2 rounds TO the endpoint, where the radicand is
    exactly zero.  The guard is on the radicand's VALUE.  The first form raised ZeroDivisionError.
