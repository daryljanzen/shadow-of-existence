## ② WHAT COUNTS AS A REPAIRED RECEIPT — FIXED NOW, BECAUSE AFTERWARDS IT IS NOT A TEST

*A receipt counts as **repaired** only when all three hold:*

1. **every one of its owed rows is either repaired or withdrawn with its case written down beside the
   repair** — never by reclassification, which is the bound's own clause;
2. the receipt **re-runs green** on this tree;

⇒ *A receipt with four rows repaired and one left is **not** a repaired receipt and scores `0`. The unit
is the receipt, which is `r7329`'s own ruling and the thing `r7331` ① caught itself balancing against.*
