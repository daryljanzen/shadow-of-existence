# The map was the stale one

Two coherence items ruled. They are the phase's two cleanest cases, and they fall on
opposite sides of it.

## Item 2 — the papers were right, and the register alone was stale

**The item.** Whether the damping-tail displacement of the inferred tilt is a tension.
Stated open in `P6`, `P15` and `P18`.

**What the papers say — and they agree with one another, which nothing in this phase had
yet done.**

- `P15` §diffusion-scale, in print: "*Not* settled, and unchanged by that run: whether a
  displacement of that size in the inferred tilt is a *tension* … what would make it a
  tension is a constraint on $n_s$ that does not run through the damping tail, which this
  fit does not supply."
- `P6`: "whether the displacement of the tilt it induces is a tension needs a constraint on
  $n_s$ that does not run through the damping tail."
- `P18`, frontier item 7: "the displacement it returns depends on the window fitted, so it
  is a tension only against an independent measure of the tilt."

**What the strike said.** `PO-24`, struck `r6801`: the displacement is $+0.0034\pm0.0031$ —
one sigma, "on a parameter the construction inherits rather than predicts," so "there is no
tension to adjudicate."

**The ruling.** *The strike and the papers disagree about nothing measured.* The strike's
own reason — an inherited input taking a different value is a different input, not a
conflict — is `P15`'s reason, at l.480, in the same words. They differ only in what they
conclude from it: the strike concluded there is nothing to adjudicate and retired the row;
the papers conclude the question is unsettled, because the instrument that would settle it
is not in hand.

That is `PO-86`'s **third outcome**, applied for the first time: the strike stands as a
stopping rule, the item is open, and **the row returns live with its blocker named.**

**And the blocker is workable, not data-gated.** `P15` l.482 names the routes itself — "the
low- and mid-$\ell$ shape, the polarisation spectra, lensing — which is what a joint fit
means and is where the question properly sits." Those data exist. Unlike `PO-47`, nothing
here waits on a measurement; it waits on a run.

**A second half no row has ever carried.** `P15` l.492: "the magnitude is not robust to a
factor of three — imposing the ratio on the measured spectrum gives the figures above, while
applying it inside the instrument's own source … gives $0.92$ in $\chi^{2}$ per bin. The
larger comes from the more faithful route, so the wash is stated at the weaker bound, and
closing that factor is named here as **work not done**." A paper saying "work not done" with
no row to carry it is what this phase exists to find. It is the same question at finer
faithfulness, so `r7013` ② — the row's half, not a row of its own.

**Carried forward, not re-run.** The Gaussian-minus-power-law residual at a correlation of
$1.000000$; the tilt's fixed seven-tenths share at either ratio; the $6.3\times$
window-to-window spread; the shape residual as an upper bound and the displacement as not
one. All of it the strike's, and all of it the row's asset. **Only the verdict is
superseded.**

**No paper edit is owed.** For this item the three papers were already coherent with one
another and already right, and the register was the sole stale artefact. No gap between
rows, no contradiction in print, nothing to rewrite — just a map that retired a live item.
It is the cleanest vindication available of reading the papers against the map rather than
the map against itself.

## Item 28 — never a corpus item at all

**The item.** Whether a child universe necessarily lives in its parent's cosmological
constant. Stated in the explainer, by the explainer, and in no paper.

**The ruling: not open.** The construction answers it. `P17`'s lap — "from the comoving
turnaround to the branch point, the cosmic time of the bead law" — puts the parent's
collapse and the child's expansion on **one curve on one substrate**, whose throat is the
single invariant $\alpha$; and `P15` fixes the expansion rate "by the cosmological constant
alone." One substrate, one scale, one curve through the branch point. There is no
inheritance to ask after: every generation lives in the same constant.

`69` corrected the explainer's sentence at `r7371` under the watch marker
`lineage-constant`. **Both pinned literals were verified in place here, in context, rather
than taken on report** — which is the rule for ruling an item, and the report happened to
be right.

The explainer's surviving clause, a small inheritance of particulars, is homed at `P7`
§inherited-datum — `PO-16`, struck on the ground that deriving the datum is a bonus rather
than a debt — so it is not a second unhomed claim.

No row, no remainder, no paper edit. **The one item of the 29 that was a defect of the
plain-language layer and not of the corpus.**

## Where the phase stands

Eight of 29 items ruled. One new row (`PO-87`), one reopen (`PO-24`), one stale map brought
current (the ledger's family view), and three items that needed nothing because the papers
were right.

And the three failure modes are now all in evidence, which was not knowable at `r7371`:

| the item was… | and… | items |
|---|---|---|
| open | the gap lay between correct strikes → a new row | 1 |
| open | a strike priced it and could not decide it → the row returns live | 2 |
| not open | the paper's own ground settles it → the strike is upheld | 3, 7, 11 |

**The register has been wrong in both directions now** — it struck a live item at `r6801`
and it carried none for a live item through eleven revisions. That is worth stating plainly,
because it is the reason the phase reads the papers first and the map second.
