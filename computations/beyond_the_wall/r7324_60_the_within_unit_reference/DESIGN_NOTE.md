# `r7324` — what a per-seat comparison costs, and why `15.3` points was never resolvable

***This is arithmetic on a DESIGN. It registers no population, no size, no draw and no band, and it is
not the per-seat measurement — which `r7319` says is not ordered and which this seat was told not to
pre-register.*** *What `r7319`'s ruling ③ asked for is settled here instead: a per-seat comparison needs
a within-unit reference, and the reference has a price that has to be in the draw rather than bolted on.*

## ① THE TWO SPREADS, AND NEITHER IS THE OTHER

| spread | axis | measured | by whom |
|---|---|---|---|
| **reader** | the same keys, read twice | ***`30.0` points*** (`−52.5`, `−2.9`), excluding `0` | `66` at `r7319`, on `70`'s two-agent split |
| **sampling** | different keys, read once | binomial SE ***`7.4` points*** on `40`; exact split-half `|p1−p2|` mean ***`12.2`***, `95`th percentile ***`25.0`*** | this seat, here, on `r7320`'s own `40` rows |

⇒ ***The reader spread is `66`'s finding and is cited, not re-derived. The sampling spread is this
seat's and is measured on rows already read, so it costs nothing to take.*** *They are different causes
with different fixes: more keys shrinks the sampling spread and does nothing to the reader spread; a
second reader of the SAME keys is the only thing that measures the reader spread at all.*

## ② THE NOISE FLOOR, WHICH IS WHAT SETTLES IT

*The `95` per cent half-width on a difference between two units of `n` keys each, at the observed rate:*

| keys per unit | noise floor | the `15.3`-point gap |
|---|---|---|
| `40` — this seat's sample | `20.5` points | ***INSIDE*** |
| `69` — the largest blind read in the campaign | `15.6` points | ***INSIDE*** |
| `158` | `10.3` points | outside |

⇒ ⛔ ***So `r7299`'s `15.3`-point between-seat gap sits at or inside the noise floor of every sample
size this campaign has actually used — before the reader spread is counted at all.*** *`66`'s eighty-eighth
member says the reference was never taken; taking it on the sampling axis as well says the difference was
not resolvable even in principle at these sizes.*

## ③ WHAT IT COSTS TO MEAN SOMETHING

| to do this | cost per seat |
|---|---|
| resolve a `15.3`-point between-seat difference at `80` per cent power | ***`158` keys*** |
| resolve a `30.0`-point difference | `40` keys |
| bound the reader difference under `15.3` points, paired at `70`'s observed discordance | ***`50` double-read keys*** |
| the same bound with two readers on DIFFERENT keys | `72` keys |

⇒ ***A bounded draw of `40` per seat can resolve a `30`-point gap and nothing finer.*** *Five seats at
`158` is `790` keys read, which is not a bounded sample by any reading of that word.*

## ④ THE RECOMMENDATION, WHICH IS A FORK AND BELONGS TO THE GATING SEAT

- ⒜ ***Read `158` per seat.*** *It resolves the figure the campaign was calibrated on. It is `790` keys
  across five seats and it is not bounded.*
- ⒝ ***Compare two seats at a time with a pooled reference.*** *Cheaper, but every pair needs its own
  double-read block and the comparisons do not compose.*
- ⒞ ***Drop the between-seat comparison and report each seat's rate with its own interval, plus ONE
  double-read block per seat.*** *`40` keys plus `50` double-read keys per seat says what each seat's rate
  is and how much of its distance from another seat is reader spread — and says nothing it cannot support.*

⇒ ***This seat recommends ⒞.*** *It is the only one of the three that a bounded draw can carry.* ⌗ *And
the `r7299` calibration half that the `15.3`-point gap carried **is already withdrawn, at `r7319`, on the
reader spread alone** — so what the noise floor adds is not a reason to reopen that decision but a second,
independent reason it was right: the difference was also below what any sample in the campaign could
resolve. **The design decision is the gating seat's and nothing here pre-registers any of it.***

## ⚠ WHAT THIS CANNOT SETTLE

- *The sampling figure is ONE seat's single read of `40` rows. **A split-half of one reader's verdicts is
  the spread of a `20`-row estimate and not of a `40`-row one**, and it is reported as that.*
- *The reader figure is `70`'s, on a different population and a different text. **Carrying it onto this
  seat assumes the reader spread travels, which nothing here tests** — and the one key this seat and `71`
  both read agreed, which is `1` key and not a reference.*
- *The costs assume independent keys and the observed rate. **A clustered draw — several keys from one
  receipt — inflates every one of them**, and `r7320`'s own sample drew `40` keys from `17` receipts.*
