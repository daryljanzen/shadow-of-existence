## THE TWO CLASSES, OPERATIONALLY — SO THE SCAN IS DECIDABLE AND NOT A VIBE

- **CLASSIFICATION ERROR.** The asserted status contradicts what the referenced passage establishes. *The
  label is wrong about the state.* ⓬'s arm is built to find exactly this.
- **ONE-STATE VIOLATION.** The asserted status is **correct**, and the referenced passage presents a **record
  of states rather than one state** — it narrates a debt and then pays it. Operational marker, from the
  ruled instance: a sentence asserting that something *is owed / is qualified / remains / must first be
  closed*, whose discharge is in the same section. *A paper presents one state, never a history of states.*

⌗ **The two are disjoint by construction**: a one-state violation requires the label to be right, so no site
can be scored both. A site can be scored **neither**, and on ⑩'s evidence most will be.
