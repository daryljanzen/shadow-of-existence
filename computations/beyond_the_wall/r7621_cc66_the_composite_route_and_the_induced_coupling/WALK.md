## What the measurement is

The order's reachable negative was *"the induced coupling needs an infrared scale the construction does
not supply, so $1/g^2 = c\log(\Lambda/\mu)$ has a free $\mu$."* **The face is compact.** A compact
manifold has no infrared infinity: its Dirac spectrum is gapped at $5/2\alpha$ — `P13`'s own figure — and
a gap is an infrared scale. So $\mu$ is *not* the free datum, and every slot of the formula fills:
$N = 4$ from the spinor rank, $\mu = 5/2\alpha$ from the gap, $\Lambda = 1/\beta$ from `r7619`.

⇒ And then:

$$\frac{\Lambda}{\mu} = \frac{1/(2\pi\alpha)}{5/(2\alpha)} = \frac{1}{5\pi} \approx 0.0637 < 1 .$$

The cutoff sits **below** the gap by a factor of $5\pi \approx 15.7$. The log is negative
($-2.754$), which would make $g^2$ negative. **But the sign is not the finding — the count is.** The
number of face Dirac modes with $|\lambda| \le \Lambda$ is **zero**: every $|\lambda| = (5/2+k)/\alpha$
exceeds $\Lambda$. ⇒ **A loop of a field with no modes under the cutoff induces nothing.** The mechanism
does not merely come out short of a number; it does not engage.

⌗ *Why that distinction is the point. "A free $\mu$" leaves Sakharov's mechanism intact and one datum
short — fix $\mu$ and you have a coupling. "Zero modes under the cutoff" says there is nothing for the
mechanism to act on, whatever $\mu$ is. The first is a gap in the data; the second is a statement about
this geometry.*

## And it fails the order's way too, which is the point of running both

With $\mu$ free, $\partial(1/g^2)/\partial\mu = -N/(12\pi^2\mu) \neq 0$ everywhere, so a free $\mu$ is a
free coupling. **Both readings give no fixed coupling, by different mechanisms** — so the conclusion does
not depend on the one identification this row makes, which is mine and not the corpus's. *I registered
that doubt in advance as the place I was least sure, and running both branches is what answers it.*

3. **The same expression on the construction's own ratio returns negative** ($-0.0930$), so the two cases
   are told apart by the instrument rather than by my description of them.
4. **The species count computed**, rank from dimension, agreeing with `r7619`'s Clifford construction.
5. **The scales compared as like quantities**, both inverse lengths with $\alpha$ symbolic, so
