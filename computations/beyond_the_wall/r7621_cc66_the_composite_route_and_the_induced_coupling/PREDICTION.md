## ⛭ WHERE I EXPECT TO DIFFER FROM THE ORDER'S NAMED NEGATIVE, REGISTERED BEFORE I COMPUTE

The order names the reachable negative as: *"the induced coupling needs an infrared scale the
construction does not supply, so $1/g^2 = c\log(\Lambda/\mu)$ has a free $\mu$."*

⚠ **I expect that to be the wrong negative, and the true one to be stronger.** The face is **compact**.
A compact manifold has no infrared infinity: its Dirac spectrum is **gapped**, with least eigenvalue
$5/2\alpha$ — `P13`'s own figure, landed at `r7615`. ⇒ **The gap IS an infrared scale, and the
construction fixes it.** So the favourable reading is available: $\mu = 5/2\alpha$, $N$ from the face's
spinor bundle rank, $\Lambda = 1/\beta$. Every slot filled.

**And then it fails for a different reason, which is the one I am registering as my expected finding:**

$$\frac{\Lambda}{\mu} \;=\; \frac{1/(2\pi\alpha)}{5/(2\alpha)} \;=\; \frac{1}{5\pi} \;\approx\; 0.0637 \;<\; 1 .$$

⇒ **The construction's cutoff lies BELOW its own infrared scale**, by a factor of $5\pi \approx 15.7$.
So $\log(\Lambda/\mu) < 0$ and $1/g^2$ comes out **negative** — and more decisively, *there is nothing to
integrate out*: the count of face-Dirac modes with $|\lambda| \le \Lambda$ should be **zero**, since
every $|\lambda| = (5/2+k)/\alpha \ge 5/2\alpha > 1/(2\pi\alpha)$.

**That is a different negative from the order's, and a stronger one.** "A free $\mu$" leaves the
mechanism intact and one datum short. "Zero modes below the cutoff" says the mechanism **does not
engage at all** on this geometry: a loop of a field with no modes under the cutoff induces nothing.
⌗ *If the mode count comes back zero, the honest report is outcome **2** by a route the order did not
name, and I will say so in those words rather than letting it read as a confirmation.*
