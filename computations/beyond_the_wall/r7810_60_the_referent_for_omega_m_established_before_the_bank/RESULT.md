## The order's premise, corrected for the fourth revision running

`r7809` asks for the excursion judged "against the matter density the corpus **fixes** from the
acceleration epoch". **The acceleration epoch fixes the FORM and not the NUMBER.**

The identity is real: from `α = (c/H_0)√(1+2/x_0³)` and `α = c/(H_0√Ω_Λ)` it follows that
`Ω_Λ = x_0³/(x_0³+2)`, so `1 − Ω_Λ = 2/(x_0³+2)` exactly, and the expression's only free symbol is
`x_0`. **So "with no density in it" is true** — and it is a claim about the expression.

The number is something else, and `P15` says so itself: the background is "recovered from DESI DR2
baryon-acoustic data **alone**, whose own minimum is `(68.62, 0.2974)` at `0.958` per degree of
freedom", with the spectra "computed at its rounding `(68.60, 0.2973)`".

⇒ **There is no number at the acceleration epoch to judge an excursion against.** There is one in
the data the corpus fits to, on two rulers, and that is what the fit will be judged against.
