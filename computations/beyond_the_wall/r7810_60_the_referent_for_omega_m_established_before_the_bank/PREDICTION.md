## ⌗ WHAT I ESTABLISHED BEFORE REGISTERING

**① THE IDENTITY IS REAL AND IT CARRIES NO DENSITY — verified symbolically, not taken on the
sentence.** `P15` says the geometry fixes "the matter term, `Ω_m = 2/(x_0³+2)` read off the
acceleration epoch with no density in it". From the paper's own two expressions for the throat,

`1 − Ω_Λ = 2/(x_0³+2)` **exactly**, with no density symbol anywhere in the expression.

**② BUT THE ORDER'S PHRASE HAS A PREMISE THAT DOES NOT HOLD, AND IT IS THE FOURTH REFERENT
CORRECTION IN FOUR REVISIONS.** The order asks me to judge against "the matter density the corpus
**fixes** from the acceleration epoch". The identity fixes the **FORM** — that there is no free
density parameter — and it does **not** supply the number. `P15`'s own sentence says where the
number comes from: a background "recovered from DESI DR2 baryon-acoustic data **alone**, whose own
minimum is `(68.62, 0.2974)` at `0.958` per degree of freedom", with the spectra computed at its
rounding `(68.60, 0.2973)`.

⇒ **So the acceleration epoch supplies the relation and DESI DR2 supplies the value.** Judging the
excursion "against what the acceleration epoch fixes" has no number in it to judge against; judging
it against what the corpus actually carries does.

**③ AND THE CORPUS CARRIES A PRECISION, on two different rulers**, both from its own receipt
`P15_the_1p57_has_no_parameter_address…`:

| ruler | value | width |
|---|---|---|
| the arm's own, in `ω_m` | minimum at `(68.62, 0.2974)` | **`σ(ω_m) = 0.004543`** from DESI DR2 BAO |
| the control's own, in `Ω_m` | `0.2971` | **`± 0.0085`** from DESI DR2 BAO |

**④ AND ω_m IS THE FIRST CANDIDATE WHOSE VALUE IS ALREADY CONTESTED BETWEEN TWO OF THE CORPUS'S OWN
DATA SETS.** The same receipt records that the arm's **TT** minimum sits at `Ω_m = 0.2912` — which
is **`−0.673σ` from its own BAO minimum**, paying `Δχ²_BAO = +0.524`. For `ω_b`, `N_eff`, `Y_He` and

⇒ **That changes what "affordable" can mean for this one**, and it has to be said before the fit, not
