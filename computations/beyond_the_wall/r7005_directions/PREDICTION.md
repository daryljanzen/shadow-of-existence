# ⛭ `r7005` → `cc66.55` — PRE-REGISTRATION: THE FAILURE MODES FIRST

*The standing guard, now twice paid: **pre-register the ways the measurement can fail to decide, not only
its outcomes.***

## ⛔ WHAT WAS FIXED BEFORE THE MEASUREMENT, AND WHEN EACH PART OF THIS FILE WAS WRITTEN

**⌗ NOTHING IS RUN THIS REVISION.** The coupling comes from three control spectra already on disk at
`DPSRC` = 1, 0.8794 and 0.6.

| item | when it was fixed |
|---|---|
| the contrast statistic — standard deviation about a running **arithmetic-mean** envelope over one acoustic period in $q$ | `r6911+cc66.40` |
| **the criterion naming what the coupling multiplies** — *a fractional change in the Doppler's oscillation amplitude relative to the monopole's* — stated in `coupling.py`'s header before either candidate input is put through it | **stated before use**, per the standing guard, and it is what makes the two readings' disagreement the finding rather than a nuisance |
| the candidate's field-reading band values | `cc66.53`, measured and gated |
| the candidate's projected-reading band values | `cc66.50`'s quantity, recomputed here from the same banks |
| the outcome and failure-mode tables below | **written after the coupling was measured.** *They introduce no criterion.* |

**⚠ AND ONE GUARD THIS REVISION IS DELIBERATELY NOT USED TO SETTLE ANYTHING.** *The order says: **"do not
revisit the envelope question to settle it."*** ⌗ Both envelopes are carried through and the difference is
reported; **no envelope is chosen**, and no conclusion below rests on choosing one.

## ⚠ THE WAYS THIS CAN FAIL TO DECIDE — FIRST, AS THE GUARD REQUIRES

| failure mode | what it would mean |
|---|---|
| **The three `DPSRC` points do not lie on one slope** | The coupling is not locally linear and a finite difference over a $12$ per cent step cannot be applied to a $1$ per cent one. ⇒ *Report the non-linearity and quote no fraction.* |
| **The coupling's sign is not the same at every band** | Then it is not a property of the construction and the contribution's sign would depend on which band is read. *No fraction could be quoted without a band-by-band sign statement.* |
| **The coupling reverses between the two envelopes** | ⇒ *The coupling question inherits the envelope ambiguity, and the order requires that to be said **before** any number is quoted.* |
| **THE TWO READINGS OF THE INPUT DISAGREE IN SIGN** | ***The worst case and the one that cannot be fixed by measuring the coupling better.*** The coupling is a multiplier; if what it multiplies is not determined in sign, neither is the contribution. ⇒ *Both must be carried, neither chosen, and the disagreement named as the finding.* |
| **The arm's coupling cannot be assumed to be the control's** | All three `DPSRC` banks are the control. *If the coupling were sensitive to the Doppler share at the level the arms differ, nothing could be transferred and the answer would be "not measurable from these banks".* |

## ⛭ AND THE OUTCOMES

| outcome | what it means |
|---|---|
| **① A constant fraction, of the right sign, near one** | The candidate is the carrier and the row closes on a prediction with no free coefficient. |
| **② A constant fraction well below one** | ***A coupling deficit***: the candidate is a real contributor of measured size, and what remains is a second source, not a second shape. |
| **③ A fraction that varies across the range** | ***A second shape mismatch***, and the fourth this sector has found. *The candidate would then be excluded as the carrier on the coupling rather than on the filter.* |
| **④ A fraction of the wrong sign** | ***The candidate works against the excess.*** Its passing sign and growth on the filter would then be a property of its departure and not of its contribution — **and the filter would be shown to have been testing the wrong object all along.** |
| **⑤ THE NULL — the fraction is not determined because its input is not** | *A real result and the one the failure-mode table exists for.* The coupling is measurable and the candidate is not, and the row's question becomes a banking question rather than a physics one. |

**⛔ AND WHAT WOULD NOT BE CLAIMED UNDER ANY OF THEM.** That the excess has been explained or that the
candidate has been excluded. *A coupling multiplied by an undetermined input is an undetermined
contribution, and "undetermined" is not "small".*
