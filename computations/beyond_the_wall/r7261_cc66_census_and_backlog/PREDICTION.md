### ⛔ AND THE DISTINCTION THAT COULD COST ME THE NUMBER, DECLARED BEFORE I LOOK

**`r7257`'s witness test was DOMAIN-LEVEL and this one is QUANTITY-LEVEL. They are not the same test
and the first does not imply the second.** *A witness class was a regex over module content proving the
corpus computes that KIND of quantity — `GRP` resolved to `53` modules, `SPECTRUM` to `46`. All `85`
passed that, by construction, or they would have been `COMPUTABLE-NOWHERE`.* ⇒ **So a high answer here
must not be credited to the test that already passed.** The rule, bound now:

> A module is **NEAREST** to a row's quantity iff it already computes a quantity **of the same kind
> over the same objects**, so that obtaining the row's quantity is a change of inputs or a short
> extension of that module rather than a new derivation. ⇒ **I will name the module AND THE SPECIFIC
> FUNCTION IN IT, per row. A row for which I cannot name a function counts as having NO nearest
> module, however close its domain looks.**

⌗ *That is the teeth, and it is the same shape as last cycle's converse column: the audit is a
per-row named thing rather than a label. The `85`'s witness classes are `GRP 40`, `ZM 13`, `BEAD 9`,
`KILLING 7`, `GEOMALG 7`, `COSMO 5`, `SPECTRUM 4`; a class is a domain and `L129_the_invariants.py`'s
`inv_dim` is a function, and only the second kind of answer counts.*

**Why `82` and not higher.** *The `GRP 40` and `ZM 13` should name functions readily — this corpus
computes group orders, invariant dimensions and zero-mode counts by name. The risk sits in
`BEAD 9`/`KILLING 7`/`GEOMALG 7`: the geometry modules compute Ricci and Killing data for SPECIFIC
metrics, and a row asking for shear or a tortoise integral on a different object may have no function
within a short extension.* **Why `82` and not lower.** *Every one of the `85` was admitted only
because corpus code computes its class of quantity NOW, and that test was applied against a declared
floor rather than by eye.*
