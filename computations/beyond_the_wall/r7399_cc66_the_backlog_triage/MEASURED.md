## ⌗ THE RULE THE READ APPLIED, STATED SO IT CAN BE CHECKED

A site states an **open claim** iff its marker sentence, read in its paragraph, asserts that
something in **this corpus** is unfinished. A marker in an ordinary sense (`remains finite`), inside
a closure (`settled, not open`), describing **another** theory's conjecture, or in a rhetorical *"it
remains to say"* that the same paragraph then says — is `NOT-AN-OPEN-CLAIM`. The kinds are read from
the **shape of the claim in its paragraph**, which is what ⑩ asks for.

⌗ ⛔ **A definitive `①`-against-`②` ruling AGAINST THE REGISTER'S EXISTING ITEMS is the
register-holder's, not this seat's.** *What is measured is the shape of each paragraph's claim;
`classified.tsv` carries the reason for all `40` so every row can be overturned on its own evidence.*

⌗ **Two instrument notes, reported rather than smoothed:** the host-paragraph lookup failed on `2`
of the `40` (sites `10` and `36`) because the sentence's first `60` characters did not match after
whitespace normalisation; both were read from the sentence alone and are marked in `classified.tsv`.
