## What the measurement actually is

The trap I registered in advance is real and I measured it rather than talked past it: **`D` commutes
with the great-circle generator exactly**, $\lVert[D,H_\theta]\rVert = 0$. A reading that stops there
concludes $e^{-\beta H_\theta}$ is a function of `D` and the coupling is fixed.

**Commuting is not being a function of.** A function of `D` is constant on every `D`-eigenspace, and on
the face's lowest one — **four**-dimensional, which the multiplicity formula $2^{\lfloor 5/2\rfloor}\binom{4}{0}=4$
and the Spin(6) chirality split independently agree on — the great-circle generator takes **two** values,
$\pm\tfrac12$.

⇒ **And the sharpest form is in the construction's own number.** With $\beta/\alpha = 2\pi$ *exactly*,
the thermal weight $e^{-\beta H_\theta}$ on that single `D`-eigenspace takes the two values $e^{-\pi}$
and $e^{+\pi}$ — $0.0432$ and $23.1407$, a ratio of $e^{2\pi} = 535.49$. **The thermal weight varies by
a factor of five hundred across a space on which any $f(D/\Lambda)$ is constant by construction.** That
is the whole result, and the figure is the construction's, not mine.

⌗ *One of those weights exceeds $1$, so the generator is not a positive Hamiltonian either. Recorded as
a strain on the identification rather than a point in its favour — it would have been easy to leave out.*

## The controls, and what they were for

1. **A cutoff that does fix a coupling** — the order asked for it by name. Fourth moments **integrated**,
   not quoted: $6$ for $e^{-u}$, $1/2$ for $e^{-u^2}$, **ratio 12**. So the coefficient is a definite
   number given $f$, and it *moves* when $f$ moves. *Without this, "no $f$, no coupling" is the same
   rhetoric I charged a dimensional argument with at `r7615`.*
2. **A true function pair** — $B = A^2 - 2A$, constant on every `A`-eigenspace including the degenerate
   one. The instrument must be able to say **yes** or its **no** is worthless (`r7613`'s lesson).
3. **A commuting pair that is not a function pair** — $\mathrm{diag}(1,1,2)$ and $\mathrm{diag}(1,2,3)$:
   commutator exactly zero, spread $1.0$ on a 2-dimensional eigenspace. This is the discriminator for the
   hinge, and it is why the trap is *measured* rather than asserted.
4. **Non-vacuity, placed at the instrument rather than the call site** — `function_of` returns the
   largest eigenspace dimension with its verdict, because on a one-dimensional eigenspace every operator
   is scalar and the test would pass for free. *`r7615`'s worse fault was exactly a test that could not
   fail; this time the guard went in before the test did.*
5. **$\Lambda$'s dimension solved** rather than asserted, which is what turned the correction above from
   a hunch into a measurement.
