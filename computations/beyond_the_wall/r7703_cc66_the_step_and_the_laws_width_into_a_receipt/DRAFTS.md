| figure | licence |
|---|---|

| rises strictly, decelerates throughout | a property of the law's seven band values as computed (`+0.0167 +0.0122 +0.0087 +0.0061 +0.0042 +0.0027`). **Shape, not a fit** — no functional form is claimed. |

## ⛔⛔ WHAT IS ATTRIBUTION AND WHAT IS NOT — the whole of this, stated once

✔ **Measured:** the law carries the step's **size**; the law has **no step**, only a ramp; the
measurement's transition is **unresolved** where the law's is resolved; and the `q ≈ 1.9` residual

step's size does not make the acceptance the step's cause; it makes the acceptance a function that
happens to cross the same ground at the same height.*
