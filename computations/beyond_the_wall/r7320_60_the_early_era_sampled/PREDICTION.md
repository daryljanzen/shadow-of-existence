## ④ WHAT COUNTS AS A WITHDRAWAL — FIXED NOW, BECAUSE AFTERWARDS IT IS NOT A TEST

*Applying the redraft to a row gives exactly one of three outcomes, and all three are counted:*

| outcome | meaning |
|---|---|
| **WITHDRAWN** | the redraft decides `REPAIR-OWED` — at a test in step 3, or at step 4's third bullet |
| **HELD** | the redraft decides `DELIBERATE` — at step 2, or at step 4's second bullet |
| **MOVED** | the redraft decides something else: `MEANING-HELD`, `NOT-A-PIN`, or undecided on the spelling border |

⇒ ***The registered quantity is WITHDRAWN alone.*** *A `MOVED` row is a changed reading and not a
withdrawal to owed, and counting it as one would inflate the figure the order is about. **Both other
counts are reported beside it.***

⌗ *Every row records the step or test that decided it, which is the redraft's own closing instruction,
and `r7313`'s standing requirement on this seat's work.*
