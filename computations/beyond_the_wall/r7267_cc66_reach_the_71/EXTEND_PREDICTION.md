**THE THREE I NAME:** `B15` and `B8`'s `lie_g` rows (*the Lie algebra generated by the ratios and
the three-cycle*) and `C3`'s `killing_residual` row (*the number of independent Killing fields*,
whose claim `P12` states as `the symmetric-pair isotropy dimensions of so(5,1) are {6,7,10}`).

⌈ **AND THE REASON, WHICH IS WHAT MAKES THIS A PREDICTION RATHER THAN A GUESS.** *`EXTEND` means
`a short extension of that function`. A short extension changes what a function COMPUTES; it does
not change the KIND of question it answers.* ⌊ ⇒ *** So a row whose function is the wrong kind is
MORE likely to be filed `EXTEND` than `INPUTS`, because `a short extension` is exactly the phrase
a classifier reaches for when a change of inputs plainly will not do and the real answer —
*a different instrument* — was never on the form. ***

⌗ *`lie_g` is a metric Killing test and `the Lie algebra generated by two matrices` is a
commutator closure. `killing_residual` settles one field against one metric and
`the symmetric-pair isotropy dimensions` is an enumeration of involutions. **Neither gap is a
short extension of anything.***

## ⓶ WHAT WOULD REFUTE IT

- ** `2` or fewer wrong-kind rows ** — the share would then be at or below `INPUTS`'s, and the
  claim that `EXTEND` ATTRACTS kind errors is dead.
- ** Any of the three named rows turning out to be a genuine short extension ** — in particular if
  the corpus already carries a commutator-closure or involution-enumeration function I have not
  found, in which case the backlog named the wrong function but the right KIND exists and the row
  is an ordinary mis-naming like `B39`'s.
- ** A fourth or further wrong-kind row among the other nine ** — that would not refute the
  direction but would refute the NUMBER, and the number is what is pinned here.
