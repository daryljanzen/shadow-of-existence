## ⚠ DECLARED: what was read before this registration, and what was not

**Read, and it is a reading of the source and not a measurement:** the potential's evolution equation
in the hierarchy is

```
Ps  = Ph - 6 Hc^2 (Onv sig + Ogv sgg) / kk^2
Php = -Hc Ps - kk^2 Ph / (3 Hc) - (Hc Gf / 2) (Ogv dg + Onv dn + Ocv dc + Obv db)
```

which is the `G⁰₀` constraint differentiated, with the source written through the Friedmann relation.
**Its form admits a constant `Φ`:** sub-horizon in matter domination the second and fourth terms
cancel against each other at `k² Φ = −(3/2) H² Ω_m δ_m`, leaving `Φ` stationary, and the same
equation reproduces the instrument's own super-horizon readings at both ends (`δ_g = −2Φ` at the
onset, `Φ → −0.901020` against the analytic `−9/10` in the matter era, both already measured).

⇒ **So the cause is NOT the equation's form, and this revision does not register that it is.** The
cancellation above is between three quantities — the rate `Hc`, the density fractions `Ω_i`, and the
contrasts `δ_i` — and it holds only if the rate in the equation is the same rate the `Ω_i` are
normalised against. **Which of those breaks it is the open question, and no arm below assumes an
answer.**

**NOT read, and not measured:** nothing has been run. No probe of the rate, the fractions or the
contrasts has been taken, and the repair is not written.
