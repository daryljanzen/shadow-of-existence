## The rule being built

> ***An asserting clause may not compare a pinned operand against an instrument run's output in the same
> comparison.***

⛭ **Why this one can work where four could not.** *Every form that failed walked a transitive closure
over names, and each died differently: a path root counted as a read, because every receipt binds its
`ROOT` from `__file__`; a name-level map identifying every one-letter variable in a file; a closure
reaching a LOOP SIBLING rather than the data; and, before all three, a signature that the prescribed
repair carries by construction.* ⇒ **This rule's unit is ONE COMPARISON and its operands, resolved ONE
HOP to the statement that binds each name, inside the scope that binds it.** *No closure means no path
root, no sibling, and no cross-scope collision: there is nowhere for the walk to wander because there is
no walk.*

⌗ **And the unit is the comparison, not the gate's condition.** *`Ⓑ②`'s condition was `_pairs >= 10`,
whose operands carry no provenance at all; the offending comparison was inside the loop that built the
counter — `norm(_a) in norm(b)`, with one side from the pinned rows and the other from the live runs. A
rule that looked only at the asserting condition would miss the very clause it is named for, which is
`r7386`'s split one more time: the site asserted is not always the site measured.*
