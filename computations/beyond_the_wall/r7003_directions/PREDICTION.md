# ⛭ `r7003` → `cc66.54` — PRE-REGISTRATION, AND THIS TIME THE FAILURE MODES FIRST

*The order's new guard is this revision's own: **"pre-register the ways the measurement can fail to
decide, not only its outcomes."*** ⌗ *So the failure modes are tabled before the outcomes, and both are
dated.*

## ⛔ WHAT WAS FIXED BEFORE THE MEASUREMENT, AND WHAT WAS NOT — IN THAT ORDER

**⌗ NOTHING IS RUN THIS REVISION**, so there is no solve to pre-register. Both questions are asked of
banks already on disk, and what matters is which criterion was fixed before being applied.

| item | when it was fixed |
|---|---|
| the three filter conditions — sign, growth, curvature of the target's sign | `r6993` and `cc66.52`, before any of these candidates |

| the outcome and failure-mode tables below | **written after the two analyses were run.** *They introduce no criterion; the three items above decide.* |

## ⚠ THE WAYS ⓵ CAN FAIL TO DECIDE — TABLED FIRST, AS THE GUARD NOW REQUIRES

| failure mode | what it would mean |
|---|---|
| **The floor comes out below band 1's centre** | The band is reachable and the third condition returns. *The whole question closes and the filter is whole again.* |
| **The floor lands inside band 1** | Neither reachable nor closed: the band is *partly* measurable, and the honest report is a band mean formed over the reachable part only, with the fraction stated. ⌗ *This is a real outcome and the messiest one.* |
| **The floor is above band 1 but the instability is also present above it** | Then the floor is not the explanation and something else closes the band; the structural statement would not be earned and must not be made. |
| **The criterion turns out not to be testable** — the floor cannot be located because the turning points are not resolved in the banked sampling | ⇒ *Report that the question cannot be settled from these banks, rather than settling it from the estimator's behaviour alone.* |

## ⚠ AND THE WAYS ⓶ CAN FAIL TO DECIDE

| failure mode | what it would mean |
|---|---|
| **The admissible variants disagree in the curvature's sign** | *"The excess decelerates" would not be supportable at all and the paragraph would have to come out* — the strongest possible answer to the order's question, and against this seat's own prior result. |
| **The variants agree but the inadmissible ones disagree** | ⇒ The claim holds *for this statistic*, and the disagreement is between statistics rather than within one. **Each inadmissible variant must then be named with the reason it is inadmissible**, or the agreement is worthless. |
| **Band 1's own value is not stable** | The single-band dependence could not even be characterised, and the claim would rest on a number the statistic does not determine. |
| **Everything is stable and the drop-one reversal persists** | The claim is supported *and* single-band. ⌗ *Then what was landed too strongly is not the claim but its independence of any one band — a distinction the paper does not currently draw.* |

## ⛭ AND THE OUTCOMES FOR ⓷, WHICH IS A QUESTION ABOUT THE FILTER AND NOT A MEASUREMENT

| outcome | what it means |
|---|---|
| **The third condition is unavailable for every candidate** | The filter is a two-condition filter and the row should say so once and stop quoting curvature. |
| **It is unavailable only for some class of candidate** | ⇒ *The useful statement is which class, and whether that class is the one the physics says can carry the excess.* **If those coincide, the filter's full strength is available exactly where it is not needed, and that is a worse finding than losing a condition outright.** |

excluded. *It passes what can be applied; that is not a promotion, and the absence of a condition is not
an acquittal.*
