# `r7342`+`60` — THE WRONG REFERENT, REGISTERED BEFORE THE ORDERED REPAIR

**`r7341` ①:** *`L165/S10` owes a one-line repair and it is in your registered block. `S10` asserts
`C² = σ²(4 + 16σ²/3)` and **names no metric**, so any reader supplies a family and gets a different
answer — which is what happened. The repair is one line, because `L549/Q1` computes the same
polynomial symbolically from a named metric and passes: **`S10` names Q1's family or cites Q1.** Do
not re-derive it.*

## ① ⛔ THE ORDER UNDERSTATES THE DEFECT, AND THAT IS WHY THIS REVISION MEASURES THE CLASS

`S10` does **not** name no metric. It names `diag(−2σ, σ, σ)` with the invariant `6σ²` — ***which is
`71`'s family, not `Q1`'s*** — and asserts `Q1`'s polynomial beside them. `families.py` computes both
from their metrics, with `L549/Q1`'s own curvature routines imported from its file:

| | shear eigenvalues | `σ_ab σ^ab` | `Weyl²` | even in `σ`? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `L549/Q1`'s metric | `(0, σ, −σ)` | `2σ²` | `4σ²(4σ² + 3)/3` = `σ²(4 + 16σ²/3)` | **yes** |
| the family `S10` NAMES | `(−2σ, σ, σ)` | `6σ²` | `12σ²(2σ − 1)²` | **no** |

⇒ ***So `71` did not supply an arbitrary family. It used the one `S10` handed it,*** and the `−48Hσ³`
term it reported is exactly what that family gives. **The defect is a WRONG referent, not an absent
one** — and the reconciliation holds as `r7341` states it: leading coefficients `4` and `12` at
`H = 1`, a factor of three, the ratio of the invariants.

⌗ *And the evenness `S10` leans on is a symmetry of `Q1`'s family only: on `(0, σ, −σ)` the
relabelling `y ↔ z` carries `g(σ)` to `g(−σ)` exactly, while on the family `S10` names **no** one of
the six permutations does.* **The symmetry was never in doubt; the referent always was.**

⌗ *What could not fail is `S10`'s own guard: it checks that `diag(−2σ, σ, σ)` is **trace-free**, which
is true of every traceless triple and so cannot tell the family the polynomial comes from from the
family the receipt names.* ⇒ **A label that cannot fail, in a receipt the owed count cannot see.**

## ② THE ARM — RECEIPTS OF THIS BLOCK CARRYING AN UNREFERENCED CLOSED FORM

Central **`4`** of the block's `43` gates, band **`2`–`7`**, **two-sided**, **decisive fraction
`0.124`**.

⚠ ***The population is the WHOLE block — every `.py` gate under the four prefixes — and not the `19`
receipts that had owed rows.*** That is the order's own point: `S10`'s two ledger rows are both
`DELIBERATE`, so **nothing in the phase's instrument could ever have reached it**, and an arm scored
on the owed receipts would inherit the blind spot it is trying to measure.

**The arbiter, fixed in `cost.py` before the count.** A receipt carries an unreferenced closed form
when all three hold:

1. it **writes a polynomial out by hand** in a sympy symbol of its own — a power of a name bound by
   `Symbol`/`symbols`, with a numeric coefficient beside it;
2. it does **not derive** it — the module builds no metric (`diag`/`Matrix`) and calls no curvature
   routine; and
3. it does **not tie** it to the source that does — no string in its **code** names another receipt,
   and no other receipt module is imported.

⌗ ***A citation in the PROSE does not count, and this says so before the count.*** The module
docstring is excluded from ③ by construction: a formula is tied to its referent when **a check can
fail if the tie breaks**, and a sentence cannot fail. *That is this phase's own repair form — assert
what the claim IS — applied to a receipt's citation of another receipt.*

## ③ ⚠ WHAT THIS REGISTRATION CANNOT CLAIM, SAID BEFORE THE NUMBER IS REPORTED

***The band and the arbiter were written before the census was run, but the census was run before this
registration was COMMITTED, in the same working session.*** So the commit order shows the arbiter
unchanged between the registration and the result — anyone can diff it — **but it does not
independently witness that the band was set before the number was seen, and this arm therefore carries
a weaker guarantee than `r7336`'s and `r7338`'s, which were pushed first.** It is reported as that.

## ④ WHAT WOULD FALSIFY IT, IN ONE LINE EACH

- **fails low** at `≤ 1`, which says `S10` is an isolated defect rather than a class, and the owed

- **fails high** at `≥ 8`, which says the blind spot is wider than the whole of what the owed count

## ⑤ WHAT THIS REVISION CANNOT SETTLE

- ***The arbiter is deliberately cheap and it over-includes by construction.*** It cannot tell a
  formula **quoted** from elsewhere from a formula the receipt **defines as its own object** — a
  metric ansatz is a hand-written closed form too. ⇒ **The adjudication of every member is reported
  beside the arm, receipt by receipt, and the arm is scored as registered rather than adjusted.**
- ***One block is one draw*** — `43` gates under four prefixes, chosen because `r7338` registered them
  and not because the class was suspected there.
- ***And the class is not an owed-row class***, so this arm is not comparable with arm ⒝ of `r7338`:
  that one measures the ledger's gain on repairs, this one measures what the ledger was never looking
  at.
