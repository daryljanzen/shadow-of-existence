## The answer to the second question first, because it is the one with a prize attached

### ⛔ Nothing in the bead fixes `K`, and the reason is structural rather than numerical

Every condition `thm:bead` and the lift impose was enumerated and each asked whether the radiation
coefficient appears in it:

| the condition | what it fixes | fixes `K`? |
|---|---|---|
| the Nariai condition `ΛG²M²/c⁴ = 1/9` | `2M`, from `Λ` alone | **no** |
| the unit-speed loci `f = 0` (the two seams) | the seam radii, from `2M` and `α` | **no** |

**Every one of them is a condition on `f`, and `f` carries `2M` and `α` and no radiation term at
all.** `thm:bead` says so in its own statement: the bead is the slicing curve of
`f = 1 − 2M/r − r²/α²`, and its turning points and branch point are that function's. **So the bead
can no more fix the radiation density than it can fix the baryon-to-photon ratio** — both are content
the geometry receives, not content it determines.

**⇒ `K` is not a prediction of the construction.** *The registration named the opposite outcome as
reachable — one of the six constraining `K` would have made the radiation density geometric — and it
did not fire.*

## The handover, and it is a distinction rather than a yes or a no

**⒜ The vacuum bead reaches `r = 0` with a finite mass function** — `2M`, the offset the Nariai
condition fixes — so the branch point is a point of its own curve, which is what the theorem's
continuation needs.

**⛔ ⒝ The bent cut's mass function diverges there.** `μ = 2M + K/r → ∞` as `r → 0`, and the rate's
leading term goes as `K/r⁴` against the vacuum's `2M/r³`. **So there is no finite handover datum at
`r = 0` on the content side.** The bend has a value at every `r > 0` and none at the branch point.

**⇒ The handover is the vacuum segment's, and the bend begins just outside it** — and what fixes
"just outside" is not geometric: it is the epoch at which the deposited plasma exists as radiation,
which the chain above traces to a measurement rather than to a locus. *This is the outcome the
registration named as reachable for this arm.*

**The bead's structure sits where the bend is a part in ten thousand, and the one place it is not is

carries no `K` — so this is a structural statement with a numerical one beside it, not the other way
round.*

## What the initial data become, and the bend supplies **more** than the vacuum cut

| the datum a Friedmann run needs | on the cuts | supplied by |
|---|---|---|
| the expansion rate | bent: diverges as `K^{1/2}/r²`; vacuum: as `(2M)^{1/2}/r^{3/2}` | both, in different orders |
| the matter density | the constant offset `2M`, Nariai-fixed from `Λ` | **both, identically** |
| **the radiation density** | the `K/r` term, **absent** from the vacuum cut | **the bent cut only** |
| the spatial curvature | zero on the constant-`τ` slice for any mass function (`r7768`) | both, unconditionally |
| the composition `η` | the inherited datum, deposited with the plasma | **neither — boundary data** |

**The one datum a Friedmann run needs that the vacuum cut cannot give is the radiation density, and
that is exactly the term the bend adds.** So what the bent cut hands over is a hot,
radiation-dominated start — which is what a nucleosynthesis run requires.

**And the approach is radiation's, measured and not assumed:** integrating `τ(r)` by quadrature and
taking `d ln r / d ln τ` between `10⁻⁴` and `10⁻³` Mpc gives **`0.500022`** on the bent cut against
**`0.666667`** on the vacuum cut. **The bend does not perturb the approach to the branch point; it
changes its exponent, from dust's `τ^{2/3}` to radiation's `τ^{1/2}`.** Both reach the branch point
in finite proper time.

## ⛔ And one gap in the handover, which this read names rather than closes

`thm:bead`'s continuation carries the collapse leg onto the conjugate branch as
`r = −(2Mα²)^{1/3}cosh^{2/3}`, with `r < 0`. Applied to the bent mass function:

* **the dust offset `2M` is even in `r` and carries across unchanged;**
* **⛔ the radiation term `K/r` is odd, so on the conjugate branch it enters with the opposite sign.**

**A negative radiation term is not radiation.** So either the conjugate branch is vacuum in its
radiation sector, or the coefficient itself reverses with the branch — and **nothing in `thm:bead`
decides which, because its continuation is of `r(τ̃)` and not of the cut's own mass function.** The
corpus states no continuation law for `μ(r)`. *That is a gap in the handover, and it is the sharpest
thing this read found that the order did not ask for.*
