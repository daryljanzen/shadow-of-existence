# Five correct strikes and the gap between them

## What was asked

The coherence phase's item `1` is the acoustic rejection: `P18` calls it "the largest open
item in the paper," `P7` says "a mechanism for that deficit is what the cosmology names as
owed," and the register had struck every row that touched it. The phase's ruling template
asks one question of each strike — does its ground answer the item the papers state, or
something adjacent?

Five rows touched it: `PO-10`, `PO-47`, `PO-56`, `PO-70`, `PO-75`.

## What was found

Each strike was read on its own, against what its own row asked.

| row | what the row asked | the strike's ground | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| `PO-10` | which run the comparison rests on | the refit is run like-for-like on one instrument, and the contradiction is gone with the pair that caused it | **upheld** |
| `PO-47` | what displaces the fourth peak | a stopping rule: 0.9–1.3σ across the admissible window, the sky unable to discriminate, three candidates unrun | **upheld** |
| `PO-56` | the contrast accounting | its own terminal clause: the accounting closes exactly and still cannot attribute, the share being the cosine of a phase offset identically and blind to the channel's amplitude | **upheld** |
| `PO-70` | the projection's extra retention, and whether it is required | constitutive rather than evidential; required; the absence of a second support structural rather than a gap in the search; the successor question filed on `PO-75` | **upheld** |
| `PO-75` | the anisotropic source | both conditions on the agreement are shown undischargeable rather than merely unmeasured — a stated limit and not an order | **upheld** |

**Five correct strikes of five narrower objects.** Nothing was wrongly struck, and nothing
is unstruck. The item the papers carry — a *mechanism* for the shape deficit — is none of
the five. It is not which run, not the fourth peak's discriminability, not the accounting,
not the retention, not the anisotropic source. It fell between them.

## The consequence

That is standing order `r7013` ① — a different kind of object — so the consequence is a
row, not a reclassification. **`PO-87` is opened** on the item itself, in `P18`'s and
`P7`'s own words, and it opens two ways at once:

- **split by workability.** The one-multipole shape residual and the damping-*scale*
  Gaussian that no tilt removes are workable now. The fourth-peak displacement is
  **data-gated**, because `PO-47` *measured* that the sky cannot discriminate there — a
  blocker named rather than a step waiting.
- **narrow.** The row records what is already ruled out, so nobody re-runs it: the driving
  within four per cent by subtraction on both arms; the damping envelope at a seventh of a
  multipole against an offset of two; the two instrument faults; the third-peak band.

## The other half of the repair

`THE_OPEN_PROBLEMS_LEDGER`'s family view declares itself "a view of the register, and the
register is the single source of truth," and all three rows it named as live had since been
struck. It is brought current in the same revision: family `3` closed, family `8` closed,
family `5`'s remainder closed at `PO-56` and live at `PO-87`.

**Zero live families of the original eight, and one live row that no family numbered.** The
eight families were worked down; the one item that outlived them all was never a family at
all, but a question that fell between five rows.

Brought current rather than rewritten — the old lines stand as what the view said, the new
block as what it says now — because that table's own rule is that nothing is erased.

## What this says about the phase

Item `1` was the phase's hardest case by construction: eleven stated-open items with every
touching row struck, and this was the one with five of them. The ruling came out neither
way the template anticipated. Not "the rows were wrong, the papers were right," and not
"the papers are stale." **The rows were right about what they asked and the papers were
right about what they claim**, and the defect was that no row had ever been opened on the
thing the papers name.

So the phase's third outcome, found at `r7371`, now has a fourth beside it: a strike can be
upheld *and* the item be open, not because the strike was a stopping rule, but because the
strike's object was never the item at all. Five of them in a row, each narrower than the
claim, is how an item goes eleven revisions with nothing live to carry it.

The three live artefacts — `P7` §frontiers, the ledger's family view, and `THE_REGISTER` —
now agree on the acoustic family for the first time since `r7037`.
