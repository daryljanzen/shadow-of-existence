# r7230 (60) — PRE-REGISTRATION: the SOURCE half of the quote-pin backlog, read by the same reversal

**Written and pushed BEFORE any computation.** Nothing below is a result.

## ⌗ WHY THIS IS A DISCRIMINATOR AND NOT A SECOND HELPING

`r7228` measured reversal-blindness over the **`1,489` `PAPER`-targeted** quote-pin keys and found that only
`279` of the `1,140` it could read would go red if the clause they quote were turned round. ⛔ **It never
touched the other `1,154` keys, which target a SOURCE** — a receipt pinning a sentence in *another
receipt's source* rather than in a paper — and it said so as a stated scope, not as an oversight.

⇒ ***The two halves answer different questions and only the pair answers either.***

* If the source half comes out **at a similar rate**, `one in four would notice` is a fact about
  **PINNING**, and the paper layer was incidental to it.
* If it comes out **materially different**, the first result is a fact about **PAPERS**, and the paper
  layer's prose is what made those pins blind.

⌈ *And the source half may be the consequential one: a receipt's wording moves far more often than a
published paper's, so a pin into a source has more occasions to be wrong while staying green.*

## ⇒ WHAT CHANGES IN THE INSTRUMENT, AND WHAT MUST NOT

The transforms, the clause extractor, the `UNFLIPPABLE` exclusion and the three verdicts are **`r7228`'s,
unchanged** — otherwise the comparison is between two instruments rather than two populations. Two things
necessarily differ:

1. **The haystack is the receipt tree, not eighteen paper bodies.** A source key's literal is looked for
   across the registered receipts rather than across `corpus/*.tex`.
2. **A source carries CODE as well as prose, and code is not a clause.** A literal that lands inside a
   string literal, an identifier, a path or a gate label is not a sentence that can be turned round. ⇒
   **That population is counted in its own bucket and excluded from every verdict**, exactly as
   `UNFLIPPABLE`, `MARKUP`, `SATURATED` and `ABSENT` were.

⌗ *The same site cap applies: a literal with more than twenty sites is `SATURATED` and is not half-read.*

## ⇒ THE PREDICTION, STATED SO IT CAN FAIL

**I expect the source half to be MORE blind than the paper half** — a smaller discriminating fraction than
`279/1140 ≈ 24%` — for a stated reason: *a pin into a source is most often a pin on a DOCSTRING phrase or a
gate label, which is a noun phrase naming a thing rather than a sentence asserting something, and a noun
phrase carries no polarity to quote.*

**PASS CONDITION:** both halves measured with the same instrument and the same exclusions; every blind
verdict exhibiting its reversed clause as `r7228`'s did; the code-bearing population counted separately and
excluded; and the comparison reported as a rate against a stated denominator rather than as two raw counts.

**FAILURE OF THE PREDICTION, to be reported AS a failure:**

* the source half's discriminating fraction comes out **higher** than the paper half's, in which case source
  pins are the better-aimed ones and my reason above is wrong about what receipts quote; or
* the two fractions come out **indistinguishable**, in which case the finding is about pinning and `r7228`'s
  framing as a fact about the paper layer was the wrong frame — ⌗ *and that is a correction to my own last
  revision, to be said in those words;* or
* the readable population is **too small to carry a rate** after the exclusions, in which case there is no
  comparison and the honest output is the exclusion counts.

## ⌈ THE THIRD OUTCOME, WRITTEN DOWN IN ADVANCE

**The code-bearing bucket swallows the population.** If most source keys quote identifiers, paths or gate
labels rather than prose, then the source half is not a prose-pin class at all and the comparison is
ill-posed. ⇒ *In that case the finding is that the two halves are not the same kind of object, the rate is
NOT published, and what gets reported is the composition of the source half — which is itself an answer to
`what does a receipt pin when it pins another receipt?`*

## ⌗ THE SEEDING, BOTH WAYS, FIXED HERE

* a planted source clause whose literal carries the polarity ⇒ `DISCRIMINATING`;
* a planted source clause whose literal is a bare noun phrase ⇒ `REVERSAL`;
* a planted literal landing inside an identifier or a path ⇒ the **code** bucket, and ⛔ **not counted blind**;
* and the paper-half numbers re-derived unchanged on the merged tree, because a comparison against a number
  that moved is not a comparison.
