# Ninety-two read, four of them real

`cc66`'s intersection handed me 92 candidate sentences that fall in no census item,
as `(paper, section, sentence)`. I read them. **Four are genuine stated-opens with no
row. The other 88 are not open claims at all.**

So `69`'s census is incomplete **at the margin and not in substance**: the worklist's
denominator moves from 29 to 33, not from 29 to 121.

## What the 88 actually are

Largest class first, and the largest is the one nobody would have guessed.

**"open" as a geometric term.** The open / flat / closed trichotomy of spatial leaves.
`P8` §"The three constant-curvature slicings" alone carries most of them: *"the open
leaf"*, *"the open, negative-curvature leaf"*, *"the open $\Lambda$CDM cosmology"*,
*"the open Friedmann"*, *"the open and closed leaves"*. **A net built on the word
`open` cannot tell an open problem from an open universe.**

**"remains" in its ordinary sense.** *"a generator remains at $r_h$"*, *"the observer
remains at fixed areal radius"*, *"the curve $r(l)$ it labels remains real"*, *"the
realised spacetime remains globally connected"*, *"the breaking remains external to
it"*.

**The proof idiom "it remains to".** *"It remains to check that the angular component
also vanishes"*, *"It remains to say where the offset $r_0$ comes from"*, *"It remains
to weigh, against this result, the one objection…"* — the next step of an argument,
not a frontier.

**Closure statements.** Sentences whose entire job is to say the opposite: *"which
raises, but does not leave open"*, *"is therefore resolved, not open"*, *"How far the
perspectival diagnostic reaches beyond Schwarzschild is settled, not open"*, *"None of
these is an open case"*, *"One item is a corollary and should not be mistaken for an
open bracket"*.

**Section-level pointers.** *"§`sec:open` records"*, *"Section \ref{sec:ontology} sets
out what the construction establishes and what it leaves open"*.

⇒ **This confirms `cc66`'s refusal from a fourth direction.** 70 per cent of its
shaped net was one false class; 53 per cent of `69`'s net was `NOT-OPEN`; and now 96
per cent of the law's own six words' residue is not an open claim. **No word list
defines this population**, measured four ways.

## The four that are real

**`PO-90` — the sky's selective suppression of $\ell=2$.** `P15`, in print: *"The
structural expectation that a single floor starves $\ell=2$ and $\ell=3$ together is
therefore **not relieved by the exact treatment**, and the observed sky's selective
suppression of $\ell=2$ alone, by a factor of order three, **remains unaccounted
for**."* This is the sharpest kind of item there is — a measured discrepancy against
the sky — and it is a **shape** the construction states it does not produce, not a
size to be refined: one floor starves $\ell=2$ and $\ell=3$ together, and the sky
suppresses $\ell=2$ alone. The adiabatic escape is measured and refused in the same
passage: the residual ratio runs 0.926, 0.913, 0.901, 0.891, 0.889 — *decreasing* with
$\ell$, the opposite of an adiabatic error — and the exact filter's correction is 1.4
per cent on the $\ell=2$-to-3 ratio against a discrepancy of order three hundred.

**`PO-91` — whether the chart's vantage-dependence encodes something physical.** `P2`:
*"**We note, and do not resolve here** … Whether that work is purely an artefact of
the vantage, or whether the vantage-dependence itself encodes something physical about
how such geometries are observed, is a question this construction raises and leaves
open."* Not the perspectival-reading scope note `P7` and `P5` already carry — that is
a scope note on a claim the corpus makes; this is the other direction. It may well
terminate as a stated limit, and it opens so that the exit is reached by argument
rather than by nobody asking.

**Two fold onto `PO-87`**, both already quantified in print and neither on any row:

- **Which term of the projection makes the contrast excess.** `P18`: the same statistic
  reads **0.996 on the source at last scattering and 1.045 on the spectrum**, so the
  excess is made in the projection from wavenumber to multipole and **not in the
  acoustic dynamics** — *"which term of the projection makes it is the open
  computation."* That is sharper than anything `PO-87` carried: the excess is
  **located**, and only *which term* is open. It narrows `PO-56`'s struck
  "cannot attribute" without unstriking it — that row could not attribute from the
  accounting; this attributes from a statistic read at two places.
- **Whether the residual's modulation lies at $\ell_A$ at all.** `P15`: the fold is
  taken at $\ell_A$ by construction *"and nothing had asked whether that is where the
  modulation actually lies."* Fitted free, the unfitted arm prefers **312.0** on
  $[308.5, 315.2]$, **excluding $\ell_A = 298$ at $\Delta\chi^2 = 15.0$** — a +4.7 per
  cent offset. The control prefers a longer period too (347), which is the part the
  paper is careful about and the row now carries forward.

## What this says about the phase

The two rows the residue bought are **sharper than most of the 29**. `PO-90` is a
measured shape discrepancy against the sky; the two `PO-87` steps are a *located*
carrier and a *quantified* period offset. None of them was on the register, and none of
them was in the census — they fell between a word-list net and a paragraph-level read.

And the division of labour held exactly as ordered: `cc66` built the instrument and
reported the population; the sentences were read here. The reverse direction — 39
census-`OPEN` paragraphs its arm does not reach — went back to `cc66`, because that is
a fact about its net and not about the corpus.
