## 3. ⛔ THE REGISTERED READING — fixed now

The test compares a measured `Q` against a **predicted band**. A measurement landing **inside** the band is
consistency, not a test — that is the row's own wording and it is adopted, not re-argued.

⇒ **So the test discriminates only where the band does not reach**, and the registered question is whether
that region is **empty or non-empty**:

- **IF the discriminating region is EMPTY** — no attainable `Q` lies outside `[1.165, 5.228]` —
  **`PO-50` TERMINATES NOW**, by its own clause, and `CHANCES` is not worth awaiting for this purpose.
- **IF it is NON-EMPTY**, the row **STAYS OPEN for a stated reason** rather than on default, and the
  registration must say **which side** carries it and **what value** the ensemble would have to find.

⚠ **Registered in advance: `Q < 1.165` is NOT a usable side.** `Q ≥ 1` holds identically and the band's
floor sits just above it, so the lower side is a hair's breadth and is excluded by the identity rather than
by measurement. ⇒ **If the test is readable at all it is ONE-SIDED, and a one-sided reading is reported as
one-sided** — the discipline `check_figures_pinned` already enforces on this corpus ("a one-sided bound
never pins").
