  STEP 1 -- P16's INTERIOR, WRITTEN DOWN AND THEN CHECKED
==============================================================================
  a(eta) = A*(1 - cos(eta))/2 + sqrt(B)*sin(eta)
  a'' + a - A/2 = 0      solves the driven oscillator: True
  a(0) = 0   -- so eta = 0 IS the branch point

  even part of a : A*(1 - cos(eta))/2
  odd  part of a : sqrt(B)*sin(eta)
  ⇒ the even part carries A (dust) alone   : True
  ⇒ the odd  part carries B (radiation) alone: True
  ⌗ That is I14's split, reproduced here from the solution rather than cited.

==============================================================================
  STEP 2 -- ⒝ DOES THE CONFORMAL-TIME PARITY REACH THE CROSSING?
==============================================================================
  The crossing's structure is set by the ORDER OF THE ZERO of a at eta = 0,
  because that is what decides whether the curve crosses or touches.
  interior                               a(eta) near 0   order   parity of a
  --------------------------------------------------------------------------
  dust only        (B=0)                    A*eta**2/4       2   EVEN
  radiation only   (A=0)    sqrt(B)*eta*(6 - eta**2)/6       1   ODD
  P16's own: dust + radiationeta*(A*eta/4 - sqrt(B)*eta**2/6 + sqrt(B))       1   NEITHER

  ** ⒝ ANSWERED, AND MY PRE-REGISTERED ANSWER IS WRONG.  The two parities are
     NOT independent.  The order of the zero at the branch point depends on
     the SPECIES CONTENT: dust alone gives a DOUBLE zero (the curve touches
     a = 0 and returns, a even), while any radiation at all gives a SIMPLE
     zero (the curve crosses, a neither even nor odd).  Radiation's odd
     amplitude is exactly what makes the zero transversal. **

  ⛔ AND IT CORRECTS r7465+cc66.10's ROUTE, THOUGH NOT ITS RESULT.  That
     revision took the crossing parity from the VACUUM slicing curve,
     r = alpha sin(theta), which is ODD with a simple zero -- and then needed
     |r| to keep the dust density positive.  P16's dust interior has its own
     scale factor with a DOUBLE zero, so for dust alone a never goes negative
     and the density is even WITHOUT the absolute value.  The conclusion
     stands and is now reached twice by different routes; the absolute value
     was load-bearing only for the vacuum curve's parametrisation.

==============================================================================
  STEP 3 -- ⒜ THE STRESS-ENERGY, PER INTERIOR, BECAUSE IT DEPENDS ON THE INTERIOR
==============================================================================
  Perfect fluid, comoving: T^mu_nu = diag(-rho, p, p, p), so T^r_r and
  T^theta_theta are both the scalar p.  Dust scales as |a|^-3, radiation as
  |a|^-4, and radiation's p is rho/3.

  ⛔ A FIRST DRAFT OF THIS STEP ASSERTED, IN PROSE, THAT EVERY SCALAR IS
     EVEN 'whatever the species content', AND ITS OWN TABLE SAID OTHERWISE.
     The table was right.  |a| is even only when a is even OR odd; with both
     species a has neither parity (STEP 2), so |a| has none and no scalar
     built on it does.  The verdict is therefore per interior:

  interior                        rho_dust     rho_rad       p_rad    rate d|a|/deta
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  dust only      (B=0)                EVEN         n/a         n/a               ODD
  radiation only (A=0)                 n/a        EVEN        EVEN               ODD
  P16's own: dust+radiation        NEITHER     NEITHER     NEITHER           NEITHER

  ** ⒜ ANSWERED, AND MY PRE-REGISTERED ANSWER IS RIGHT ONLY FOR A SINGLE
     SPECIES.  For a one-species interior T^r_r and T^theta_theta are EVEN,
     as priced -- and the reason priced was right too: p is a SCALAR in the
     comoving frame, so it carries no radial index for the crossing's sign
     flip to act on.  For P16's OWN interior they are NEITHER, because the
     reflection does not map the member to itself at all (STEP 5). **

==============================================================================
  STEP 4 -- THE CONTROL: a component whose parity IS the other one
==============================================================================
  If EVEN were an artefact of taking absolute values, a component that
  genuinely flips would come out even too.  Read on the two interiors where
  the parity is defined at all:
    dust only      (B=0)     scalar: EVEN     rate: ODD      ✔ they differ
    radiation only (A=0)     scalar: EVEN     rate: ODD      ✔ they differ

  ** THE CONTROL BITES on both: the rate is ODD where the scalars are EVEN,
     so the even answer is not an artefact of the absolute value. **

==============================================================================
  STEP 5 -- ⒞ AND ⒟: THE INVOLUTION, AND WHAT DISCHARGES
==============================================================================
  For the DUST-ONLY interior the map is exactly r7465+cc66.10's:
      (rho(eta), p(eta), v(eta))  |->  (rho(-eta), p(-eta), -v(-eta))
  with rho and p even and v odd, so applying it twice is the identity: an
  involution, hence a bijection.  ** ⒞ holds for dust. **

  ⛔ FOR P16's OWN INTERIOR IT DOES NOT FOLLOW, AND THIS IS THE FINDING.
     With radiation present a has a SIMPLE zero and no definite parity
     (STEP 2), so eta -> -eta is not a symmetry of the solution: it maps
     P16's interior to a DIFFERENT member of the same family, the one with
     the radiation amplitude reversed in sign.
       a(-eta) = -A*cos(eta)/2 + A/2 - sqrt(B)*sin(eta)
       which is a(eta) with sqrt(B) -> -sqrt(B): True
     ⇒ the reflection is still a BIJECTION -- it is invertible, and the
       scalars are even -- but it is an involution on the FAMILY and not on
       the member: it carries (A, +sqrt B) to (A, -sqrt B).

  ** ⒟ ANSWERED, AND MORE NARROWLY THAN PRICED: the crossing carries P16's
     interior through, and nothing is lost -- but what it hands out the other
     side is the SIGN-REVERSED radiation amplitude, not the same member.
     So the discharge holds, and the crossing is NOT species-blind. **

  60's r7462: the papers name sectors the crossing leaves free, the

    - the COMPOSITION is fixed on the APPROACH, not at the crossing: P16

    - the RADIATION AMPLITUDE's SIGN is the one thing the crossing itself

      reversed.
  ⇒ ** ⒠ ANSWERED AS PRICED in its conclusion -- no sector is left FREE by

     thing the crossing changes is determined by it, not left open. **
