# r7228 (60) — PRE-REGISTRATION: the quote-pin that survives the REVERSAL of its own sentence

**Written and pushed BEFORE any computation.** Nothing below is a result.

## ⌗ WHY THIS, AND WHY IT IS NOT A RE-READING OF WHAT IS ALREADY READ

`PO-78`'s headline is that `2,287` quote-pins assert the presence of a sentence another seat owns and
**none has been read.** Two subclasses have been read since:

* `r7201+70.1` measured **`MULTI`** — `249` `PAPER` keys whose literal occurs twice or more in the
  file its trace names, so the pin *survives the REMOVAL of its sentence* while any copy stands.
* `r7190`'s `S3` read the **`OPEN`** subclass in full — `24` keys that go red on the success of the
  work they cite.

⇒ **Both are blindnesses of the pin's SITE.** This revision asks a different question, about the pin's
CONTENT: *not whether the sentence can be deleted without the pin noticing, but whether it can be
**reversed** without the pin noticing.* A pin whose literal is a noun phrase lifted out of the middle
of a claim is still present, word for word, after the claim has been negated, requantified, or turned
round — and the pin is still green.

⌗ *This is `r7207`'s lesson moved one layer down. A gate that enumerates what a page HAS can verify
every one of them and never see what is missing. A pin that asserts a string is PRESENT can verify its
presence and never see the sentence around it change sides.*

## ⇒ THE TEST, FIXED BEFORE IT IS RUN, AND IT ASSERTS NO JUDGEMENT ABOUT THE LITERAL

⛔ **No word-list is applied to the literal, and no intent is guessed.** The transforms act on the
HOST SENTENCE in the real paper; whether the literal survives is then a pure substring question.

1. For each baseline key whose target is `PAPER`, find every occurrence of the literal in the corpus
   `.tex` files and take the host SENTENCE around each.
2. Enumerate the states that sentence may produce under a **closed, declared set of
   meaning-reversing transforms**: insert or remove a negation at the first finite auxiliary or
   copula; swap a member of a fixed antonym table; swap a quantifier (`all`↔`some`, `every`↔`no`,
   `both`↔`neither`); change every numeral.
3. Apply each transform. **A transform that does not change the sentence is discarded, and a host that
   no transform changes is counted `UNFLIPPABLE` and NOT counted blind** — that is this instrument's
   recall limit, stated rather than estimated.
4. **`BLIND`** iff at least one transform changed the host and **every** changed host still contains
   the literal. **`DISCRIMINATING`** iff some changed host does not.

⌗ *`BLIND` is therefore established by EXHIBITING the reversed sentence that the pin still passes on,
per key, computed from the shipped paper text — not inferred from the literal's shape.*

## ⇒ THE PREDICTION, STATED SO IT CAN FAIL

**I expect `BLIND` to be the larger blindness and a NEW class:** more than `249` keys — more than
`MULTI` — and mostly disjoint from both `MULTI` and `OPEN`, because site-multiplicity and
openness-wording are properties of where a pin points and this is a property of what it quotes.

**PASS CONDITION:** every `BLIND` verdict carries the reversed host sentence it survives; the
`UNFLIPPABLE` bucket is reported as its own number and not folded into `BLIND`; the overlap with
`MULTI` and with `OPEN` is measured rather than assumed; and the seeding below discriminates.

**FAILURE OF THE PREDICTION, to be reported AS a failure:**

* `BLIND` comes in at or below `249`, in which case reversal-blindness is the *smaller* problem and
  `MULTI` remains the right place to spend the backlog's attention; or
* `BLIND` is almost entirely inside `MULTI` (overlap `>= 90%` of `BLIND`), in which case this is
  `70`'s class renamed and **I will say so and file nothing**; or
* `BLIND` is empty, in which case the corpus's pins carry their claims' polarity and the worry was
  mine and not the corpus's.

## ⌈ THE THIRD OUTCOME, WRITTEN DOWN IN ADVANCE

**`UNFLIPPABLE` dominates** — the transform set is too weak to move most host sentences at all. Then
the measurement is about the INSTRUMENT and not about the corpus, and the number that matters is the
fraction of hosts the set can move. ⇒ *In that case I report the coverage figure as the finding,
decline to publish a blind count that rests on an unmeasured majority, and the class stays open with
what it would take to close it named.*

## ⌗ THE SEEDING, BOTH WAYS, FIXED HERE

* a planted host whose literal carries the polarity (`... is NOT present on the leg`) ⇒ must come out
  `DISCRIMINATING`;
* a planted host whose literal is a bare noun phrase inside a claim ⇒ must come out `BLIND`;
* a planted host carrying no transformable token at all ⇒ must come out `UNFLIPPABLE`, and ⛔ **must
  not be reported blind** — the instrument that cannot move a sentence has not shown the pin survives
  anything.
