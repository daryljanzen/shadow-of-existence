## ⇒ THE RULE, FIXED BEFORE IT IS RUN

A tilt is a log-derivative of the output spectrum. **So the enumeration is not a list of physical
stories; it is the list of k-DEPENDENT FACTORS in the construction's own expression for its output.**
The rule:

1. Write the output spectrum as the construction writes it.
2. Take `d ln · / d ln k`. A product becomes a SUM, one term per factor.
3. **The channels are the terms of that sum.** A term that is constant in `k` is not a channel; a
   proposed mechanism that modifies one factor is not a term but a sub-candidate OF that term.

⌗ *This is derivable from structure because the construction fixes the expression. It cannot reproduce
the history of attempts, which is the discriminator `r7213` asked for.*
