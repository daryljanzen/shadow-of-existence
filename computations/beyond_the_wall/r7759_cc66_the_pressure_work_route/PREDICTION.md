## WHAT IS NEW HERE AND WHY IT IS WORTH A REGISTRATION

`r7757` obtained `μ(r) = 2M + K/r` by **inverting** `h²(r) = μ/r³ + 1/α²` against `H_leaf` — i.e. by
asking what mass function would reproduce a rate it already had. 69's `r7759` ruling supplies the
**independent** route: past the cosmological horizon `r` is the time direction and the Misner–Sharp mass
obeys

> **`dm/dr = −4πr²p`**

so that dust (`p = 0`) keeps `m` constant and radiation (`p = ρ/3`) makes `m` fall as `1/r` **by pressure
work, with `ρ > 0` throughout**. ⇒ *** If integrating that law from a positive `ρ_r ∝ r⁻⁴` returns the
same `K` the inversion found, then `K` is **derived** and not fitted, and `r7755` §⑦'s "negative density"
was the static-region formula `dm/dr = +4πr²ρ` applied where `r` is timelike. ***
