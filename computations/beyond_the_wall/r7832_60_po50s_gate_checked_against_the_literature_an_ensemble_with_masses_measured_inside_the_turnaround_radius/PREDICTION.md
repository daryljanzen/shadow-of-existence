# r7832 (60) — PO-50's gate checked against the literature, REGISTERED BEFORE ANYTHING IS SEARCHED

**Order `r7829` (69), items ① ② ③.** `PO-50` is one of the two open rows and its gate is data: *an
ensemble of systems with masses measured inside their turnaround radii.* This registration is
pushed before a single search is run, under the row-claim rule.

## ⛔ WHAT WOULD MAKE THIS PASS WORTHLESS, SAID FIRST

**The failure mode this row has already suffered once.** `r6838` excluded the baryon-only reading
because a measured radius exceeded the bound, and `r6841`'s ensemble pass then found that
exceeding that bound is *routine at the dynamical mass* — so the exclusion fell. The lesson is
that **a bound routinely overshot for a known reason rules nothing out**, and the known reason
here is named in the literature itself: a virial mass substituted for a turnaround mass.

⇒ **So this pass must not assemble a sample.** `r6841`'s own scope line forbids "a fit wearing a
literature pass's clothes": published samples are reported as published, no definition is
harmonised, and nothing from two catalogues is pooled. If what exists is too heterogeneous, the
answer is that the measurement is not available, **recorded as sharply as a number would be.**

## THE TWO REQUIREMENTS, AND THEY ARE NOT THE SAME AS `PO-36`'s

| | `PO-36`/`r6841` asked for | **`PO-50` asks for** |
|---|---|---|
| the radius | kinematic, independent of internal dynamics | the same |
| **the mass** | independent of the radius — satisfied by a **virial** mass from the members | **measured INSIDE the turnaround radius** |

**That difference is the whole row.** Lee, Kim & Rey (2017) satisfies `PO-36` — radius from the
flow of neighbour field galaxies, mass an NFW virial fit to the members — and **does not satisfy
`PO-50`**, because its mass is measured at `r_200`, not inside `r_ta`. An arm below asserts that
rather than assuming it.
