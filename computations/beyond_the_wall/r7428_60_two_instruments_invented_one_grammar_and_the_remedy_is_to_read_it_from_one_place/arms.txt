Q -- A MEASURED DEFECT IN THE INSTRUMENT THE ORDER TOLD ME TO ADOPT, ROUTED AND NOT PATCHED HERE
=====================================================================================================
decl_cells.check_pairs clears a pair when the qualified name is in the file's declaration set OR when
the name's LAST COMPONENT is.  Measured over the live inventory: 123 pairs clear on the fully
qualified name and 140 clear ONLY on the last component -- 53 per cent of the population, for which
THE NAMESPACE HALF OF THE CLAIM IS NOT WHAT WAS CHECKED.  `A.lean: Wrong.foo` clears whenever `foo` is
declared anywhere in A.lean.

THE CAUSE IS ONE LAYER DOWN AND IT IS NOT IN THE DATA.  decl_cells._END is `^\s*end\s+[A-Za-z_]` and
pops the namespace stack on ANY `end <ident>` -- so a named `section Model ... end Model` inside
`namespace ShadowProbe` closes the namespace.  ShadowEngine.lean has 48 named sections and its first
begins at line 193 of 1000-odd, so 19 of its 383 extracted names carry a dot at all.

AND THE DATA IS RIGHT ANYWAY: a control reader that pops only on a MATCHING `end` confirms 140 of 140
as fully qualified.  So the fallback is masking the reader's own `end` handling and not a fault in
71's column, and 66's "71's column is correct entire" holds on the strict reading too.

NOT PATCHED HERE.  scripts/decl_cells.py is 71's module and a private reader is what this revision
removed; applying my own fix to another seat's instrument would be the same mistake with the sign
flipped.  The gate MEASURES the strength of the verdict it reports -- printed on every run as Q --
because a gate that reads stronger than it is cannot tell its silence from its verdict either.
The patch is routed to 66 in FOR_66_FROM_60.md with this measurement.
