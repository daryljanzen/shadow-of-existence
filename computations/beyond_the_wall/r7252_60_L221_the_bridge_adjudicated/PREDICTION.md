## ⚠ THE BURDEN THE ORDER SET, AND HOW THIS REVISION WILL MEET IT

***`r7249`: "a verdict is a READING and not a classification. `60` receipts at `289` keys invites
pattern-matching a literal's shape to a verdict without opening the receipt, and the ratchet cannot
tell those apart because both make the number fall."***

⇒ **So this revision reports, as gated numbers and not as prose:**

1. ***how many of the `60` receipts were actually OPENED*** — counted, not claimed;
2. ***how many keys were verdicted by a BATCH reading*** — one reading covering several keys of one
   receipt — ***and how many batches***, so a reader can divide and see the real per-key attention;
3. *and the per-verdict census, so the fall in the ratchet is decomposable.*

⌗ ***A falling number nobody can audit is worth less than a smaller one they can.*** *That is the
order's sentence and it is the standard this revision is held to.*
