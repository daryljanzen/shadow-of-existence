THE GENERAL DEFECT, WHICH THE ORDER ASKED FOR AND WHICH THE MEASUREMENT CHANGES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

`r7453` ④ put it as: *a row carrying two counts over two populations will have one read
for the other, and naming which is which at every site is the only fix.*  The
measurement supports a different and larger statement.

 ① ** THE HAZARD THAT ACTUALLY FIRED IS ONE COUNT OVER MANY DATES, NOT TWO COUNTS OVER
   TWO POPULATIONS. **  The standing state is re-emitted in full each revision rather
   than amended, so the row holds `29` values of ONE field as live text with nothing
   marking which is current except position.  ⇒ `r7453`'s own `FIFTY-SEVEN` is the
   `r7257` copy of the RIGHT field: the right population, the wrong date.

 ② ** AND NAMING WHICH IS WHICH IS NECESSARY BUT NOT SUFFICIENT. **  A site must also
   say whether its number is a GLOBAL TOTAL or an ORDINAL WITHIN A PARENT, because the
   row's third series is the second kind and the word *face* is used for both it and
   (in the order) for the series that does have a total.

 ③ ** THE ONLY FIX THAT SCALES IS THE ONE THE ROW ALREADY APPLIES ELSEWHERE: one place
   for the live value. **  A field re-stated per revision is a copied list in time, and
   this row's whole subject is copied lists.
