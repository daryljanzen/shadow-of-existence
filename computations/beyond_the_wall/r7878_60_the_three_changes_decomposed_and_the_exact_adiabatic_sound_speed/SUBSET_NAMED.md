# ② — THE SUBSET, NAMED BEFORE ITS `A` IS QUOTED

`PREDICTION.md` registers ②'s second arm as `NUCA=1` with *"whichever subset ① shows is sound —
chosen by ①'s verdict and **not** by which subset gives the better `A`"*, and requires that *"the
subset is named in the result before its `A` is quoted, so the choice cannot be read off the
answer."*  **This file is that naming.**  It is committed before the subset's legs exist, so the
order of the record is itself the evidence: nothing of the subset's amplitude had been computed
when the choice was made, because nothing of the subset had been run.

## ①'s verdict, as measured

Two registered rows fire at once — `Ⓒ` (the weight change alone **crosses** one: TT `A` goes to
`0.99510` at `0.30` eV) and `Ⓓ` (the weight change alone takes EE **out of band**: `\lvert A-1
\rvert = 0.05961` at `0.60` eV, against `0.05`).  Both rows are about **one** member of the three,
and they are the only rows that fire.

*The continuity-only and Euler-only arms carry no registered expectation — `PREDICTION.md` says
they are "measured to attribute the overshoot, not to pass or fail".  What they are read for here
is the one thing ①'s verdict needs: whether either of them is the member ①'s rows condemn.  Neither
is.  Neither crosses one, and both leave EE inside the `0.05` band at both sums.*

## The naming

⇒ **The subset is `NUPARTS=CE`** — continuity and Euler, with the weight change dropped.

It is named by **exclusion**: ① condemns exactly one of the three members, and `CE` is the maximal
subset that does not contain it.  That rule is fixed by ①'s verdict alone and could have been
written down before ① was run; what ① supplied was only *which* member to exclude.  ⛔ **No
amplitude of any `CE` leg has been computed, and `gen_parts.sh` does not yet carry its specs.**

⌗ **AND THE NAMING IS AGAINST THIS SEAT'S OWN EXPECTATION, WHICH IS WHY IT IS WORTH WRITING DOWN.**
*The weight change is the one the closure deficit of `r7876` asked for: it is the term that puts
back the mass the massless source weight omits, and `r7876` measured the omission as `0.81` and
`0.67` of the excess.  So the subset ① shows is sound is the subset that **drops the only change
with a closure argument behind it**, and keeps the two that have none.  That is not a repair of the
physics and it is not offered as one — it is what the registered rule names, and the rule is
followed rather than argued with.*  Whatever `CE`'s `A` turns out to be, it is read under `Ⓔ`–`Ⓗ`
as registered, and ⛔ **no limit is read through it unless `Ⓔ`.**
