# The item that left by reclassification

Coherence item 8 — the detailed worldline and field dynamics of matter crossing the branch
point — took a whole turn on `P7`, and the ruling turned twice before it landed. The second
turn is the finding.

## First reading: a stated limit

`P15` §scope says in print: "What remains open is the *detailed* worldline and field dynamics
of the crossing for a concrete matter model — a depth left for a matter model to supply, and
resting on a crossing shown well posed rather than merely postponed." `P5` asks the narrower
question, whether a concrete model's history reaches the branch point. `P1` points at both.

Against that, `P7`'s body said the crossing is "settled on both halves," in six places.

The first reading made it a **stated limit** under `r7013` ②: the two results `P7` gives are
structural and hold for any matter model (the crossing acts on oscillatory content and none
arrives oscillating; a comoving worldline terminates and the analytic continuation carries
on), `P16` computes a concrete model's *approach* with a nuclear network run on it, and
nothing in the corpus rests on a through-crossing evolution. An edit saying so was written.

## Then the mandatory read found the paper's own header

`P7`'s canon note, line 119, Daryl-directed:

> **OPEN = the crossing's matter DYNAMICS (the "how"), NOT its unitarity (the "whether",
> settled).**

`P7`'s body contradicted `P7`'s header, and the header is the authority. **The limit reading
was withdrawn before it was committed.** That is the second revision in two in which the
word-for-word final read caught this seat's own edit rather than someone else's prose.

## Then the ledger showed why the item had no home

`THE_OPEN_PROBLEMS_LEDGER`'s **family 1** is titled with the item verbatim: *Matter
branch-point crossing worldline-and-field dynamics.* Inside that one entry:

| | |
|---|---|
| `c54.113` | "**THE WORLDLINE HALF, AND THE FAMILY CLOSES.** There is no worldline crossing to compute." |
| `c54.115` | retires the item from `P7` §frontiers, on that closure |
| the entry's own `OPEN:` line, unchanged | "the *detailed* worldline-and-field dynamics for a concrete model" |

**A closure taken on the worldline half does not reach an open line naming a concrete
model's dynamics.** The `ESTABLISHED:` line is right, the `OPEN:` line is right, and the
closure between them was taken on a narrower ground than the item it closed. That is
`PO-86`'s third outcome for the second time.

Three artefacts carried the open — `P15`, `P5`, and `P7`'s canon note — and two carried the
closure: this family entry, and `P7`'s body.

## What landed

- **`PO-88` is opened** on the item. `r7013` ① and not a reopen: `PO-30`, which the census's
  register column names, was struck at `r6758` on "the geometry supplies no choices" — the
  matter content's generative law, a different object. **No row has ever carried this one.**
  The row records what is already settled so none of it is re-run, states its discharge, and
  states a terminal exit that is a *result* — that the two structural results leave the
  through-crossing evolution no freedom at all, which is the exit `c54.113` reached for and
  demonstrated for the worldline only.
- **`P7` is rewritten to one state.** Six over-claiming sites corrected; the trailing
  paragraph that declared the crossing settled folded into a **new enumerated item 4**
  (`frontier:crossing`), classed **work** by that section's own distinction; the section head
  now reads "of the four below, two are work and two are boundaries"; the Daryl-directed
  comment's count carried forward with its reason. Recompiled clean, 87 pages.
- **The ledger's family 1 is brought current** and live again at `PO-88`, kept rather than
  rewritten.

**`P7` §frontiers has gone from three enumerated items to four** — the first time this
programme's canonical open-problems section has grown.

## Why this is the phase's largest finding so far

An item left the corpus's **one** open-problems section **by reclassification** — a family
"closing" on a narrower ground than its own open line — and that is the single move this
programme's rules say a list may never shrink by.

The phase has now found:

| | |
|---|---|
| the register struck a live item (`r7381`, `PO-24`) | wrong in one direction |
| the register carried no row for a live item through eleven revisions (`r7379`, `PO-87`) | wrong in the other |
| `P7`'s canonical list retired a live item on a narrower closure (`r7387`, `PO-88`) | wrong in the only direction that matters for a list |

The first two were the register's. This one is the list's, and the list is the artefact a
reader uses to choose what to work.
