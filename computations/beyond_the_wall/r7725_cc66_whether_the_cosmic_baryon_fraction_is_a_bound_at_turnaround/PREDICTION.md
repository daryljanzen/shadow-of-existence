## 2. ⛔ THE ONE UNWORKED STEP — and it is NOT the comparison 60 already made

Node 60's result ends by setting the required `33`–`56` per cent against a cosmic baryon fraction, i.e.
**against a MEAN.** ⇒ A mean is something a single cluster can in principle exceed, so that comparison makes
the escape *implausible* and does not close it. ⌗ *I began by intending to propose exactly that comparison as
new work. It is already in `r7484`. Checking first is the only reason this registration is about something
else.*

**The unworked step is whether, AT THE TURNAROUND RADIUS SPECIFICALLY, the cosmic fraction is a BOUND.**
The argument to be tested:

> `r_ta` is by construction the boundary of a **closed accretion region** — inside it material is still
> infalling, outside it joins the Hubble flow, so **nothing has left**. Redistributing baryons *within* a
> closed region cannot change the enclosed fraction *at its boundary*. ⇒ `f_b(< r_ta)` is the composition
> of the material that collapsed, lowered by any feedback that expelled baryons and raised only by a
> primordial enrichment of that patch. **So at `r_ta`, and at no smaller radius, the background fraction is
> an upper bound rather than an average.**

⇒ **This is a statement about which radius was chosen**, which is why it is available at `r_ta` and not at
`r_500`, where baryons genuinely can be and are depleted or concentrated across the boundary.
