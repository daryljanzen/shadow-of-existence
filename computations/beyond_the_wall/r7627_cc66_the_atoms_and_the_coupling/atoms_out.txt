----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  (6) WHAT ANY MECHANISM NEEDS, AND WHETHER THE CONSTRUCTION SUPPLIES IT
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    a. A REFERENCE SCALE at which the coupling is quoted.
       The construction fixes NO absolute scale: alpha is carried symbolically end to
       end (r7615, r7619, r7621 all declare it so), and every datum above is either
       dimensionless or a ratio of the two lengths.  A coupling is quoted AT AN ENERGY;
       a set of pure ratios names no energy.  ⇒ NOT SUPPLIED.
    b. And even taking the construction's OWN two scales rather than an absolute one,
       r7621 measured Lambda = 1/(2 pi alpha) BELOW the gap 5/(2 alpha), with ZERO modes
       between: Lambda/mu = 1/(5 pi) = 0.063662 < 1.  ⇒ no interval to run over.
    c. A MATCHING CONDITION -- a statement that some particular number IS the coupling
       at that scale.  Nothing in the data picks WHICH of the 11049 numbers, or where.
       ⇒ NOT SUPPLIED.

    ⛭ AND THE REASON UNDERNEATH ALL THREE, which is dimensional and not numerical:
      a gauge coupling IS an action normalisation.  The atoms are lengths, integers and
      spectral ratios -- geometry.  The toy fixes its coupling because it is GIVEN an
      independent dimensionful coupling; this construction is given no such datum for a
      gauge field.  ⇒ No monomial in these atoms is a coupling REGARDLESS of its value,
      which is why the near-misses above are numerology and not evidence.
