⇒ **So the clause does not fail on its grounds.**  It fails on *every admissible stress-energy*.

## ⌈ ② THE DECOMPOSITION, AND THE THREE SECTORS WHERE THE FREEDOM LIVES

A concrete matter model's stress-energy is not one object.  Relative to the collapse worldline
congruence it is `ρ`, `p`, `q_a`, `π_ab`, a composition vector, and perturbation content in three
irreducible types, with the time-stacking, the filter's action and the crossing's duration beside
them: **eleven sectors, and the clause is one sentence over all of them.**

Of the eleven the corpus speaks to **9** at this locus (`7` on the sentence column; the registered
rule's paragraph fallback carries the other two).  `q_a` is **ABSENT** from the eighteen papers
entirely — the one arm that hit as predicted — and the vector sector is **STATED-AWAY**.

**THE FIVE REFUTERS, as the locator reports them:**

**Ⓡ① THE COMPOSITION IS NOT CONSTRAINED BY THE CROSSING AT ALL, IN PRINT.**
`CR_framework.tex:801`: *"So the frontier's two halves are constrained differently — the
spectrum's by the crossing's selection rule, **the composition's not at all by it** — and a
derivation of the composition must be sought on other grounds."*
⇒ A sector the crossing does not constrain cannot be a sector in which every admissible input
crosses identically.  **The paper names the sector and sends its derivation elsewhere.**

**Ⓡ② AND THE SAME PAPER AT `787`** carries the composition's one crossing result and its limit
together: *"baryon number crosses conserved and the branch point relabels its sign, so the
construction has no baryogenesis and needs none."*  **Conserved-and-relabelled is a map, and it is
injective** — which is the opposite of a degenerate one.

**Ⓡ③ FREE-STREAMING NEUTRINOS CARRY ANISOTROPIC STRESS AT THE BRANCH POINT, IN PRINT.**
`CR_cosmology.tex:563`: *"the free-streaming neutrinos the cooling-leg nucleosynthesis requires

decoupling**."*  And the isotropization is not the crossing's doing: `CR_cosmology.tex:2849`
states *"the isotropy is **earned on the approach** and **preserved by** the crossing, and the two
halves are of different kinds: the first is a **damping calculation with a rate, a timescale and a
residual**, the second follows from the zero-duration…"*
⇒ **A residual, plus a required species that carries anisotropic stress at the locus, is
admissible stress-energy that the crossing preserves rather than erases.**  `Ⓕ` predicted this
sector STATED-AWAY; it is COVERED, and its site is a counterexample to the clause rather than
support for it.

**Ⓡ④ AND THE DECISIVE ONE: THE SECOND GROUND IS BOUNDED IN WAVENUMBER BY THE PAPERS THEMSELVES.**
`CR_synthesis.tex:1793`: *"**The premise that every mode is frozen at the handover carries a
qualification**, and it is worth having because it names where the argument would fail: the
imaginary-time segment before the branch point removes a mode's oscillatory content **strongly

acoustic band sits on the safe side by construction, and the low-wavenumber end is where it would
bite."*
⇒ *Zero oscillatory content* is true **above a stated scale** and the corpus says so.  Below it,
content arrives excited.  **The clause's ground is quantified where the papers quantify it, and
the clause is not.**

**Ⓡ⑤ AND THE ROW'S OWN NAMED STEP IS A STATEMENT THAT THE THROUGH-CROSSING EVOLUTION IS OPEN.**
`cosmogenesis_paper.tex:549`: *"The exact regulated peak — **how deep the compression runs on the
