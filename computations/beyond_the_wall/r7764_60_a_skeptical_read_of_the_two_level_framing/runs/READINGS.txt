ARM 1 -- what the trampoline's licence was ARGUED for, in the source's own words
  [PASS]
  r7760 found the licence SENTENCE general in its own words.  This arm asks the other
  question: what the argument around it rests on.  Three things in the source decide it.

  ⛔⛔ ⒜ THE SOURCE'S OWN TARGET IS THE AVERAGE DENSITIES OF MATTER **AND RADIATION**:
      "This second insight raises a critical question: must the dynamical evolution of the universe necessarily be determined by the average densities of matter and radiation filling space? If general relativity is fundamental, cosmological space-time must satisfy Einstein's equation."
      "However, if existence is a fundamental property of the noumenological three-dimensional universe, with cosmological time universally well-defined across gravitational fields, voids, solar systems, and black holes, it seems implausible that this evolution depends solely on the average densities of matter and radiation."
      "Beyond challenging the synchronicity assumption, this framework questions the core premise of FLRW cosmology: that the universe's expansion rate is determined primarily by fields like the average densities of matter and radiation."
  ⇒ THIS IS THE STRONGEST THING AGAINST THE FRAMING AND IT IS THE PROGRAMME'S OWN
  FOUNDING QUESTION.  The leaf rate IS the average densities of matter and radiation,
  term for term.  The framing says every measured quantity takes it -- so everything
  observable is governed by exactly what the source set out to deny governs the
  expansion, and the denial is kept only where nothing is measured.

  ⛭ ⒝ AND THE SOURCE'S OWN THRESHOLD CLAUSE CUTS THE OTHER WAY, which a one-sided read
  would leave out:
      "Much like cosmic time appears to be an absolute property, according to which our three-dimensional universe is well-defined within a four-dimensional foliated space-time geometry, it may be that the universe also has an absolute expansion rate that is unaffected by local gravitational interactions e"
  ⇒ `except where densities exceed a threshold' is the source ALLOWING the rate to be
  affected where density is high, which is exactly the early universe.  So the source
  permits the two levels to part at high density -- on its own terms, not only on the
  framing's.

  ⌗ ⒞ and what the local-warping language is about, for the record:
      "A clearer example is gravitational lensing."
      "Locally, gravitationally bound structures---from molecules and stars to galaxies and clusters---do not participate in the Hubble flow."
  ⇒ lensing, ripples, bound structures: the averaging premise is there, and a uniform
  bath is not in it.  ARM 2 measures what that costs.
