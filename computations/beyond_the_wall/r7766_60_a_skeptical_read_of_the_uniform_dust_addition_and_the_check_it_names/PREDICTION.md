## ① The addition's own premise: is the Misner–Sharp mass of pressureless matter constant?

The addition rests on it. `r7762` established the law that holds where the radial coordinate labels
the epoch: the **pressure-work** relation, `dm/dr = −4πr²p` in the units that revision used, and NOT
the mass-inside-radius relation. Setting `p = 0` in it is one line.

**Measured:** the derivative of the mass function demanded by that law at `p = 0`, taken symbolically,
and the mass function it integrates to.

**Criterion met if** the law at `p = 0` gives `dm/dr = 0` identically and so `m = const`, with the
constant unconstrained by the law.

**Reachable negative:** the law could carry a term that survives `p = 0` — a `ρ`-dependent piece, or
an `r`-dependent factor — in which case dust's mass would run with the epoch and the addition's
premise would be false. The arm reports which.

## ② Is the cut's offset dust's mass — does the geometric rate's matter term *contain* matter?

The addition says the constant mass **is** the cut's offset `2M`, and that this *may be why the
geometric rate has matter's form*. That is an identification of two things the corpus names
separately, so it is checked rather than assumed.

**Measured:** (a) the radial equation `h² = μ/r³ + 1/α²` (P15 `sec:properframe` step iii) with
`μ = 2M` constant, reduced symbolically to a rate in `(1+z)`; (b) the exponent that term carries;
(c) the density read off that term through the same constitutive law as ①; (d) the paper's own
translation `Ω_m = 2/(x₀³+2)`, `Ω_Λ = x₀³/(x₀³+2)`, and whether (c) reproduces it; (e) the Nariai
condition's own fixing of the offset, which P15 states in terms (*"the Nariai condition fixes
[the offset] at `α/√3`"*), carried through to `Ω_m/Ω_Λ`.

**Criterion met if** the constant-`μ` term is exactly a `(1+z)³` term, the density read off it is the
dust density, and the Nariai-fixed offset reproduces the paper's own `Ω_m/Ω_Λ` to better than one part
in `10¹⁰`.

**Reachable negative:** the exponent could come out other than `3` (it is `μ/r³` against a rate, not
a density by inspection), or the Nariai-fixed offset could give an `Ω_m/Ω_Λ` that misses the paper's
translation — either of which breaks the identification and so the addition's explanation.

## ③ "The content level and the geometric level should coincide exactly" — and can that measurement fail?

The two rates stand in the relation `H²_leaf = H²_stack + H₀²Ω_r(1+z)⁴` (`r7760`, `r7764`). Putting
`Ω_r = 0` in that makes them identical **by inspection of the formula**, so the obvious measurement
cannot fail and is not offered as one. `r7762` was caught by exactly this shape of non-test and said
so; this arm says it in advance.

**Measured:** the non-trivial version — whether the **leaf** rate's matter term and the **stacking**
rate's offset are ONE dust or two. Concretely: the leaf rate as the corpus writes it, the stacking
rate as ① and ② give it, and whether the matter content appears once or twice when the two are
compared term by term at `Ω_r = 0` and at the fitted `Ω_r`.

**Criterion met if** the matter term appears exactly once — the leaf rate adds radiation to the
stacking rate and no second matter term — and the arm states plainly that the `Ω_r = 0` coincidence
is algebraic and not a measurement.

**Reachable negative:** the leaf rate could carry its own matter term alongside the stacking rate's
offset, in which case the dust is double-counted and the two levels do not coincide for dust at all.

## ④ ⛔ The check the addition names: a comparison, or an identity?

*"Ωm from the acceleration epoch against the refit's Ωm is then the check that matter's actual density
is the geometric offset."* Before that is treated as a test, this arm establishes what it ranges over
— the standing guard, and `r7762`'s finding that a measurement offered for a true claim may be one
that cannot fail.

**Measured:** (a) the acceleration epoch solved symbolically from the rate of ②, i.e. where the
second derivative of the areal radius changes sign, in terms of `x₀`; (b) whether `1+z_acc = x₀` is
an identity of that rate or a numerical coincidence; (c) `Ω_m` obtained from (a) through the paper's
translation, against `Ω_m` as the refit reports it, both as numbers; (d) **where the corpus's own
`z_acc` comes from** — the same baryon-acoustic fit that returns `Ω_m`, or a dataset independent of
it — read from P15's text.

**Criterion met if** the arm returns a definite verdict on whether the named check has content, with
the symbolic identity either established or refuted, and (d) answered from the paper rather than
assumed.

**Reachable negative — and this is the arm's point:** if `1+z_acc = x₀` is an identity and `Ω_m` is
the same translation of `x₀`, then the two sides of the named check are the same number by algebra,
the check cannot fail, and it tests nothing about whether matter's density is the geometric offset.
The opposite is equally reachable: if the corpus fixes `z_acc` from data that do not enter the `Ω_m`
fit, the comparison is a real one and this arm says so.

## ⑤ What *would* test "matter's actual density is the geometric offset"?

If ④ comes back an identity, the addition's question is still a good one and deserves an answer
rather than a dismissal.

**Measured:** an enumeration of candidate determinations of the matter density that do **not** pass
through the epoch `x₀` — at least: the baryon density `ω_b` with a measured baryon fraction,
matter–radiation equality `1+z_eq = ω_m/ω_r`, the growth of structure, the acoustic driving, and the
Hubble–Eddington reading — each marked *independent of the rate's own fit* or *not*, with the reason,
and each read from P15's own text rather than supplied.

**Criterion met if** every candidate is marked with a reason, and the arm states which of them (if
any) could return a number that disagrees with the geometric offset.

**Reachable negative:** every candidate may turn out to be a reading of the same epoch, in which case
the honest answer is that the construction **cannot** be caught here — and that is a fact about the
construction worth printing, not a failure of the arm.

## ⑥ Does the addition defeat `r7764`'s part ⒞, narrow it, or leave it?

`r7764` ⒞ held that the trampoline's licence is that the cosmological description is good *while the
warping averages out*, and that **a uniform bath has nothing to average**. The addition answers that
uniform content still gravitates and still changes the expansion.

**Measured:** the two epochs separately. For **dust**: whether, on the addition's own reading, there
is any gap between the levels to average away. For **radiation**: the gap recomputed from this line's
own instrument at the redshifts `r7764` used, and whether the addition supplies anything that averages
it.

**Criterion met if** the arm states, for each of the two components, whether ⒞ survives, and reports
`r7764`'s figures as recomputed rather than quoted.

**Reachable negative:** the addition may dissolve ⒞ entirely — if the uniform-dust reading shows the
levels never differ for *any* component in a way that needs averaging, then ⒞ was aimed at a gap that
does not exist, and this arm withdraws it. `r7762` withdrew a published conclusion of this line's; the
same is available here.

## ⑦ "No content sets it": does the uniform-dust reading contradict the framing's own sentence?

The framing says of the geometric level: *"it is the lapse, and no content sets it."* The addition says
the cut's offset **is** pressureless matter's Misner–Sharp mass. If both hold, content sets the
offset.

**Measured:** the framing's sentence and P15's own statements about what fixes the rate (the abstract's
*"its rate fixed by the cosmological constant alone, with radiation and matter inherited content ...
rather than terms that source the expansion"*, and the Nariai condition's fixing of the offset), each
located in the printed text; then whether the Nariai condition's tying of the offset to `Λ` resolves
the tension, and at what cost.

**Criterion met if** the arm either names a genuine contradiction between sentences both in print, or
shows the resolution and names its cost in terms.

**Reachable negative:** there may be no tension at all — if the offset is fixed by `Λ` through the
Nariai condition, then no content sets it even though a content quantity equals it, and the arm says
so.

## ⑧ "The field equations fixing the rate from its density" against "readings of one cosmic clock"

The addition has the density fix the rate. P15 has the matter density as *"readings of one cosmic
clock, not drivers of the expansion"*. Those are opposite directions of one relation.

**Measured:** whether the difference survives a change of clock — the newest standing guard. The
relation of ② is written in both directions and asked whether either direction carries content the
other does not: a parameter the one fixes and the other leaves free, or a reachable state the one
permits and the other forbids.

**Criterion met if** the arm returns one of: the difference is content (name the parameter or the
state), or it was never a difference (show the relation is the same in both directions).

**Reachable negative:** both. If the construction leaves the density free and derives the rate, the
two readings differ and the paper's sentence is the loose one; if the relation is a bijection with no
free parameter either way, the addition and the paper agree and the apparent disagreement was a
reading of one identity from two sides.

## ⑨ The trampoline source: does it commit to lumps?

The addition's *"nothing in the trampoline is particular to lumpy matter"* is a claim about the source.

**Measured:** the ERC trampoline passage in `resources/`, read for (a) whether its furniture for the
cosmological description's validity is local and lumpy (lensing, ripples, bound structures) or admits
a uniform layer; (b) whether an **expanding** background appears in it at all, or only Minkowski;
(c) its threshold clause, verbatim.

**Criterion met if** all three are answered with located text, and the arm says whether the addition's
claim about the source is supported by the source.

**Reachable negative:** the source may state the analogy in terms that exclude a uniform layer — in
which case the addition is a **repair** of the analogy rather than a reading of it, which is a
different thing and worth naming; or it may already carry the uniform case, in which case `r7764`'s ⒞
missed text that was there.

## ⑩ Does the uniform-dust reading move `r7764`'s other four parts?

**Measured:** parts ⒜ (it concedes the founding question), ⒝ (the geometric level is empirically idle),
⒟ (`P7`'s inverse warning and third level) and ⒠ (a universal with no stated exception), each asked
whether the addition changes its verdict, with the change named or its absence stated.

**Criterion met if** each of the four carries an explicit verdict: strengthened, narrowed, unchanged,

**Reachable negative:** the addition could strengthen ⒜ rather than weaken it — if dust's density is
the offset, then the geometric rate's own matter term is an average density, and the founding question
is conceded at the geometric level too. The arm does not assume which way this goes.
