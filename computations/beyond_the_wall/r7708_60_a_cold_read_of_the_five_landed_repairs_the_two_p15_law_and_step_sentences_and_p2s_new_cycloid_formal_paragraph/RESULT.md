## ITEM ① — THE TWO LAW-AND-STEP SENTENCES: SIZE, PLACE AND SHARPNESS STAY APART

**The predicate registered in advance was whether the sentences separate three claims**, and
they do:

- **size** — "so the law carries the step's size", with `0.48` under the law against `0.48` as
  measured, agreeing to a part in a hundred and both inside the third-to-two-thirds the step

- **sharpness** — named as the exception, in both sentences: "reproduces that step's size
  **without reproducing its sharpness**", and "the measurement's transition is unresolved at
  every width and the law's is resolved at every one".
- **place** — **claimed for neither.** This is the one that could have gone wrong, because the
  receipt's own translation test shows it would be false: under a `+0.30` shift of the read
  interval the excess's half-rise moves `−0.2962`, nearly the whole shift, while the law's

**The width ratio carries the caveat that licenses it.** The receipt forbids one form in so
many words — *"the contrast is therefore stated as a ratio at fixed smoothing and never as a

out below about `h`, so a value at the floor means unresolved and **not** that a width was
measured. P15 says "the wider by some seven times at identical smoothing" — loose wording for a
ratio of two *readings* — but the clause that makes it legal, *"the measurement's transition is
unresolved at every width"*, is in the same sentence. **So: no defect.**
