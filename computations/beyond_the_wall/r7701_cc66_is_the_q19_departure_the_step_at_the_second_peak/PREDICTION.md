⇒ *** So the step's half-rise falls inside the departure's band almost by construction. *** ⌗ *"Both

**② THE BAND IS SEVEN TIMES WIDER THAN THE FEATURE.** The paper's own locating of the step says its

departure, the band reading cannot establish that they are one object; it can establish consistency in
