## ⌈ THE SELECTION CRITERIA — FIXED NOW, APPLIED AFTERWARDS, AND NOT NEGOTIABLE AFTER A NUMBER IS SEEN

A system enters the sample if and only if **all four** hold, each established from the source's own
words rather than from a label:

- **Ⓢ①** the turnaround radius is located **from the velocity field** — a zero-velocity surface, a
  Hubble-flow turnaround, an infall-profile zero crossing. **An overdensity radius (`R_200`,
  `R_vir`, `R_500`) disqualifies the system outright**, however it is renamed.
- **Ⓢ②** a **baryonic** mass is reported, **measured independently of that radius** and from a
  different observable than the kinematics (X-ray gas plus stars, or `HI` plus stars).
- **Ⓢ③** a **total or dynamical** mass is also reported, so both arms of the comparison exist.
- **Ⓢ④** the source states a **cosmology**, so `Λ` is either the instrument's or the difference can
  be propagated rather than ignored.

⌗ **EVERY system examined will be listed with the criterion that kept or dropped it**, including the
ones dropped. *A selection whose rejections are invisible is a selection nobody can check.*
