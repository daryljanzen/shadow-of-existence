--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  ⛔ AND THE FIRST READING OF THIS WAS MINE AND IT WAS WRONG, WHICH THE ROW'S OWN
    WORDS CORRECT: r_HE IS A BOUND, NOT AN EQUALITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
      The block above inverts r_HE as an EQUALITY and so reads a dark fraction of
      0.34 to 0.48 off it.  That number is an artefact of the inversion.  The row says
      what the instrument is: "the BOUND-to-attained factor is parameter-free".  r_HE is
      the LARGEST radius that can still be turning around for a given enclosed mass --
      the LambdaCDM bound the turnaround literature calls a bound and tests for
      violation.  ⇒ So the test is not "which mass does the inversion return" but
      ** IS THE OBSERVED RADIUS INSIDE THE BOUND EACH MASS CHOICE ALLOWS? **
      ⌗ A bound inverted as an equality is a different instrument, and it gave a
        different answer.  Recorded rather than quietly replaced.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  ⌈ THE ONE RESIDUAL, QUANTIFIED RATHER THAN WAVED AT: THE APERTURES DIFFER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
      The baryonic mass is independent of R0 -- which is what Ⓢ② asked for -- but it
      is NOT measured WITHIN R0.  The gas mass carries no stated aperture, and the
      total Planck infers from it is quoted 'on a scale up to 2 times larger than the
      virial radius' -- about 3.4 Mpc against R0 = 7.0 to 7.8 Mpc.  So the baryonic
      content inside R0 is AT LEAST the value used, and the violation above is an
      upper bound on the evidence rather than a floor.

      ⇒ For the baryon-only bound NOT to be violated, the baryonic mass inside R0
        would have to be 1.70 to 2.35 times the gas-plus-stars
        measured inside about 3.4 Mpc -- i.e. 2.63e+14 to 4.12e+14 Msun of baryons.
        That is 33 to 56 per cent of the independently measured
        TOTAL, so it would require Virgo to be baryon-dominated inside R0.

      ⇒ ** SO WHAT IS MISSING IS NOT A SAMPLE -- ONE EXISTS AND IT DECIDES THE
        DIRECTION -- BUT A BARYONIC MASS MEASURED OUT TO THE TURNAROUND RADIUS. **
        That is a different clause from the one the row carries, and naming it is
        the part of this that the row cannot do for itself.
