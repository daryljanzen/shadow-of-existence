## ⌈ ② ⛔ AND THE FIRST READING WAS MINE AND IT WAS WRONG — A BOUND INVERTED AS AN EQUALITY IS A DIFFERENT INSTRUMENT

The run first inverted `r_HE` as an **equality**, `M = Λc²R₀³/3G`, and read a dark fraction off it:
`f_b = 0.34`–`0.48` against the cosmic `0.157`. **That number is an artefact of the inversion and is
printed in the receipt beside its correction rather than deleted.**

The row's own words say what the instrument is: *"the **bound**-to-attained factor is
parameter-free."* `r_HE` is the **largest** radius that can still be turning around for a given
enclosed mass — the same bound the turnaround literature tests for violation. ⇒ **So the question is
not which mass the inversion returns, but whether the observed radius is inside the bound each mass
choice allows.** Read that way the four determinations answer it unanimously.

> ⛭ **The error was mine, it was found by re-reading the row's sentence rather than by the data, and
> the two readings gave different answers** — which is why the discarded one is kept in the output.

## ⌈ ④ THE ONE RESIDUAL, QUANTIFIED — AND IT IS WHY THE ROW'S CLAUSE IS WRONG

`Ⓢ②` asked for a baryonic mass **independent of** the radius, and got one. **It is not measured
WITHIN the radius.** The gas mass carries no stated aperture, and the total Planck infers from it is
quoted *"on a scale up to `2` times larger than the virial radius"* — about `3.4` Mpc against
`R₀ = 7.0`–`7.8` Mpc. So the baryonic content inside `R₀` is **at least** the value used, and the
violation is an upper bound on the evidence rather than a floor.

> **For the baryon-only bound not to be violated, the baryonic mass inside `R₀` would have to be
> `1.70` to `2.35` times the gas-plus-stars measured inside `~3.4` Mpc — `2.6`–`4.1 × 10¹⁴ M☉`,
> which is `33` to `56` per cent of the independently measured total.** *It would require Virgo to
> be baryon-dominated inside its own turnaround radius, against a cosmic baryon fraction of `~18`
> per cent.*

⇒ ⚑ **SO WHAT IS MISSING IS NOT A SAMPLE — ONE EXISTS AND IT DECIDES THE DIRECTION — BUT A BARYONIC
MASS MEASURED OUT TO THE TURNAROUND RADIUS.** **That is a different clause from the one the row
carries**, and naming it is the part of this the row could not do for itself. *The row says the
instrument half is built and the sample half is missing; the sample half is now supplied, and what
it reveals is a third half nobody had separated out.*
