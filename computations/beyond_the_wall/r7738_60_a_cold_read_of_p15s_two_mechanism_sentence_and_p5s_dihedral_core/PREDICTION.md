## Ⓐ — THE `P15` SENTENCE, LOCATED AND READ

**Ⓐ① The four stems are present, each on the raw file and on the flattened copy.** The passage in
`corpus/CR_cosmology.tex` carries, in order, the stems *"the located transition belongs to the
contrast between the arms and not to their sources"*, *"That exclusion is about the sources' own
ratio, not about their difference once it is projected"*, *"But only the projection's contribution
is structured in multipole"*, *"The step's size, by contrast, is in both arms"*.
*Reachable negative:* a stem absent, or present raw and absent flattened.

**Ⓐ② Each printed figure appears in the paper and in the receipt.** `1.0076`, `1.0035`, `97.93`,
`22.39`, `93.6`. The witness is the character offset of each in both files.
*Reachable negative:* a figure in the paper that the receipt does not carry.

**Ⓐ③ PREDICTED: the sentence CANNOT be read as "the sources carry the step", and the reason is a
partition across the four stems** — stem 1 assigns the **location**, stem 2 concedes only
**presence**, stem 3 assigns the **ℓ-structure**, stem 4 assigns the **size** to both arms. The arm
asserts that the word **`located`** is inside stem 1 (so stem 1 is not a claim about the step
simply), that stem 2's concession verb is one of absence (*"not absent from"*) and not of carriage,
and that stem 4 explicitly gives the size to both arms.
*Reachable negative:* `located` missing from stem 1, or stem 2 asserting carriage.

**Ⓐ④ The classifier, on both classes (G4).** A reader that decides of a sentence whether it asserts
*the sources carry the located step* is run on the four real stems **and** on a fabricated sentence
that does assert it. PREDICTED: `0` of `4` real stems flagged, `1` of `1` fabricated flagged.
*Reachable negative:* the fabricated sentence not flagged — in which case the `0 of 4` means
nothing, which is `r7736 ⓷`'s lesson exactly.

**⛔ Ⓐ⑤ PREDICTED DEFECT: the three percentages in one sentence are NOT the same kind of
quantity.** The receipt computes `97.93` and `22.39` as **rms** fractions
(`wrms(l-part)/wrms(template)`) and `93.6` as a **variance** share (`vk/(vk+vs+vx)`), and the

measures the **amplitude** reading of the same quantity, `sqrt(0.935623)`, and asserts it is a
**different printed figure** from `93.6`.
*Reachable negative:* the two readings agree to the printed precision, in which case nothing is
lost by dropping the word and there is no defect.

**Ⓐ⑥ The two printed amplitudes belong to ONE binning.** `1.0076`/`1.0035` are the receipt's
**primary**; the secondary is `1.0195`/`1.0045`. The sentence attributes the exclusion to *"two null
constructions and two binnings"* without saying which binning the printed pair is.
*Reachable negative:* the pair is binning-independent, or the sentence names the binning.

**Ⓐ⑦ The repair that corrected `0.4642` left NO twin (the `r7732 ⑤` check).** Every occurrence of

## Ⓑ — `P5`'s σ SENTENCE AGAINST THE LEAN

**Ⓑ① Every `gp_` name the paper cites exists in `Groupoid.lean`, and a name that does not exist is
not found (G5).** The reader classifies each hit as a `theorem`/`def`/`lemma` **declaration** and
not as a docstring mention.
*Reachable negative:* a cited name present only in a comment.

**Ⓑ② `gp_σ_roots`'s swap is labels `0 ↔ 1` with `2` fixed, and `gp_roots_pos`'s positivity is for
labels `0` and `1` — the SAME two.** Computed from the declarations' own statements, not assumed.
*Reachable negative:* positivity established for a label the swap does not move.

**Ⓑ③ The interval `(0, π/3)` is σ-stable**, so the exchange the sentence names stays inside the
interval where it claims positivity. `σ(w) = π/3 − w`.
*Reachable negative:* `σ` carrying an interior `w` out of the interval, which would make the
positivity claim about roots at a `w` where it was never established.

**Ⓑ④ The third root is the NEGATIVE one on that interval**, so *"fixes the third"* is not a
positivity claim in disguise. Witness: `r₂(w) = (2/√3) sin(w + 4π/3) < 0` on `(0, π/3)`.
*Reachable negative:* `r₂ > 0` somewhere inside.

**Ⓑ⑤ The two parametrisations agree EXACTLY.** `2M = r₀ − r₀³` with `r₀ = (2/√3) sin w` equals
`(2/(3√3)) sin 3w` — checked symbolically, not numerically.
*Reachable negative:* a residual that is not identically zero.

**⛔ Ⓑ⑥ PREDICTED DEFECT: the sentence says "the three roots", and at `w = π/6`, which lies
STRICTLY INSIDE `(0, π/3)`, there are only two.** The discriminant of `r³ − r + 2M` at
`2M = (2/(3√3)) sin 3w` is `4 − 27(2M)² = 4cos²3w`, which vanishes on `(0, π/3)` at `w = π/6`
alone — the **closed form**, because `r7732 ⓷` was bought by a grid that could not resolve a
near-tangency. There `r₀ = r₁ = 1/√3` and `2M = 2/(3√3)`, the Nariai mass, so the two roots σ
"exchanges" coincide and the exchange is of a root with itself. The arm asserts **exactly one**
such interior `w` and that it is `π/6`.
*Reachable negative:* no interior degeneracy (the sentence is then unqualifiedly right), or more
than one (the finding is larger than one point).

**Ⓑ⑦ The paper's own `gp_σ_fixed` paragraph names `π/6` — but LATER.** The arm reports the line
numbers of the σ sentence and of the `gp_σ_fixed` paragraph and asserts the order.
*Reachable negative:* the disclosure standing before the sentence, which would close Ⓑ⑥ to a
cross-reference.

## Ⓒ — `71`'s ANTI-HOMOMORPHISM NOTE

**Ⓒ① `gp_ψ` is a MONOID HOMOMORPHISM, machine-checked.** Its type is
`DihedralGroup 3 →* Equiv.Perm (ZMod 3)` and it is built by `gp_act`, whose `map_mul'` is proved.
So the "anti" is **not** a failure of homomorphy.
*Reachable negative:* `gp_ψ` typed as a bare function, in which case homomorphy is unproved.

**Ⓒ② What IS "anti" is the SIGN of the label map: `e = −id`, so `ψ(r i) : k ↦ k − i` while the
angle goes `w ↦ w + i·(2π/3)`.** Verified against `gp_root_perm`'s own direction
`r_{ψ(g)k}(g·w) = r_k(w)` by evaluating both sides over all `6 × 3` pairs.
*Reachable negative:* the direction `k ↦ k + i` reproducing `gp_root_perm`.

**⛭ Ⓒ③ PREDICTED: the sentence is IMMUNE, and exactly measurably so — σ is one of exactly TWO
group elements on which the two sign conventions for the label map agree, and the only nontrivial
one.** Compare `ψ(g)` built with `e = −id` against `ψ'(g)` built with `e = +id`, over all six
elements of `D₃`. PREDICTED: they agree on `r 0` and `sr 0 = σ` and differ on the other four.
*Reachable negative:* they agree on more or fewer than two, or disagree on `σ` — in which case
`gp_σ_roots` would depend on the convention and `71`'s note would bite.

**Ⓒ④ And the paper makes no τ-labelled root claim**, which is the claim the convention WOULD
reach. Witness: the count of printed sentences naming both `τ` and a root label.
*Reachable negative:* such a sentence existing.
