#
# ** WHY THIS FILE EXISTS. **  A grep over the papers finds 123 places where the corpus says a
# thing is not settled.  ** Reading them shows most are not gaps: ** they are deliberate holdings
# (not claimed), scope statements, descriptions of STANDARD physics' open problems, or claims
# already answered two sentences later.  ⇒ *** Without a recorded verdict per item, that reading
# has to be redone every time anyone asks what is open — which is exactly what kept happening. ***
#
# ** FORMAT: ** id | paper | verdict | the claim (trimmed).  ** One line per DISTINCT claim ** —
# 123 raw hits deduplicate to 113 sentences.

# ** THE VERDICTS: **
#   DO-NOT-ASSERT    a deliberate, marked holding.  Correctly stated; nothing owed.

#   METHOD-PROSE     prose about how the corpus marks maturity.  Not a claim.
#   SELF-ANSWERED    the next sentence closes it.  ** The class that has misled this line five times. **
#   REGISTERED       a real gap, already carried by a PROTECTED_OPEN item or a register row.
