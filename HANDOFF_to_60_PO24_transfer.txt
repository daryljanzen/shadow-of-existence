WHAT IS OWED

P15 names it in its own voice: "not a missing idea but a computation this sequence
owes and has not yet run, and the paper's own open edge rather than another's." It is
the end-to-end branch-point-to-recombination transfer that would carry the
single-phase datum all the way through and confirm the height pattern digit by digit.

Its own receipt names the gap. C22, titled THE END-TO-END NUMBER, reaches both ends --
low-ell (a 7.5% power deficit from the absent early ISW) and high-ell (0.843 at l_D
falling to 0.459 at 2 l_D) -- and states that BETWEEN them, at the acoustic peaks, the
chain does not reach, because Phi decays by order two there and the decay is
k-dependent through k^2/3H.

DO NOT START WITH THE BUILD. START WITH THE CONTROL.

The instrument is computations/beyond_the_wall/ACOUSTIC_two_arm.py, 1195 lines. It
carries a proper line-of-sight Boltzmann integral:

  Delta_l(k) = INT S(k,eta) j_l(k(eta_0 - eta)) d(eta)
  S = g (Theta_0 + Psi) + e^-tau (Phi' + Psi') + (1/k^2) d/d(eta) [g theta_b]

Run on the LambdaCDM arm, whose answer is known and whose transfer is validated
against CAMB, it gets the POSITIONS right and the HEIGHTS wrong:

  arm                      l1    l2    l3    P1/P2   err      P1/P3   err
  LambdaCDM control        220   524   804   2.721   22.7%    4.496   97.5%
  CR, CRAMP=entry          204   508   804   1.935   12.7%    2.578   13.2%
  THE SKY                  220.6 538.1 809.8 2.217            2.277

So the defect is isolated to the height machinery, it is SHARED by both arms, and it
is not a CR defect. CR with the derived datum actually beats the control on both
ratios. An instrument that cannot reproduce a spectrum whose answer is known cannot be
trusted about one that is not.

FIRST STEP: make the LambdaCDM control reproduce CAMB's heights. It is bounded, it is
checkable, and there is a right answer to check against -- CAMB gives P1/P2 = 2.200
against the sky's 2.2564 +- 0.0772.

THEN THE BUILD ITSELF

It requires first SPECIFYING HOW THE FLUCTUATIONS GRAVITATE on the geometrically fixed
background -- the piece that sets the high-ell driving envelope. Standard Boltzmann
codes cannot supply this: they tie radiation's gravity to its presence and so cannot
represent the content-not-rate split the layered rate rests on. That specification is
the actual content of the build and it does not exist yet.

The pieces you are joining DO exist and are receipted:
  - collapse leg, closed form: Psi = 3 Psi_i (sin x - x cos x)/x^3, x = k eta/sqrt3,
    even in x; Theta-hat = Theta_0 + Psi removes the source exactly
  - expanding leg: Phi = C_1 + C_2/eta^5, growing mode constant (C11_early_isw)
  - the join: Phi -> Phi_i at the branch point for every k, giving
    Phi_exp = (9/10) Phi_i scale-invariantly (C21_the_superhorizon_transfer_closes)

DO NOT CONFUSE THE TWO INSTRUMENTS

PO-10's strike put LambdaCDM at P1/P2 = 2.200 and CR at 2.185, both inside 0.9 sigma.
Those were produced THROUGH CAMB with a suppression factor applied to the CR arm.
That is a legitimate comparison and it is NOT a bespoke transfer. The first instrument
works and does not answer the question; the second asks the question and its height
machinery does not yet work. Conflating them is what hid this item for revisions.
