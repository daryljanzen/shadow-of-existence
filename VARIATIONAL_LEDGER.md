## ★★★ 1 · THE OPENING QUESTION — the Carter constant

*The corpus makes a strong claim, in three papers, in nearly the same words:*

> *P9: "the hidden symmetry of the Type-D family---**the Carter constant that general relativity carries as an
> unexplained gift**---is the substrate's maximal symmetry surfacing in that corner."*
> *p0: "the Carter constant **as the substrate's own maximal symmetry surfacing**."*
> *P7: "Type D is the separable corner where **the substrate's symmetry surfaces as the Carter constant** rather
> than the edge."*

*And the mechanism it gives is **separability**: "the vacuum condition itself splits into a pure-$r$ piece and a
pure-angle piece."*

> ***THE QUESTION: is that an EXPLANATION of the Carter constant in the sense the claim needs?***

**Why the variational field is where this is adjudicated, and why the absence matters here specifically.** *A
conserved quantity earns the name **explained** when it is produced by a symmetry — and the theorem that does
that producing is **Noether's**, which appears in the corpus zero times. **The Carter constant is the standard
counterexample to the naive form**: it comes from a **Killing tensor**, not a Killing vector, and so is not a
Noether charge of a point symmetry of the action. That is exactly why the literature calls it **hidden**.*

***So the corpus claims to explain the thing whose whole reputation is that the usual explanation does not reach
it — and it makes the claim without the apparatus in which "explained" is normally judged. Either the claim is
stronger than the corpus has shown, or the substrate route reaches what the point-symmetry route cannot, and
saying which is the bake's first job.***
