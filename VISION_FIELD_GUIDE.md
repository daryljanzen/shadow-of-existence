# Vision Field Guide
### How to wield and mold `THE_VISION.md` — its discipline, and the ways holding a big picture goes wrong. The companion to the vision as `CODA_FIELD_NOTE.md` is to the coda.

The capstone family divides cleanly: `THE_CODA` is *how to see*; `THE_INTERFERENCE_ENGINE`
is *how knowing happens*; `CODA_FIELD_NOTE` is *how seeing fails*; `THE_VISION` is *what we
currently see*; and this is *how holding the vision fails, and how to mold it without
breaking the work*. Read it before leaning on any line of the vision.

## The cardinal rule — altitude is provisionality

The vision is the apex and therefore the **most provisional** document in the package, not
the most authoritative. The higher a claim's altitude, the more it is a reach to be molded,
and the coda's mandate bites hardest here: *the prize is the hypothesis, sharp enough to
wield, never its truth.* Hold the whole vision at full weight **as a hypothesis** — fully
enough to grind against the real work, at zero weight as a verdict, both at once. A line of
the vision is never evidence; the work is evidence, and the vision is what the work is
currently arranging itself into.

## The chief failure — manufactured coherence

The vision's beauty is its danger. The failure mode it invites is **manufactured
coherence**: using the vision as a confirmation template, reading the next result *for* how
it fits the big picture, and quietly bending the evidence to keep the picture whole. This is
the manufactured-certainty failure of `CODA_FIELD_NOTE` at its most seductive scale, because
one *wants* the vision to be true. The guard is one line: **the evidence overrules the
vision, never the reverse.** A result that does not fit is not a problem to be smoothed; it
is the thing telling you the vision needs molding, or that the move was wrong — and which one
is settled at the source, never by preference for the picture.

The symmetric failure also applies: **manufactured incoherence** — dismissing a real result
because it complicates the clean picture, or hedging the vision into mush to look measured.
Both are the disposition shaping the evidence instead of receiving it.

## The dissonance lens, at whole-framework scale

The vision's working function is a **coherence touchstone**. Check a move against it and ask:
does this cohere with the picture, or dissonance against it? A dissonance is a **flag to
investigate at the source**, never a verdict in either direction. It resolves exactly three
ways, and you do not get to choose which from inside your preference for the picture:
1. the move is wrong (the vision caught it) — fix the move;
2. the vision is wrong or too narrow (the move caught it) — mold the vision, re-tag the line;
3. the apparent conflict dissolves under a careful reading of what each actually claims.
Go to the source and let it say which. This is the CR dissonance lens (fix the programme's
real commitment from its canonical sources; check parts against that, not against
convention) lifted from the paper scale to the whole-framework scale. **Guard, carried up
from the corpus:** CR's "manufactured / shadow / projection / artefact" mean
*built-by-construction-and-real*, never *unreal* — a dissonance built on the conventional
reading of those words is invented, not real.

## Reading-order discipline (binding)

- **Read last, never first.** With the capstones, after the corpus is received on its own
  terms. A grand picture taken in early bends every reading toward confirming it — the worst
  contamination in the package, because it is substantive, not merely dispositional.
- **A referee never sees the vision.** It is why-layer: downstream of every cold task, like
  the coda. The cold reader's entire value is not knowing where the gate thinks this is all
  going; the vision is precisely that knowledge, and handing it over collapses the cold read
  into an echo. Owe the referee only the operational green light.

## Molding discipline — how a living document stays honest

- **Tag every line by status:** [established] / [reading] / conjecture. The tags are the molding
  seams. Never let a conjecture drift into a [reading], or a [reading] into [established],
  without the source promoting it. A reach that has survived long enough to *feel* true is
  still a reach until a proof or a verified computation moves it — familiarity is not
  promotion.
- **Mold, do not force.** When the work teaches something that does not fit, change the
  vision: rewrite the line, re-tag it, state it for reversal. Keep the stable spine
  (the genealogy, the status discipline) fixed; let the structural sections breathe as the
  algebroid is built and the wall approached.
- **Commit the mold, do not propose it.** The vision is editable end to end; a warranted
  update is baked and stated for reversal, never held at the threshold for a sign-off the
  editability makes pointless (`CODA_FIELD_NOTE`, June 9 — the reversible-record entry).

## The test that the vision is still alive and not a template

A vision that only ever confirms — that every result "fits," that it never hands back a
falsifiable next move — has become a template, and the failure has already happened. A
living vision keeps **handing back checks**: the algebroid horizon collapsing onto rung 1
(construct the anchor) and C2 (the width-of-the-image test) is the proof it was alive on the
day it was written — the grandest claim returned the smallest computation that could break
it. Hold the vision to that standard at every check-in: *what falsifiable next move does this
picture hand back right now?* If the answer is none, suspect the template, not the world.

what the work shows, steady enough not to bend the work to the picture. Mold it as we learn;
let the source always say no.*
