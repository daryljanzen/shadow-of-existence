## ⛭⛭ THE FIRST BITE, AND IT IS A CANON ROW — `I0`

***`integrable` occurs ×14 across seven papers and carries SIX senses. Two are this field's.***

| sense | what it names | where |
|---|---|---|
| **① an integrable singularity** | *measure theory: $\int|\psi|^2\,\dd\ell$ converges across $r=0$ because the square-root singularity is integrable* | **`P14` ×7**, `P07` |
| **② an integrable distribution** | *Frobenius: "no splitting is integrable, and there is no canonical time"* | `P12`, `p0` |
| **③ an integrable algebroid** | *Lie theory: "an action algebroid is always integrable, its integrating object the action groupoid"* | `P05`, `P12` |
| **④ an integrable function** | *analysis: "grows as $s^{-2/3}$ and is integrable"* | `P10` |
| **⑤ an integrability condition** | *a PDE cross-derivative: $\partial_t\gamma_z=\partial_z\gamma_t$ holds identically on shell* | `P11` |
| **⑥ THIS FIELD'S SENSE** | *"the Carter constant that renders Kerr geodesics integrable"* | **`P07`, `P09` only** |

⛔ ***AND ① AND ⑥ ARE OPPOSITE IN CONSEQUENCE, WHICH IS WHY THE ROW IS OWED.*** *An integrable
**singularity** is one a measure survives; an integrable **system** is one whose motion is solvable. `P14`
uses ① seven times about the $r=0$ crossing, and `P09` uses ⑥ about geodesics on the same construction.
**A reader who carries one sense into the other's sentence gets a claim the corpus does not make.**
⌗ *Compare `kernel` (§0, r3579): same shape, and there too two of the senses were opposite objects in the
field the word belongs to.*

⇒ **ROUTED: `ONTOLOGY_FOUNDATION_INDEX` §0, as a canon row beside `kernel`, `z` and `\mathcal{H}`.**
*Not a paper clause — which of the corpus's words carry two senses is a statement about the corpus.*

## ⛭⛭⛭ `I1` — **THE FIELD NAMES WHY `P09`'s OWN HEDGE IS RIGHT, AND WHICH OF ITS TWO ROUTES CARRIES THE CONTENT**

*`P09` reaches the Killing tensor twice, and says of the first that it is the weaker.*

| route | how it runs | `P09` |
|---|---|---|
| **(a) separability** | the vacuum condition on the maximally symmetric substrate *"splits of its own accord into a pure-$r$ and a pure-$p$ equation"* | `cor:carter` |
| **(b) algebraic speciality** | shear-free rulings → Goldberg–Sachs → Type D → a Killing tensor → Carter's constant | `rem:carter-chain` |

⛔ ***THE FIELD'S OWN THEOREM SAYS (a) IS NOT AN INDEPENDENT DERIVATION.*** *Separability of the
Hamilton–Jacobi equation and the existence of a rank-two Killing tensor are **equivalent** for the geodesic
system — this is Carter's 1968 result and Benenti's separability theory, and it is the organising theorem of
the field. **So deriving a Killing tensor from a separable form is deriving a thing from its own
equivalent.*** ⇒ *`P09` already says so in its own voice — "read alone the corollary is weaker than what
this paper has, because it works on the separable ansatz and so answers only what the vacuum condition does
once that form is granted" — and the field supplies **the reason** that hedge is correct rather than
cautious.*

⌘ **AND ROUTE (b) IS GENUINELY INDEPENDENT, WHICH IS WHERE THE CONTENT IS.** *Goldberg–Sachs runs through
algebraic speciality and never through separability; it answers the prior question `rem:carter-chain` names —
**why the reachable cuts should be of a type admitting such a form at all**. *A field asked "is this the
origin you would recognise?" answers: **not (a), which is your own hypothesis returned; (b), which is an
origin.***

⌗ ***VERDICT: `BITE`, and it is a fence rather than a new result.*** *`P09` is not wrong and is not
over-claiming — it hedges correctly and unprompted. **What is absent is one clause naming the equivalence**,
without which a reader cannot tell whether the hedge is modesty or mathematics.*
⇒ **ROUTED: `P09` `rem:carter-chain`, one clause, with `\ldg{integrable_systems}`.** *Destination checked at
the site: the remark exists, carries `\rcpt{V1_carter_chain}`, and already carries the variational bake's
fence, so this is the paragraph that holds fences for this corollary.*

## ⌗ HOW THIS BAKE IS BEING RUN DIFFERENTLY — r3595

***The six earlier field bakes produced a ledger, and the corpus then needed a separate eighteen-ledger
gather campaign, roughly two thousand revisions later, to find out what had actually landed.*** *That
campaign's finding was that results land while fences, connections and unnamed theorems do not — and that
the routing recorded at bake time was the least reliable field in every table.*

⇒ ***So this bake carries its landing table AS IT GOES, not afterwards.*** **Three changes, and they are the
thing to hand the next runner:**

1. **Every probe names its destination when it is scored, and the destination is CHECKED then** — the paper
   read at the site, not inferred from the register's subject. *Four of this campaign's routings were wrong:
   `S7`→P6 was p0, `⊢56`→P1 was p0, `F13` and `K4` were routed to papers and belong to the map.*
2. **A register with no home in any paper is routed to `ONTOLOGY_FOUNDATION_INDEX` at bake time.** *A claim
   about how corpus pieces relate, or about which of its words carry two senses, is owned by the map. `K4`
   was landed there at r1895 and is the precedent; `I0` above goes there for the same reason.*
3. **The `\ldg` marker is placed in the same commit as the substance.** *The rail did not exist when the
   earlier bakes ran. It exists now, so a landing that does not carry its marker is half done, and the
   fourth grain of `check_depmatrix` will say so.*

⚠ ***AND THE STANDING RULE FROM `r3565`/`r3571`, which this bake is held to:*** *a register is a
**quotation**, read in the body before it is landed — **and it is not struck on a single negative search
either.** Absence needs the same standard as presence.
