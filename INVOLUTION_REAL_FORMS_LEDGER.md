## ⛭⛭ WHAT BOUNCED — the load-bearing number is right, and it is a Cartan invariant

`P07` excludes $\su(3)$ from the substrate's holonomy algebra partly on this: *"a compact algebra of
dimension eight cannot sit in a group whose maximal compact is six-dimensional."*

**Computed from scratch, on explicit $5\times5$ matrices:** $\so(4,1)$ has dimension 10; $\theta(X)=\eta
X\eta$ is an involutive Lie-algebra automorphism; its $+1$ eigenspace $\mathfrak{k}$ has dimension
**6** and its $-1$ eigenspace $\mathfrak{p}$ dimension 4; $[\mathfrak{k},\mathfrak{k}]\subset\mathfrak{k}$,
$[\mathfrak{k},\mathfrak{p}]\subset\mathfrak{p}$, $[\mathfrak{p},\mathfrak{p}]\subset\mathfrak{k}$; and
$-B(X,\theta Y)$ is positive definite.

⇒ ***So $\theta$ is a Cartan involution, $\mathfrak{k}=\so(4)$ is the maximal compact, and `P07`'s six
is the dimension of a Cartan involution's fixed-point set — computed here, named nowhere in the corpus.***

⌗ **And the corpus's signature flip IS the unitary trick.** *The Wick rotation $J=\mathrm{diag}(1,1,1,1,i)$
carries $\so(4,1)$ into $\so(5,\mathbb{C})$ with $\mathfrak{k}$ untouched and $\mathfrak{p}$ sent to
purely imaginary — i.e. $\mathfrak{k}\oplus\mathfrak{p}\mapsto\mathfrak{k}\oplus i\mathfrak{p}$, the
compact real form. **Verified on every basis element.*** ⇒ *`P05`'s signature-flip involution $\xi$ and
`P13`'s real-form argument are one structure, in different papers, under no shared name.*
⛭ *Sixth instance of the corpus's characteristic shape, after the Atiyah sequence, $N_{\rm eff}$, the
baby universe, matched-procedure control and Ambrose–Singer.*


## ⛔⛔⛭ WHAT BIT — the real-form enumeration is INCOMPLETE, and the uniqueness claim fails

> $\SO(3,3)$ ... **the compact form is the unique real form of $\SO(6,\mathbb{C})$ that admits $\su(3)$
> at all**."*

⛔ ***$\so(6,\mathbb{C})\cong\mathfrak{sl}(4,\mathbb{C})$ has FIVE real forms, not four.***
