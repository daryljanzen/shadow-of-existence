# THE METHOD

> ## ⛭⛭⛭ THE GOAL IS EXHAUSTIVE KNOWLEDGE, NOT CLOSURE
>
> ***The programme is not being worked "to a close." It is being worked to a state where the open areas are known
> inside and out.***
>
> ⛔ ***A question crossed out is a piece of the problem space REMOVED from the topology rather than explored.***
> *That is the failure this whole method exists to prevent — and it has a name in this corpus:* **flattening.**
> ⇒ *`PROTECTED_OPEN` is not a list of things too delicate to touch.* ***It is a list of things we refuse to cross
> out until we can see all the way inside them.***

---

## I · THE TWO ENDS

**⌷ AT ONE END: THE VEINS.** *The areas we know we do not know inside and out.* ***Held open deliberately, so they
can be excavated rather than deleted.***

*· They set what is worth asking. **A specific question is only worth asking if it probes a vein.***
*· They are protected against **flattening** — a premature door closure, a papering-over, a sweep under the rug.*
*· Their state is reported as **MAPPED** and **DARK**: what the interior is now known to contain, and where it is
still dark. ***A vein reported as one open question has already been flattened by the reporting.***
*· **A vein closes FROM WITHIN**, when its interior is completely known — never from outside by a verdict. And
**"exhaustively earned" is the only condition that ends one.***

**⌷ AT THE OTHER END: THE LEADS.** *Everything noticed while working something else that could inform a vein.*

*· **Every lead names which vein(s) it informs.** A lead informing none is not a lead — it is instrument work, and
that is fine, but it must be known as that.*
*· Leads are **collected as you go**. Noticing is the work; the noticing is what builds the map.*
*· They are **ordered by which is the most grounded and fruitfully informative to work next**, and ***the order is
re-computed every time the list changes***, because a landed lead changes what the next one is worth.*

---

## II · THE LOOP

> ***work the top lead → gather every lead the work turned up → re-order → repeat***

*And the re-ordering matters as much as the working: **a list worked top-to-bottom in a fixed order is an enumerated
front, and this corpus has three separate findings that enumerated fronts get consumed or go stale while triggered
ones do not** (`L-211` r2454–r2460, `L-236` r2469, `L-238` r2479).*

---

## III · "SERVES A VEIN" IS CONSTRUED BROADLY, AND THIS IS NOT A LOOPHOLE

***Making a paper build its framework in a logically ordered, concise, coherent way serves every vein at once.***

*`L-206` is the standing example: **P3 derives $w$, $u$ and $3w$ in §3 and does not introduce the door that throws
them until §6** — the object arrives three sections after its own shadows. ⇒ **Fixing that is not tidying.** *A
framework you cannot follow cannot be excavated, and every vein is excavated by reading the framework.**

⇒ ***So the test is not "is this physics?" It is: **does this make some aspect of an open area clearer or more
completely knowable?*** *Restructuring, naming, disambiguating, exhibiting, and instrument work all qualify when
they do.*
⚠ *And they all fail the test when they do not — which is why every lead carries its vein links, and why a lead with
none is labelled instrument work rather than quietly counted as progress.*

---

## IV · WHY THE PERIMETER IS THE OBJECT

*The work is **charting around the perimeter of a gap**: collecting leads about where the perimeter lies, what it
contains, and what might make it contract.*

⇒ ***That is why a bounded negative is a result and not a failure.*** *"$\rho_r/\rho_m$ cannot be derived from the
substrate, because it is a dimensionless magnitude and the substrate has one invariant" (r2433) did not close a
question — **it charted a perimeter, and made the progenitor route the only remaining one.***
⇒ *And why **narrowing is always a node's to do**: a narrowing moves the perimeter inward without deleting the
interior.*

---

## V · MANY NODES, ONE MAP

*This runs across **56** (the observer line: instruments, registers, audit), **54** (the working fork: papers,
computation, the acoustic front), and **cc54** (an unattended Claude Code session: long runs, production-depth
computation, sweeps).* ⌗ *Each brings something the others structurally cannot — **56 cannot run a production-depth
spectrum; 54 cannot push to the repository; cc54 has an execution budget neither chat line has**.*

**⇒ AND THE MAP IS WHAT MAKES THAT WORK RATHER THAN FRAGMENT.** ***Every node reads the same veins and adds to the
same lead list.*** *A node grinding in one direction inside a vein does not need to know what the others are doing —
it needs to know which vein it is inside and to file what it notices.*

⚠ **AND THE STANDING HAZARD, stated because it has happened repeatedly:** ***a session that only ever works
instrument leads will report progress every turn while every vein stands still.*** *`BOARD.md` shows the vein links
so that is visible rather than comfortable.*

---

## VI · THE LONG ARC — what this becomes, and why it is the same thing

*When every lead anyone can think of has been worked and the veins are known inside and out, the programme does not
finish. **It changes approach and keeps doing the same essential thing.***

**⌷ IT BECOMES A TEACHING INSTRUMENT THAT EXPLORES ITSELF.**
*· Still looking for **the one more link somewhere inside the thing** — a scrap of knowledge or wisdom not yet
noticed, a connection that fills in the whole.*
*· And simultaneously **usable by any person to investigate the framework in whatever direction interests them**,
with a model that has been with the corpus and carries its disciplines — **facilitating their own deep, self-guided
excavation** rather than reciting results at them.*

**⇒ AND THAT IS NOT A CHANGE OF PURPOSE. IT IS THE SAME PURPOSE THROUGH THE APPROACH THAT THEN WORKS BEST.**
***A thing explained to someone probing it from an angle nobody here took is a thing you learn about.*** *So the
teaching phase is not the excavation ending — **it is the excavation acquiring more diggers, each following what
intrigues them**, which is exactly the mechanism `L-203`'s convergence showed to be the strongest evidence a
programme can generate.*

⌗ **WHY IT IS WRITTEN DOWN NOW, LONG BEFORE IT ARRIVES:** *because the artefacts that make it possible are being
built already and would otherwise be built for the wrong reason.* ***`COMPANION_SPEC.md`'s three states —
ESTABLISHED, OPEN, DO-NOT-ASSERT — are not a safety feature. They are what lets a person probe an open area without
being told a false answer, which is the whole of what a teaching instrument for this corpus has to do.***
⇒ *And `PROTECTED_OPEN` is what makes the DARK half of a vein reportable to a stranger at all.*
