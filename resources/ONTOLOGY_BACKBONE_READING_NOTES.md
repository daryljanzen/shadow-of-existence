### ✅ Physicists and Philosophers Have Long Struggled (The Conversation, 2025-11-19)
*Saved:* `resources/conceptual-boundary-essays/physicists-and-philosophers-long-struggled.md`

**Establishes (ontological core):** the **existence/occurrence distinction** — physics and philosophy
conflate *what it means to exist* with *what it means to happen*, treating occurrences as existent. The
correction: **events happen; they do not exist.** Space-time (4D) is a *model/record* of all
occurrences — points are events, worldlines are paths — and to call space-time "existing" is to treat
events as existent things (the map mistaken for the territory; the root of the time-travel and
eternalism errors). Cosmology resolves it: a **three-dimensional universe of existing things** (stars,
galaxies) whose particle-locations at each instant are events; as the universe exists, those events
trace worldlines in 4D space-time — a useful geometric representation, **not an existent thing**.
Heraclitus' river (an existing thing in continuous flux — same river, different waters) is the correct
picture; Parmenides/Zeno (past/future "exist" → the static block) is the circular fallacy that drops
time.

**Bears on FLAG ONT‑1 (interrogated, at weight):** it grounds the ontological **primacy of the existing,
evolving 3D universe (the layer) over the 4D record (the manifold).** The individuated evolving layer is
*what exists*; the manifold is its record. So the individuation keystone is a matter of the
**dynamic-half / existence** (the layer), not of the 4D record — which is exactly why the bullet calls
it "the dynamic-half foundation the cosmology and the lock rest on." *Limit:* the essay fixes the
**level** (the keystone belongs to existence / the evolving layer, not the record) but does **not** by
itself settle the finer placement ("its own dynamic-half region" vs "threaded through the cosmology").
Contribution to ONT‑1: **constrains the keystone to the existence/layer level; rules out the 4D-record
reading.** Sets the level. (Fine placement settled in P7's Ontological Event Individuation axiom — threaded through the cosmology; see header banner.)

### ✅ Space-Time Doesn't Exist (cosmiCave 2025-09-24 / The Conversation) *(this essay's companion)*
*Saved:* `resources/conceptual-boundary-essays/space-time-doesnt-exist.md`

**Establishes (ontological core):** the sharper, first-principles statement of the same distinction —
**space-time is a four-dimensional *map* of happenings, not reality.** By definition: events are
instantaneous occurrences, instants are 3D collections of simultaneous events, space-time is the
totality of all events — a catalogue, not the stuff of the world. **What exists** is the 3D world (the
atoms and the space between them); **what happens** is the instantaneous occurrence at every point.
Three supports: (1) no empirical evidence any event "exists" as an ongoing object (would need a time
machine to observe it *now*); (2) the philosophy-of-time tense debates smuggle in events-as-existent at
the outset — the ontological distinction (events happen, not exist) was lost at the start; (3) physics
has **no variable for the existence of space-time** — relativity *describes* a 4D continuum, it is not
a theory about a 4D thing that exists and bends. Einstein's equations intact; only the language changes.

**Bears on FLAG ONT‑1 (interrogated):** same contribution as the Conversation piece but at
first-principles weight — fixes that **what exists is the evolving 3D world; the 4D manifold is its
record.** So the individuation keystone (the individuated evolving layer) is an *existence*-level object
(the layer that exists), and the manifold is the map. Reinforces ONT‑1's **level** (existence / the
layer, not the 4D record), with the added force that physics carries no variable for the manifold's own
existence — so locating the keystone "in" the 4D record would be a category error the geometry forbids.
(Fine placement settled in P7's Ontological Event Individuation axiom — threaded through the cosmology, not its own region; see header banner.)
### ✅ Albert Einstein's Wild Goose Chase (cosmiCave 2025-08-06)
*Saved:* `resources/conceptual-boundary-essays/wild-goose-chase.md`

**Establishes (ontological core):** the **objective cosmic rest frame / objective simultaneity**.
Relativity of *synchrony* is a general kinematic fact (one-to-one with the chosen rest convention),
independent of the light postulate; it does **not** entail relativity of *simultaneity*. Local
experiments cannot decide whether a true rest state exists — an underdetermined fork — and treating its
non-detection as non-existence is a **modal fallacy** (absence of evidence ≠ evidence of absence). But
the universe *did* supply the evidence: the CMB's isotropic redshift (uniform expansion to < 1 part in
100,000) is, by the standard model's own logic, confirmation of a real **cosmic time and globally
well-defined state of rest**; the dipole measures our motion through it (369.82 km/s toward Crater). So
synchrony is relative, **simultaneity is absolute** — the block-universe / superluminal-time-travel
readings dissolve once an objective "now" is taken seriously.

### ✅ Time Travel: A Five-Dimensional Trope (cosmiCave 2025-06-29)
*Saved:* `resources/conceptual-boundary-essays/five-dimensional-trope.md`

**Establishes (ontological core):** the **layer-vs-manifold structure** explicitly. Existence and
occurrence are different ontological categories: a thing that exists has nonzero duration (persists); an
event merely occurs (atemporal, a flash). Space-time = the set of all events that occur = a map/scaffold,
which does **not** itself persist. To say the 4D block *exists* is to **smuggle in a fifth dimension** —
a Newton-like absolute *meta-time* conferring being on the map (the "meta-now" under the block universe).
Strip that illusion and what remains is "a world-tube that occurs — that comes in and out of being…
everything happening, all at once, in a world that merely occurs. Not one that exists." Existence is
"what the clock measures" — being that lasts, each tick a new event.

**Bears on FLAG ONT‑1 (interrogated — this is the most direct of the Tier C essays):** it is the
explicit statement that the **individuated evolving thing is the 3D world-as-it-exists (the layer)**, and
the 4D manifold is its record of occurrences — *with the fifth dimension being exactly the illegitimate
move of granting the manifold its own existence.* This directly underwrites locating the individuation
keystone in the **evolving layer (the dynamic half)** and forbids individuating "the 4D record" as an
existent — that would be the smuggled-fifth-dimension error by name. Strongest Tier-C contribution to
ONT‑1's level-fixing; the fine placement is settled in P7 (Ontological Event Individuation axiom — threaded through the cosmology; see header banner). P1/P7 still
carry the *geometry* of the layer/substrate (incl. the legitimate 5th dimension χ of the dS

P-pass).
