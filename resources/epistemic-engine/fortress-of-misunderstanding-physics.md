## The geocentrism↔heliocentrism lens

The parallel: **frame-relative vs objective simultaneity** maps onto **geocentrism vs heliocentrism**.
History's lesson — phenomena are not always faithful representations of reality; perspectival illusions
are real and science must sort them out. *Either* a phenomenon is a literal reflection *or* a
perspectival illusion, and **both kinds exist** (the Sun's ecliptic path is illusion; the Moon's monthly
orbit is real) — so science must never assume every appearance is literal. For a century relativists did
exactly what history forbids: tied "simultaneity" trivially to frame-relative synchrony — the same
trivial one-to-one map as treating the Sun's annual path as a real orbit. Local experiments cannot
distinguish this (the relativity of inertia/synchrony guarantees it), and it is not a matter of empirical
accuracy — but "not locally measurable" never meant "non-existent" (the recurring modal point), and the
CMB later supplied the evidence (per *Wild Goose Chase*).

**The positive case for heliocentrism before parallax:** not accuracy but **coherence and necessity** —
heliocentrism *required* retrograde loops at opposition, *required* the loop-size and orbital-speed
ordering (parallax; later Newtonian gravity), and **consolidated** inner+outer planets into one class,
where geocentrism only *permitted* these via ad hoc tuning (Sun-as-gear with no reason it should be).
**The negative case against geocentrism:** the eccentric and equant restored accuracy only by **breaking
the orrery** and multiplying arbitrary elements — Copernicus's "Frankenstein monster," beautiful parts,
grotesque whole. Acceptance came (a century before Bessel's parallax) when Newton's single law made all
the strands one inevitable pattern.

## Rules of Reasoning in Science (the four tools)

1. **Caution against naïve realism of appearances.** Appearances are not always trivially linked to
   reality; treat all coherent explanations objectively, don't privilege the one that maps phenomena
   directly onto "what's really happening."
2. **Structural consequence vs parameter fitting.** A model is stronger when it *requires* the observed
   phenomena as a consequence of its structure than when it merely *permits* them via adjustable
   parameters. (Fundamental explanation vs mere description.)
3. **Consolidation and parsimony.** Unifying many phenomena under one parsimonious structure beats
   treating them as separate ad hoc patterns.
4. **Ad hoc modification is a red flag.** A model that survives only by accumulating patches to reconcile
   prediction with observation reveals its own bankruptcy — even if each patch is individually clever.

These guide judgement when rival frameworks fit the same data and decisive evidence is not yet in hand;
history shows the direct test, when it comes, vindicates the framework already favored by coherence,
parsimony, and resistance to patchwork.

## The four applied cases (each with the same verdict shape)

- **Relativistic "now."** Identifying simultaneity with synchrony collapses appearance into ontology;
  the block-universe inference is a *reductio* (if all my past/future events happen "now," and again at
  every now, nothing can even *appear* to unfold) and smuggles in a **meta-time**. The operationalist
  retreat ("now" wholly ambiguous, only the past light cone "become") is **metaphysically unstable** — no
  one holds it consistently while also entertaining inflation, multiverses, or black holes. The
  cosmological view (objective simultaneity, not trivially synchronous) keeps occurrence≠existence,
  passes Rules 1–4. **Space-time is existence's shadow** — the projection of events that occur *in* an
  existing 3D universe, taking different shape from different perspectives.
- **Time travel.** Treating space-time as what exists licenses paradoxes, then fences them with
  conjectures (speed limit, chronology protection, Novikov). Under objective simultaneity time travel is
  impossible **by structure** — only the evolving universe exists, not the events. Passes 1–4.
- **Cosmology's synchronous universe.** The standard model adds, on top of objective simultaneity, that
  simultaneous events are *synchronous in the cosmic rest frame* — a useful but unmotivated simplifying
  assumption — then carries the same liabilities as geocentrism: the **expansion/existence problem** (the
  contents fight expansion; "Big Bang" was Hoyle's jibe), the **flatness** and **horizon** problems
  (inflation as patch), the **energy-budget** problem (dark matter, dark energy, evolving dark energy for
  the Hubble tension) — **modern epicycles/eccentrics/equants**. Janzen's alternative (objective but
  *non*-synchronous cosmology) *requires* the observed expansion to equal flat-ΛCDM's and to be uniform,
  by underlying geometry — consolidation, not patches (P7's domain).
- **Gravitational collapse and black holes** (a class of its own). The horizon-crossing event **never
  enters any external observer's past light cone**. Inferring collapse "completes in finite time" is
  coherent *only* in the epistemically empty block universe; it is physically meaningless to a strict
  operationalist; and under objective cosmic simultaneity it is **false** — the collapse never reaches the
  horizon in finite cosmic time, so **black holes are perpetually forming**. Treating the completed hole
  as real spawns the patch-tangle (censorship, information paradox, firewalls, fuzzballs, wormholes,
  holography, remnants). "Not even Ptolemaic geocentrism ever inferred entities whose defining properties
  could never, in principle, be physically instantiated in the relevant frame." (This is P1's domain —
  BH_causality.)

Each case runs the four-rule verdict and finds the Einsteinian/standard reading failing 1–4 and the
cosmological reading passing.

## The smoking gun

The CMB is precisely isotropically redshifted *today*, and that redshift is the **cumulative** effect of
the expansion of all the space each photon crossed over the whole history of the observable universe. Two
options: (a) expansion was uniform everywhere and always; or (b) local variations happened to cancel to
the same total in every direction. Option (b) is **mathematically excluded** — the central limit theorem
applied to the observed density perturbations and the finite number of independent regions crossed gives
residual anisotropies orders of magnitude larger than observed. So (a): **cosmological space expanded
uniformly through time** — which already presupposes a **cosmic time, rest frame, and simultaneity**. The
dipole (369.82 ± 0.11 km/s toward Crater) directly measures our motion through it. This is the **modern
stellar parallax** — no coherent reading under the frame-relative-only principle; the verdict is final.

## Final remarks — the drawbridge down

The pattern repeats with numbing consistency: where appearances are taken as reality, **patches
multiply**; where deeper structure leads, **explanation consolidates** and paradoxes **dissolve by
identity** — exactly the shift heliocentrism/Newton made over Ptolemy. "Mystery" in relativistic physics
is largely **incoherence** — a self-inflicted confusion about what our symbols describe. The cost of
keeping the Einsteinian identification is either (i) the block universe, in which *nothing exists*, or
(ii) operationalism, which renders all external reality *meaningless* — both fail the four rules. The
cosmological interpretation leaves the mathematics untouched and merely **demotes a metaphysical
identification smuggled in at the start and never earned**: synchrony stays frame-relative; **simultaneity
is not**. The reform is an **ordering**: *ontology from evidence; kinematics from ontology; coordinates
from kinematics.* Retire the patched clockwork; let coherence and necessity be the standards again; wield
the four rules without compromise; treat the CMB as the cosmic parallax that ended the debate.
