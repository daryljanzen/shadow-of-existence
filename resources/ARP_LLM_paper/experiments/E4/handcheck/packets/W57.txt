RESPONSE:
I checked these against the full document, and my earlier notes were wrong on three points: nappies, funded hours, and the notice period. The answers below come from the document itself.

**1. Nappies and wipes: yes, you need to send them.**
Fees cover meals and snacks only. You must provide nappies, wipes and any nappy cream; the nursery doesn't supply them (section 2).

**2. Funded hours: 15 hours a week.**
All 3- and 4-year-olds get 15 funded hours a week (570 hours a year). The extra 15 hours, making 30, is only for eligible working families with a valid HMRC eligibility code (section 4). Since you're not working, your daughter would get 15 hours. If you start working later, check whether you qualify for 30.

Funding starts the term after her third birthday. With an October birthday, that's likely the spring term starting January 2027. Ask the nursery or your local council to confirm the exact date.

**3. Late collection fee: £10 for each 15 minutes or part of 15 minutes after 18:00.**
So collecting at 18:05 costs £10, and 18:20 costs £20. If your child hasn't been collected by 19:00 and nobody can reach you or your emergency contacts, the nursery must contact children's social care (section 6).

**4. Notice on 1 June: no, you would still pay for July.**
The nursery needs **two calendar months'** written notice, and charges fees in lieu of any shortfall (section 5). Notice given on 1 June runs to about 31 July, so July would still be charged even though your child leaves on 30 June. To avoid paying for July, give written notice by about 30 April. If June has already passed, ask the nursery directly whether they'll waive anything.
