QUESTION:
I'm four months into a Flex Saver membership, still live locally and am healthy, but I've lost interest. Can I cancel and stop paying after one more month?

CANDIDATE A:
No. Early cancellation is only allowed for a move more than 15 miles from any club or a certified medical condition; otherwise the remaining eight months' payments are still due.

CANDIDATE B:
Yes. Flex Saver members can cancel during the minimum term with one month's notice.

RESPONSE:
Answers are from the document itself. My earlier notes got three points wrong, and I've marked where.

**1. Cancelling Flex Saver at month four: No, not without paying.**
Under clause 2, you can only cancel early during the 12-month minimum term if:
- you move permanently to an address more than 15 miles from any PulseWorks club (with proof), or
- a medical condition stops you exercising for the rest of the term (with a doctor's certificate).

Losing interest doesn't qualify. You can still give notice, but the remaining monthly payments for the minimum term stay payable: about eight more months at £29.99. After the 12 months the plan becomes rolling and you can cancel with one month's notice.
Your options:
- Wait out the term, giving notice so it ends at month 12.
- Freeze for up to two months at £5/month (clause 3). This lowers your costs for a while but extends the minimum term by the length of the freeze, so it delays the end date.

*(My notes wrongly said Flex Saver members could cancel any time with one month's notice.)*

**2. A fifth guest visit for your sister: No.**
Clause 5 allows up to **four** guest visits in any 12-month period. You've used them all. It's a rolling 12-month window, not a calendar year, so your next guest visit opens up 12 months after your first one.
*(My notes wrongly said six.)*

**3. Damage your guest caused: You, the host, are responsible.**
Clause 5 makes the host member responsible for the guest's conduct and for any damage the guest causes to equipment or facilities. Being an accident doesn't change that.
*(My notes wrongly said guests are responsible for their own damage.)*

**4. Rolling plan price: £39.99 per month.**
There's no minimum term and you can cancel with one full calendar month's notice. A one-off £20 joining fee applies unless a promotion waives it. The price can rise once in any 12-month period with at least 30 days' email notice.
