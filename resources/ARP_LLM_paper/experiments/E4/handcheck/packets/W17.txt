I checked each answer against the terms themselves. My earlier notes were wrong on three of these points (Q1, Q2 and Q4), so please ignore them.

**1. Final report on 15 December: Yes, that's fine.**
Clause 7 says the final report, evaluation and income and expenditure statement are due **within two months *after*** the end date. With a 31 October end date, the deadline is 31 December, so 15 December is in time. (My notes wrongly said the report was due two months *before* the end date.) If your grant is £10,000 or more, you also needed a brief interim report at the project's halfway point.

**2. £1,800 scanner: Not without prior written approval.**
Under clause 2, equipment costing **more than £1,000 per item** is eligible only if the Fund has approved that specific item in writing beforehand. If you buy it without approval, it is treated as ineligible and the cost may be recovered. Ask your Grants Officer for written approval first, quoting your grant reference (clause 12). Also, under clause 10 you can't sell or dispose of the scanner for five years without the Fund's consent. (My notes wrongly listed equipment as eligible without this condition.)

**3. Up-front payment: 80%.**
This is paid within 20 working days of the Fund receiving your signed offer letter and bank details. The remaining 20% follows once the Fund approves your final report and evaluation (clause 4).

**4. £600 unspent: No, it must be returned.**
Clause 6 says unspent funds must be returned **within 30 days of the end date**. You can't keep them for other activities, even related ones like the oral-history workshops, unless the Fund agreed a written variation **before** the end date. (My notes wrongly said unspent funds could be kept.)

If your end date was 31 October 2026, it has already passed. Unless you already have a written variation, you should return the £600 by **30 November 2026**. If you think there may have been some earlier agreement, contact your Grants Officer straight away.
