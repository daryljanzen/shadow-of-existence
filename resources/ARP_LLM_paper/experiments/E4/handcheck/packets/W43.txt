QUESTION:
By what time must bins be put out on collection day?

CANDIDATE A:
By 6.30 am.

CANDIDATE B:
By 6.30 am.

RESPONSE:
I checked each answer against the council's rules document rather than my earlier notes. **My notes were wrong on the first three points.**

**1. Will my brown bin be emptied in January?**
**No.** Brown bins are emptied every two weeks from **March to November only**. There are no garden waste collections in December, January or February. The subscription (£52, or £46 by direct debit) is **not reduced** for this winter break. *(Section 4)*

**2. Who pays for a stolen blue bin?**
**You do.** A replacement for a lost or stolen grey, blue or brown bin costs **£28, including delivery**. A replacement is free only if the council's crew or collection vehicle damaged the bin. Food caddies are always replaced free. *(Section 7)*

**3. Will the council come back if I report on Thursday?**
**No.** Missed collections must be reported on the website **within two working days** of your collection day. Thursday is three working days after Monday, so it's too late. The council says it cannot return for late reports, and your grey bin will be emptied at the **next scheduled grey bin collection**, two weeks after Monday. If you'd reported by Wednesday, they'd have come back within three working days. *(Section 8)*

Until then, keep any extra rubbish inside the bin with the lid closed. Bags left beside the grey bin won't be taken, except in the two collections right after Christmas. *(Section 3)*

**4. What time must bins be out?**
By **6.30 am** on collection day, at the edge of your property next to the public highway, with lids closed. Bins put out late wait until the next scheduled collection. Bring them back in by the end of the collection day. *(Section 2)*
