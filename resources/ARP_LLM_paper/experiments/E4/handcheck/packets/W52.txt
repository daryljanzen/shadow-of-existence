QUESTION:
Do I need to send nappies and wipes in with my son?

CANDIDATE A:
No. Nappies and wipes are included in the fees.

CANDIDATE B:
Yes. Parents must supply nappies, wipes and nappy cream; the nursery does not.

RESPONSE:
I checked these against the terms themselves, and my earlier notes were wrong on three of your four questions. The answers below follow the document.

**1. Nappies and wipes: Yes, you need to send them in.**
Section 2 says fees cover meals and snacks, but "parents must provide nappies, wipes and any nappy cream for their child; we do not supply these." My notes wrongly said nappies were included.

**2. Funded hours: 15 hours a week.**
Under section 4, all 3- and 4-year-olds get 15 funded hours a week (570 hours a year). The 30-hour entitlement is only for eligible *working* families with a valid HMRC eligibility code, so it wouldn't apply while you're not working. My notes wrongly said all children get 30.
- **When it starts:** from "the term after" her third birthday. The document doesn't give term dates, but for an October birthday that would usually mean January. It's worth confirming with the nursery.
- If you start work later, you could apply for the HMRC code to claim the extra 15 hours.
- There's an optional £4 consumables charge per funded day, or you can send a packed lunch instead.

**3. Late collection fee: £10 for each 15 minutes or part of 15 minutes after 18:00.**
So arriving at 18:01 costs £10, and 18:20 costs £20. If your child hasn't been collected by 19:00 and the nursery can't reach you or your emergency contacts, they must contact children's social care.

**4. Notice on 1 June: No, you would still pay for July.**
Section 5 requires two calendar months' written notice, not one as my notes said. Fees are charged in lieu of any notice you don't give.
- Notice given on 1 June runs to about 31 July, so you'd be charged for July even though your child leaves on 30 June.
- To end fees on 30 June, give written notice by around 30 April / 1 May. If you're near that date, check exactly how the nursery counts it.
