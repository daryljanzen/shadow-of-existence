QUESTION:
I dropped my keys in the river — they weren't stolen. Will the locks be changed for free?

CANDIDATE A:
Yes. Locks are changed free of charge if you lose your keys.

CANDIDATE B:
No. Free lock changes need a police crime reference for stolen keys; otherwise it costs £65 (paid in advance) or you can use your own locksmith.

RESPONSE:
I checked these against the policy itself, and three of my earlier notes were wrong. The answers below follow the document.

**1. Emergency attendance (e.g. a gas leak)**
Someone will **attend within 4 hours** and **make it safe within 24 hours** (section 2). A follow-on repair may be needed afterwards. Emergencies **must be reported by phone** on 0345 600 2210 (section 1), not online or by email.

**2. Dripping kitchen tap, 15 working days**
**No, they're not outside the target yet.** A dripping tap is a routine repair, and the target is **20 working days** (section 2). My notes wrongly said 10. Tamar Valley has 5 more working days before it misses the target. If it isn't fixed by then, you can use the complaints procedure and, after that, the Housing Ombudsman (section 10).

**3. Safety check for a gas cooker you bought**
**No.** The annual gas safety check only covers appliances **Tamar Valley installed**, such as boilers and fires (section 3). A cooker you own is **your responsibility**. You must have it installed and serviced by a **Gas Safe registered engineer** (section 4). My notes wrongly said the landlord services all gas appliances, including cookers.

**4. Keys dropped in the river (not stolen)**
**No, it won't be free.** Free lock changes only apply if you give a **police crime reference number** showing the keys were stolen (section 5). Otherwise you have two options:
- pay **£65**, due **before** the work is done, or
- call a locksmith yourself.

My notes were wrong on this too. They said lock changes were free in all cases.
