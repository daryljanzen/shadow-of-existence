QUESTION:
Will my brown garden waste bin be emptied in January?

CANDIDATE A:
No. There are no garden waste collections in December, January or February.

CANDIDATE B:
Yes. Brown bins are emptied fortnightly all year round.

RESPONSE:
**1. No.** Brown bins are emptied every two weeks from March to November only. There are no garden waste collections in December, January or February, and the subscription isn't reduced to account for this (section 4).

**2. You do.** A replacement grey, blue or brown bin costs £28, including delivery, if the bin was lost or stolen. Replacements are free only if the council's crew or collection vehicle damaged the bin. Food caddies are always replaced free (section 7).

**3. No.** You must report a missed collection within two working days of your collection day. A report on Thursday about a Monday collection is three working days later, which is too late. The council won't make a return visit, and the bin will be emptied at your next scheduled grey bin collection. If you're still within the window, for example on Tuesday or Wednesday, report it right away. The crew would then return within three working days of your report (section 8).

**4. By 6.30 am** on your collection day. Bins must be at the edge of your property next to the public highway, with lids closed. Late bins aren't collected until the next scheduled collection (section 2).

**Correction to my earlier notes:** three things in them were wrong, and the answers above follow the document instead:
- Garden waste is **not** collected all year. There are no collections from December to February.
- Lost, stolen or otherwise damaged bins cost **£28** to replace. They're free only if the crew caused the damage.
- The deadline for reporting a missed collection is **two** working days, not five.
