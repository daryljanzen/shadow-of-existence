QUESTION:
A customer complains about what she was told on a call to the Halden Money credit desk eight months ago. Should a recording still exist?

CANDIDATE A:
No. Call recordings are only kept for 90 days.

CANDIDATE B:
Yes. Halden Money desk recordings are kept for 5 years for FCA reasons.

RESPONSE:
**Correction first:** my earlier notes had three errors that affect these answers. The document itself says:

- Unsuccessful applicants are kept for **6 months**, not 12.
- Deletion is the responsibility of each department's **Information Asset Owner**, not the IT Service Desk.
- Halden Money credit desk call recordings are kept for **5 years**, not 90 days.

The answers below follow the document.

**1. Subject access request deadline**
Halden must respond within **one calendar month**. For complex requests this can be extended by two further months. The request must also be passed to the DPO **on the day it arrives** (section 6).

**2. Marketing's old spreadsheets**
This is the job of **Marketing's Information Asset Owner (IAO)**, normally the head of Marketing (section 2).
- The IAO must make sure the department's records are reviewed and deleted when their retention period ends.
- The IT Service Desk only does technical deletion from central systems when an IAO tells it to. IT does not decide what gets deleted.
- Line managers make sure staff follow the policy, and the DPO owns the policy.
- Before deleting anything, check with Legal that none of it is under a legal hold (section 4).
- Paper copies go in the confidential waste consoles. Electronic files are deleted from live systems.

**3. Rejected candidate, nine months ago, not in the talent pool**
**No, we should not still hold her CV.** Records for unsuccessful applicants are kept for 6 months after she was told of the decision, so hers should have been deleted about three months ago. The only exception is a legal hold.
- Treat her question as a subject access request and pass it to the DPO today.
- If the CV is still held, tell her honestly when you respond, then delete it.
- Report the missed deletion to Recruitment's IAO.

**4. Halden Money credit desk call from eight months ago**
**Yes, a recording should still exist.** Calls to the Halden Money desk are kept for **5 years** because of Financial Conduct Authority requirements. The 90-day rule applies only to ordinary customer service calls.
- Her complaint file must be kept for 3 years after the complaint is closed.
- If she asks for a copy of the recording, handle that as a subject access request through the DPO.
- If the complaint might go to the Financial Ombudsman or to court, tell Legal so they can consider a legal hold.
