Market Analysis: Launching a Digital Subscription for the Calderbrook Courier
Commercial Strategy, Calderbrook Media, April 2026

1. Purpose
The Calderbrook Courier's website is free to read and funded by display advertising, which has declined by about 12% a year for three years. This paper assesses the market for a metered digital subscription, in which readers can view five articles a month free before being asked to subscribe, and recommends a price and launch plan.

2. Our audience
The website had an average of 420,000 monthly unique users (MUU) in 2025, of whom around 60% live in the Courier's circulation area. The printed Courier has 11,200 subscribers paying £18.50 a month for six-day home delivery, plus around 6,000 daily news-stand sales. Print subscribers are older on average than web readers, and most also use the website.

In our March 2026 survey of print subscribers (1,850 responses), 30% said they would cancel their print subscription and take a digital-only subscription instead if one were offered at under £6 a month.

3. Evidence from comparable titles
We obtained data, under confidentiality agreements, from four regional titles that launched metered subscriptions between 2022 and 2024.

Table 1. Comparable regional titles, second year after launch
Title | Monthly unique users | Digital subscribers | Subscribers as % of MUU | Monthly price
Title A | 360,000 | 6,800 | 1.9% | £5.99
Title B | 280,000 | 3,100 | 1.1% | £4.99
Title C | 390,000 | 9,400 | 2.4% | £7.99
Title D | 300,000 | 4,200 | 1.4% | £3.99

All four titles reported that subscriber numbers stabilised by the end of the second year, and that churn settled at between 3% and 5% a month after promotional periods ended.

4. Price
Conversion rates across the comparable titles are highest for the titles charging the lowest prices, which suggests that our audience will respond best to a low price point. We therefore propose a price of £4.99 a month, with an introductory offer of £1 for the first three months.

5. Projection
The average conversion rate of the four comparable titles is 2.0% of MUU. Applied to our 420,000 MUU, that gives about 8,400 subscribers by the end of year two. At £4.99 a month, 8,400 subscribers would generate around £503,000 a year in subscription revenue.

Table 2. Year-two projection
Item | Annual amount
Subscription revenue | £503,000
Subscription platform and payment fees | £85,000
Marketing | £60,000
Net contribution | £358,000

We expect a modest reduction in display advertising revenue as page views from non-subscribers fall when they reach the meter; the comparable titles reported falls of 5% to 8% in page views. Our display revenue was £1.1 million in 2025, so a fall of this size would cost between £55,000 and £88,000 a year, which is well covered by the projected net contribution.

6. Launch plan
- Platform selection and integration: May to July 2026.
- Soft launch with a ten-article meter: September 2026.
- Full launch with a five-article meter: November 2026.
- Review of price and meter after six months.

7. Conclusion
The evidence from comparable titles shows a viable market for a Courier digital subscription. At £4.99 a month, a price that the comparable titles' experience suggests will maximise take-up, we project around 8,400 subscribers and a net contribution of £358,000 a year, more than offsetting the expected effect on display advertising. We recommend approval to proceed with the launch plan.
