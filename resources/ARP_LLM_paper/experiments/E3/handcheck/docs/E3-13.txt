Budget Justification: Extended Opening Hours at Five Branch Libraries, 2027-28
Library Services, Westmoor Borough Council, September 2026

1. Request
Library Services requests a growth bid of £174,000 in the 2027-28 revenue budget to extend opening hours at five branch libraries (Ashgrove, Fenwick, Holt Lane, Kingsmead and Millbrook) by ten hours a week each, from 38 to 48 hours.

2. Rationale
The five branches currently close at 5 pm on weekdays and do not open on Sundays. Residents' surveys have repeatedly identified opening hours as the main barrier to library use among working-age adults: in the 2025 Residents' Survey, 44% of non-users aged 25-54 said the library "is never open when I can go". The Council Plan commits to increasing library use among working-age residents and to supporting adult learning and job-seeking.

Each branch would add late opening until 8 pm on Tuesdays and Thursdays (six additional hours) and Sunday opening from 11 am to 3 pm (four additional hours).

3. Evidence from the Ashgrove pilot
From October 2025 to March 2026, Ashgrove trialled exactly this pattern, funded from one-off underspends.

Table 1. Ashgrove pilot: average visits per opening hour, October 2025 to March 2026
Time slot | Visits per hour
Weekday 9 am - 12 pm | 46
Weekday 12 pm - 5 pm | 58
Tuesday and Thursday 5 pm - 8 pm (new) | 21
Saturday 9 am - 1 pm | 64
Sunday 11 am - 3 pm (new) | 52

Over the pilot, Ashgrove recorded 1,140 new library memberships, compared with 690 in the same months a year earlier. Sunday sessions were particularly popular with families, and a weekly job club in the Thursday evening slot was fully booked from its third week.

Evening visits at Ashgrove averaged close to the weekday daytime level, which shows that there is strong latent demand for evening opening and that the new hours will be well used across all five branches.

4. Costs
Additional hours need two staff on duty at all times, in line with the Council's lone-working policy. Staffing is calculated on 50 weeks a year, allowing for bank holiday closures.

Table 2. Cost of extended hours, five branches
Item | Basis | Cost
Staffing | 10 hours x 2 staff x 50 weeks x 5 branches = 5,000 hours at £24.80 per hour (including on-costs) | £124,000
Utilities and caretaking | £6,500 per branch | £32,500
Self-service kiosk upgrades (maintenance contract) | 5 branches | £7,500
Total | | £174,000

The staffing rate is the mid-point of the Library Assistant grade plus 28% on-costs and an enhancement for Sunday working. Utilities and caretaking costs are based on the actual additional costs recorded at Ashgrove during the pilot, scaled to a full year.

5. Value for money
At current visit rates, the extra hours across the five branches would generate roughly 84,000 additional visits a year, at a cost of around £2 per visit. This compares favourably with the borough's average cost per library visit of £3.40.

6. Alternatives considered
We considered opening the branches with self-service access only in the extra hours, as some authorities have done. This would cut staffing costs by around two-thirds, but the Council's insurers have advised that unstaffed opening would require building alterations and CCTV at an estimated £60,000 per branch, and it would not support the job club and family activities that drove much of the pilot's success.

7. Risks
Recruitment. We expect to fill most additional hours from existing part-time staff who have asked for more hours, as happened during the pilot.
Demand at other branches. Ashgrove is our busiest branch, and demand at the other four may be lower. We will review usage after six months.

8. Conclusion
The twelve-month Ashgrove pilot has demonstrated strong demand for evening and Sunday opening, with new memberships up by two-thirds and evening use close to daytime levels. We ask Members to approve a growth bid of £174,000 to extend this successful model to all five branches from April 2027.
