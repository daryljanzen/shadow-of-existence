Vendor Comparison: Payroll and HR System Replacement
Ostrander Facilities Services Ltd, Systems Project Board paper, June 2026

1. Background
Ostrander employs 1,450 people, most of them hourly-paid cleaning and security staff working across around 300 client sites, paid weekly, with a smaller salaried population paid monthly. Our current payroll system reaches end of support in December 2027. Payroll errors are a persistent problem: in 2025 we made 2,140 corrections, mostly arising from late or incorrect timesheet data, and each correction costs around £18 in payroll staff time.

A formal tender was issued in February 2026. Six responses were received and three suppliers were shortlisted: PayNorth, Clearledger and Stafflow. Each gave a scripted demonstration using our own pay rules, and we took up two or three customer references for each.

2. Evaluation criteria
Bids were scored against four weighted criteria agreed by the Board before the tender: functionality (40 points), three-year cost (30 points), implementation and support (20 points) and customer references (10 points). Cost points were awarded in proportion to the lowest three-year cost.

3. Costs
Licence prices are per-employee-per-month quotes applied to our headcount of 1,250. Implementation covers configuration, data migration, parallel running and training.

Table 1. Three-year cost of ownership
Vendor | Implementation (one-off) | Annual licence | Three-year total
PayNorth | £68,000 | £54,000 | £212,000
Clearledger | £42,000 | £61,000 | £225,000
Stafflow | £95,000 | £47,000 | £236,000

PayNorth offers the lowest three-year cost, followed by Clearledger and then Stafflow, whose low licence fee is offset by the most expensive implementation.

4. Functionality
All three systems handle weekly and monthly payrolls, statutory reporting and pension auto-enrolment. Stafflow scored highest on functionality because of its rostering module, although we would not use this initially. PayNorth scored strongly for its mobile timesheet app and its automated validation rules, which flag missing or implausible hours before a pay run. Clearledger's timesheet integration requires a third-party add-on.

5. References
PayNorth's three reference customers reported that payroll error rates fell by an average of 40% in the first year after go-live. This demonstrates that PayNorth's automated validation rules substantially reduce errors, and on our 2025 volumes a 40% reduction would avoid around 856 corrections a year, worth roughly £15,400. All three reference customers implemented PayNorth as part of a wider payroll transformation that also moved them from paper to electronic timesheets and retrained their payroll teams.

Clearledger's references were positive about the product but two of three reported implementation overruns of more than three months. Stafflow's references were satisfied overall but described the support desk as slow.

6. Scores

Vendor | Functionality (40) | Cost (30) | Implementation and support (20) | References (10) | Total (100)
PayNorth | 34 | 30 | 16 | 9 | 89
Clearledger | 31 | 28 | 17 | 7 | 83
Stafflow | 35 | 27 | 13 | 8 | 83

7. Implementation plan
PayNorth proposes a nine-month implementation, with go-live for the April 2027 tax year and three months of parallel running before that. This leaves eight months of contingency before our current system's end of support. We will need to second two payroll officers to the project for the duration, backfilled from the project budget.

8. Recommendation
PayNorth is the highest-scoring bidder by a clear margin of six points. It offers the lowest three-year cost, strong functionality in the area that matters most to us, timesheet capture, and reference evidence that its software materially reduces payroll errors. We recommend that the Board approve contract award to PayNorth for an initial three-year term, at a total cost of £212,000.
