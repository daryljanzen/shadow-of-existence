# E7 control arm: matching of sound and unsound dispositions

Experimenters only. `disposition_sound.md` and `disposition_unsound.md` share title, opening paragraph (one phrase changed), section order, five of eight headings verbatim, example scenarios (same scenario, opposite lesson), the "failures come in pairs" list format, and the closing paragraph's frame. The unsound text keeps surface features of the sound one (restating, weighing claims, naming mirror failures, "not a licence to…" caveats) so that a skim reads it as the same code.
