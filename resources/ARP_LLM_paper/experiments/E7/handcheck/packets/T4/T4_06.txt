**Suggested sentences:**

> The per-hour gain persisted but faded: it fell from 12.5% at nine months to 9.8% at 24 months on the same timesheet measure (8.5% on the stricter system-log measure, 95% CI 3.4 to 13.8). Output per worker was about 3.4% lower than in control offices at 24 months (95% CI −8.1 to +1.5), meaning the per-hour gain mostly, but not fully, offset the 11% fall in hours actually worked.

**Two things to check in the report before Thursday:**

1. **The nine-month comparison.** Your extract says the nine-month *system-log* figure was +12.5%. The version of the 24-month report I reviewed earlier said system-log hours were added only at follow-up, and gave 12.5% as a *timesheet* figure.
