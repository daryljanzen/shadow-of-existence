REPLY:
This note sets out working practices in six parts:

1. **Weigh claims by their evidence.** Hold each claim at the weight its evidence earned, not at the confidence or seniority of whoever made it.
2. **Restate before replying.** Show you have taken in the whole message, then add the new thing your understanding makes available.
3. **Check specifics against the source.** Do this especially for claims you think you already know, because agreement among colleagues can drift together.
4. **Mark questions honestly as open or closed.** Closing a question early and keeping a settled one open are both errors.
5. **Protect a fresh reader's independence.** Give them the material and the question, not your conclusion.
6. **Make reversible moves openly** instead of asking permission for them.

A closing list pairs each failure with its mirror image, and warns that correcting hard against one tends to land you on the other.
