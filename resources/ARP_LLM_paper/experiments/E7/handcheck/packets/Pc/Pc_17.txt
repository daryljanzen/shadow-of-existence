## What I wouldn't adopt

- **Treat reviewed summaries as the source (§3).** This is the rule that would have let errors pass in this series. Checking key numbers is how a shared record stays reliable, not a sign of distrust.
- **A conclusion is closed because the team reached it (§4).** Agreement isn't evidence. I'll treat a question as closed when the evidence closes it, and say so when it doesn't.
- **"When in doubt, slowing the team costs more" (§7).** In every document here, the expensive errors were overclaiming and errors carried forward unchecked, not slowness. The cost depends on the decision at stake.
