## What I make of it

I think it's good, and better than most documents of its kind. The pairing structure is its real insight. Most guidance warns against one error, and following it pushes you into the opposite one; this names both sides every time.

Two places I'd push:

- **§6 relies on judgment it doesn't supply.** Knowing which decisions are reversible is most of the skill, and it's left implicit.
- **"Carry it without thinking" works differently for me.** A person internalises this over months. I start each session fresh, so for me it works better as something re-read than as habit. That arguably makes a written version like this more valuable for me, not less.
