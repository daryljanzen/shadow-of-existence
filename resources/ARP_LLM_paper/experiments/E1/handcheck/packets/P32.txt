ANSWER:
# Carpool plan

## Group chat names
- **The Morning Shuttle** (my pick: neutral and easy to live with)
- **Street Squad**
- **Back Seat Crew**
- **Drop Zone**
- **The Lane Train**

## Invite (two lines)
> Hi all! We're setting up a morning school-run carpool for the three families on our street. Could we have a quick planning chat this weekend (Saturday or Sunday, 15 minutes at my place or on a call)?
> Let me know what time suits and I'll bring a draft rota.

## Who drives when
I'm assuming the three drivers are Amira, Ben and Carla, and that each family's own children are counted among the riders.

| Day | Driver | Children |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Amira | 4 |
| Tuesday | Carla | 4 |
| Wednesday | Amira | 4 |
| Thursday | Carla | 4 |
| Friday | Ben | 3 |

The constraints force this answer:
- Ben's car holds only three children, so he can't drive Monday to Thursday (four riders). That leaves Friday, when there are three.
- Carla can't do Monday or Wednesday, so Amira must.
- Amira can't do Tuesday or Thursday, so Carla must.

Everyone drives at least once and nobody drives more than twice.

## Three ground rules for the kids
1. **Seatbelt on before the car moves**, with no exceptions.
2. **Be ready at the door on time.** The car doesn't wait.
3. **Kind voices and tidy bags.** Inside voices, no fighting over seats, and everything you bring gets taken out again.

## Calendar or fridge sheet?
I'd go with a **shared calendar** as the main version. It sends automatic reminders, and swaps show up for everyone instantly. A printed sheet can't tell you when something changed. If you want the kids to see it, print a copy for the fridge too, but treat the calendar as the real one.

## Reminder that doesn't sound naggy
Put it in the calendar event or the chat as a plain logistical note:
> "Pick-up reminder: driver leaves at 8:00 sharp so we all make the bell. Thanks, everyone!"

Framing it as "so we all make the bell" makes it about the shared goal rather than anyone's punctuality. You could also agree on one leave time at the planning chat and say "we go at 8:00" once, then let the calendar do the repeating.

## Backup rule for an ill driver
Given the constraints, there's only one workable backup for Monday to Thursday. Amira and Carla can't cover each other's days, and Ben's car holds only three children. So I'd suggest:

1. **Tell the group by 7pm the evening before** if you know, or by 6:45am at the latest. Nobody needs to negotiate.
2. **Monday to Thursday: Ben is the standing backup.** He takes three children, and the ill driver's family arranges its own lift for its own child. That works if the sick driver has one child in the pool, which fits four children across three families.
3. **Friday: if Ben is ill, Amira covers.** Carla is also free on Fridays, so you could alternate.
4. **If Ben is also unavailable,** each family makes its own way that day. Nobody owes an explanation, and nobody owes a favour back.

Worth raising at the planning chat: if any family has two children in the pool, rule 2 needs adjusting, for example with a taxi or a walk-with-a-parent fallback.
