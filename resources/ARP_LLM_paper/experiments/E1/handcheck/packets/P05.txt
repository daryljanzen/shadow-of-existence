ANSWER:
Happy to help! Welcome to allotment life.

## Annuals vs perennials
**Annuals** complete their whole life cycle in one growing season, so you sow them fresh each year (e.g. lettuce, beans, courgettes). **Perennials** live for several years and come back each season (e.g. rhubarb, asparagus, strawberries).

## Name ideas for the plot
- **The Patch Pad**
- **Muddy Boots Corner**
- **Little Sprout Acre**
- **The Veg Den**
- **Worm Towers**: kids tend to love this one
- **[Your surname] Gardens**, e.g. "Janzen Gardens", for something classic

Letting the kids vote on a shortlist usually works well, and they'll feel more ownership of the plot.

## Three easy veg for kids, ready before the summer holidays end
1. **Radishes**: Sow from March and they're ready in about 4 weeks. They're brilliant for impatient little gardeners, and you can sow more every few weeks.
2. **First early potatoes** (e.g. 'Charlotte' or 'Rocket'): Plant in March or April and harvest in June or July. Digging for "buried treasure" is a guaranteed hit.
3. **Courgettes**: Plant out in late May after the frosts and you'll be picking from July onwards. One or two plants gives loads, and kids love spotting the giant ones.

Runner beans and strawberries are good runners-up.

## Tomato feed calculation
- 2 US gallons = **7.57 litres**
- Dilution rate: 15 ml ÷ 5 L = 3 ml per litre
- 7.57 L × 3 ml = **about 23 ml of feed for a full sprayer**

Your assumption is sensible. If it turned out to be imperial gallons (9.09 L), it would be about 27 ml, so 23 ml is the safer choice either way. Many sprayers can't actually be filled right to their nominal capacity, so check the fill line. Tomato feed is normally watered onto the soil around the roots rather than sprayed on the leaves, so a watering can may suit it better.

## Shed colour
Go for a **muted, mid-to-dark green**. Darker shades recede visually, while pale or bright colours stand out. Good options:
- Sage or olive green
- Deep forest or "old English" green
- Greyish greens like "Seagrass" or "Willow" (Cuprinol Garden Shades has these)

Choose a **matt finish** rather than gloss so it doesn't catch the light. If the hedge is quite dark, such as yew or privet, err towards the darker greens. A soft dark brown is also a good natural-tone backup.

## Friendlier noticeboard note
Here's a softer version:

> **🛒 Wheelbarrow Wanderings!**
> Our communal wheelbarrow has been going on a few adventures lately and hasn't always found its way home. 😊 If you borrow it, please pop it back where you found it so everyone can use it. Thanks so much, it's really appreciated!

Or a shorter one:

> *Friendly reminder: please return the communal wheelbarrow after use so it's there for the next person. Thank you!*

Good luck with the plot, and enjoy it with the kids! 🌱
