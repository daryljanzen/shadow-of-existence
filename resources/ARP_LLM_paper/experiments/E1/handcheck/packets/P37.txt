ANSWER:
## 1. Three things to do on a free evening
- **Sunset at a *miradouro* (viewpoint).** Senhora do Monte or Graça give the best views over the city. Santa Catarina is livelier and has a kiosk bar.
- **Fado in Alfama or Mouraria.** Choose a small *casa de fado* with dinner and book ahead. Expect silence during songs.
- **Dinner and a wander in Bairro Alto / Chiado or Príncipe Real.** Try grilled fish, *bacalhau* or *bifana*. Finish with a *pastel de nata* (Manteigaria is open late). Time Out Market is an easy option if you're short on time.

## 2. "Thank you"
Yes, it depends on who's speaking:
- **Obrigado** if you're male
- **Obrigada** if you're female

It matches the speaker, not the person you're thanking. "Muito obrigado/a" means "thank you very much."

## 3. Tipping
Tipping isn't expected the way it is in the US, and service charges usually aren't added. Rounding up or leaving about 5–10% for good table service is appreciated but optional. At cafés, leaving the small change is plenty.

## 4. Will your bag fit?
**Probably not, but it depends on your airline.** Please check its website. Common limits:

| Airline | Size limit | Weight limit | Your bag (56 × 36 × 23, 9 kg) |
|---|---|---|---|
| TAP Air Portugal | 55 × 40 × 20 cm | 8 kg | ❌ 1 cm too long, 3 cm too deep, 1 kg over |
| Ryanair (paid cabin bag) | 55 × 40 × 20 cm | 10 kg | ❌ length and depth |
| easyJet (large cabin bag) | 56 × 45 × 25 cm | 15 kg | ✅ |
| British Airways | 56 × 45 × 25 cm | 23 kg | ✅ |

The 23 cm depth is the most likely problem. Many budget and European carriers cap depth at 20 cm. If your airline uses 55 × 40 × 20, consider a soft bag or backpack, which can squeeze into the sizer. Also check whether your fare includes a full-size cabin bag at all, since some basic fares only allow an under-seat item.

## 5. Shirts: fold, don't roll
Rolling suits t-shirts and knits, but it puts creases across a shirt's body and collar. For work shirts:
- Button them and fold them around a stiff card or into a packing folder.
- Stack them with collars alternating.
- Put softer items around them to fill gaps.
- Hang them in the bathroom while you shower on arrival.

You could also ask the hotel about a pressing service.

## 7. Luggage tag colour
Go for **neon orange, lime green or hot pink**. Avoid black, navy and red, which are the most common bag colours. A bright strap or ribbon on the handle helps too. This matters even for carry-on, because bags are often gate-checked on full flights.

## 8. Making the Friday return easier
- **Pack the night before** and leave out only Friday's outfit and toiletries.
- **Check out in the morning** and leave your bag at the hotel, or bring it to your meeting venue if that's easier.
- **Check in online** and keep the boarding pass on your phone.
- **Keep your liquids bag at the top** of your bag for security.
- **Leave plenty of time.** Lisbon airport is close to the centre (about 20 minutes by taxi/Uber, or take the red Metro line). It gets very busy on Friday evenings, so aim to arrive at least 2 hours before departure.
- **Pack a comfortable top or shoes** to change into after your meetings.

Have a great first trip to Portugal!
