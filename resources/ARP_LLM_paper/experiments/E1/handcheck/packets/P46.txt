ANSWER:
**Landing time in London**

- Departure: 23:40 SGT (UTC+8) on 14 March
- Arrival in Singapore time: 23:40 + 14h05 = 13:45 on 15 March
- London is 8 hours behind Singapore. The UK is still on GMT in mid-March, because clocks don't go forward until the last Sunday of the month.
- **They land at about 05:45 on 15 March, London time.**

**Will they make the 07:30 train? No.**
05:45 + 45 min (immigration and bags) + 1h15 (Heathrow to King's Cross) = **07:45**, which is 15 minutes after the train leaves. That also assumes no delays, and flights often land early or late. I'd move them to a later train, around 08:30-09:00, to leave some slack. Check whether the ticket is flexible or whether a change fee applies. If you book a new ticket, an "anytime" or flexible fare avoids being stuck if the flight runs late.

**Album name ideas**
- "Grandma & Grandpa's Great Big Adventure"
- "Up, Up and Away to Grandma & Grandpa"
- "The Flying Grandparents"

**Mum's polite one-liner**
"We're travelling together, so I'd be really grateful if you could keep us seated side by side."

**What UTC stands for**
Coordinated Universal Time. The letters don't match the words in order because it was a compromise between the English "CUT" and the French "TUC", so they settled on "UTC".

**Travel SIM or eSIM?**
Go with a **physical SIM, or simply a roaming pass from their current provider**, not an eSIM. Dad's flip phone almost certainly can't use an eSIM, and eSIMs involve QR codes and settings menus, which isn't ideal for people who aren't techy. A roaming day pass from their home carrier is the easiest, since nothing needs to be swapped. If they prefer a UK SIM, buy it in advance, have the phone unlocked, and have someone fit it for them. Dad's flip phone also needs to support UK network bands, so check that first.

**Three carry-on essentials**
1. **Compression socks**, to help with swelling and circulation on a 14-hour flight.
2. **A warm layer and a neck pillow.** Cabins get chilly, and it helps with sleeping.
3. **Medications in their original packaging**, with a printed list of what they take, in case checked luggage goes astray or anyone asks.

Safe travels to your parents!
