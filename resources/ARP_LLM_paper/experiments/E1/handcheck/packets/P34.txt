ANSWER:
Here's everything from your list.

## 1. What "Incoterms" are

Incoterms are a set of standard international trade rules that say who pays for what and who is responsible for the goods at each stage of a delivery. A three-letter code on the paperwork, such as EXW, FCA or DAP, tells everyone where responsibility and risk pass from the seller to the buyer.

## 2. A better name for "Stuff to send"

**"Outbound Shipments"**

If you want it sorted further, add subfolders such as `To Book`, `Booked – Awaiting Collection` and `Sent`.

## 3. Stretch wrap or strapping for mixed boxes?

**Use stretch wrap.** Mixed boxes come in different sizes, and wrap holds them all together as one block. Strapping works best on uniform, rigid or very heavy loads. If the pallet is heavy or tall, use both: wrap first, then strap over the top.

## 4. Shipping cost

**I can't give you the final figure yet, because I need the actual weight of each pallet.**

The volumetric weight is:

- Per pallet: 120 × 80 × 150 = 1,440,000 ÷ 6,000 = **240 kg**
- Three pallets: **720 kg**

The carrier charges whichever weight is higher:

- **If each pallet weighs 240 kg or less**, the cost is 720 kg × £0.45 = **£324.00**
- **If any pallet weighs more than 240 kg**, use its actual weight instead. Multiply the total by £0.45.

**£324 is the lowest the shipment can cost.** Pallets this size often weigh more than 240 kg, so weigh them or check the packing list before quoting the customer.

## 6. Three checks before a pallet leaves

1. **Labels and paperwork match.** The delivery address, the pallet count ("1 of 3", etc.) and the delivery note should all agree with the order.
2. **The load is secure.** Boxes should not overhang the pallet edges, the wrap should be tight, and the heaviest items should be at the bottom.
3. **The pallet itself is sound.** Look for broken boards or protruding nails. Make sure the weight and height are within the carrier's limits.

Since this is one of your oldest accounts, it's worth taking a photo of each finished pallet before collection. If there's a damage dispute later, the photos show how it left the warehouse.
