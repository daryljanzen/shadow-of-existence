ANSWER:
Your £8,000 request is over the funder's limit, so please fix that before submitting.

**Funding check**
- 50% of £14,500 is **£7,250**.
- The rule is up to 50% *and* a maximum of £8,000, so the lower figure applies: the most you can request is **£7,250**.
- Your match funding would then need to be at least **£7,250**.

Revise the request to £7,250 and adjust the budget table and any totals to match. If you want to ask for more, the only way is a higher eligible total cost, for example by including costs you've left out. Check the guidance on whether volunteer time or in-kind contributions count before relying on that.

**Match funding**
Match funding is the part of the project cost covered by sources other than this funder, such as your own fundraising, other grants, local council or business donations, or sometimes in-kind contributions like donated materials or volunteer time. Funders usually want to know how much you have, how much is confirmed versus pending, and where it comes from. Check whether this funder accepts in-kind support or only cash.

**Project title options**
- Growing Together: Accessible Community Garden Expansion
- [Garden Name]: Raised Beds, Paths and People
- Growing Together for All: Opening Up [Garden Name]

The first is probably the clearest. Swap in your garden's name if it has one.

**Tightened summary sentence**
"Our project will bring people of all ages and backgrounds together in a shared local space."

**Three realistic outcome measures for after a year**
1. **Participation:** the number of individual people and regular weekly attendees, using a simple sign-in sheet or register.
2. **Diversity of reach:** the number of participants from target groups (for example older people, families, or people with mobility needs), recorded via a short optional monitoring form.
3. **Facilities and activity delivered:** the number of raised beds and metres of accessible path installed, plus the number of sessions or events run by the coordinator.

If you have capacity, add a short participant survey (such as "I feel more connected to my neighbours") as a bonus measure, but those three are easy to track.

**Supporting letters**
I can't see the funder's guidance, so check whether it specifies a format. If it doesn't, the safest choice is a **signed letter on the partner's letterhead, sent as a PDF**. It looks more credible than a plain email and is easy to attach. An email is usually only acceptable if the guidance says so. Each letter should be dated and say what the partner will contribute or why they support the project.

**"About us" for the cover page**
"We are a volunteer-run group of around thirty people who have been growing, learning and sharing together in our local community garden since 2019. Our mission is to create a welcoming green space where neighbours of all ages and backgrounds can meet, grow food and look after their shared environment."

Adjust the mission line if it doesn't match how you describe yourselves. Good luck with the deadline!
