**Friendlier delivery text**

Check the length once you've filled in the real name, company and time, to stay under 160.

**Three things the driver should check before handing over**
1. **Finish and surface:** no new scratches, chips, marks or damage from transit, and photograph anything he finds.
2. **Structure and moving parts:** it's stable and not wobbly, and drawers, doors and hinges work.
3. **Completeness:** it matches the order, with all the parts, hardware, keys and fittings.

**Paper or emailed delivery note?**
Email by default. It can't be lost, it's easy to search later, and it can carry photos and a signature. Offer paper only to customers without email or who ask for it. Businesses like the shop and office may want a copy either way.

**Proof of delivery (for the part-timer)**
Proof of delivery is a record, such as a signature, photo or timestamped note, showing that the item arrived at the right place in good condition and was accepted by the customer.
