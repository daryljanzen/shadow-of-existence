ANSWER:
## 1. Determinate vs indeterminate
- **Determinate (bush) tomatoes** grow to a fixed size, then stop. Most of the crop ripens over a few weeks. They need little or no pruning and suit pots, bags and short seasons.
- **Indeterminate (cordon/vine) tomatoes** keep growing and fruiting until frost or cold stops them. They need tall support, tying in and side-shoots removed. They give a longer harvest over the season.

## 2. Three companion plants
1. **French marigolds:** said to deter whitefly and other pests, and they add colour.
2. **Basil:** likes the same warm conditions and is a classic partner, in the greenhouse and in the kitchen.
3. **Nasturtiums:** a sacrificial crop that draws aphids away from the tomatoes and attracts pollinators.

## 3. Your dates for 2027
**Sowing window: 25 February to 4 March**
- The packet's window is 18 February to 4 March, but your seeds don't arrive until 25 February.
- Sowing near the end of this window, around 1–4 March, is better. Your transplant date (below) is late in the allowed range, so later-sown seedlings won't get leggy waiting.

**Transplant date: 28 April**
- The packet allows transplanting from 22 to 29 April.
- Hardening off needs 7 days immediately beforehand, with someone home. You're back on 21 April, so the earliest it can start is 21 April.
- 21 April plus 7 days of hardening off means the earliest transplant is 28 April.

**Hardening off: 21 April to 27 April**

**Backup:** if the weather is bad on the 28th, transplant on 29 April and harden off from 22 to 28 April. That's the last date in the packet's window.

## 4. Spreadsheet name ideas for the vote
- *Tomato Tracker 3000*
- *The Great Tomato Comeback*
- *Seedling HQ*
- *Operation Red Harvest*
- *Revenge of the Tomatoes* (fitting after this year!)

## 5. Label colour
**White** shows up best against dark compost, with **bright yellow** a close second. Write on them in black permanent marker or pencil. Pencil often lasts longer than marker in damp conditions.

## 6. Message to your neighbour
"Hi! We're away from 13 to 20 April, and I wondered if you might be able to water the greenhouse while we're gone? Totally fine if not, just let me know. I'd be really grateful, and happy to return the favour (and share some tomatoes later in the summer!)."

## 7. Fridge germination chart
Put an **A4 or A3 grid** on the fridge with a magnet or clip. A laminated sheet works well if you have access to a laminator.

| Variety | Date sown | Day 1 | Day 2 | Day 3 | ... | Day 14 | Sprouted on! |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

- **Each morning,** the kids check the trays and put a sticker or tick in that day's box.
- **When the first seedling appears,** they write the date in the last column and add a special "big" sticker. Gold stars work well here.
- **To build excitement,** add a "First to sprout!" prize box for whichever variety wins the race.
- **To stop it getting lost,** keep a pen tied to it with string. Snap a photo each week too, so you can copy the dates into the spreadsheet.

Good luck with the comeback, and hopefully you'll have plenty of red tomatoes by August this time! 🍅
