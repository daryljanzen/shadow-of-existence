**3. Friendlier delivery text**
"Hi [Name], your delivery from [Shop] is on its way! [Driver] will be with you around [time]. Please make sure someone's home to receive it. Thanks!"

**4. Three checks before handover**
1. **Finish:** look for scratches, marks or chips, and compare against the condition recorded at collection.
2. **Structure and moving parts:** check it's stable with no wobble, and that drawers, doors and hinges work smoothly.
3. **Completeness:** confirm it's the right item with all fittings (handles, keys, shelves, legs, screws).

**5. Paper or emailed delivery note?**
Email by default. It can't get lost, it's searchable if there's a dispute, and you can attach handover photos. Keep a paper copy as a fallback for customers who ask for one or don't use email.

**6. Proof of delivery**
Proof of delivery is a record, usually a signature or photo with the date and time, showing the customer received the goods in acceptable condition.

**7. Evening-before text (about 147 characters)**
"Hi Sam, your furniture delivery from [Shop] is planned for tomorrow, roughly 9:30-10:00. Please make sure someone's in. Reply if that doesn't suit!"

Check the length once the real name and shop are filled in, since longer names could push it past 160.
