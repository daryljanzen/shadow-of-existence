# Did Einstein Misconstrue Relativity's Real Meaning?

**Author:** Daryl Janzen. **Source:** cosmiCave.org, 2025-10-01. **Tier:** C (conceptual
geometric-boundary constraint). **Saved:** r269 gathering (fetched at source, clean transcription).

---

Einstein's relativity is confirmed from the everyday to the cosmic — GPS (10 km/day drift without it), atomic-clock geodesy, gravitational lensing, gravitational waves measured to 21 decimal places. And yet: did the man who derived it fail to understand its *true meaning*? "I'm convinced he did."

## The basics, by a game of catch

Playing catch with a friend a fixed distance away, throwing at constant speed:
- **Scenario A:** both stand still; the ball is always airborne the same time.
- **Scenario B:** your friend walks toward you as you throw; they intercept it sooner.
- **Scenario C:** after you throw, *both* of you walk (you backward, they forward), keeping constant separation; the ball is caught at the same place as in B.

Two questions decide how relativity is interpreted: (1) In C, does the ball take the same time as in A, or as in B? (2) If your separation stays perfectly constant and you're not accelerated, can you be said to be moving at all? Most people say C matches B, and that in C you are both moving. **Einstein's interpretation answers "No" to (2), and so makes C match A.** (The relativistic corrections — time dilation, length contraction, velocity addition — are vanishingly small here and don't depend on photons, so they can be set aside; the point is interpretive.)

## The essence — and the ambiguity of "now"

The local physics is independent of what you define as "at rest." Define the Earth at rest: in C the ball takes the same time as in B. Define *yourself* at rest (the Earth scrolls past like a treadmill): in C the ball takes the same time as in A. Both descriptions are equally valid by local relativity — so motion is commonly taken as purely relative. Adding a second friend on the opposite side and throwing two balls simultaneously: from the Earth-at-rest frame the balls arrive at different times in C; from the you-at-rest frame they arrive together. This is the **relativity of simultaneity** — whether distant events are "simultaneous" is treated as a matter of arbitrary frame choice.

## The philosophical fork

Either (a) motion, space, and "now" are all relative with no objective fact of simultaneity; **or** (b) relativity is fully consistent with a *true* state of rest, a true "space," and an objective "now" — relativity only says a valid description *can* be given from any frame. Under (b) there is a well-defined set of events happening now in Andromeda, independent of your motion toward or away from it. The fork comes down to whether we *insist*, with Einstein, that the most objective reading of C is "you are at rest, the balls caught simultaneously." **But what if there actually is a true state of rest in our universe?**

## Cosmology's absolute time

Hubble's Law, read in the standard model, is not Doppler motion but uniform expansion of a 3D space (the inflating-balloon picture). Its redshift–distance relation is **isotropic** — the same in every direction. Cosmologists hypothesized space is uniform and uniformly expanding; initially expected to hold only on the largest scales (the universe is lumpy, so non-uniform expansion was expected). Yet the evidence — above all the CMB — shows expansion has been **precisely uniform**: the primordial (Sachs–Wolfe) anisotropy survives at only 1 part in 100,000, and even that is matter-distribution gravitational redshift, not anisotropic expansion. So any anisotropy in the expansion of space itself must be **< 1 part in 100,000 in all directions**.

Therefore reality consists of an objective, continually expanding "space" that defines an **absolute state of rest and simultaneity**; and the CMB dipole measures our velocity through it: **369.82 ± 0.11 km/s toward Crater**. "The universe has not only revealed that there *is* a true state of rest, but has let us measure our exact velocity through it." Einstein's equations remain flawless — but his preferred *interpretation* must shift to align with the evidence: there really is a cosmic rest frame, and we move through it.
