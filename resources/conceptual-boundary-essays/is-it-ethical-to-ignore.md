# Is it Ethical and Intellectually Responsible When Academics Ignore or Suppress Challenges to Their Preferred Frameworks?

**Author:** Daryl Janzen. **Source:** cosmiCave.org, 2026-02-16. **Tier:** C (conceptual
geometric-boundary constraint) — also the **ethics** argument and the capstone that maps the four-essay
arc. **Saved:** r269 gathering (fetched at source, clean transcription).

---

Writing for the public about time is an exercise of **authority**, and authority carries an obligation:
not to pass conceptual errors off as inevitable conclusions, nor philosophical preferences as
scientifically mandated facts. For over a century the standard rhetoric — "the passage of time is
incoherent," "reality is a fixed 4D block," "experience is an illusion" — has done exactly that, resting
on **category errors that are correctable** and on **interpretations of relativity that are optional at
best, empirically falsified at worst**. When a clear demonstration of those errors is offered — grounded
in linguistic analysis, ontological precision, historical diagnosis, and empirical science —
**intellectual responsibility requires engagement**. To ignore the corrective and keep repeating the old
story is not neutrality; it is the perpetuation of confusion, "an agenda surviving through willful
ignorance." For experts who shape public understanding, accuracy is not optional; challenges to validity
must not be swept aside. (This is the **PST ethics argument** named in the backbone — paired with
*Einstein's Cosmological Considerations* as its historiographic foundation.)

## The challenge, as one interlocking argument (the four-essay arc)

A meta-point runs throughout: a "psychological projection" or relativistic "no-absolute-now" story only
has bite **if one has already made specific ontological and interpretive choices**. The essays identify
those choices, show most are category mistakes, then use cosmology to adjudicate what the physics allows.

1. **The Linguistic–Ontological Diagnostic** (*What Exactly Is Space-Time?*): diagnoses the foundational
   confusion — conflating *existence* with *occurrence* — and shows it smuggles a hidden extra temporal
   dimension into the block-universe picture.
2. **The Ontological Reconstruction** (*Space-Time Doesn't Exist*): "space-time exists" is not merely
   unsupported but **impossible as a basic concept** — events are occurrences, not existents; reifying
   space-time misapplies the verb "exist" and corrupts both physics and philosophy of time.
3. **The Historical Forensics and Conceptual Cleanup** (*Physicists and Philosophers Have Long
   Struggled*): traces the 2,500-year category error (Heraclitus → Parmenides → Zeno → modern
   eternalism); corrected, Augustine's paradox dissolves, time-travel paradoxes vanish, and cosmology
   gives the resolution — space-time a *model of happenings*, not an existent.
4. **The Relativistic–Cosmological Adjudication** (*Did Einstein Misconstrue Relativity?*): relativity
   has an interpretive fork — Einstein's option ossifies the ancient category error; the cosmologically
   coherent alternative is fully consistent with the equations. Cosmology resolves the fork with decisive
   empirical evidence for an **objective cosmic rest frame, objective cosmic simultaneity, and an
   *existing* 3D universe in which events happen.**

## The two corrections, stated as rules

- Eradicate: *"objects, places **and** events/instants exist."* Replace with: *"events/instants **happen**
  — continuously — **while** objects and places exist."* (Space-time is a 4D manifold of events — the
  totality of happenings, not a thing that exists.)
- Eradicate the falsified inference: *"if two events appear to have happened at the same time, they must
  have happened simultaneously."* Replace with: *"that is a **local** phenomenon with no implications for
  true, global simultaneity"* — global simultaneity, absolute cosmic time, space, and rest being
  established empirically through cosmology.

## In sum

We got locked onto the wrong horns of two forks: the ontologically incoherent **"events/instants exist"**
horn, and the subversive **"relativity abhors an objective now"** horn. The arc shows how to correct both
and how the universe's own evidence *forces* the corrections. When evidence and conceptual clarity
converge, ignoring the correction is "a refusal to update one's prior commitments to reflect reality —
and for those who guide public interpretation of science, that refusal has consequences."
